term: "VRR auction"
match: ["VRR"]
short: "Variable Rate Repo: an RBI auction in which banks borrow money from the RBI for a fixed period."
---

The RBI holds VRR auctions when the banking system is short of cash. Banks bid for funds and the interest rate is set by the auction. It has little direct effect on ordinary people, but it shows how much cash is available in the market.
