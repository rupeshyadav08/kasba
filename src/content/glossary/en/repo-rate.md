termId: repo-rate
term: "Repo rate"
match: ["रेपो रेट", "repo rate"]
short: "The interest rate at which the RBI lends money to banks for short periods."
---

When the RBI raises the repo rate, money becomes costlier for banks, and EMIs on home and car loans usually go up. When it cuts the rate, loans are expected to get cheaper. Fixed deposit rates are affected too.
