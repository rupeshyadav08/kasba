term: "LOC (List of Candidates)"
match: ["LOC"]
short: "The official list of students who will sit a board exam, sent by the school to the board."
---

Boards such as CBSE ask schools for this list every year before exams. It has the student's name, parents' names, date of birth and subjects. The same details are later printed on the admit card and mark sheet, so the list must be filled in correctly and on time. For regular students the school fills it in, not the student.
