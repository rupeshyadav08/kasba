term: "eKYC"

short: "Online identity check through Aadhaar, needed to receive money from many government schemes."
---

eKYC confirms who you are through Aadhaar: either with an OTP sent to the mobile linked to Aadhaar, or with a fingerprint or iris scan (biometric) at a Common Service Centre (CSC). In schemes such as PM-KISAN, an incomplete eKYC can hold up the next instalment.
