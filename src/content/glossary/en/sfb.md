short: "RBI-licensed banks that mainly serve small businesses, farmers and low-income households."
---

Small finance banks offer the usual services: savings accounts, fixed deposits and loans. Deposits in them are covered by DICGC insurance up to the set limit, as in other banks. The RBI writes separate rules for them.
