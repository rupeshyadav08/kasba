Under the Information Technology Rules, 2021, Kasba has appointed a grievance officer based in India. If you believe a Kasba story breaches our Code of Ethics, please contact them.

## Process and timelines

1. Email the story link, the reason for your complaint, and your name and contact details.
2. We acknowledge the complaint within **24 hours**.
3. We decide on it and tell you within **15 days**.
4. If you are not satisfied, you will be able to appeal to a self-regulating body. Kasba is applying for membership of one; its name and contact will be listed here as soon as membership is confirmed.

For ordinary errors, see our [Corrections policy](/en/corrections).
