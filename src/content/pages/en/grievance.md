## Process and timelines

1. Email the story link, the reason for your complaint, and your name and contact details.
2. We acknowledge the complaint within **24 hours**.
3. We decide on it and tell you within **15 days**.
4. If you are not satisfied, you may appeal to the self-regulating body Kasba is a member of: [Self-regulating body name and contact].

For ordinary errors, see our [Corrections policy](/en/corrections).
