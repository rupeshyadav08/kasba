We will sometimes get things wrong. When we do, we fix it quickly and say so openly.

## How to report an error

Email [admin@kasba.co.in](mailto:admin@kasba.co.in) with the story link and what is wrong. Every story also has a "Report an error" link.

## What we do

- Factual error: we fix the story and add a "Correction" note at the top saying what changed and when.
- Minor spelling or language fixes: we may correct these without a note.
- Updates: when the official source adds information, we update the story and show the "Updated" time.
- Removal: only rarely, and we explain why at the same address.

We aim to reply within 24 hours. For formal complaints, see [Grievance redressal](/en/grievance).
