As a digital news publisher, Kasba follows the Code of Ethics in Part III and the Appendix of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. This includes:

1. The **Norms of Journalistic Conduct** of the Press Council of India
2. The **Programme Code** under section 5 of the Cable Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995
3. Not publishing content prohibited under any law in force

## What this means in practice

- Accuracy: every fact comes from an official source, linked in every story.
- Fairness: we keep news separate from opinion.
- Privacy and dignity: nothing about an individual without a sound basis.
- Corrections: mistakes are corrected openly ([Corrections policy](/en/corrections)).
- Complaints: a [grievance redressal](/en/grievance) process with time limits.
