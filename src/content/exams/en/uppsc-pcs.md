The **Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC)**, Prayagraj, handles the state's main recruitment: PCS, RO/ARO and other posts. PCS has three stages: preliminary, main and interview.

The number of posts, eligibility, age limits and dates are given in each notification; read it before you apply.
