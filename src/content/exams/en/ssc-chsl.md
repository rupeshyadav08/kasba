SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination) is held by the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)**. It recruits Class 12 pass candidates as clerks and data entry operators in central government offices.

The number of posts, eligibility, age limits and dates are given in each notification; read it before you apply.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
