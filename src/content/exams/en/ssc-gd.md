The SSC GD exam is held by the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)**. It fills Constable (General Duty) posts in the Central Armed Police Forces such as BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP and SSB, Rifleman posts in the Assam Rifles, and some other forces.

Selection usually runs through a computer-based test (CBT), a physical efficiency and standards test (PET/PST), and a medical. Eligibility, age limits and vacancies are set out in each year's notification.
