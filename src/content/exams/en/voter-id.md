The **Election Commission of India** maintains the electoral roll and issues the voter ID (EPIC). To vote, your name must be on your area's electoral roll; having the card alone is not enough.

Adding a name, corrections and downloads are all free. During a roll revision, the commission announces dates for claims and objections.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
