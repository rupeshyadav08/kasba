From December to January, **dense fog** and **cold waves** are common in north India. The IMD issues district-wise **yellow, orange and red** warnings.

Dense fog delays trains and flights and raises the risk of road accidents. In a cold wave, take special care of elderly people and children.

As soon as a new warning for UP, Bihar, Delhi or MP comes out, Kasba adds its story to this page.
