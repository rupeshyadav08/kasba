When Delhi-NCR's air turns bad in winter, the **Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM)** puts stages of **GRAP** (Graded Response Action Plan) in force. The higher the AQI, the stricter the curbs.

A change of stage can change rules on construction, some vehicles and schools. When a stage is lifted, measures of the earlier stages can stay in force.

As soon as a new CAQM notice comes out, Kasba adds its story to this page.
