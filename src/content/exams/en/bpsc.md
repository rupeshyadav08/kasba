The **Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC)**, Patna, handles major recruitment for the Bihar government. The Combined Competitive Exam and the teacher recruitment exam are the best known.

The number of posts, eligibility, age limits and dates are given in each notification; read it before you apply.
