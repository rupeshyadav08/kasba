**Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana** has two parts. **Gramin (PMAY-G)** pays homeless families and those in kutcha houses, in instalments, to build a pucca house. **Urban (PMAY-U)** is for poor and middle-income city families.

Avoid anyone who asks for money to get you a house: there is no fee to be included.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
