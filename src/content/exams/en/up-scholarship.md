The Uttar Pradesh government's **Scholarship and Fee Reimbursement** scheme gives eligible students a scholarship and reimburses fees. Applications are made online every year.

Dates for applying, correcting and submitting the form at the institution are in each year's schedule.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
