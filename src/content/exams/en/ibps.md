The **Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)** holds common recruitment exams (CRP) for public sector banks and regional rural banks: PO, clerk, specialist officer and RRB.

The number of posts, eligibility, age limits and dates are given in each notification; read it before you apply.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
