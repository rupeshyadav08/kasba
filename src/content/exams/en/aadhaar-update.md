Aadhaar is issued by the **Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI)**. Banks, ration, pensions and most government schemes need a working Aadhaar and a linked mobile number.

UIDAI changes update fees and free-update dates from time to time. See the stories on this page for the latest. Beware of calls asking for an OTP in the name of Aadhaar update.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
