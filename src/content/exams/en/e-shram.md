**e-Shram** is the national database of unorganised workers, run by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. Registration gives an e-Shram card with a 12-digit UAN, which makes it easier to deliver social security schemes to the worker.

You need Aadhaar, an Aadhaar-linked mobile number and a bank account to register.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
