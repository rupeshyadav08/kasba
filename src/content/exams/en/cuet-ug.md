**CUET (UG)** is the common entrance test for undergraduate courses at central universities such as Delhi University, BHU and the University of Allahabad. It is conducted by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)**.

Subjects, dates and fee are in each year's Information Bulletin. After the score, each university admits through its own process.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
