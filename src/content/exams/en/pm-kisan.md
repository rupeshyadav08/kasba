**Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)** is a central government scheme that pays eligible farmer families **₹6,000 a year**, in **three instalments of ₹2,000**, straight into their bank account.

To receive an instalment, **eKYC must be complete** and the bank account must be linked to Aadhaar. eKYC can be done on the PM-KISAN portal with an OTP, or with a fingerprint at a nearby Common Service Centre (CSC). State governments verify land records.

When a new instalment, an eKYC deadline or a rule change is announced, Kasba adds a story about it to this page.
