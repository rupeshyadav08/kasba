**Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS)** runs Kendriya Vidyalayas across India. Admission, teacher recruitment and rule notices come out on its website every year.

In November 2026 KVS launched new digital portals for parents, teachers and former students.

As soon as a new KVS notice comes out, Kasba adds its story to this page.
