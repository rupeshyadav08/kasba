The **Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)** manages provident fund (PF) and pension for salaried workers. Each month both employee and employer deposit a share of wages.

Everything about PF works through the UAN, so keep it active and link Aadhaar, PAN and your bank account to it.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
