**PM Vishwakarma** is the central government's scheme for traditional artisans and craftspeople. Artisans in 18 trades get an ID card, skill training, help for tools and low-interest collateral-free loans.

Only one member per family can benefit. Registration is through a Common Service Centre (CSC).

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
