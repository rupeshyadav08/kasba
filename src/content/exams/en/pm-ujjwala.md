**Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)** gives women of poor households a deposit-free LPG connection, so that homes are free of smoke from wood or coal.

To keep receiving the subsidy, complete Aadhaar e-KYC at your gas agency.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
