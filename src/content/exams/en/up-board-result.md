The **Board of Secondary Education, Uttar Pradesh (UPMSP)**, known as UP Board, holds the High School (Class 10) and Intermediate (Class 12) exams. It is one of the largest school boards in the country.

Board exams are usually held in February and March, and results come out around April. Notices on the exam schedule, practical dates, compartment exams and scrutiny are published on upmsp.edu.in.

Kasba checks the board's website every 30 minutes, so a story about a result or a new notice reaches this page quickly.
