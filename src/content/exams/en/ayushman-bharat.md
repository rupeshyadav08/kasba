**Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)** gives an eligible family **cashless treatment of up to ₹5 lakh a year**, for hospitalisation at empanelled government and private hospitals.

The card is free. At the hospital, meet the Ayushman Mitra and show your card or Aadhaar. Avoid anyone who asks for money for a card.
