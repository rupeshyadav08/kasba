**Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)** pays farmers when crops are damaged by natural calamity, pests or disease. The farmer pays a very small premium: 2% for kharif, 1.5% for rabi.

Insurance must be taken afresh each season, and the state sets the last date.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
