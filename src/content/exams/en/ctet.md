CTET (Central Teacher Eligibility Test) is held by the **Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)**. It is the eligibility test for teaching posts in Kendriya Vidyalayas, Navodaya Vidyalayas and many other schools.

It has two papers: **Paper I** for teachers of classes 1 to 5 and **Paper II** for classes 6 to 8. Candidates can take both. The CTET certificate is now valid for life.

The exam is usually held twice a year. Notices on applications, admit cards and results are published on ctet.nic.in, and Kasba adds them here in plain language.
