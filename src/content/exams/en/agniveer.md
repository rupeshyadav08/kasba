Under the **Agnipath scheme**, young people are recruited into the Army, Navy and Air Force for four years. They are called **Agniveers**. Each service recruits separately and has its own website.

Age, eligibility and dates are in each notification; read it before applying. Beware of touts who ask for money: applications are made only on the official website.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
