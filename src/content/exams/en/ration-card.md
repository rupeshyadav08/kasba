A ration card is the document for getting subsidised or free grain under the **National Food Security Act (NFSA)**. State food departments issue it, so applications and corrections go through the state portal.

To keep getting ration, every member on the card needs **e-KYC**. e-KYC is free.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
