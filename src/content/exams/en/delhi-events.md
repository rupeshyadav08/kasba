Delhi hosts government fairs, exhibitions and festivals all year: the trade fair at **Bharat Mandapam**, craft and tribal fairs at **Dilli Haat**, and food and khadi festivals at **Sunder Nursery** and **Connaught Place**.

This page gives dates, venues, timings and ticket details of such events from official notices.

As soon as a new event is announced, Kasba adds its story to this page.
