The **Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC)**, Lucknow, recruits for the state's Group C posts. For most of them, candidates first take the **PET** and then the main exam for the post.

The number of posts, eligibility, age limits and dates are given in each notification; read it before you apply.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
