The **Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)** holds the Civil Services Examination every year to recruit for the IAS, IPS, IFS and other central services. It has three stages: prelims, mains and interview.

UPSC also holds CDS, NDA and other exams. The number of posts, eligibility, age limits and dates are given in each notification; read it before you apply.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
