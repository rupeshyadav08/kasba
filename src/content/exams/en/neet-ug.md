**NEET (UG)** is the national entrance exam for MBBS, BDS and AYUSH courses in medical colleges. It is conducted by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)**.

The exam date, fee and eligibility are in each year's Information Bulletin. After the result, admission is through counselling.
