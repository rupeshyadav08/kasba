**VB-G RAM G** (Viksit Bharat - Rozgar aur Aajivika Guarantee Mission, Rural) replaced **MGNREGA** and has been in force since **1 July 2026**. Adult members of rural households get a legal guarantee of **125 days** of wage work in every financial year.

Attendance is marked on the **NMMS app**, which now uses face authentication to stop fake attendance. Grievance officers handle complaints at gram panchayat, block and district level.
