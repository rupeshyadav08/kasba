SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level Examination) is held by the **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)**. It fills graduate-level Group B and Group C posts in central government ministries and departments.

The exam is computer-based and held in tiers. The number of posts, eligibility, age limits and dates are given in each notification; read it before you apply.

When a new official notice about it comes out, Kasba adds the story to this page.
