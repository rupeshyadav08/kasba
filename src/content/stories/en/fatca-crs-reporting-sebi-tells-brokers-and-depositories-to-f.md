## What has happened

SEBI issued a circular on 11 November 2026. It asks all registered intermediaries, stock exchanges and depositories to carry out FATCA and CRS reporting in line with the new Income Tax Rules, 2026.

## What changed in the law

According to the circular, the Income Tax Act, 2025 and the Income Tax Rules, 2026 replaced the Income Tax Act, 1961 and the Income Tax Rules, 1962 with effect from 1 April 2026. SEBI's earlier circulars of 2015 and 2024 were issued under the 1961 Act and 1962 Rules. Now FATCA/CRS reporting is governed by Section 508, Rules 238, 239 and 240, and Form 166.

## Who it covers

The obligation applies to entities that qualify as Reporting Financial Institutions (RFIs). RFI is defined in Rule 238(8) and Financial Institution in Rule 238(3). The circular says all registered intermediaries that are RFIs must ensure compliance with Rules 238 to 240.

## What intermediaries must do

Intermediaries must build AEOI reporting and due diligence requirements into their customer onboarding and KYC frameworks. The circular lists four things to review and update: self-certification collection procedures that capture the validity indicator; Controlling Person identification systems that capture roles such as settlor, trustee, protector, beneficiary or other; account classification systems that capture account type and pre-existing/new status; and Form 166 XML generation software that includes all new mandatory fields. The circular says missing required fields will cause validation errors on the Income Tax portal.

Intermediaries must also upload the FATCA and CRS certification obtained from clients onto the systems of KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs). The circular states clearly that the onus of obtaining and reporting the certification, and of related compliance, lies solely with the intermediary.

## Where to get help

The circular refers to the CBDT's Guidance Note on FATCA and CRS dated 24 July 2026. The IT Rules, 2026 and the guidance note are available on the Income Tax Department website. The circular itself is on the SEBI website under 'Legal → Circulars'.
