## What has happened

In a press release dated November 23, 2026, RBI said the next date for premature redemption of Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Scheme 2021-22 Series I is November 25, 2026. On that date, redemption will be at ₹12,612 per unit.

## How the price was worked out

According to RBI, the redemption price of SGB is based on the simple average of the closing price of 999 purity gold of the previous three business days, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA). For this redemption, those three business days were November 19, 20 and 23, 2026.

## When early redemption is allowed

As per the government notification, premature redemption is permitted after five years from the bond's issue date, on a date when interest is payable. This tranche was issued on May 25, 2021, so the next such date is November 25, 2026.

## Who is affected

This matters to investors holding SGB 2021-22 Series I bonds who want to exit after five years. People who did not buy this tranche are not directly affected.

## What holders can do

If you hold this tranche and want to redeem on November 25, 2026, contact your bank or the institution you bought the bonds from. You can check the price and date in RBI's press release.
