## What has happened

BCCI's Women's Selection Committee has announced the India U19 squad for the ACC U19 Women's Asia Cup. The tournament is to be held in the UAE. BCCI issued the notice on November 28, 2026.

## Who is in the squad

The squad has 15 players. Bhavika Ahire is the captain and also a wicketkeeper. Anaadi Tagde has been named vice-captain. Shreya S Chavan is the second wicketkeeper.

The other players are Ira Jadhav, Nidhi Mahto, Purva Siwach, Mahak Narwase, Maitri Maniar, Ishwari Awasare, Deeya Yadav, Samaira Raghav, Kumari Palak, Uma Kashvi, Achsah Parmar and Palak Singh.
