## What changes in Delhi

According to IMD's press release dated 24 November 2026, Delhi is expected to have mainly clear skies on 24 November, with the maximum temperature likely in the range of 24-26°C. Mist or shallow fog is likely at a few places on the mornings of 25 and 26 November.

The release says the minimum temperature is likely to be 09-11°C on 25 and 26 November and 08-10°C on 27 November.

## Where dense fog is warned

IMD says dense fog is very likely over Haryana, Chandigarh and Delhi on 26-27 November. Dense fog warnings are also given for Punjab during 24-27 November, Himachal Pradesh during 24-26 November and Chhattisgarh on 24 November.

The release notes that in the past 24 hours, visibility was reported at 200 metres in Gaya, Bihar.

## What the fog can affect

According to IMD, dense fog may affect some airports, highways and railway routes, and driving can become difficult with slower journey times. Power lines may also trip in very dense fog.

On health, the release says dense fog contains particulate matter and other pollutants which, if exposed to, get lodged in the lungs and can increase episodes of wheezing, coughing and shortness of breath. People with asthma, bronchitis and other lung problems may be affected by long exposure, and pollutants can irritate the eyes, causing redness or swelling.

## What precautions to take

IMD advises using fog lights while driving and staying in touch with airlines, railways and state transport for journey schedules.

For health, the release advises avoiding going out unless there is an emergency and covering the face.

## Where to see the full forecast

The release links to the national weather forecast at mausam.imd.gov.in and to district-wise warnings on the districtWiseWarningGIS page.
