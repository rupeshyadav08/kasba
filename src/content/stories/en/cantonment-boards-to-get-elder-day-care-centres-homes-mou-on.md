## What has happened

The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, and the Directorate General of Defence Estates under the Ministry of Defence will sign a memorandum of understanding (MoU) in New Delhi on 16 November. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Minister of Social Justice and Empowerment Dr. Virendra Kumar will be present.

The aim is to create a framework of cooperation between the two organisations for implementing senior citizen welfare schemes under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY) in cantonment boards across the country.

## Who benefits

Under the partnership, the infrastructure and administrative network of cantonment boards will be used, while the department will provide financial assistance and technical expertise for the schemes. This is expected to give senior citizens in cantonment areas access to care, support and a life of dignity.

According to N. Sajeesh Kumar, Joint Secretary in the ministry, financial support will also be provided for residential senior citizen homes of 25 to 50 beds, as needed. This is meant to strengthen institutional care facilities for the elderly.

## What changes

New day-care centres and other facilities for senior citizens will be set up in cantonment boards under the MoU. Day-care centres already run by cantonment boards will also be strengthened with the department's support.

Another key part is training young people as caregivers for the elderly. Skill training will be given through the department's training and partner organisations, which is expected to build a trained caregiving workforce and create new employment opportunities for youth.

## Awareness camps too

Awareness camps will be held to inform senior citizens about the ministry's welfare schemes, with participation from public representatives, leading social workers and local communities. Students in cantonment board schools will take a pledge, and activities will be held to encourage dialogue between young people and senior citizens.

## Why this is being done

According to the release, about 10 crore people in India were aged 60 or above in 2011, and this number is projected to reach about 23 crore by 2036. Changing family structures and growing urbanisation are making day-care centres near home and trained caregivers more important.

The MoU also takes forward the 'Vayo Samman National Campaign', a national drive launched by the ministry on 4 November 2026 to strengthen ties between generations.

## What is the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana

It is the department's main scheme for the welfare of senior citizens, aimed at helping them live healthy, empowered, dignified and self-reliant lives. Its components include an integrated programme for senior citizens, senior citizen homes, training for elderly care, the national helpline Elderline-14567, the Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana and other initiatives that promote active and healthy ageing.
