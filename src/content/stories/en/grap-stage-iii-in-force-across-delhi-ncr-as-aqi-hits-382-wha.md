## What happened

On 6 November 2026, the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) issued its daily Air Quality Index (AQI) bulletin. It recorded Delhi's daily average AQI at 382, which falls in the 'very poor' category.

The same day, the GRAP sub-committee of the CAQM met. It reviewed air quality in the Delhi-NCR region along with forecasts from IMD/IITM. The sub-committee observed that unfavourable meteorological conditions were preventing pollutants from dispersing properly, which worsened air quality.

## What has changed

The sub-committee decided to implement the Stage-III action plan under the existing GRAP across the entire NCR with immediate effect. This applies together with all the Stage-I and Stage-II measures already in force.

All concerned NCR agencies, including the state pollution control boards (PCBs) and DPCC, have been advised to strictly enforce the measures under all three stages during this period.

## Who is affected

This step concerns the health of ordinary people living in Delhi and the NCR. IMD/IITM forecasts suggested the AQI could reach the 'severe' category in the coming days, so the plan is meant to stop air quality from worsening further.

## What you should do

The sub-committee has urged citizens to follow the measures listed in the Stage-I, Stage-II and Stage-III citizen charters and to cooperate in implementing them.

These include: walk or cycle for short trips; choose less polluting means of transport; combine small trips to reduce the number of journeys; postpone private gatherings that increase traffic and the risk of exposure to pollution outdoors; and do not use coal and wood for heating.

House owners may provide electric heaters to security staff or other employees working at their premises, to avoid burning bio-waste, wood or municipal solid waste (MSW) in the open. Adopt hybrid schooling in primary schools and work from home when instructed.

## What next

The Commission is closely monitoring the situation and will review air quality regularly in the coming days. The current GRAP order is available on the Commission's official website, caqm.nic.in.
