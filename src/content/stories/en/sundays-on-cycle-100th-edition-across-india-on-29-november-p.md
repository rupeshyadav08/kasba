## What happened

At the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, on 15 November 2026, India's Fit India Movement presented the 'Sundays on Cycle' initiative. The session was held on the theme 'Sundays on Cycle: from a weekly ride to a lifestyle'. India invited all countries to hold at least one community cycle ride in the coming year.

## What it is about

Sundays on Cycle was described as a practical example of sustainable living under Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment). Mayank Srivastava, Deputy Director General of the Sports Authority of India, said building cycle lanes is not enough; people will use them only if they choose to cycle. He said that if cycling becomes a habit before dependence on cars grows, emissions can be prevented from being created at all.

## Who benefits

The initiative is for ordinary people who want to cycle in their town or city. The ride has been held every Sunday since 22 December 2024, and no Sunday has passed without one. About 37 lakh people have joined at more than 30,000 places in India. On World Bicycle Day this year the target was 1 lakh cyclists, but about 4 lakh people took part.

## What happens next

The 100th edition of Sundays on Cycle will be held across India on 29 November. Fit India has said it will share a Sundays on Cycle toolkit to help other cities start their own cycling programmes. People have been invited to cycle in their own communities that day.

## Where the rides are held

The programme is organised by the Sports Authority of India under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports. Partners include the Cycling Federation of India, Navvrati Foundation, FitSphere, Samakrit Health Centre, My Bharat and others. Rides are held every Sunday in all states and union territories, including at Sports Authority of India regional centres, National Centres of Excellence, SAI training centres, Khelo India State Centres of Excellence and Khelo India centres.
