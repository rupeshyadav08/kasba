## What has happened

Indian Railways has opened online applications for recruitment to Station Master posts. The recruitment is being held under Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 08/2026. According to the information released by the Ministry of Railways, a total of 1,265 Station Master posts will be filled across the country.

The application window opened on 27 October 2026 and will close on 25 November 2026.

## What has changed

The Railway Board has decided that from 2026, Station Master recruitment will be notified separately. This means a dedicated notice will be issued for this post, clearly setting out the eligibility requirements, the examination process and how vacancies are distributed. This gives candidates all the key information in one place.

## Who benefits

The recruitment is for people who have completed graduation. According to the notice, any graduate can apply for this post. The vacancies are spread across several railway regions, so candidates from different parts of the country can apply for these posts.

## Where the vacancies are

CEN 08/2026 gives the RRB-wise vacancy distribution. RRB Mumbai has the highest number, with 177 vacancies. Jammu-Srinagar has 122 and Chennai has 119 vacancies. The remaining vacancies are spread across other Railway Recruitment Boards and regions, and the full details can be seen in the official notification.

## How to apply

Candidates are advised to carefully read the eligibility conditions, vacancy details and application guidelines before submitting the form. The online application portal is rrbapply.gov.in.

The detailed notification, exam syllabus and RRB-wise vacancy details are available on the Railway Recruitment Board website, rrb.indianrailways.gov.in. For updates and instructions related to the recruitment, candidates should rely only on official RRB communication.
