## What has been announced

According to a Railway Ministry release, Indian Railways is starting a new Vande Bharat Sleeper Express service between Varanasi and New Delhi. Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath and Union Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw will flag off the service from Varanasi on 31 October. The release is dated 30 October 2026.

## Where the train will stop

The train will run from Varanasi via Prayagraj, Kanpur Central, Aligarh and Ghaziabad to New Delhi. The release says these stations connect major urban centres and also give rail access to nearby districts.

## Timings

The train will leave Varanasi at 8:50 pm and reach New Delhi at 6:10 am the next day. On the return journey, it will leave New Delhi at 9:40 pm and reach Varanasi at 7:10 am the next day. This lets passengers travel overnight and arrive in the morning.

## What changes for travellers

The release says the Vande Bharat Sleeper will save 55 minutes on the Varanasi-New Delhi journey and 35 minutes on the New Delhi-Varanasi journey compared with the existing Shiv Ganga Express. The service will run six days a week. The train from Varanasi will not run on Friday and the train from New Delhi will not run on Saturday.

## Who benefits

The release says the service will be especially useful for pilgrims, tourists, students, working people and families who often travel between eastern Uttar Pradesh and the National Capital Region. Varanasi draws pilgrims, domestic and foreign tourists and students through the year, and better overnight rail links with New Delhi will help those travelling for religious, educational, business, administrative and tourism purposes.

## Facilities on board

The release says the Vande Bharat Sleeper has been designed for long overnight journeys. It lists features such as a regenerative braking system to save energy, arrangements to reduce noise inside coaches, CCTV cameras, emergency alarm push buttons, smoke and fire detection, and a train collision avoidance system (TCAS).

For passengers, it mentions special berths and toilets for passengers with disabilities, space for luggage, charging sockets, Type-A and Type-C USB ports, Wi-Fi and vacuum toilets. According to the release, the first air-conditioned class will have shower facilities with hot water.

## What is not known yet

The release does not say when bookings will open, what the fare will be, or from which date the train will run regularly. Check official railway sources or your station for those details.
