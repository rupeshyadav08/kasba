## What has happened

The Rural Development Ministry issued information on the Lakhpati Didi initiative on 15 November 2026. It said that by June 2026, 3.76 crore rural self-help group (SHG) women had become Lakhpati Didis. Another 5.70 crore women were identified as potential Lakhpati Didis.

## Who is a Lakhpati Didi

A Lakhpati Didi is an SHG member whose household earns at least Rs 1 lakh a year. The whole family's income is counted, not an individual's. Income from farm and non-farm work is included. It is counted after expenses and should hold steady across farming seasons or business cycles.

## How the initiative works

It runs under Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana-National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM). DAY-NRLM organises women from poor rural families into self-help groups, where they save and connect with banks, loans, training and markets. As of 12 November 2026, DAY-NRLM had organised about 10.13 crore members into 94.40 lakh groups.

Identification uses the LokOS platform and the Digital Aajeevika Register. Trained community resource persons (CRPs) prepare business plans for families. Women then get coordinated support for three years, including training, finance, livelihood assets and market access.

## How the target grew

The initiative was announced on 15 August 2023 with an initial target of two crore women. In February 2024 it was raised to three crore. That target was achieved ahead of time by December 2025. In February 2026 the target was raised to six crore Lakhpati Didis by March 2029.

## What the evaluation found

An IIM Ahmedabad evaluation spoke to 6,531 people across 17 states and union territories. It found a 61 per cent rise in the average monthly household income of Lakhpati Didi families and a 61.7 per cent rise in average monthly savings.

## What next

The notice gives no application date or new form. For more information, see the Rural Development Ministry's website lakhpatididi.gov.in or ask your village SHG.
