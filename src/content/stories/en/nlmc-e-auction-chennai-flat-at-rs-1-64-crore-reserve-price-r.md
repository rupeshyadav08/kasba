The National Land Monetisation Corporation (NLMC), a fully government-owned company under the Department of Public Enterprises in the Finance Ministry, has announced an e-auction of a residential flat on Poonamallee High Road in Kilpauk, Chennai. The Request for Proposal (RFP) number NLMC/2026/01 was issued on 5 November 2026.

## What has changed

NLMC issued Corrigendum-1 on 13 November 2026. Through it, the pre-bid meeting has been moved from 20 November 2026 to 28 November 2026 at 11 am. The corporation has made clear that all other terms and conditions of the RFP, including the remaining timelines, stay the same.

## Key details of the auction

The property is in Kilpauk, Chennai, and its reserve price is Rs 1.64 crore. The earnest money deposit (EMD) is Rs 50,000. The last date to register and deposit the EMD is 7 December 2026. The e-auction will be held on the portal https://eauction.enivida.com.

## What the pre-bid meeting covers

At this meeting, prospective bidders can get clarifications on the property, eligibility requirements, the registration process, the EMD, the bidding process and other provisions of the RFP. NLMC has said that interested buyers may also visit and inspect the property, subject to the arrangements and conditions set by the corporation, before taking part in the auction.

## Who this is for

The auction is for people who want to buy or invest in a flat in one of Chennai's well-developed residential areas. It is part of an NLMC initiative to e-auction 12 residential flats located in Prayagraj (Uttar Pradesh) and Chennai (Tamil Nadu). It has no direct effect on ordinary readers.

## What to do next

Those who wish to take part should read the RFP and Corrigendum-1 carefully and go through the property documents. Full details are available on NLMC's website https://nlmc.dpe.gov.in and the e-auction portal https://eauction.enivida.com.

## What is NLMC

NLMC is a fully government-owned company under the Department of Public Enterprises in the Finance Ministry. It was set up to help put surplus and under-used land and building assets of central public sector enterprises and other government bodies to productive use through transparent e-auctions.
