## What has happened

SSC issued a notice on 25 November 2026. It says that CRPF has sent a notice about the PET and PST for the Constable (GD) in CAPFs and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination 2026. That notice mentions a change in the PET/PST centre of candidates in Manipur.

## Who it applies to

The change concerns candidates from Manipur whose CBE result for this recruitment was declared on 12 October 2026. According to SSC, CRPF is the Nodal CAPF for this examination and has scheduled the PET/PST. The PET/PST is being held from 20 November 2026 onwards.

## What candidates should do

Candidates whose centre has changed should check the notice issued by CRPF. SSC has attached that notice for the information of candidates. Confirm your centre and timing before reaching the test venue.

## What else to know

The notice mentions only the change of centre in Manipur. It says nothing about centres changing for candidates in other states. For any detail about your centre, rely on the official notices from SSC or CRPF.
