## What has happened

The National Land Monetisation Corporation (NLMC) has issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) for the e-auction of 11 government-owned residential flats in Prayagraj. The RFP was released on 5 November 2026. NLMC is a wholly government-owned company under the Department of Public Enterprises, Ministry of Finance.

According to the notice, the auction invites prospective home buyers, individual investors and other eligible bidders to take part in a transparent and competitive online auction for properties in well-developed residential areas of Prayagraj.

## Which flats are on offer

The auction covers 11 newly built flats across three housing schemes. Juhi Awas Yojana on Muir Road has 5 flats (2 BHK) with a built-up area of about 107 to 108 square metres. Parivartan Awas Yojana on Thornhill Road has 4 flats (2 BHK and 3 BHK) with an area of about 106 to 139 square metres. Agnipath Awas Yojana on TB Sapru Road, Civil Lines has 2 flats (3 BHK) with built-up areas of about 96 and 112 square metres respectively.

The notice says these properties are in areas with easy access to educational institutions, health facilities, commercial establishments and other essential civic amenities.

## What are the dates

The pre-bid meeting will be held on 20 November 2026 at 11:00 am. It gives bidders a chance to get clarifications on the auction process, eligibility conditions, required documents and payment process.

The last date to register and deposit the Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) is 5:00 pm on 7 December 2026. The e-auction will run from 11:00 am to 5:00 pm on 11 December 2026.

## What should interested bidders do

Interested bidders are advised to study the RFP carefully and complete all formalities before the deadline. The RFP, detailed property information, eligibility requirements, EMD provisions and bidding process are available on the e-auction portal https://eauction.enivida.com.

## Where to get help

For more information, contact Shri Lolark Shukla (+91 94204 79930) or Shri Ankush Rana (+91 95403 71598), or email am-nlmc[at]gov[dot]in.

## Why NLMC exists

NLMC was set up to further the central government's asset monetisation objectives by facilitating the monetisation of surplus land and building assets of central public sector enterprises and other government entities. The company says it aims to use the economic potential of surplus public assets and promote their productive use.
