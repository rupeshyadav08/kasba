## What has been released

SSC has released the final answer keys of the Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026. Question papers cum response sheets have been uploaded along with them. This is stated in a notice issued by SSC on 3 November 2026.

The result of the computer based exam had already been declared on 12.10.2026. Candidates can now view their answer key and response sheet.

## Where to see the marks

The marks of candidates who appeared in the computer based exam have also been made available on the Commission's website, https://ssc.gov.in. To see the marks, candidates have to log in with their Registered ID and Password.

## How long the facility stays open

The facility will be available from 03.11.2026 (5 pm) to 17.11.2026 (5 pm). After that, these documents will not be available on the website.

## What candidates should do

Candidates should take a printout of their final answer key, question paper cum response sheet and scorecard. SSC has clearly said these will not be available after the time limit.

To view the answer key and response sheet, candidates have to log in with their Roll Number and Password. SSC has given a link for this on its website.
