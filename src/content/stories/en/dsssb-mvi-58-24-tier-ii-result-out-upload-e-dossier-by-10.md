## What has happened

DSSSB issued a notice on 19 November 2026 declaring the marks and result of the Tier-II exam for Motor Vehicle Inspector (post code 58/24). It also released the list of candidates shortlisted to upload e-dossiers.

The post is in the Transport Department, Government of NCT of Delhi. According to the notice, there were 20 vacancies for the post: UR-08, EWS-04, OBC-04, SC-01 and ST-03, including PwBD vacancies.

## Exam and shortlisting

The Tier-I exam was held on 4 October 2025. A total of 199 candidates who scored at or above the cut-off marks in their categories were provisionally shortlisted for Tier-II. The Tier-II exam was held on 1 July 2026, and 179 of these 199 candidates appeared.

Based on Tier-II performance, 62 candidates have been shortlisted for the e-dossier. The notice gives the minimum marks for calling e-dossiers in each category, such as 142.35 for UR, 120.95 for EWS, 132.39 for OBC, 132.58 for SC, 101.44 for ST and separate marks for the PwBD categories.

## When and how to upload the e-dossier

Shortlisted candidates must log in to the e-dossier module in their individual accounts in the OARS module and upload all required documents. These include education certificates, professional or experience certificates, degree and mark sheets, caste certificate, admit cards and proof of government service or Ex-Serviceman status, as applicable.

The e-dossier link will be active from 26 November 2026 to 10 December 2026. The notice clearly states that if a candidate fails to upload documents in this period, the candidature will be rejected and no further opportunity will be given on any ground. Candidates are also being informed by SMS and email.

## If there is a technical glitch

If a candidate cannot upload documents due to a technical glitch in the OARS module, they may report it to the DSSSB helpdesk email helpdesk-dsssb@delhi.gov.in with supporting evidence such as screenshots within 48 hours after the e-dossier period ends. If the glitch is substantiated, an additional three days may be granted, but only to those who report within the specified timeline.

## What next

The notice also says that being asked to upload an e-dossier does not give any right to selection. Final selection will be made purely on merit against the notified vacancies, provided the candidate fulfils all eligibility conditions. The shortlisting is also subject to the outcome of any pending court cases.
