The Union Cabinet has approved an extension of the Aqua Line under the Noida Metro Rail project. The extension will run from Noida Sector 51 to Greater Noida Sector 4. According to the notice, the corridor will be 8.449 km long and will have 5 elevated stations. The approval was given at a Cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi.

## What changes

The notice says that once this corridor opens, Noida and Greater Noida will have an active metro network of 71.878 km. The new line will also offer an interchange with the Blue Line at Noida Sector 61, so passengers can change lines there to continue their journey.

## Who benefits

The notice says this phase will connect several major areas directly to the metro network. These include Gaur City Mall, H and S Mall and office space, Blue Sapphire Mall and commercial space in Greater Noida Sector 4, IKEA in Noida Sector 51, Anthurium Mall in Sector 73, offices of Tech Mahindra and TCL Tech in Sector 60, the Oil Industry Development Board in Sector 73, and Golden Eye office and commercial space in Greater Noida Sector 4.

The notice also names Delhi Public School in Noida Sector 122, Kailash Hospital, Rama Hospital in Sectors 70 and 62, Cloud 9 Hospital in Sector 51, and housing societies such as Cleo County Residency in Sector 122 and Gaur City 1 and 2 in Greater Noida Sector 4.

## Effect on roads and the environment

According to the notice, the extension is expected to reduce traffic congestion in Noida and Greater Noida. Easier movement of vehicles on the roads could cut travel time and improve road safety. The notice also says the growing metro network will mean a significant fall in carbon emissions compared with petrol- and diesel-run transport.

## What people need to do

Nothing for now. This is only an approval for the line extension. The notice gives no date for construction to begin or for trains to start, and no fare or application details.
