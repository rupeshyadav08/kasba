## What has happened

The National Testing Agency (NTA) issued a notice on 7 November 2026 inviting online applications to empanel observers at its examination centres. The last date to apply is 6 December 2026, and applications are accepted only on the portal observermanagement.nta.gov.in.

NTA conducts national-level entrance and eligibility exams such as NEET (UG), JEE (Main), UGC-NET, CSIR-NET and CUET (UG and PG) at several thousand centres across the country and, increasingly, overseas. On each exam day, NTA posts an independent observer at every centre, separate from the City Coordinator and Centre Superintendent, who reports directly to NTA.

## Why this pool is being built

According to the notice, observers have so far been arranged exam by exam, which led to last-minute shortfalls and substitutions. NTA now wants a standing, pre-verified pool spread across regions, so deployment orders can be issued well in advance and observers get enough time for briefing and travel.

## Who can apply

The notice lists three categories. Category A covers retired Central or State Government officers above Pay Level 8 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix, aged between 60 and 65 on the last date of application, engaged on a 2-year contract.

Category B covers retired Armed Forces or CAPF personnel of the rank of Subedar Major or equivalent and above, up to 65 years of age, with no lower age bar. Category C covers retired or serving teachers and professors of government schools, colleges and universities, including professors, principals, PGTs, lecturers and certain other government posts named in the notice. Retired teachers must be 60 to 65 years old; serving teachers have no upper age limit as long as they are in service and have an NOC from their employer.

Every applicant must have no pending disciplinary or vigilance proceeding, must be physically fit for observer duties, and must be willing to travel outside their headquarters if required.

## Duties and pay

An empanelled observer attends a mandatory briefing meeting a day before the exam and gets ₹2,000 plus ₹1,000 conveyance, totalling ₹3,000. On the exam day, the payment is ₹3,000 plus ₹1,000 conveyance, totalling ₹4,000. The fixed ₹1,000 conveyance applies only to same-city deployment.

Duties include checking candidate identity, frisking protocol, the seating plan, distribution and collection of test material, and CCTV and power backup arrangements. Any irregularity or malpractice must be reported directly to NTA, and a report has to be filed on the NTA portal within the notified time.

## Documents and next steps

Applicants must provide a photo identity card, the latest appointment or service certificate, and a self-declaration. Serving applicants need a vigilance clearance certificate, while retired applicants need a police verification or character certificate not older than 12 months. No deployment order is issued until all documents are on record.

An observer removed from the pool will be told the reasons and may make one representation within 30 days. The full notice is available on nta.ac.in.
