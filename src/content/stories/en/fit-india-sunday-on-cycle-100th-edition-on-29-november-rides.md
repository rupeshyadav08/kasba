## What has been announced

The Sports Ministry has said the 100th edition of Fit India Sunday on Cycle will be held on 29 November. This edition is dedicated to celebrating the Olympic movement in India, with the theme #IndiaReadyForOlympics.

According to the notice, Union Minister for Youth Affairs and Sports Dr Mansukh Mandaviya will lead the cycling event from Pimpri-Chinchwad in Pune, where about 50,000 cyclists will take part.

## What has changed

The initiative began in December 2024 with only 250 locations and 500 participants in New Delhi. The ministry says it has grown into a public movement in two years. Its 99th edition, held on the theme of Constitution Day, saw lakhs of citizens take part at more than 26,000 locations across the country.

For the 100th edition, young people will cycle in every district with the support of My Bharat. Events will also be held at 100 prominent iconic places across the country, where 100 Indian Olympic athletes will join.

## Who benefits

The campaign is for people who want to make cycling and fitness a habit. The ministry says the aim is to inspire young people to make sport and fitness part of their lifestyle.

The campaign draws on the Prime Minister's call of 'Fitness ka Dose, Aadha Ghanta Roz' - at least half an hour of physical fitness every day. The notice also mentions special events on themes such as the fight against obesity, Nasha Mukt Bharat and tackling pollution.

## What to do next

If you want to join a cycling event in your district, keep 29 November in mind. The notice does not say where or at what time district events will be held, so check with your district sports office or local organisers.
