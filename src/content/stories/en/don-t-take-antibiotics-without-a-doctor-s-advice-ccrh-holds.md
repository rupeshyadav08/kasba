## What happened

The Central Council for Research in Homoeopathy (CCRH), under the Ministry of Ayush, held a national campaign on antimicrobial resistance (AMR) in the national capital on 19 November 2026. The theme was 'One Health, One Effort: Prevent AMR Now'. The aim was to promote the responsible use of antimicrobial medicines, strengthen public awareness and highlight the importance of scientific research and coordinated efforts.

## What AMR is

AMR occurs when microorganisms such as bacteria, viruses, fungi and parasites develop resistance to the medicines used to treat the infections they cause. This makes infections progressively harder to treat. According to CCRH, AMR is a serious public health challenge, and tackling it needs coordinated efforts across human health, animal health, agriculture and the environment under the 'One Health' approach.

## Who benefits

This campaign concerns ordinary people. Union Minister of State for Ayush (Independent Charge) and Minister of State for Health and Family Welfare Shri Prataprao Jadhav said antibiotics should not be used without thought and are not needed for every illness. He said antibiotics are not effective against viral infections such as the common cold and flu. Self-medication, using leftover medicines and taking antibiotics without medical advice add to the growing AMR challenge.

## What has changed

At the event, CCRH launched a dedicated webpage on AMR and released a bilingual booklet on AMR awareness. These aim to make reliable information easy for people to reach and to encourage the responsible use of antimicrobial medicines. An AMR-related pledge was also administered, reaffirming a collective commitment to responsible use and to efforts to prevent and control AMR.

## What you can do

The minister stressed four priorities: use antibiotics judiciously as per medical advice and complete the full prescribed course; strengthen infection prevention through clean drinking water, hand hygiene, cleanliness, food safety and vaccination; strengthen research, laboratory infrastructure and surveillance systems; and include AMR and the rational use of medicines in medical education and the training of health professionals. Referring to Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi's 'Mann Ki Baat' message of 28 December 2025, he repeated: do not take antibiotics without a doctor's advice.
