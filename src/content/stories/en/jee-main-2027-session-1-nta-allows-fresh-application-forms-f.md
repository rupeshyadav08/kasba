## What has happened

NTA issued a public notice on 30 October 2026. It said that several candidates had written to NTA about errors made while filling the online application form for JEE (Main) 2027 Session 1. In the interest of candidates, NTA has decided to allow them to submit a fresh application form, even if they have already generated an Application Number or paid the examination fee.

This notice comes after an earlier public notice dated 24 October 2026.

## Who is covered

The facility is for candidates who have filled the JEE (Main) 2027 Session 1 form and made a mistake in it. A new Application Number will be generated for each fresh form. All details, including photograph, signature and documents, must be entered and uploaded again.

## Which form will be valid

According to NTA, only the last form for which the examination fee has been successfully paid and the Confirmation Page generated will be considered valid. If a candidate submits a fresh form but does not complete the fee payment and generate the Confirmation Page, the earlier form for which these steps were completed will remain valid.

## What about the fee

The examination fee paid against an earlier form will neither be refunded nor adjusted against the fresh application. In other words, the fee has to be paid again for the fresh form.

## What are the dates

A fresh application must be completed, including fee payment, on or before 22 November 2026 up to 11:50 PM. From 23 November 2026 to 26 November 2026 up to 11:50 PM, forms can be submitted with a late fee.

## If you only need to correct details

Candidates who only need to amend certain details are advised to use the correction facility instead of submitting a fresh form. This facility is available from 27 November 2026 to 29 November 2026.

## Rule on multiple forms

Submitting multiple application forms does not entitle a candidate to appear more than once in the same session. A candidate who appears, or attempts to appear, in the examination using more than one Application Number will be treated as having used Unfair Means (UFM), and action will be taken as per the Information Bulletin. Merely holding more than one Application Number, without appearing using them, will not be treated as UFM.

## Where to get help

For further clarification, candidates may contact the NTA Help Desk at 011-40759000 or 011-69227700, or email jeemain@nta.ac.in.
