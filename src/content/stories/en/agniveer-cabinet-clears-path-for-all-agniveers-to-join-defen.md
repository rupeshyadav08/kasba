## What was decided

The Union Cabinet on 4 November 2026 approved an arrangement concerning Agniveers. Under it, the way is now open for all Agniveers to be absorbed into the defence forces as regular soldiers.

According to the Defence Ministry release, as per the earlier arrangement, 25 per cent of Agniveers will be given the opportunity for regular service in the defence forces on the basis of merit.

## What happens to the other 75 per cent

For the remaining 75 per cent of Agniveers, the Cabinet has approved one more service period of two years. This option is voluntary, meaning Agniveers who wish to can take it up.

After this additional two-year service period is completed, all Agniveers will get the opportunity to be recruited as regular soldiers, subject to their wish and to meeting the prescribed selection criteria.

## Why this decision was taken

The release says that over the past four years the defence forces assessed Agniveers' performance, and the assessment gave a positive response on their overall work.

An objective evaluation for organisational needs and operational/strategic efficiency found that Agniveers performed excellently in terms of skill, level of motivation, physical health/fitness and technical proficiency. The Cabinet's decision was taken to better align Agniveers' aspirations with organisational efficiency.

## What the second service period offers

According to the release, during the second service period Agniveers will be given higher remuneration, an improved service fund package and seniority-related benefits.

## Where the Agnipath scheme stands

After four years of the scheme, detailed feedback was gathered on its effectiveness and on Agniveers' performance. Some achievements listed in the release include a wider reach of Agniveer recruitment across districts of the country, a more standardised and transparent recruitment process, and doors opening for women in various branches of the defence forces.

The release says the Agnipath scheme will continue.
