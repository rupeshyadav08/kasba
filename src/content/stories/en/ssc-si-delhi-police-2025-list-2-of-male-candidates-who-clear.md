## What has been released

SSC has released List-2 of male candidates who qualified in PST/PET for the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) Examination 2025. These candidates will now appear in Paper-II.

The list gives each candidate's roll number, name, father's name and mother's name. It is available on the SSC website, ssc.gov.in.

## Who benefits

Male candidates whose names are on this list can appear in the next stage, Paper-II. The list is only of those who cleared PST/PET; it is not the final selection list.

## What candidates should do

Candidates who appeared in PST/PET should check their roll number in the list. Those whose names are on it should prepare for Paper-II. If a name is not on the list, the next stage of this recruitment does not open from this list.

## Where to get more information

Check the SSC website, ssc.gov.in, for the list and further information.
