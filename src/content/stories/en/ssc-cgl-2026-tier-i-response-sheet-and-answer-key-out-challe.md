## What has been released

SSC has released the response sheet and tentative answer key for the Combined Graduate Level Examination 2026 (Tier-I). The exam was held from 30 September 2026 to 28 October 2026 at centres across the country. Candidates can now view their response sheet and the answer key on ssc.gov.in.

## How to check

Candidates need to log in on the SSC website using their Registration Number and Password. This facility is available only for the stated period.

## How to challenge and what it costs

If a candidate believes an answer is wrong, they can file a challenge online from 9:00 PM on 4 November 2026 to 9:00 PM on 7 November 2026. The fee is Rs 50 per question or answer challenged. No challenge will be accepted after 9:00 PM on 7 November 2026, and challenges sent in any other mode will not be entertained.

## What to keep in mind in the challenge module

SSC said the sequence of questions and options in the challenge module may look different from the exam. This is because, in the challenge module, the question and answer sequence is the same for all candidates of a particular shift, while in the actual exam the sequence differed from candidate to candidate. The answers a candidate selected in the exam will be shown exactly as chosen.

SSC had issued detailed guidelines for the Challenge/Objection Management System on 10 April 2026. Candidates are advised to read these guidelines on the website before filing a challenge.

## Other things to note

The saved question paper is strictly for the candidate's personal use and self-analysis, SSC said. An undertaking to this effect is included in the Challenge Management portal and will be visible on login.

After the given time, the response sheet and tentative answer key will not be available. Individual requests for them will not be entertained later.
