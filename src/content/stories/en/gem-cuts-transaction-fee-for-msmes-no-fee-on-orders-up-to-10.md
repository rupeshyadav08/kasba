The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is the online marketplace where government departments buy goods and services and businesses sell to them. On 28 October 2026, GeM held an event with 'Laghu Udyog Bharati' (LUB) under 'GeM Samvad 2026' to explain reforms meant to make public procurement easier and cheaper for micro and small enterprises (MSEs).

## What has changed

GeM has cut its transaction fee from 0.50% to 0.30% of the order value. Orders up to ₹10 lakh now carry no transaction fee; the earlier limit was ₹5 lakh. For orders above ₹10 crore, the maximum fee is capped at ₹3 lakh, against ₹72.50 lakh earlier. According to the release, about 97% of orders placed on GeM attract no transaction fee.

Separately, the requirement to deposit caution money as part of the onboarding process was removed in July 2025. The vendor assessment fee for original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), applicable from January 2024, has also been reduced by 66% to 92%.

## Who benefits

The reforms benefit micro and small enterprises that take, or want to take, government orders on GeM. GeM CEO Shri Mihir Kumar said at the event that these reforms have lowered the cost of participation, simplified processes and improved access to finance. Ordinary citizens are not directly affected.

## Help with money

GeM Additional CEO Shri Ajit B. Chavan said that, in line with the Union Budget 2026-27 announcement, GeM has been connected with all five TReDS exchanges. He said that along with this facility, collateral-free loans through GeM SAHAY, based on purchase orders received on GeM, will help address working capital and timely payment problems faced by MSMEs.

## Where to get help

The GeM-LUB partnership, formed through an agreement in 2024, has held 108 virtual conferences in addition to several in-person events across India. LUB President Shri Madhusudan Dadu and National General Secretary Shri Om Prakash Gupta said LUB will expand its reach to MSMEs through its all-India office network, weekly GeM-LUB virtual conferences and facilitation counters run by trained volunteers at various offices.
