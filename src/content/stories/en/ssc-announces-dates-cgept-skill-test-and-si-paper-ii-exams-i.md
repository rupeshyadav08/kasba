## What has been decided

In a notice dated 2 November 2026, the SSC has set out the schedule for three examinations. According to the notice, the Coast Guard Enrolled Persons Test (CGEPT) for Batches 01/2027 and 02/2027 will be held on 17 and 18 November 2026. It will be a computer-based examination (CBE).

The second is the Annual Departmental Typing Test (English/Hindi) and Stenographer Skill Test (English/Hindi) Examination, 2025, to be held on 20 and 21 November 2026.

The third is Paper-II of the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs Examination, 2025, on 28 November 2026 in CBE mode.

## Who is affected

This information matters to candidates appearing for these examinations. If you have applied for any of them, note the date of your exam.

For people not appearing in these exams, the notice has nothing directly relevant.

## What to do now

The notice advises candidates to visit the Commission's website at regular intervals for further updates. The dates fall in November 2026, so there is little time left and preparation should be planned accordingly.

## Where to get more information

Further information on the examinations will be available on the SSC website, ssc.gov.in. The notice states that it was issued by the Under Secretary to the Government of India.
