## What RBI has done

On November 23, 2026, RBI issued draft amendment directions on 'Preventive Safeguards' for electronic banking transactions (EBTs). Comments are invited from regulated entities and members of the public until December 14, 2026.

## Why this step

RBI said it had issued Amendment Directions dated June 24, 2026 on 'Customer Protection in Fraudulent Electronic Banking Transactions', which strengthened the framework for limiting a customer's liability in unauthorised EBTs. RBI says that framework mainly deals with liability after a fraud, so a need was felt to strengthen it from a preventive angle by giving customers greater control over the manner and extent to which digital payment channels are used.

## On negligence by a bank

RBI also said that, after a review to bring uniformity in bank practices and to protect customers against certain acts of omission or commission by banks, it has decided to expand the scope of the definition of 'negligence by a bank' in those Amendment Directions.

## Which banks it covers

The draft directions are separate for different kinds of banks: commercial banks, small finance banks, payments banks, local area banks, regional rural banks, urban co-operative banks and rural co-operative banks. They propose changes to the Responsible Business Conduct Directions issued by RBI's Department of Regulation.

## How to send comments

According to RBI, feedback can be sent in two ways. One, through the 'Connect 2 Regulate' section on its website, following the link given against each document. Two, by email, with the subject line carrying the full name of the draft and the type of regulated entity.

## What it means for an ordinary customer

This is only a draft, so no new rule or right takes effect now. Those who want to raise something about digital payments can send their comments to RBI by December 14, 2026.
