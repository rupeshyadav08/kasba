## What has happened

SSC issued a notice on 17 November 2026. It says that for some candidates, the dates of the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) of SSC Constable (GD) Examination, 2026 clash with the SI in DP and CAPFs exams.

According to the notice, SI in DP and CAPFs Exam, 2026 (Paper-I) and SI in DP and CAPFs Exam, 2025 (Paper-II) are scheduled from 23.11.2026 to 28.11.2026.

## Who is affected

The notice is for candidates appearing in both the SSC Constable (GD) 2026 PET/PST and the SI in DP and CAPFs exams whose dates fall on the same days. SSC has given a way for such candidates to seek relief.

## What to do

If your dates clash, inform the PET/PST conducting authority by email. The email address given in the notice is: dcrecruitmentcrpf@gmail.com

Include your name, roll number/registration number, details of both exams and relevant supporting documents. The notice also asks candidates to mention their name, registration/roll number and the dates of both exams in the email.

## What happens next

SSC said such cases will be examined and, where appropriate, the PET/PST dates of SSC Constable (GD) Examination, 2026 will be considered for rescheduling. The notice does not say how or when candidates will be told about a changed date.

## Where to get more information

The notice is available on the SSC website. Candidates should keep checking official SSC notices for any update on rescheduling.
