## What was released

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation released consumer price index (CPI) data for October 2026 on 12 November 2026. This index is built on the new base year 2024=100. In simple terms, CPI is the measure that shows how much costlier or cheaper the ordinary person's everyday basket of goods has become over a year.

## How much inflation

Retail inflation in October 2026 was 6.09% (provisional). It was 6.63% in rural areas and 5.41% in urban areas. Food inflation stood at 8.94%, at 9.09% in rural areas and 8.68% in urban areas. So food prices rose faster than overall prices.

## What got costlier, what got cheaper

Onion saw the sharpest rise, becoming 116.22% costlier over a year. Garlic rose 60.74%, silver jewellery 52.39%, ginger 48.32% and lemon 47.30%. On the other side, potato fell 14.24%, cauliflower 10.57%, coconut 5.60%, brinjal 3.34% and cabbage 1.92%.

## How the data is collected

Field staff of the National Statistical Office, which works under the ministry, personally visit 1,407 selected urban markets (including online markets) and 1,465 villages across the country to collect prices. In October 2026 the response rate was 100% in both rural and urban markets. Prices of petrol, diesel and LPG are taken as on the 15th of the month.

## What next

The November 2026 CPI will be released on 14 December 2026 (Monday). If that day is a holiday, it will come on the next working day. State-wise and item-wise indices for October 2026 can be downloaded from the ministry's eSankhyiki portal, esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in. The ministry also said state-level item indices should be used with care, as some may be based on limited samples of price quotations.
