## What happened

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas issued a statement on 7 November 2026 saying that E20 petrol supplied by the state-run oil marketing companies (OMCs) meets the prescribed quality standards.

The statement followed a Karnataka government press note claiming that petrol sold at some retail outlets in the state contained 26% to 31% ethanol.

## What the sample checks found

According to the ministry, quality monitoring by the OMCs and the Centre for High Technology (CHT) covered 893 samples of E20 fuel from 27 states and union territories. These included 330 samples from Indian Oil, 280 from BPCL and 283 from HPCL.

The samples were tested at refinery and OMC quality-control laboratories. Ethanol in all 893 samples was within the prescribed 19–21% range. In Karnataka, all 56 samples tested had ethanol between 19% and 20%, with an average of 19.30%.

## What the oil companies questioned

The three companies said the Karnataka claims do not match checks carried out under the prescribed legal and technical process. They raised concerns about the October 2026 sampling exercise, in which company representatives were not present.

Under the rules, six one-litre samples are drawn and signed by all parties, the companies said. They said they did not receive the retention samples, outlet-wise details or laboratory reports. They also said the results rest on certificates from a third-party laboratory rather than the prescribed laboratory.

## On mileage and repair cost claims

The statement says claims of about ₹62,000 crore in annual mileage loss to consumers and about ₹39,000 crore spent on vehicle repairs need careful examination.

The ministry cited its own statement of 12 August 2025, which said mileage may drop by about 1–2% in vehicles made for E20 and by about 3–6% in some other vehicles.

## What is being asked now

The three OMCs have asked the Karnataka government to share all documents related to the samples, outlet-wise laboratory reports and details of the testing method used. They have also sought independent testing at an authorised laboratory in the presence of state government and OMC representatives.
