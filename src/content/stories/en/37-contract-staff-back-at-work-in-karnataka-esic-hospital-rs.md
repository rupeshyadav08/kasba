## What happened

The machinery of the Chief Labour Commissioner (Central), under the Ministry of Labour and Employment, intervened in an industrial dispute. As a result, 37 contract workers of the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) Model Hospital in Karnataka were re-engaged. According to the ministry's release, their pending wages of about Rs 7.40 lakh were also paid.

## What the dispute was about

The matter was raised by the association of contract workers of the Karnataka State ESIC Model Hospital. Their complaint was that nursing orderlies and support staff were removed from work before their contract period had ended. In other words, workers whose contracts were still running were taken off duty.

## How it was settled

The release says the matter was taken up for conciliation without delay. ESIC, as the principal employer, and the contractor were both called to take part. The settlement followed the intervention of the Assistant Labour Commissioner (Central), Bengaluru, under the guidance of the Deputy Chief Labour Commissioner (Central), Bengaluru.

## What the workers got

All 37 affected contract workers were taken back on duty from 23.10.2026. Their pending wages for September 2026, about Rs 7.40 lakh, were also paid. The release says the timely settlement protected these workers' jobs and ensured their pending wages were cleared.

## Who is affected

The direct benefit went to the 37 contract workers employed at this hospital. Ordinary readers in other states are not directly affected. The ministry described the settlement as part of its effort to protect workers' interests and resolve industrial disputes through talks.

## What to do next

The release gives no application, form or deadline for the public. Workers facing a similar problem can approach the office of the Labour Commissioner (Central) in their area, where conciliation of industrial disputes is taken up.
