## What has happened

The National Health Authority (NHA), under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, has said the Ayushman Vay Vandana scheme completed two years on 29 October 2026. The scheme was launched by the Prime Minister on 29 October 2024.

It is part of the Ayushman Bharat-Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY), the government's health insurance scheme that covers the cost of hospital admission. Vay Vandana extends those benefits to every citizen aged 70 and above, whatever their social or economic status.

## Who benefits

Under the scheme, all senior citizens aged 70 and above are eligible for health cover of up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for hospital admission, for secondary and tertiary care.

For families already covered under AB PM-JAY, members aged 70 and above get an additional top-up cover of up to Rs 5 lakh a year. This extra cover is only for these seniors and is separate from the cover available to family members below 70. This way seniors get extra financial protection while the rest of the family keeps its own cover.

According to the ministry, about 6 crore senior citizens linked to around 4.5 crore families across the country fall in this group.

## How much treatment in two years

As of 20 October 2026, 23.46 lakh hospital admissions were approved under the scheme. The total value of these cases was Rs 5,668.80 crore.

Cataract surgery was the most used treatment, with 4.71 lakh procedures, about 20.1% of all approved treatments. Chronic haemodialysis came next with about 2.90 lakh treatments. Dialysis is needed regularly by patients whose kidneys do not work properly.

## Where the most money went

By value of approved treatments, percutaneous transluminal coronary angioplasty (PTCA) accounted for the largest share, at about Rs 649 crore. PTCA is a procedure used to treat blocked heart arteries.

Other major treatment categories include total knee replacement, management of acute flare-ups of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), acute febrile illness, and treatment of acute kidney injury and kidney failure.

## What next

The ministry said the scheme is now entering its third year. Efforts will continue to widen publicly funded health coverage and reduce the burden of hospital costs on seniors and their families.

The notice does not say where to apply to join the scheme or which documents are needed. Eligible seniors can ask at their nearest hospital or the local office linked to Ayushman Bharat for this information.
