## What happened

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment issued a fact-check report on 31 October 2026. It responds to a report published the same day in Bhopal's 'Central Chronicle', which claimed that the Madhya Pradesh Employment and Training Council (MPAPCET) paid crores based on placement data without verifying it. The ministry called the claim completely false and baseless.

## Where the money came from

According to the ministry, the Commissioner's office of the Scheduled Caste Development Department sanctioned Rs 3,241.37 lakh for skill development training under "Special Central Assistance to the Scheduled Caste Sub-Plan". Against this, MPAPCET received Rs 2,945.37 lakh in total during 2021-22. This included Rs 1,296.00 lakh under order number 4695 of 26 November 2021 and Rs 1,649.37 lakh under order number 10283 of 25 March 2022.

## How the training was run

MPAPCET issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) for 2021-22 to train Scheduled Caste beneficiaries from below-poverty-line families. After selection, contracts were signed with 34 training provider institutions and work orders were issued. Six of these institutions did not start work. The remaining 28 institutions trained 4,952 trainees. The ministry said the council's training programme follows standards set by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), and all activities are managed through a dedicated portal that handles trainee registration, daily attendance tracking, assessment and placement data upload.

## What the payment rules say

Under Schedule IV, clause 2.1.1 of MSDE gazette notification number 349 dated 4 December 2020, training providers in batch mode are paid in three instalments. The first instalment is 30% of the batch amount, paid when training begins. The second is 40%, paid on successful completion of certification, based on the number of certified candidates after adjusting advance payment. The third is 30%, paid on achieving 70% verified employment, with desk verification and placement verification as per standard rules and three months of continuous employment.

## What was actually paid

According to the ministry, the first instalment is paid when a training batch starts after physical verification of trainees. The second is paid after training is completed and trainees are assessed. This assessment is done by agencies nominated by the National Council for Vocational Education and Training (NCVET), and trainees need at least 70% attendance to be eligible. The ministry said institutions received only the first and second instalments, and neither is linked to placements.

## Why the third instalment was not paid

The ministry said institutions submitted post-training placement information in hard copy based on job offers or offer letters, but such information cannot release the third instalment. For that, institutions must upload placement details on the portal as per the prescribed criteria, and only then can the portal generate a payment claim. The ministry said no such claim was generated for any institution, so no third instalment was paid. The third instalment will be paid when candidates complete training and start a job; for now, the institutions are helping with placements.
