## What the festival is

TRIFED (Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India), which works under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, is holding 'Tribes India-Aadi Mahotsav 2026' at Delhi Haat, INA, New Delhi from 16 to 30 November 2026. According to the release, about 200 tribal artisans are displaying their products at 115 stalls.

## What visitors will find

The stalls carry traditional crafts, handloom cloth, handicrafts and forest-based products. People linked to handloom and handicrafts from different parts of the country, and representatives of Van Dhan Vikas Kendras, are also taking part.

## Why the event is being held

The release says the aim is to reduce the distance between tribal producers and mainstream markets. Artisans get a chance to talk directly to customers and to understand how the retail market works. This helps them learn about changing customer tastes, judge demand and find ways to grow their business.

## What it means for ordinary people

The release invites the general public to visit the festival. People in and around Delhi can go to Delhi Haat till 30 November 2026 to see and buy tribal art and indigenous products. For readers in other states, the release announces no application, recruitment or scheme, so there is nothing for them to do.

## Where to get more information

For confirmation of dates, venue and timings, check official TRIFED or Delhi Haat information. The release gives no contact number or website beyond this.
