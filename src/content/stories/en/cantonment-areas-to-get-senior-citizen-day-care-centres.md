## What happened

The Defence Ministry and the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) in New Delhi on 16 November 2026. The aim is to strengthen care for senior citizens living in cantonment areas. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment Dr. Virendra Kumar were present at the signing.

On the Defence side, the work will be done through the Directorate General Defence Estates (DGDE).

## What changes

Under the MoU, day-care centres and senior citizen homes will be set up in cantonment areas, and existing centres will be strengthened. Caregivers for the elderly will also be given skill training.

DGDE has identified 8 cantonments where new day-care centres can be opened — Barrackpore (West Bengal), Belgaum (Karnataka), Jhansi (Uttar Pradesh), Khadki and Pune (Maharashtra), Secunderabad (Telangana), St. Thomas Mount (Tamil Nadu) and Delhi.

## What about existing centres

Existing day-care centres in Ambala (Haryana), Deolali (Maharashtra), Jabalpur (Madhya Pradesh), and Ranikhet and Lansdowne (Uttarakhand) are proposed to be strengthened. The senior citizen homes in Ayodhya and Lucknow cantonments are also proposed for expansion and strengthening. At present, these centres and homes are run mainly with grants from the Defence Ministry.

## Who pays and who provides facilities

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment will give financial support under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY). This includes support for running and maintaining existing day-care centres and senior citizen homes that cantonment boards run from their own budgets. DGDE and cantonment boards will provide suitable premises and basic facilities such as electricity, water supply and sanitation. The Social Justice Ministry will give technical guidance, training modules and operational support.

## Training for caregivers

A key part of the MoU is skill training for young people in caring for the elderly. The notice says India's elderly population is growing, so demand for trained caregivers is expected to rise. The initiative is meant to help build a pool of trained caregivers in and around 12 cantonments: Barrackpore, Belgaum, Jhansi, Deolali, Khadki, Pune, Secunderabad, St. Thomas Mount, Ambala, Delhi, and Firozpur and Jalandhar (Punjab).

## What next

A joint working group of representatives from both ministries will be formed to coordinate, implement and monitor the work. The Defence Minister called for a social audit of these centres and homes to check the quality of services. The notice also notes that the Defence Estates Department is marking 100 years since it was set up in 1926.

For now, the notice gives no application, form or deadline for the public.
