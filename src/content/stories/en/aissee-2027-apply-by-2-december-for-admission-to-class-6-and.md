## What has happened

The National Testing Agency (NTA) issued a public notice on 3 November 2026 inviting online applications for the All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE)-2027. The Sainik Schools Society has given NTA the responsibility of conducting this exam.

The exam is for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 in Sainik Schools and New Sainik Schools across the country for the academic year 2027-28.

## Who is affected

Families whose children want to study in a Sainik School. Admission is through this entrance exam, so applying first is necessary. Applications are accepted online only.

## What are the dates

Online applications can be submitted from 3 November 2026 to 2 December 2026, up to 5 pm. The fee must also be paid within this period through credit/debit card, net banking or UPI.

Those who miss this window can apply from 3 December 2026 to 6 December 2026 with a late fee of Rs 500. The fee payment in this window must also be completed by 5 pm on 6 December 2026.

Corrections in the application particulars can be made from 7 December 2026 to 9 December 2026.

## What is the exam like

The exam will be held on 31 January 2027. It will be a pen-and-paper test with OMR sheets, and the questions will be Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ).

The Class 6 exam will be of 50 minutes and is available in three mediums. The Class 9 exam will be of 80 minutes and will be held only in English medium.

The city of examination will be announced on 21 January 2027, and admit cards can be downloaded from the NTA website from 25 January 2027. The date of results will be announced later on the website.

## How to apply

Apply online only at the NTA website https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/. Fill the form carefully and read the AISSEE 2027 Information Bulletin before applying.

Details such as the scheme, duration, medium and syllabus of the exam, eligibility criteria, the list of Sainik Schools and New Sainik Schools with their tentative intake, reservation of seats, exam cities and passing requirements are given in this Information Bulletin, available on the NTA website.

## Where to get help

For any query, call the NTA help desk at 011-40759000 or 011-69227700, or write to aissee@nta.ac.in. Keep checking the NTA website for the latest updates.
