## What happened

A high-level team of the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM), which works for Delhi-NCR and nearby areas, held a review meeting at the district administrative complex in Sangrur on 31 October 2026. The team included CAQM technical member Dr S.D. Attri and Scientist 'C' Shri Vijay Kumar.

The meeting was attended by Deputy Commissioners, Senior Superintendents of Police, officials of the Punjab Pollution Control Board (PPCB) and the agriculture department from Sangrur, Patiala and Malerkotla districts, and flying squads of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB).

## What was reviewed

The main purpose was to review the district administration's preparations for managing paddy stubble and the steps being taken to stop stubble burning in these districts. The team also reviewed arrangements for the ongoing harvest season.

## What was seen on the ground

In Ahmadabad village of Malerkotla district, the team saw 'in-situ' machinery, which manages stubble within the field itself, at work. The agriculture department also demonstrated the working of a 'Super Seeder' machine. The team met farmers, who assured that they would not burn paddy stubble and would manage it using in-situ machinery.

The team visited villages Bassiark, Kapiyal and Jhaneri in Bhawanigarh tehsil of Sangrur district. At hotspot village Gharachon, in-situ and ex-situ (outside the field) stubble management was demonstrated. The team was told about arrangements to provide Crop Residue Management (CRM) machinery to farmers for in-situ management. Farmers also described the difficulties they face in using this machinery.

## What else stubble is used for

On Dhuri Road in Malerkotla, the team visited an ex-situ stubble use unit that makes duplex board and paper. The unit uses paddy stubble as fuel in its 20 TPH capacity boiler. Industry representatives said about 17,000 metric tonnes of paddy stubble would be used in their boiler furnace every year.

At Nadampur village in Bhawanigarh, the team visited a unit that makes pellets from paddy stubble. These pellets are later used as fuel in thermal power plants. The team was told that such units help promote ex-situ stubble management and give farmers an alternative to burning crop residue.

## What officials were told

CAQM officials asked the departments concerned to keep strict monitoring and make continuous, concrete efforts to stop stubble burning. The team advised officials to ensure effective stubble management as promised in their action plans, and said action on the ground should match the targets set.
