Akashvani has launched the first season of ‘AIR Ubharte Sitare’ (AIR Rising Stars), a nationwide series of special music programmes in which only young artistes under 30 years of age will perform. The announcement was made on 15 November 2026.

## When and where

The first season will be held at 26 places across the country from 17 to 30 November 2026. The programmes will be organised by 26 Akashvani centres: Shimla, Dehradun, Delhi, Lucknow, Varanasi, Jaipur, Jalandhar, Suratgarh, Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Dharwad, Bengaluru, Mysuru, Madurai, Thiruvananthapuram, Tiruchirappalli, Kolkata, Imphal, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Sangli, Nagpur, Puducherry, Jammu, Guwahati and Rampur. Each centre will hold a programme at a suitable venue featuring promising young artistes from its region.

## Who gets a chance

The programmes will mainly feature recognised young artistes under 30 who are associated with Akashvani. If suitable recognised young artistes are not available in a particular category, eligible non-recognised young artistes may also be given an opportunity, as selected by the concerned Akashvani centre under the prescribed guidelines.

## Which music styles

The programmes will cover Hindustani classical music (vocal and instrumental), Carnatic classical music (vocal and instrumental), light and folk music, Western music, and other regional styles as required by the concerned Akashvani centres.

## Prizes

Cash prizes and certificates will go to the three artistes whose performances are watched the most on YouTube till 15 January 2027. The first prize is ₹20,000, the second ₹15,000 and the third ₹10,000.

## Where to watch

The programmes will be broadcast through the network of Akashvani centres. They will also be shown on Doordarshan, Akashvani's 'Ragam' YouTube channel, the YouTube channels of the 26 Akashvani centres, the 'NewsOnAir' mobile app and Prasar Bharati's 'Waves OTT' platform.

## Why this initiative

The initiative is part of the celebrations of Akashvani's 90 years. According to the notice, Akashvani has a long tradition of holding the 'Akashvani Sangeet Sammelan', of which 67 editions have been completed; that platform is for top-grade and 'A' grade established artistes. ‘AIR Ubharte Sitare’ is a separate initiative meant to give young recognised artistes a dedicated platform, since they often get limited opportunities to perform at such prestigious events.
