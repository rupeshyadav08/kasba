## What has been released

SSC has released a tentative vacancy list for the Grade 'C' Stenographers Limited Departmental Competitive Examination, 2025. The list is as on 22 October 2026 and shows how many posts are available in different services and categories.

## How many posts in total

According to the notice, there are 267 tentative posts in all. These include 195 posts in the UR category, 48 in SC and 24 in ST. The list also shows separate posts for the PwBD category.

## Which services have posts

The list names Central Secretariat Stenographers Services, Railway Board Secretariat Stenographers Service, Armed Forces Headquarters Stenographers Service, Election Commission of India Stenographers Service, Indian Foreign Service Branch (B) Stenographers and Central Vigilance Commission. For example, Railway Board Secretariat has 9 posts and Armed Forces Headquarters has 22 posts. Election Commission of India has 1 post.

## About the PwBD category

The notice says at some places that if a PwBD category such as OA or OL is not available, it can be exchanged with another available PwBD category. At Armed Forces Headquarters, three posts are set aside for VH, OH and PwBD-others, and the OH and PwBD-others posts can be interchanged with available PwBD candidates.

## Who should take note

This notice is not directly for the general public. It concerns government employees who are in the process of moving to Grade 'C' stenographer posts through the departmental exam. Such candidates can check the number of posts for their category and service. Since these numbers are tentative, they may change.
