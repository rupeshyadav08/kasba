## What has been released

SSC has put up the tentative answer keys and candidates' response sheets for five papers of the Junior Engineer Examination 2026 on its website. These papers are Civil & Structural Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science and Information Technology, and Physics (excluding Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering). The exam was held from 2 November to 6 November 2026 at centres across the country.

## How to check

Candidates can log in on ssc.gov.in using their Registration Number and Password. Their response sheet and the answer key will then be available.

## How to raise an objection

If you object to an answer, you can file a challenge online from 19 November 2026, 6 PM to 22 November 2026, 6 PM. The fee is Rs 50 per question. Objections received after 6 PM on 22 November will not be entertained, and objections sent by any other mode will also not be accepted.

## Points to keep in mind

In the challenge module, the order of questions and options may differ from how they appeared in the exam. This is because the module shows the same order of questions and answers for all candidates of a shift, while in the actual exam the order differed for each candidate. The answers you selected in the exam will be shown accurately in the module.

You can challenge only in the language in which you attempted the exam, but you can view questions in both Hindi and English. SSC issued detailed guidelines for the challenge system in a notice dated 10 April 2026, available on its website.

## What else you should know

The saved question paper is strictly for personal use and self-analysis. An undertaking to this effect is included in the challenge portal. Response sheets and answer keys will not be available after the specified time, and individual requests for them later will not be entertained.

The tentative answer key for the remaining paper, Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, will be published separately after the exam for that paper.
