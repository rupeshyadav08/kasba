## What has happened

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) issued a rejection notice on 30 October 2026 for the posts of PGT (Maths) Male and PGT (Maths) Female in the Directorate of Education (DoE), GNCTD. The post code is 825/24.

## Whose candidature was rejected

The notice lists 5 roll numbers whose candidature has been rejected. The reason given against each is the same: dossier not uploaded.

## What changes

This notice applies only to these five candidates. The notice also states that DSSSB reserves the right to rectify errors and omissions, if any, detected at any stage.

## What to do next

Candidates whose roll numbers appear on the list can see the notice on the DSSSB website. The notice does not give any further instructions. For those not on the list, the notice has nothing for them.
