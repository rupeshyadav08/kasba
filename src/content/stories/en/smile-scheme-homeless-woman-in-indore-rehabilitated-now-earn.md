## What happened

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment issued a release on 28 October 2026. It said that under the SMILE scheme, a homeless woman in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, received rehabilitation and livelihood support. The release calls her Meera Thakur, a changed name.

## What is the SMILE scheme

SMILE stands for Support for Marginalised Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise. It is a ministry scheme meant to empower people in need through rehabilitation and livelihood. According to the release, it includes safe housing, medical care, psychological counselling and continuous rehabilitation support.

## The woman's story

According to the release, the woman had passed Class 11 and had earlier made flower garlands and worked as a kitchen helper. After a family dispute, she faced serious personal and financial problems. After losing her savings and important documents, she fell ill and became homeless. With no reliable income, she began begging.

## Rescue and treatment

On 16 March 2024, a team of the Param Pujya Rakshak Adinath Welfare and Educational Society (PRAWES) rescued her from the Shani temple premises at the district court in Indore. She was admitted to Arvindo Hospital, where severe anaemia was treated and nutrition was looked after. Cataract surgery on both eyes was also successful.

## Skills and livelihood

As her health improved, she joined yoga, meditation and music therapy sessions. She learned to make carpets and doormats from old sarees, stitching, beaded garlands, incense sticks, and eco-friendly items from cow dung such as idols, diyas and havan kunds. In July 2025 she took part in a workshop held by the National Institute of Social Defence (NISD). She now runs the kitchen at the rehabilitation centre and prepares breakfast for about 50-60 beneficiaries. Selling her handicrafts at fairs and exhibitions, she earns about Rs 8,000 a month.

## What it means for ordinary people

The release does not give any application process, date or eligibility rule for the public. It is an account of a successful rehabilitation. People who want to know about such help can contact the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment or the concerned department in their district.
