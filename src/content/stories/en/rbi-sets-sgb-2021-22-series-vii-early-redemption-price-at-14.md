## What has happened

In a press release dated 30 October 2026, RBI said the premature redemption price for Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) 2021-22 Series VII has been fixed at ₹14,167 per unit. This redemption is due on 2 November 2026.

SGB is a government scheme in which investors buy bonds instead of physical gold. Details of each series come from a government notification. For this series, the notification was dated 21 October 2021 and the bonds were issued on 2 November 2021.

## When early redemption is allowed

According to RBI, premature redemption is permitted only after five years from the date of issue, and only on the date on which interest is payable. For this series, that date is 2 November 2026.

## How the price is worked out

The redemption price is based on the simple average of the closing price of 999 purity gold of the previous three business days. These rates are published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA). This time the three days were 28, 29 and 30 October 2026, and their average gives the price of ₹14,167 per unit.

## Who benefits

This benefits only investors who hold SGB 2021-22 Series VII bonds and want to redeem early on 2 November 2026. People who did not buy this series are not affected.

## What to do next

If you hold bonds of this series and want to redeem early, ask your bank or the institution you bought the bonds from about the process. You can confirm the price and date from this RBI press release.
