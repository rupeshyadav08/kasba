## What is happening

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and the Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (IEPFA) are jointly organising the 8th Niveshak Shivir in New Delhi on November 17, 2026. It is being held with the support of Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) and Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs).

Among the participating MIIs are BSE, CDSL, NSE and NSDL, and among the RTAs are KFIN Technologies Limited, MUFG Intime India Private Limited, Bigshare Services Pvt. Ltd., Datamatics Business Solutions Ltd. and Purva Sharegistry India Pvt.

## What the camp will help with

According to the notice, the aim of the Shivir is to sort out pending share-related problems of investors. It will help with the transfer of unpaid dividends up to 7 years. It will also assist investors with KYC and nomination updates with the company.

Help will be available for transmission of securities, that is, getting shares transferred to another person's name. The camp will also assist in resolving pending IEPFA claims of unclaimed and unpaid shares and dividends, and in the issuance of duplicate share certificates.

## Where and when to go

The Shivir will be held at India Habitat Centre, Jacaranda Hall, Near Airforce Bal Bharati School, Lodhi Road, Lodhi Estate, New Delhi - 110003. Entry is from Gate No. 3 on Vardhaman Marg.

The date is November 17, 2026, and the time is 10 AM to 4 PM.

## What to carry

For entry into the Shivir, shareholders or their representatives are advised to bring their official government identification documents. These include Voter ID, Aadhaar Card, PAN card and Driver's license.

## Who benefits

The camp is useful for people who hold shares in a company and whose dividend, share certificate, KYC, nomination or IEPFA claim is stuck for some reason. People who do not hold shares are not directly affected.
