## What the notice says

In its press release of 25 November 2026, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) has said dense fog conditions are very likely in isolated pockets of Haryana, Chandigarh and Delhi, and Punjab, from 25 to 28 November. Similar dense fog warnings are given for Himachal Pradesh from 25 to 29 November, Assam & Meghalaya and Manipur & Tripura from 25 to 27 November, and Odisha from 25 to 26 November.

In the past 24 hours, dense fog was reported in isolated pockets of Himachal Pradesh and Odisha. According to the release, visibility was 70 metres at Bolangir in Odisha and 100 metres at Bilaspur in Himachal Pradesh.

## Delhi's forecast

A separate forecast for Delhi says the sky will be mainly clear on 25 November. Mist is likely in the morning on 26 November, while 27 November may see mist or shallow fog at isolated places in the morning. On 28 November, mist or shallow fog is likely at a few places, with moderate fog at isolated places.

Delhi's minimum temperature is likely to be between 08°C and 10°C on 26 and 27 November. On 28 November it is likely to be around 09°C to 11°C.

## What problems fog can cause

According to IMD, dense fog may affect some airports, highways and railway routes. Driving can become difficult and journeys may take longer, and without precautions it may lead to some road traffic collisions. Power lines may also trip in very dense fog.

There are health warnings too. Dense fog contains particulate matter and other pollutants which, if inhaled, can lodge in the lungs and increase episodes of wheezing, coughing and shortness of breath. Long exposure may cause respiratory problems for people with asthma, bronchitis and other lung-related conditions. The pollutants can also irritate the eyes, causing redness or swelling.

## What to do

IMD advises being careful while driving or travelling and using fog lights while driving. Keep in touch with airlines, railways and state transport for the schedule of your journey. For health, it advises avoiding going out unless there is an emergency and covering the face.

## Where to check more

IMD says district-wise warnings and detailed weather information are available on its website. The release also notes that forecast accuracy decreases as the lead period increases.
