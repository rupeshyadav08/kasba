## What happened

On 26 October 2026, the Jaipur (Khatipura)-Tatanagar-Santragachi Express train was launched. Union Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw and West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari flagged off the train. The Railway Minister joined the event through video conferencing.

West Bengal minister Dilip Ghosh, Mayor Sunil Kothari, MPs Vidyut Baran Mahato and Manju Sharma, and other public representatives and citizens were present at the event.

## Who benefits

According to the Railway Minister, the train links Jaipur with Jamshedpur and Kolkata. It is expected to improve rail connectivity between Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand and West Bengal.

The notice says the new service will help migrant workers living in Rajasthan, business communities, students and other travellers moving between different regions. The Railway Minister said railway development is being planned around the needs of the middle class and economically weaker families, for whom rail is a major affordable means of transport.

## Station redevelopment

The Railway Minister said more than 1,300 stations across the country are being redeveloped under the Amrit Bharat Station Scheme. In Jharkhand, 57 stations are being redeveloped, and work has been completed at several of them, including Godda, Govindpur Road, Lohardaga, Madhupur and Muri.

In West Bengal too, several stations are being redeveloped, including Santragachi, Jalpaiguri Road, Jaychandi Pahar, Haldia and Kamakhya Guri.

## Other railway work

The Railway Minister said a four-line expansion is under way on the rail line connecting Howrah, Kharagpur, Chakulia and Tatanagar. The 4.5-km section of Kolkata Metro linked to Belghata-IT Centre has been completed and trial runs have begun; it will provide connectivity to the Green Line.

Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari thanked the Railway Minister for the railway infrastructure projects in the state. He said the average annual railway budget for West Bengal was Rs 4,380 crore during 2009-2014, and has risen to Rs 14,205 crore for 2026-27.

## What to do next

The notice does not give the train's timings, stops or fares. Passengers should check official railway information for timings and stops before planning a journey.
