## What has happened

The Union Cabinet on 25 November 2026 approved continuing the Postal Department's Estates Management Scheme. It is a central sector scheme with a total provision of ₹1,491 crore over five years. The scheme will run from financial year 2026-27 to 2030-31 and will be implemented in phases.

## What the scheme aims to do

The scheme aims to modernise the Postal Department's buildings and estate infrastructure. It covers the construction, reconstruction and renovation of post offices and residential colonies. It also includes developing a uniform brand identity for postal buildings and conserving historic postal buildings linked to India's heritage. The government says this will strengthen first-mile delivery of postal services, promote financial inclusion and improve citizen-centric services.

## Where the money will go

Under the plan, 190 new departmental postal buildings will be built on the department's vacant land, so post offices now running in rented buildings can move into their own premises. Fifty dilapidated postal buildings will be demolished and rebuilt. As many as 2,550 departmental sub-post offices will be upgraded and 2,500 post offices in rented buildings will be modernised. Twenty prominent historic postal buildings will be restored and 150 residential colonies will be renovated.

## Who will get better facilities

Under the Accessible India Campaign, buildings will get ramps and Braille signage so that persons with disabilities can reach post offices more easily. Women's toilets and feeding rooms for infants will also be provided. Buildings will get sustainable features such as solar power and rainwater harvesting. A permanent building and campus for the Centre for Excellence in Postal Technology (CEPT) will be set up in Bengaluru.

## What ordinary people need to do

Ordinary people do not have to apply for this scheme or pay anything. It is about the Postal Department's buildings and estates. When a post office in your area moves into a new building or is renovated, you can expect better facilities for postal, banking, insurance and Aadhaar services there.
