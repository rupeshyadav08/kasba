## What has changed

The Ministry of Jal Shakti has extended the deadline for citizens to send suggestions on the Jal Sanchay Jan Bhagidari (JSJB) 2.0 portal to 30 November 2026. The portal has been open to the public since 26 January 2026. With the extension, people can keep sending their views on the water structures built under the initiative.

## Who can give feedback and on what

Citizens across the country can share their suggestions and views on the water conservation, rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge and water storage works reported under JSJB 2.0. This applies especially to the artificial groundwater recharge and water storage structures built under the initiative.

## What the campaign is

According to the ministry, the campaign was envisioned by the Prime Minister on 6 September 2024 in Surat, Gujarat. Its aim is to turn water conservation into a true people's movement through active public participation. Till 31 May 2026, more than 1.5 crore structures related to rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge, water conservation and storage had been reported across the country.

## Why citizen feedback matters

The ministry says citizens can help identify maintenance needs, monitor how well structures are working and improve their long-term usefulness. The feedback will also help verify and assess the assets reported under the initiative.

Under this initiative, districts, states and union territories and other stakeholders are recognised on the basis of the number of completed artificial groundwater recharge and water storage projects. That is why citizen participation in the feedback process is seen as particularly important. The ministry expects this to improve the quality and reliability of reporting, promote transparency and encourage districts and urban local bodies (ULBs) to maintain the structures properly.

## How to send suggestions

Citizens and stakeholders can submit their suggestions and views through the JSJB 2.0 portal. The ministry has given this link: https://jsjb.mowr.gov.in/DashboardJsjb.aspx

The ministry's aim is to strengthen a sense of community ownership of water conservation structures and give momentum to the nationwide campaign for sustainable water management and water security.
