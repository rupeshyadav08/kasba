Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan released two sets of figures at a press conference in Delhi on 26 November 2026. The first was the final production estimate for major crops of 2025-26. The second was the first advance estimate for the 2026-27 kharif season.

## What the 2025-26 record shows

According to the ministry, total foodgrain output was 3785.38 lakh tonnes, which is 208.06 lakh tonnes more than the 3577.32 lakh tonnes of 2024-25. Rice output rose from 1501.84 lakh tonnes to 1551.54 lakh tonnes, and wheat from 1179.45 lakh tonnes to 1220.64 lakh tonnes.

Total pulses stood at 272.40 lakh tonnes, with moong setting a new record at 43.96 lakh tonnes. Chana was 125.13 lakh tonnes and tur 35.99 lakh tonnes. Oilseeds reached a record 432.55 lakh tonnes, including groundnut at 131.71 lakh tonnes and rapeseed and mustard at 137.66 lakh tonnes. Sugarcane was 5037.29 lakh tonnes, cotton 280.06 lakh bales and jute and mesta 94.28 lakh bales.

## How the kharif 2026-27 estimate looks

The minister said this year the return of El Nino, a delayed monsoon in June and low soil moisture in states such as Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh affected the sowing of paddy, maize, tur, soybean and cotton. Despite this, kharif foodgrain output is estimated at 1671.43 lakh metric tonnes, which is 97.67 lakh tonnes more than the average of the last five years.

Kharif rice is estimated at 1219.05 lakh tonnes and nutri-cereals at 381.05 lakh tonnes. Kharif pulses are estimated at 71.32 lakh tonnes and kharif oilseeds at 239.47 lakh tonnes.

## What the government is focusing on now

According to the minister, after achieving adequate rice and wheat output, the government's focus is now on crop diversification and self-reliance in pulses and oilseeds. Efforts are being made through the Dalhan Mission and Tilhan Mission to raise production.

## How the figures were prepared

The ministry said the figures were prepared using information from states and union territories, remote sensing, digital crop surveys and consultations with industry representatives. The minister said the kharif 2026-27 figures are early estimates and will be revised after actual yield data from crop cutting experiments at harvest.

## What this means for ordinary people

This notice is not about any application, payment or deadline, so ordinary consumers do not need to do anything because of it. It is the government's estimate of how much grain, pulses and oilseeds the country produced and expects to produce, which helps in understanding the supply situation.
