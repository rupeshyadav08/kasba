## What happened

The five-day eye surgery camp held under 'Operation Drishti' at the military hospital in Meerut concluded on 25 November 2026. According to the Defence Ministry notice, a team of specialist doctors of the Armed Forces Medical Services (AFMS) performed a total of 412 successful surgeries, restoring patients' vision.

The notice says these surgeries took place between 22 and 24 November 2026. As many as 197 needy citizens were also operated on at the camp. They included people suffering from cataract, glaucoma, retina-related problems and growths on the eye.

## Who benefited

A major achievement of the camp was the successful surgery of about 60 patients who had gone completely blind because of cataract. According to the notice, the surgeries brought light back into their lives.

Patients did not reach the camp directly. Over the past two months, AFMS eye specialists and medical officers screened more than 12,000 patients for cataract and other eye diseases in Baghpat, Bulandshahr, Hapur, Shamli and Moradabad. The notice says the drive took medical facilities to people living in remote towns and villages.

## How many were screened in Meerut

About 5,000 patients were screened during the camp at the military hospital in Meerut. Those who needed surgery were brought to the military hospital.

According to the notice, the 412 surgeries here are the highest number at any eye surgery camp so far. All of them were performed by a team of specialist doctors from the Army Hospital (Research and Referral). Brigadier Sanjay Kumar Mishra, head of the eye department, led the team that runs 'Operation Drishti'. The Meerut camp was held under the aegis of Headquarters Surya Command.

## What Operation Drishti is

According to the notice, the campaign holds large-scale surgical camps to take modern eye care to remote and underserved areas of the country.

Air Marshal Sandeep Thareja, Director General of the Armed Forces Medical Services, appreciated the medical team's contribution. The notice says he stated that AFMS is providing medical facilities not only to serving personnel and dependants of ex-servicemen but also reaching ordinary citizens who do not easily get such facilities.

## What next

The camp is over, and the notice does not give a date for the next camp or a way to apply. People who need an eye check-up or treatment can consult their nearest government hospital or eye doctor.
