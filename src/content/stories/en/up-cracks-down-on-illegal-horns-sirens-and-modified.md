## What happened

On the directions of Uttar Pradesh Transport Minister (independent charge) Dayashankar Singh, the transport department has acted against unauthorised hooters, sirens, multi-tone horns and modified silencers fitted in vehicles. The department says the drive is meant to curb rising noise pollution.

Additional Transport Commissioner (Enforcement) K D Singh said that between 04 April 2026 and 14 November 2026, action was taken across the state in noise-pollution cases. This included 47,258 vehicles fined and Rs 898.97 lakh collected as compounding fee.

## What Operation Peace did

To check noise pollution, a three-day special drive called 'Operation Peace' was run from 28 September 2026 to 30 September 2026. It targeted modified silencers, pressure horns, hooters and sirens.

During this drive, 746 vehicles were fined and Rs 45.40 lakh was collected as compounding fee.

## Who needs to take note

The transport minister appealed to all citizens, vehicle owners and event organisers not to use pressure horns, hooters or modified silencers in their vehicles. He said that while celebrating, people should make sure their joy does not become a problem for another family.

According to the notice, very loud noise can be life-threatening for children, the elderly and heart patients. The minister asked people to follow traffic and environment rules and respect the prescribed noise standards.

## What happens next

The transport minister has directed department officials to keep running drives with a zero-tolerance policy against vehicles that make more noise than the standards allow and against those who carry or operate unauthorised siren systems.
