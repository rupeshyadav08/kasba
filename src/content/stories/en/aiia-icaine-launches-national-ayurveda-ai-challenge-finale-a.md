## What happened

The All India Institute of Ayurveda (AIIA), New Delhi, marked the sixth foundation day of its incubation centre AIIA-iCAINE. On the occasion, it launched a national innovation challenge to encourage the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in Ayurveda. Two strategic MoUs were also signed, an Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) facility for Ayurveda was launched, and a capacity-building workshop on entrepreneurship and design thinking was held.

## What the challenge involves

The challenge is called 'AyushTech Innovate: National Open Ayurveda AI Challenge'. It is being organised by Techfest, IIT Bombay, with the support of the Ayush Ministry and AIIA. It will use IIT Bombay's open-source Ayurveda large language model 'AyurParam'. The aim is to encourage reliable, transparent, safe and practical AI solutions for Ayurveda.

The challenge will focus on areas such as Ayurvedic clinical practice, retrieving information from classical texts, research, treatment planning and public health awareness. Its grand finale will be held at IIT Bombay from 15 to 18 December 2026.

## The two agreements

Under the agreement with the World Ayurveda Foundation (WAF), a 'AI in Ay – Secretariat for Artificial Intelligence in Ayurveda' will be set up. This collaborative platform will help advance AI-based research, innovation and entrepreneurship in Ayurveda. Activities under the partnership will include joint research, funding and CSR proposals, capacity-building programmes, hackathons and startup incubation.

The agreement with Reliance Retail Limited (Pahadi Local) aims to turn classical Ayurvedic knowledge into commercially viable skin-care products. Formulation-related advice will be provided, while Reliance Retail will handle product development, testing, manufacturing and marketing.

## Why the IPR facility

The IPR facility for Ayurveda aims to raise awareness about protecting intellectual property. It will help Ayurveda innovators and entrepreneurs understand the importance of protecting their innovations. The facility will encourage protection of innovations moving towards product development and commercialisation.

## Achievements so far

AIIA Director Prof. (Vaidya) P.K. Prajapati said that over the past six years the incubator has supported 52 problem statements and mentored more than 100 startup ideas. Nine startups received seed funding of ₹3 lakh each, which helped them take their ventures forward.

## What AIIA-iCAINE is

AIIA-iCAINE was set up on 29 October 2021 under the mandate of the Ayush Ministry. It is India's first public-sector Ayurveda business incubator. It supports the growth of Ayurveda-based products, services and enterprises through mentoring, capacity building, institutional partnerships and industry engagement.

## What this means for ordinary people

There is no form to fill or deadline to meet for ordinary patients because of this news. The initiative is meant for researchers, students and people working in technology and startups. For the general public, any effect would come over time in the form of better Ayurvedic services and products.
