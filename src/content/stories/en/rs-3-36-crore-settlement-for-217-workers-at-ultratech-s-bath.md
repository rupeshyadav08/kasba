## What happened

The Assistant Labour Commissioner (Central), Jalandhar, working under the office of the Chief Labour Commissioner (Central) of the Labour and Employment Ministry, made conciliation efforts. As a result, two memoranda of understanding were signed on 14 October 2026 between the management of UltraTech's Bathinda unit and workers' unions.

The agreements were signed with the Indian Trade Union Centre (CITU) and the All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC). The ministry described this as an important step for workers' welfare and harmonious industrial relations.

## Who benefits

The agreements are expected to give about Rs 3.36 crore in financial benefit to 217 workers over the next five years. This benefit is directly linked to the employees working at UltraTech's Bathinda unit.

## When the benefits apply

A key feature of the agreements is that, as per their terms, the agreed benefits will apply retrospectively from the second quarter of the year. Retrospective means the benefits are treated as applying from an earlier date.

## How it was achieved

According to the ministry, the agreements were possible through constructive dialogue and cooperation between management and workers' representatives. The Chief Labour Commissioner (Central), Jalandhar, also took an active part.

The ministry called this an example of how conciliation can produce a solution acceptable to both sides and promote collective bargaining.

## What this means for ordinary readers

This news concerns the employees of one company's unit and their unions. It has no direct effect on the general public, and readers do not need to take any action.

The ministry also said the step reflects its commitment to protecting workers' interests, facilitating amicable settlement of industrial disputes and promoting fair labour practices.
