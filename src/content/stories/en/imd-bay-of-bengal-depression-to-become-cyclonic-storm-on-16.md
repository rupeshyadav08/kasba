## What has happened

In a press release dated 15 November 2026, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said a depression lies over the southeast and adjoining southwest Bay of Bengal. It is moving north-northwestwards and is likely to intensify into a cyclonic storm over the central parts of the south and adjoining west-central Bay of Bengal on 16 November. It may become a severe cyclonic storm over the west-central Bay of Bengal on 17 November and then move northwestwards towards the Andhra Pradesh coast.

## Who needs to be alert

According to IMD, heavy to very heavy rain at a few places, with isolated extremely heavy rain, is likely over coastal Andhra Pradesh and Yanam on 19-20 November. Isolated heavy to very heavy rain is likely over Tamil Nadu on 18-19 November and over Odisha from 19 to 21 November. Odisha may also see thundersquall with strong winds during 19-21 November.

## What it means for north India

Dense fog is very likely over Uttar Pradesh from 15 to 17 November. On the morning of 15 November, fog was reported in Bareilly, Kushinagar, Ayodhya and Azamgarh. Isolated to scattered rain is likely over Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, west Uttar Pradesh and east Rajasthan on 15 November. Parts of Madhya Pradesh may get rain around 20-21 November.

## Warning for fishermen

Fishermen out at sea have been advised to return to the coast by today, 15 November. They have been advised not to venture into the Andaman Sea and along and off the Andaman and Nicobar Islands till 17 November, the southeast Bay of Bengal till 17 November, the southwest Bay of Bengal during 15-19 November, the central Bay of Bengal during 16-20 November and the northwest Bay of Bengal during 19-20 November.

## What to do next

If you live in an affected coastal area, keep checking IMD's district-wise warnings. More details are available on mausam.imd.gov.in, including the national forecast bulletin and the district-wise warning page. The fishermen warning can be seen at rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in.
