The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment said on 12 November 2026 that a scholar has completed her PhD in genetics and plant breeding with support from the National Fellowship for Scheduled Caste Students (NFSC) scheme. The release says the scholar's name has been changed.

## What the scheme is

The NFSC scheme is run by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. Its aim is to give financial support to students from Scheduled Caste communities for higher studies and further research, so that a lack of money does not stand in the way of their education. According to the Ministry, the scheme works to make higher education and research opportunities easier to reach.

## What happened in this case

According to the release, the scholar received about Rs 11 lakh under the scheme during 2022 to 2025. This help eased the financial burden of her doctoral studies and let her focus on her studies and research. The release says she had faced several challenges in higher education because of financial hardship.

## What came next

After completing her PhD, she now works as a market supervisor at the Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC) in Kangra, Himachal Pradesh. The release says the support also helped improve her family's social and economic situation.

## Who benefits

The scheme is for students from Scheduled Caste communities who want to pursue higher studies or research. The Ministry says such initiatives strengthen educational inclusion and create opportunities for social and economic empowerment.

## What to do next

This release does not give any application date, process or eligibility rules. Students who want to know more about the scheme should check the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment's official information for the full details.
