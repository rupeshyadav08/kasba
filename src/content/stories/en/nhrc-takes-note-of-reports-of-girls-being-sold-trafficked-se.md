## What has happened

The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) has taken suo motu cognisance of a media report published on 30 October 2026. According to the Commission, the report said that in several districts bordering Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan, minor girls are sold by their parents to middlemen.

The Commission shared this in a release issued on 3 November 2026.

## What the report says

As per the release, the report said that parents sell their daughters to middlemen for Rs 3 lakh to Rs 5 lakh, citing poverty and other reasons. The middlemen then use fake documents showing the children as adopted to move them elsewhere and traffic them.

According to the report, on adulthood their personal details are changed to hide their identity, and they are sold for sex work in various cities of Maharashtra for Rs 35 lakh to Rs 40 lakh. The report mentions four specific incidents of trafficking.

## What the Commission said

The Commission said that if the information in the media report is correct, it raises a serious issue of violation of the human rights of the affected girls. It has therefore sent notices to the Chief Secretaries and Directors General of Police of Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan and Maharashtra, asking for a detailed report within two weeks.

## What happens next

At this stage, the Commission has only sought information. Any further step will be decided after the states send their reports. The release does not say where the report must be sent or what action the Commission may take next.

## What this means for ordinary people

The notice gives no application, form or deadline for citizens, so readers do not need to do anything. The matter is at the stage of information-gathering; no allegation has been proved against anyone.
