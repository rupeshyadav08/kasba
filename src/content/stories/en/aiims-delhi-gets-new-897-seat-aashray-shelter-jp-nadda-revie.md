## What happened

Union Health and Family Welfare Minister Jagat Prakash Nadda visited AIIMS, New Delhi, on 29 October. He reviewed the cleanliness, sanitation and waste management work being done there under Special Campaign 6. During the visit he also inspected the newly built patient waiting lounge at the AIIMS Trauma Centre extension campus, which is proposed to be used as a shelter called 'Aashray'.

## What the shelter offers

The lounge was built by Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), Ghaziabad, under its corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiative. Its total built-up area is 2,970 square metres and it has seating for 897 people. The building has primary and secondary waiting areas, entry and exit gates, ramps, toilets and other supporting facilities.

According to the notice, the facility is proposed to be used as an 'Aashray Shelter' so that economically weaker patients coming to AIIMS for treatment, and the people who come with them, get a safe, dignified and weather-protected temporary place to stay. It would replace temporary tent shelters with a permanent, covered option, and reduce the need for people to stay in the open around the hospital.

## Who benefits

AIIMS currently runs 'Aashray' as a 24-hour humanitarian shelter facility, mainly helping patients who come from outside Delhi and the people accompanying them. That means people who have no place of their own to stay in the city.

## Cleanliness and savings steps

The notice says AIIMS has adopted several practices to improve cleanliness. These include gang washing, dedicated cleaning staff for emergency and OPD toilets, end-of-day cleaning of the OPD, regular training for sanitation workers, timely payment of wages, and monitoring of cleaning supplies and mechanised cleaning equipment.

Steps have also been taken to save water and energy. There are 11 waste water treatment plants with online monitoring and smart metering. About 19.6 lakh litres of treated waste water is reused every day for gardening. Power consumption came down by 7.68 lakh kWh, a drop of 10.5 per cent. Compared with the same period in 2024, electricity expenses fell by about Rs 1.98 crore during January-October 2025.

## Getting around the campus

Forty free electric vehicle shuttle services run inside the campus for patients and their attendants. There are also 30 EV chargers linked to a smart monitoring system and 114 power points at four locations.

## What next

The minister spoke with sanitation workers and appreciated their role in keeping the hospital clean. According to the notice, the Health Ministry is stressing through Special Campaign 6 that cleanliness should be made an ongoing process. Ordinary readers do not need to do anything; for information on the shelter or facilities, contact AIIMS.
