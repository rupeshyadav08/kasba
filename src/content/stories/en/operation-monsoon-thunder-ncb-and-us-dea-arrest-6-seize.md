## What happened

On 21 November 2026, Home Minister Amit Shah posted on X congratulating the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) and the US Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) for the success of 'Operation Monsoon Thunder'. The operation was jointly launched to stop cross-border drug trafficking. The NCB is India's anti-drug agency and the DEA is its US counterpart.

The Home Minister said the arrests and seizures made in India and the US under this campaign show the strength of the India-US partnership.

## What the operation found

According to the results given in the release, six people were arrested from four cities in India. About 3,000 parcels were identified, containing nearly 5 lakh tablets, which had been sent inside the US.

Data of more than 59,000 people linked to the US was shared with the US DEA. The US DEA seized two consignments.

## What the US Ambassador said

US Ambassador to India Sergio Gor also posted on X praising the operation. He said the NCB, in close coordination with the DEA, disrupted a transnational drug trafficking network that was supplying illegal drugs across the US. He mentioned the arrest of six individuals and thanked the Home Minister for his leadership.

## What happens next

According to the release, further investigation is on. It aims to identify the masterminds and drop shippers, trace money transactions and uncover wider international links.

## What this means for ordinary people

This news has no application, form, deadline or money linked to it for citizens. It only shows that agencies of both countries are working together to stop drug trafficking.
