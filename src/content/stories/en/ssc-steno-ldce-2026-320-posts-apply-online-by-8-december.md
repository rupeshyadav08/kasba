## What is this exam

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) issued the notice for the Grade 'C' Stenographers Limited Departmental Competitive Examination, 2026 on 17 November 2026. It is meant for government stenographers already working in the listed services who want their names added to their cadre's Select List. Candidates from outside these services cannot apply.

## Which services are covered

According to the notice, the exam covers six services/cadres: Central Secretariat Stenographers Service (DoPT), Railway Board Secretariat Stenographers Service, Armed Forces Headquarters Stenographers Service, Indian Foreign Service Branch(B) Stenographers, Election Commission of India Stenographers Service and Central Vigilance Commission. Including ECI and CVC depends on the issue about their recruitment rules being resolved.

## How many posts

The tentative vacancies reported for recruitment year 2026 add up to 320 — UR 231, SC 69 and ST 20. The largest share, 287 posts, is for the Central Secretariat Stenographers Service (DoPT). A separate figure of 11 posts is shown for PwBD candidates.

## How to apply

Applications are online only, at ssc.gov.in. Candidates must first complete One Time Registration (OTR); an OTR made on the old website ssc.nic.in will not work on the new site. The Commission advises opting for Aadhaar-Based Authentication during OTR. Submit only one application, as multiple applications may lead to rejection.

## Where to send the form

After applying online, the printed form with the required documents, verified by the candidate's service or cadre controlling authority, must reach the Regional Director, SSC (Northern Region), Block No.12, CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi-110003 by 22.12.2026 (6 pm). For candidates in Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep or abroad, the date is 29.12.2026 (6 pm). Applications not forwarded by the Head of Department or Head of Office will not be considered.

## When and where is the exam

The Computer Based Examination will be held in Feb-Mar 2027 in Delhi-NCR only. The Commission may change the schedule and centre, and no request for a centre change will be entertained. The exam has three parts — Computer Based Examination (Part-A), Skill Test (Part-B) and Evaluation of Service Records (Part-C). More details are on ssc.gov.in and sscnr.nic.in.
