## What has happened

The second phase of Census 2027 has begun in Uttar Pradesh. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath addressed the launch event. In this phase, self-enumeration will take place first, followed by house-to-house collection of details.

## When will it happen

Self-enumeration in the state will run from 16 to 30 November 2026. After that, house-to-house enumeration will be held from 4 December 2026 to 4 January 2027. In the first phase, the listing and counting of houses was completed. Now information about every person will be collected.

## What will be asked

A total of 40 questions will be asked, covering religion, caste, education, occupation, mother tongue and other demographic aspects. The details will be recorded in prescribed forms. The notice describes this as India's first digital census exercise.

## Why the census matters

The chief minister said reliable data from the census will help prepare a solid plan for the state's development. So far, most development schemes in the state have been based on older census data. Without correct information, a large section of society stays cut off from schemes.

He gave the example of the Musahar community of eastern Uttar Pradesh, where many families did not even have ration cards. According to him, Census 2027 will help identify deprived groups correctly and link them to government schemes. Reliable data will also help decide where schools, health centres and roads are needed.

## What you should do

Young people have been asked to use the self-enumeration facility and help their parents, elders and neighbours enter their details online. Keep the identification number you receive after completing self-enumeration, and share it when census staff visit your home.

## Where to get help

A census helpline number, 4855, has been issued to resolve any problem or disruption. District magistrates will themselves lead the drive and review its progress every day.
