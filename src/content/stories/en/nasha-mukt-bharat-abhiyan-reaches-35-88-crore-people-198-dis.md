## What the campaign is

Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan (NMBA) is an initiative of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, launched on 15 August 2020. It is a nationwide awareness campaign against drug abuse. According to the ministry, it has now grown into a people's movement in which state governments, districts, educational institutions, civil society organisations and local stakeholders take part.

## How far it has reached

Till 12 November 2026, the campaign had reached more than 35.88 crore people. These include over 14.35 crore youth and 11.33 crore women. The ministry said that in 2021 the reach was only about 1.4 crore people. More than 34.07 lakh awareness activities have been held in educational institutions, encouraging students and young people to discuss the harms of drug use.

## Which districts it covers

The campaign began in 272 districts identified as especially vulnerable to drug use. In August 2022 it was expanded to 372 districts, and since 15 August 2023 it has been in force in all districts of the country.

## Treatment and counselling

Along with awareness, the ministry has strengthened treatment and counselling services. The number of people taking treatment and counselling rose from 2.08 lakh in 2020-21 to more than 9.11 lakh in 2025-26. The country now has 198 district de-addiction centres.

## What is Nasha Mukti Mitra

Nasha Mukti Mitra is a key part of the campaign, in which citizens join as volunteers to spread awareness and help those in need reach support. More than 2 lakh citizens had registered as Nasha Mukti Mitra till 12 November 2026.

## Why the campaign was started

According to the ministry, the first national survey in 2018 showed that more than 7 crore people in the country were affected by substance use disorders, including about 1.2 crore children and 58 lakh women. To deal with this challenge, the ministry started the campaign as a major awareness effort under the National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction (NAPDDR), which focuses on prevention, treatment and rehabilitation.
