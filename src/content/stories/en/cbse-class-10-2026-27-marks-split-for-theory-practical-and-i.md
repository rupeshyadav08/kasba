## What has been released

CBSE has released the subject-wise marks bifurcation for the Class 10 and Class 12 examinations 2026-27. It lists each subject's code, the duration of the theory exam, and the maximum marks for theory, practical, project and I.A. It also states whether an External Practical Examiner will be appointed for a subject.

## How Class 10 marks are divided

In most Class 10 language subjects, theory carries 80 marks and I.A carries 20. These subjects have no practical or project marks. Mathematics (Standard) has 80 marks for theory, 50 for practical and 20 for I.A. Subjects such as Science, Social Science, Hindi Course-B and Home Science have 80 theory and 20 I.A marks.

Music-related subjects follow a different split. They have 30 marks for theory, 50 for practical and 20 for I.A. Painting has 30 theory, 50 practical and 20 I.A marks.

## Points to note

According to the list, no External Practical Examiner will be appointed for any subject. Practical A/B sets will not be supplied. The list also states that theory A/B sets will not be supplied.

## What students need to do

The notice gives no application or deadline for students. Students can check their subject code and marks split so they know how many marks each part of the exam carries while preparing. For more details, the circular is available on the CBSE website.
