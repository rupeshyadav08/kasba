## What has happened

The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) has released a list of revised maximum retail prices (MRP) for 543 brands of cancer medicines. The list was issued on 28 October 2026 and the new prices take effect from 9 November 2026. NPPA works under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers and the Department of Pharmaceuticals.

MRP is the maximum price at which a medicine can be sold at a retail shop.

## Why the prices changed

The list includes information submitted by companies up to 27 October 2026. Earlier, on 17 October 2026, NPPA announced trade margin rationalisation (TMR) for 137 non-scheduled cancer drugs in the public interest, to address excessive trade margins, make cancer treatment more affordable and improve access to these medicines. Manufacturers were directed to submit their revised lists to NPPA.

## Which medicines cost less

The list includes brands from well-known companies. For example, RGP Life Sciences' Anarzol (Anastrozole 1 mg, 10 tablets) has an MRP cut from Rs 695.76 to Rs 73.00. Abbott Healthcare's Bevacizab (Bevacizumab 100 mg/4 ml injection, 4 ml vial) has an MRP cut from Rs 21,767.70 to Rs 5,930.13.

Similarly, Dr Reddy's Azacitin (Azacitidine 100 mg injection) comes down from Rs 6,337.00 to Rs 1,495.04, Natco's Dasnat 20 (Dasatinib 20 mg, 60 tablets) from Rs 2,812.50 to Rs 1,085.93, and RGP Life Sciences' Debrumab 60 mg from Rs 22,031.25 to Rs 5,941.00.

## Which patients benefit

According to NPPA, the 137 non-scheduled cancer drugs include immunotherapy medicines used to treat more than 20 types of cancer. These include Denosumab, used by breast and prostate cancer patients; Pegfilgrastim, used to prevent neutropenia in cancer patients; and Bevacizumab, approved for the treatment of colon, ovarian and cervical cancer.

## The list will grow

NPPA said the list will be updated as manufacturers submit their revised prices for non-scheduled cancer drugs. The full list of 543 brands is available on the NPPA portal, which is updated regularly.

## What patients and families should do

Check the price printed on the pack when buying a medicine. After 9 November 2026, brands on the list should be sold at the new MRP. For the full list and updates, check the NPPA portal.
