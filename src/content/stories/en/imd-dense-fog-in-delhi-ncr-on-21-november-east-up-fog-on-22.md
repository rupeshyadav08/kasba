## What the notice says

In a press release issued on 21 November 2026, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said that under the influence of easterly waves, isolated heavy rainfall is likely over Tamil Nadu from 23 to 27 November and over Kerala from 24 to 26 November.

According to the release, dense fog conditions are very likely over Delhi, Haryana, Chandigarh and Punjab on 21 November. Dense fog is likely over East Uttar Pradesh during 22-23 November.

## Weather in the past 24 hours

The release says very heavy rainfall was recorded over Gangetic West Bengal and Odisha in the 24 hours ending at 8:30 am on 21 November. Heavy rainfall was recorded over Chhattisgarh. The highest rainfall was at Digha (18 cm), Balasore (14 cm) and Korba (9 cm).

## What happened to Cyclone Akvan

According to the release, Cyclone "Akvan" lay over south coastal Odisha and adjoining north coastal Andhra Pradesh on 20 November. It weakened into a Deep Depression and then a Depression on 20 November, became a Well Marked Low Pressure Area over central Odisha late that night, and further weakened into a Low Pressure Area over north interior Odisha in the early hours of 21 November. At 8:30 am on 21 November it lay over north Chhattisgarh and neighbourhood as a Low Pressure Area.

## Region-wise forecast

Over Northeast India, isolated to scattered rainfall is likely over Arunachal Pradesh and Assam & Meghalaya during 21-24 November. Fairly widespread to widespread rainfall is likely over Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram & Tripura during 21-22 November, with isolated heavy rainfall there in the same period.

Over South Peninsular India, fairly widespread to widespread rainfall is likely over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry & Karaikal during 24-25 November. Isolated thunderstorm, lightning and gusty winds of 40-50 kmph gusting to 60 kmph are likely over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry & Karaikal during 23-27 November. Kerala & Mahe may see gusty winds of 30-40 kmph gusting to 50 kmph during 24-25 November.

## Delhi-NCR forecast

For Delhi, the release says the sky will be mainly clear on 21 November, with maximum temperature likely in the range of 26-28°C. On 22 November the sky will be partly cloudy, with mist or shallow fog at most places in the morning. Shallow fog is likely at many places on 23 November and at a few places on 24 November.

## What you should do

Take care while travelling in the morning in fog-affected areas. IMD has given links on its website for district-wise warnings and for fishermen warnings, where you can check the latest forecast for your district.
