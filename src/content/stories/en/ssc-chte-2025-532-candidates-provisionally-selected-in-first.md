## What has happened

SSC has declared the First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA) for the Combined Hindi Translators Examination 2025 (CHTE-2025). In this round, 532 candidates have been provisionally recommended for Identification Verification (IV). The allocation has been done as per the notice dated 05.06.2025, corrigendum dated 18.07.2025, addendum dated 27.06.2026 and the notice dated 03.03.2026 on the sliding mechanism.

## Who is affected

This is for candidates who appeared in Paper-II of CHTE-2025 and submitted their online option-cum-preference from 08.09.2026 to 11.09.2026. A total of 2,724 candidates submitted preferences, and 532 have been provisionally selected.

## Basis of selection

Selection is based on merit-cum-preference and performance in Paper-I and Paper-II. The minimum qualifying marks in Paper-II are 30% for UR, 25% for OBC/EWS and 20% for all other categories. In case of a tie, merit is decided by total marks in Paper-II, then total marks in Paper-I, then date of birth (older candidates placed higher), and then alphabetical order of names.

## Which posts are included

The posts include Junior Translator, Junior Hindi Translator, Junior Translation Officer, Senior Translator, Senior Translation Officer and Sub Inspector (Hindi Translator) in CRPF, across various ministries and departments.

## What to do next

Candidates provisionally selected should check SSC's website for instructions on the IV process. The notice does not state any further date or procedure.
