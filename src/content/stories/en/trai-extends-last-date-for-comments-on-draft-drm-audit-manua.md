## What has happened

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) issued the ‘Draft Telecommunication (Broadcasting and Cable) Services DRM-based Digital Addressable Systems Audit Manual’ on 9 October 2026. It is referred to as the ‘draft DRM Audit Manual’. TRAI had invited comments from stakeholders on it, with 30 October 2026 as the last date.

TRAI has now extended this date to 13 November 2026. This was stated in a Ministry of Communications release dated 30 October 2026.

## Why the date was extended

According to the release, several stakeholders asked for more time. They said the subject involves complex technical, operational and regulatory aspects that need detailed examination. Keeping these requests in mind, TRAI decided to extend the last date for written comments to 13 November 2026.

TRAI has also made clear that no further request for extending the deadline will be considered. This means any comments must be sent by 13 November 2026.

## How to send comments

TRAI has said comments should preferably be sent electronically. Two email addresses have been given for this — advbcs-2@trai.gov.in and jtadvbcs-3@trai.gov.in.

For any clarification or information, Advisor (B&CS) Dr. Deepali Sharma can be contacted at +91-11-20907774.

## Who benefits

The extension is for stakeholders connected with cable and broadcasting services who want to give their views on the draft. They now have a few more days to prepare and send their comments.

The release has no direct effect on ordinary viewers' daily life. It does not announce any new service, rate or rule; it only extends the date for sending comments.
