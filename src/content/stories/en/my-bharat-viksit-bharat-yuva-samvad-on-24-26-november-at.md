## What has been announced

The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports has announced that My Bharat will hold 'Viksit Bharat Yuva Samvad' on the occasion of Constitution Day. The programme will run from 24 to 26 November 2026. According to the notice, it will be held at more than 500 colleges in over 600 districts across more than 30 states and union territories.

## Who it is for

The initiative aims to connect young people in the 15 to 29 age group with India's development journey, its achievements and its future aspirations. The notice says youth will be encouraged to become active partners in the resolve to make the country a developed nation by 2047.

## What will happen at the events

In keeping with the spirit of Constitution Day, participants will read the Preamble of the Constitution together at their institutions. There will also be interactive discussions and awareness sessions. According to the notice, well-known youth icons will lead sessions at participating universities and colleges and give specially prepared presentations on 'Viksit Bharat@2047'. These will cover the country's progress in poverty reduction and socio-economic change, and efforts to improve people's living standards and livelihoods through inclusive growth and welfare initiatives. Progress in curbing Naxalism and restoring peace will also be presented.

## Focus on a drug-free India

The notice says the programme will stress the importance of building a healthy, empowered and drug-free society. It will explain how substance use affects a person's abilities, family well-being, social progress and national productivity. The message is 'a drug-free youth for a developed India'.

## Who will speak

Padma awardees will also interact with students, sharing their life experiences, personal struggles and thoughts, according to the notice. They will talk about the challenges they faced, what they achieved and the values that kept them going.

## Registration and information

The notice says the My Bharat portal (mybharat.gov.in) will serve as the digital platform for registration and programme-related activities. Going forward, My Bharat chapters may be set up in participating colleges and universities. For registration and the latest information, visit www.mybharat.gov.in.
