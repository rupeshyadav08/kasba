## What happened

NHAI, which works under the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, said on 23 November 2026 that net revenue at seven operational Multi-Lane Free Flow (MLFF) toll plazas rose 19.84 per cent compared with the period before MLFF was introduced there.

## What MLFF is

Normally a vehicle has to stop at a toll plaza to pay. Under MLFF, it does not need to stop. According to NHAI, this makes vehicle movement smoother, cuts congestion and travel time, lowers fuel use and emissions, and improves transparency in toll collection.

## Where it runs

The system began at Choryasi toll plaza in Gujarat on 1 May 2026. It was then rolled out at eight more plazas: Mundka, Daulatpura, Gharaunda, Manoharpura, Shahjahanpur, Paranur, Nemili and Chennasamudram. Nemili and Chennasamudram started in November 2026, so they are not included in the revenue figures.

## What the numbers show

According to the release, the seven operational plazas together earned about Rs 538.95 crore, against a pre-MLFF benchmark of about Rs 449.72 crore, meaning about Rs 89.23 crore in additional revenue. Average growth across the seven plazas was 21.10 per cent. Paranur in Tamil Nadu saw the highest growth at 26.95 per cent and Daulatpura in Rajasthan the lowest at 15.66 per cent. Shahjahanpur in Uttar Pradesh recorded 16.57 per cent.

## Action against toll evasion

The release says some vehicle owners deliberately hide, cover or tamper with number plates to avoid paying toll. NHAI is working with state governments and enforcement agencies to act against such violations. Police check posts have been set up at some MLFF locations, including Gharaunda toll plaza, where such vehicles are stopped and fined under the relevant provisions of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.

## How dues are being recovered

NHAI is using AI-based tools, including vehicle fingerprinting and integration with the VAHAN database, to identify such vehicles and trace their registered owners. E-notices are being sent to recover unpaid toll, and if they are not paid, services linked to the VAHAN portal for those vehicles are being blocked as per rules. The release says about 60 per cent of the value of e-notices issued at these plazas has already been recovered.

## What next

NHAI said it is committed to expanding barrier-less tolling across the entire national highway network and will keep strengthening technology-based monitoring and enforcement with state governments.
