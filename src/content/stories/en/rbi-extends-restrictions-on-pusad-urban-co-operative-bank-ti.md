## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended its directions on The Pusad Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd., a bank based in Pusad, Yavatmal district, Maharashtra. In its order dated November 5, 2026, the RBI said the directions are extended for a further three months from the close of business on November 7, 2026, to the close of business on February 7, 2027.

## What the earlier orders said

The RBI had first issued directions to the bank under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, on November 6, 2025, for six months up to the close of business on May 7, 2026. These were later extended up to the close of business on November 7, 2026, by an order dated July 31, 2026. The latest order extends them once more.

## Why the RBI extended it

The RBI said it is satisfied that, in the public interest, it is necessary to keep the directions in force beyond November 7, 2026. The extension is subject to review.

## Who is affected

The order concerns people who bank with this co-operative bank. The RBI also stated that the extension should not by itself be read to mean that it is satisfied with the bank's financial position. All other terms and conditions of the earlier direction remain unchanged.

## What to do next

The order does not ask customers to take any new step or meet any deadline. For details about how the directions apply, customers can check with their bank branch or the RBI's official communication.
