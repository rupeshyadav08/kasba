## What is this advertisement

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has issued Advertisement No. A-6/E-1/2026 for the U.P. Judicial Service Civil Judge (Junior Division) Exam 2026. The advertisement is dated 20 November 2026. It invites online applications.

## Who can apply

The advertisement names the post as U.P. Judicial Service Civil Judge (Junior Division). Details of eligibility, age limit and other conditions are not given in the notice. Interested candidates should check the full advertisement on the UPPSC website.

## How to apply

Online applications can be submitted from 20 November 2026 to 21 December 2026. The fee must also be paid by 21 December 2026. Apply through the official UPPSC website https://uppsc.up.nic.in.

## Corrections to the form

If there is any mistake in the application, it can be corrected from 20 November to 28 December 2026. The last date for fee reconciliation is also 28 December 2026.

## Who is affected

This advertisement is for those seeking recruitment as Civil Judge (Junior Division) in Uttar Pradesh. It does not directly affect the general public. Candidates who wish to appear for this exam should apply within the given dates.
