## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended the period of its directions on Nagar Sahakari Bank Ltd., Etawah. The directions were issued under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. In a statement dated 17 November 2026, RBI said the period is extended beyond the close of business on 18 November 2026, up to the close of business on 18 February 2027.

## What the earlier order said

RBI had issued these directions on 15 May 2026 for six months, that is up to 18 November 2026. RBI said it was satisfied that, in the public interest, it was necessary to extend the period further, and so the directions now run for three more months.

## Who is affected

This concerns the customers and depositors of Nagar Sahakari Bank Ltd., Etawah. RBI also stated clearly that the extension should not by itself be taken to mean that it is satisfied with the bank's financial position. In other words, this is a notice about the length of the directions, not a comment on the bank's health.

## What changes now

According to RBI, all other terms and conditions of the directions remain unchanged. The rules that applied so far will continue to apply until 18 February 2027. The statement does not mention any new change in those conditions.

## What to do next

The notice gives customers no new form, date or action. If you are a customer of this bank and want information about your account, contact the bank. The official RBI statement can be read on RBI's website.
