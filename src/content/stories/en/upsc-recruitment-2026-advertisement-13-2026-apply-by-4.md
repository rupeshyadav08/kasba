## What has happened

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has invited applications from eligible candidates for direct recruitment to various posts in the Government of India. The Commission has uploaded Advertisement No. 13/2026 and the necessary instructions for candidates on its website, upsc.gov.in.

## When and where to apply

Interested candidates can apply through the online application portal upsconline.nic.in from 14 November 2026 to 4 December 2026. The last date to apply is 4 December 2026.

## Who should pay attention

This notice is for people preparing for government jobs who are looking at UPSC direct recruitment. The Commission has requested candidates to follow the detailed instructions given in the advertisement.

## What to do next

Start by reading Advertisement No. 13/2026 on upsc.gov.in, which carries the posts and the instructions for candidates. Apply on upsconline.nic.in only after going through what the advertisement says.

## Where to get more information

For full details on the posts, eligibility and the application, see the Commission's website upsc.gov.in. The portal for applying is upsconline.nic.in.
