## What has been fixed

In a press release dated November 18, 2026, RBI said holders of SGB 2020-21 Series II whose premature redemption is due on November 19, 2026 will get ₹13,345 per unit. This price is only for redemption on that date.

## How the price was worked out

According to RBI, the redemption price of an SGB is based on the simple average of the closing price of 999 purity gold for the three business days before the redemption date. This price is published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA). This time, the prices of November 16, 17 and 18, 2026 were averaged.

## When early redemption is allowed

The notice says premature redemption of a Sovereign Gold Bond is permitted after five years from the issue date, on a date when interest is payable. This series was issued on May 19, 2020, so the next such date is November 19, 2026.

## Who benefits

Investors who bought this series and want to redeem on this date will get ₹13,345 per unit. People who do not hold this series are not affected.

## What to do next

The notice does not say where to apply for redemption. Contact your bank, post office or the place you bought the bond from.
