## What has happened

The Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare has proposed an amendment to the Drugs Rules, 1945. The aim is to make it mandatory for medicine manufacturers to formally authorise the person who releases drug batches and to keep prescribed records for every batch produced.

## What the current system says

Under existing manufacturing licences, producers have to give details of competent technical staff responsible for manufacturing and testing. But there is no specific provision to name the person authorised to release a batch. The proposed amendments seek to fill this gap.

## What the proposal changes

Under the proposed framework, applicants seeking a manufacturing licence will have to appoint a person with suitable qualification and experience for batch release. They will also have to maintain records of batch release as per prescribed requirements. The amendments also propose a prescribed format for a 'batch release certificate'.

## Why batch release matters

Batch release is an important step in drug production. Before any batch is released for distribution, its quality standards and documents are reviewed in detail. The ministry says these proposed requirements will fix clear responsibility for this work, allow tracking of batch release decisions and add a layer of monitoring over product quality.

## Who is affected

The ministry expects these amendments to help strengthen compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) among producers and further firm up the regulatory framework for drug quality assurance. The proposal concerns medicine manufacturers and those linked to licensing; ordinary patients do not need to do anything under it.

## How to send suggestions

Anyone with objections or suggestions on the proposed amendments can send them to Under Secretary (Drugs Regulation), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, U-6, Work Hall, C Wing, First Floor, Kartavya Bhawan-1, New Delhi-110001, or email drugsdiv-mohfw[at]gov[dot]in. The official gazette can be seen through the link given in the ministry's release.
