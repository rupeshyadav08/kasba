## What has happened

RBI issued a notice on November 17, 2026. It says the next date for premature redemption of Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) 2020-21 Series VIII is November 18, 2026. Investors who redeem on this date will get ₹13,499 per unit.

## How the price was fixed

According to the notice, the SGB redemption price is based on the price of 999 purity gold. It uses the simple average of the closing price for the three business days before the redemption date, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA). This time, the average of the prices on November 13, 16 and 17, 2026 worked out to ₹13,499 per unit.

## The early redemption rule

As per the government notification cited in the notice, an SGB can be redeemed early only after five years from its issue date. This can be done on a date when interest is payable. This series was issued on November 18, 2020, so the next such date is November 18, 2026.

## Who benefits

This information is for people who bought SGB 2020-21 Series VIII bonds. Such investors can redeem on November 18, 2026 and get ₹13,499 per unit. People who did not buy these bonds are not directly affected.

## What to do next

Whether to redeem is the investor's own decision. For this, contact the bank or post office from which the bond was bought. Full details of the price and date are in this RBI press release.
