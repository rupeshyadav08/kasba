## What happened

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology launched the Semicon 2.0 web application portal under the Semicon India programme on 6 November 2026. With this, the application process for the second, third and fourth pillars has begun. Union Minister Shri Ashwini Vaishnaw made the announcement.

## What Semicon 2.0 is

The Union Cabinet has approved Semicon 2.0 under the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) with a financial outlay of Rs 1,27,500 crore. It is the next phase of India's semiconductor journey and focuses on six pillars: chip design, machines and materials, setting up more fabs, the ATMP/OSAT industry, research and development, and talent development.

## What happened in the first phase

Under Semicon 1.0, 12 projects were approved in six states with total investment of more than Rs 1.64 lakh crore. Of these, 5 projects had started commercial production by October 2026. EDA tools were given to 105 startups, 24 startups got funding support and 15 received venture capital funding. Under the Chips to Startup programme, 332 institutions received EDA tools and about 70,000 students have been trained so far.

## 12 more approvals under ECMS

Continuing the approvals of 106 applications linked to a previously announced investment of Rs 69,548 crore, the ministry approved 12 more applications under ECMS with an estimated investment of Rs 7,503 crore. These are expected to bring production worth Rs 39,045 crore and 7,127 direct jobs. The approvals cover multi-layer PCBs, relays, heat sinks, speciality ceramics, copper foil, anode material, cathode active material, polypropylene film, camera rims and capital goods.

## Who benefits

The projects are in Gujarat, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Punjab, Tamil Nadu and Uttar Pradesh. The minister said about 50 percent of the units approved under the scheme belong to the MSME sector. ECMS has so far approved 118 applications covering 34 products across 16 states, expected to bring potential investment of Rs 77,051 crore, estimated production of Rs 5,73,146 crore and direct employment for 81,755 people.

## What it means for ordinary people

The scheme is for companies, so ordinary citizens have no application to make and no deadline to meet. Details are available on the official website of the Ministry of Electronics and IT.
