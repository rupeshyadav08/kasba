## What happened

The Railway Protection Force (RPF) organised the 8th All India Conference of Government Railway Police (GRP) chiefs in New Delhi, which concluded on 23 October 2026. The meeting was meant to discuss new security challenges, share good practices and strengthen coordination among the RPF, GRP and state police.

It was chaired by RPF Director General Sonali Mishra. Railway Board Chairman and CEO Satish Kumar, Railway Board members and GRP chiefs from different states and union territories took part.

## What changes with RailMadad

A key feature was the linking of GRP personnel to the RailMadad platform by the Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS). The aim is to improve passenger complaint redressal, share information on time and monitor complaints better. RailMadad is the system where rail passengers file complaints.

## Handling festival crowds

Crowd management during Diwali, Chhath and other festivals was discussed. Northern Railway's Delhi Division described work done with Delhi Police, GRP and Delhi Metro authorities, including CCTV monitoring, public announcements, barricading, designated holding areas and special control rooms. Advance planning and coordinated deployment of security staff were stressed.

## Stone-pelting and other challenges

Stone-pelting on trains was described as a challenge. Patrolling at hotspots, better surveillance, strict action against offenders and strong RPF-GRP coordination were emphasised. Joint training, preventing sabotage, sharing information immediately when narcotics are found, handling digital evidence and sharing intelligence were also discussed.

## New initiatives

South Central Railway's "Pink Patrol: Eyes in the Sky, Shakti on the Ground" was showcased, featuring women-led work and drone-based surveillance. Central Railway's Pune Division presented Project Disha, which connects children living near railway tracks with educational and recreational activities and safety awareness.

## What it means for passengers

This is a meeting of officials; no application, deadline or rule for passengers was announced. Still, better complaint monitoring and crowd management preparations may help rail travellers.
