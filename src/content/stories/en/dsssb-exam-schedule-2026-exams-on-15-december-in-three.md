## What has happened

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) issued an examination schedule on 23 November 2026. According to it, exams will be held on 15 December 2026 in three shifts. The exam is for Technical Assistant (Hindi), Assistant Archivist Grade-1 and Electrical Overseer Sub Inspector posts.

## Who is affected

This information is for candidates who have applied for these posts. The notice says these posts are linked to Delhi Archives and the Labour Department. People who did not apply for these posts are not affected.

## Exam timings

The exam will be held in three shifts. The first shift is from 09:00 AM to 11:00 AM, the second from 01:00 PM to 03:00 PM and the third from 05:00 PM to 07:00 PM. Which shift a candidate's exam is in will be known from the e-admit card.

## About the e-admit card

The board has said that the e-admit card will be issued and candidates must download it. The board has advised candidates to keep checking the DSSSB website and the postal department portal regularly. The board has also said it will send a system-generated message to all candidates.

## If no message arrives on the mobile

The notice clearly states that if a candidate does not receive any information on their registered mobile number for any reason, it does not give them any right to an extension of the e-admit card download date or a re-examination. Candidates are therefore advised to keep checking the website themselves.

## Where to get information

For exam-related information, visit the DSSSB website https://dsssb.delhi.gov.in. The board's address is FC-18, Institutional Area, Karkardooma, Delhi-110092 and the phone number given is 011-22378380.
