## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issued a direction on 30 October 2026. It says that five entries in the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) Taliban Sanctions List have been amended. The Ministry of External Affairs passed on this information to the RBI, based on UNSC press release SC/16494 dated 29 October 2026.

## What this list is

A UNSC committee set up under resolution 1988 (2011) keeps a list of individuals and entities associated with the Taliban. People on this list face certain restrictions set by the United Nations. The committee adds or changes names on the list from time to time.

## What banks have been told

Under Section 51A of the UAPA, banks and financial institutions must not hold any account in the name of a person or entity on this UNSC list. The RBI reminded them to strictly follow Chapter IX of its KYC directions and the procedure laid down in the UAPA Order dated 2 February 2021, amended on 22 April 2024.

## Who is affected

This direction is not directly for ordinary depositors or the general public. It is for commercial banks, small finance banks, payment banks, urban and rural co-operative banks, RRBs, NBFCs and other financial institutions. These entities have to keep their screening process up to date.

## Where to send a de-listing request

The direction says that if a bank receives a request to remove a name from the list, it must forward it electronically to the Joint Secretary (CTCR), Ministry of Home Affairs. Individuals or entities seeking removal can also apply directly to the UNSC Focal Point for Delisting, or through their country.

## Where to see the list

The updated lists are available on the UNSC website. They cover individuals and entities linked to ISIL (Da'esh), Al-Qaida and the Taliban. The RBI has asked banks to take note of these changes and ensure full compliance.
