According to information released by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Union Minister Shri Jayant Chaudhary attended an event in Mumbai marking NSDC's listing on the National Stock Exchange's Social Stock Exchange platform. On the occasion, NSDC issued its first Zero Coupon Zero Principal instruments, in which NABARD invested Rs 4 crore.

The notice says the programme will prepare 600 youths from economically weaker OBC families in Madhya Pradesh for international employment. Of these, 300 will be trained in Japanese for work opportunities in Japan and 300 in German for Germany. The training will be provided by NSDC International Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of NSDC.

The estimated cost of the programme is about Rs 11.25 crore. Of this, 15 per cent is the trainees' share, 35 per cent is supported through funds raised on the Social Stock Exchange and 50 per cent is to be provided by the Madhya Pradesh government. The government share is linked to successful international placement of candidates.

The notice says candidates who get jobs are expected to earn about Rs 1-1.2 lakh a month in Japan and Rs 1.1-3.2 lakh a month in Germany, depending on the job role and employment conditions.
