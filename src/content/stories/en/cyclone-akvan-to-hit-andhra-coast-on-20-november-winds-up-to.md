## What has happened

In a press release dated 17 November 2026, IMD said Cyclone Akvan is over the central parts of the south and adjoining westcentral Bay of Bengal. It had intensified from a Deep Depression into a Cyclonic Storm on the evening of 16 November. It is now moving slowly north-northwestwards and gaining strength.

## How strong will it get

According to the release, it is very likely to become a Severe Cyclonic Storm around the evening of 17 November. It may then become a Very Severe Cyclonic Storm around the morning of 19 November. It is likely to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coast between Nellore and Tuni around noon on 20 November.

## Wind and sea

At landfall, the maximum sustained wind speed may be 120-130 kmph, gusting to 145 kmph. The westcentral Bay of Bengal may see such strong winds from the evening of 19 November until landfall. Sea conditions are likely to become very high to phenomenal from 19 November.

## Where the rain will fall

Heavy to very heavy rain at a few places, with isolated extremely heavy rain, is likely over Coastal Andhra Pradesh and Yanam on 19-20 November. Telangana may see isolated heavy to very heavy rain on 20-21 November. Odisha may see isolated heavy rain on 18 November and very heavy rain on 19-21 November. In central India, isolated heavy rain is likely over East Madhya Pradesh and Vidarbha on 21 November.

## Where the sea may surge

At landfall, a storm surge of 1.0 to 2.0 m above the astronomical tide may inundate low-lying areas of Prakasam, Bapatla, Krishna, West Godavari, Konaseema and Kakinada districts of Andhra Pradesh. The peak surge is expected over Bapatla, Krishna and West Godavari from the morning to afternoon of 20 November.

## Warning for fishermen

Fishermen are advised not to venture into parts of the Bay of Bengal until the dates given: the southeast Bay of Bengal till 18 November, the southwest and westcentral Bay of Bengal till 20 November, the eastcentral till 18 November, and the northwest during 19-20 November.

## What about UP and Delhi

The release says dense fog is very likely over East and West Uttar Pradesh on 17-18 November. Dense fog is also likely over Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi and Punjab on 19-21 November. In the past 24 hours, dense fog was reported in Bareilly.

## Where to get more information

For the latest updates, see the national weather bulletin on mausam.imd.gov.in. District-wise warnings are on the districtWiseWarningGIS page, and fishermen warnings are at rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in.
