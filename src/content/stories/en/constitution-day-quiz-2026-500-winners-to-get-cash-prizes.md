## What has happened

The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, along with MyGov and MyBharat, has launched the Constitution Day Quiz 2026. The quiz is open from 18 November 2026 to 26 November 2026. It is being held on the occasion of Constitution Day on 26 November.

## Who benefits

A total of 500 winners will be selected. The top 100 winners will each get a cash prize of Rs 5,000, while the next 400 winners will each get Rs 2,000. In addition, all participants will receive an e-participation certificate.

## What the quiz covers

According to the ministry, the quiz encourages citizens to test their knowledge and learn more about the making of the Indian Constitution, their voting rights and the values that shape the country's democracy. It is part of ongoing efforts to spread constitutional awareness.

## How to take part

Citizens can take part through https://www.quiz.mygov.in and https://mybharat.gov.in/quizzes/yuva/. The quiz is available in both Hindi and English. Those who wish to take part must do so before 26 November 2026.
