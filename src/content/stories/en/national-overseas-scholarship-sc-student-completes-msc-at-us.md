## What happened

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment issued a release on 28 October 2026. It said that with help from the National Overseas Scholarship scheme, a Scheduled Caste student completed a Master of Science degree at Brown University in the US. The release gives the student's name as Aarav Patel (changed name).

## How the scheme helped

According to the release, assistance of USD 89,816 was given in financial year 2023-24. It was approved on 26 June 2023. The assistance covered tuition fees, a maintenance allowance, visa fees, medical insurance and travel expenses.

The release says the high cost of education at leading international universities was the biggest hurdle before him. With the scheme's support he could study at Brown University and gain knowledge, skills and a credential linked to his professional goals.

## Who the scheme is for

As per the release, the scheme is for eligible students from Scheduled Castes and other specified categories. Its aim is to help such students reach good academic institutions abroad. The scheme helps with tuition and other necessary costs of studying overseas.

The full eligibility conditions are not given in the release; they are set out in the scheme's guidelines.

## What next

The release does not give any application date, application process or information about the next instalment. Students who want to use the scheme should look at its guidelines and keep track of information from the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
