## What has happened

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) issued a direction on 27 November 2026. It expands the use of the 1601 numbering series in the second phase of implementation. Until now, this series was allotted to entities in banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) and the government sector. More sectors have now been added.

## Which companies will get the numbers

Phase two covers retail, e-commerce, hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, telecom service providers, internet service providers, automobile sales, automobile repair and servicing, travel agencies, commercial airlines, food aggregators, ride-hailing aggregators and loan service providers.

Not every company in these sectors will get a number. According to TRAI, only entities with an annual turnover of Rs 500 crore or more in at least one of the last three financial years are eligible. Distribution platform operators, DTH, headend-in-the-sky, IPTV service providers and multi-system operators must have at least 10 lakh subscribers in any one month during the last 12 months. Numbers will be allotted only to companies registered in India under applicable laws.

## What happened earlier

In the first phase, through its direction of 10 August 2026, TRAI allowed the 1601 series to be allotted to utility services, logistics and courier services. The new direction adds more sectors in the second phase.

## What changes for customers

According to TRAI, the 1601 series is meant only for service and transaction voice calls. Its use for promotional voice calls is strictly prohibited. Numbers are allotted only to verified entities, which helps customers identify genuine service and transaction calls.

TRAI also said the arrangement helps check impersonation fraud by people who use ordinary 10-digit mobile numbers to pose as representatives of well-known companies and mislead people.

## What companies have to do

Access providers must complete registration of all eligible entities and their migration to the new series within 180 days of the direction's issue date. They must also widely publicise the availability of the series among eligible entities.

Access providers must submit a progress report to TRAI every 15 days, and a final compliance report within 15 days after the 180-day implementation period ends. TRAI has said it may issue notifications from time to time to include other sectors or eligible entities, based on its review of implementation.
