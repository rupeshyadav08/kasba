## What has happened

The District Legal Services Authority, Lucknow has said that a special Lok Adalat will be held on 28 November 2026 (Saturday) at the District Court, Lucknow. The Lok Adalat is for pending traffic challan cases under the Motor Vehicles Act.

## Who is this for

According to DLSA Secretary Sonali Poonia, the main aim is to settle these pending challan cases quickly and easily through mutual agreement. This will help litigants get quick and easy justice and also reduce the burden of pending cases in courts.

## What litigants should do

The District Legal Services Authority has appealed to all concerned litigants in Lucknow district whose vehicle challan matters are pending in court to attend the special Lok Adalat on 28 November 2026, either in person or through their lawyer, and get their cases settled.

## Under whose guidance

The event is being held under the guidance of District Judge and DLSA Lucknow Chairman Malkhan Singh. The notice also says the step is being taken as per directions of the Allahabad High Court and the Uttar Pradesh State Legal Services Authority.

## Who is not directly affected

The notice does not mention any specific action for people who do not have a traffic challan case pending in court.
