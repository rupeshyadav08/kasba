## What has happened

The Department of Commerce has extended the deadline for Component II of the RELIEF initiative under the Export Promotion Mission (EPM) to 31 March 2027. The extension was notified through Notification No. 37/2026-27 dated 30 September 2026.

The government took this step in view of continuing geopolitical disruptions in West Asia and their impact on maritime logistics in the Gulf and nearby regions.

## What is RELIEF

RELIEF stands for Reimbursement, Export facilitation, Logistics and other steps. It was launched on 19 March 2026 as a targeted measure to help Indian exporters facing war-related export risks, including a sharp rise in freight costs, high insurance premiums and disruptions in the Gulf and wider West Asian sea corridors.

## What Component II offers

Component II aims to encourage exporters to take Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (ECGC) cover for exports to specific regions, with 95 percent risk cover.

The benefit is available for single policies and whole turnover policies taken on or after 16 March 2026. Under this component, the premium paid by eligible exporters stays protected if it rises above the pre-disruption level during the specified period.

## Which cargo is covered

The covered cargo categories include Full Container Load (FCL), Less than Container Load (LCL) and refrigerated (reefer) containers. However, energy exports are not included.

## How to apply

Exporters who want coverage under RELIEF must file their claim and related documents through the ECGC customer portal www.ecgcltd.in.

Guidance on creating a user ID and password, completing exporter registration and filing a claim is available through dedicated video tutorials on ECGC's official YouTube channel, @ecgcltd7016.

## Impact on ordinary people

This decision does not directly concern ordinary consumers or citizens. It benefits businesses and exporters who ship goods abroad and take ECGC cover.

The department said the extension reflects the government's commitment to strengthening export momentum, maintaining trade flows and supporting Indian exporters amid continuing uncertainty in geopolitics and maritime logistics.
