## What has been launched

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) launched the 'Network Anubhav' platform on 4 November 2026. It is a geospatial and analytical dashboard, meaning a portal that shows information on a map by location. It includes both user test data and TRAI's independent drive test data. The user test data comes from checks done through TRAI's MySpeed and MyCall apps.

## What you can see on the map

Users can view test results separately as cellular or fixed broadband. These can also be sorted by service provider and technology, with measures such as download and upload speed. The map can be navigated and zoomed to see where tests were done and how they are spread. Filters, legends, supporting tables and downloadable reports help make sense of the results.

## How to read the speed figures

The platform reports percentile values calculated from tests in a given area. P-50 is median speed: in half the tests, speed was equal to or above this value. P-80 is for mobile broadband, where 80 per cent of tests recorded speed equal to or above it. P-90 is for fixed broadband, where 90 per cent of tests recorded speed equal to or above it. TRAI makes clear that user-test based figures do not give a complete assessment of network quality by standard methodology, but they do give useful information about other users' experience in different places.

## A way to deal with spam

Through the 'Anubhav' portal, DND (Do Not Disturb) services can now be reached via a web interface. Users can set their preferences for commercial messages and calls, file complaints about unwanted commercial communication, track the status of their complaints, and view complaint information in total or by service provider.

## Who benefits

The platform gives consumers, service providers and TRAI information about various network measures. Through a secure interface, telecom service providers can view data on call quality, coverage and internet tests done by consumers, and identify specific locations for further analysis using location, date, time and technical data, and use this to improve their networks. TRAI can use the collected information for evidence-based monitoring of consumer experience and to improve network performance.

## What you can do

The portal can be opened at https://anubhav.trai.gov.in. TRAI has encouraged consumers to contribute regularly through its MySpeed and MyCall apps, as more participation will improve the coverage and usefulness of the information on the platform. For more information, you can write to advisorit@trai.gov.in.
