The Rural Development Ministry has said the fifth edition of its national gender equality campaign 'Nai Chetna – Pahal Badlav Ki' will be launched on 25 November 2026. That day is observed as the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. The campaign will run for one month, from 25 November to 23 December 2026, across the country under the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana–National Rural Livelihoods Mission.

## When and where it begins

The launch will be done by Union Minister for Rural Development and Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare Shri Shivraj Singh Chouhan. The event will be held on 25 November 2026 at 4:00 pm at Bharat Ratna C. Subramaniam Auditorium, NASC Complex, Dev Prakash Shastri Marg, Pusa, New Delhi. According to the notice, Union Minister for Women and Child Development Smt. Annpurna Devi, Minister of State for Law and Justice (Independent Charge) Shri Arjun Ram Meghwal, and Supreme Court judge and Executive Chairman of the National Legal Services Authority Justice Vikram Nath will be present.

## What this edition focuses on

The theme of Nai Chetna 5.0 is "From Access to Agency: Empowering Women through Livelihoods and Equal Opportunities for a Viksit Bharat". The ministry says four linked areas will get special attention: recognising women as economic contributors, strengthening their access to and control over assets, promoting safe mobility for women, and ensuring an equal sharing of unpaid care work. The campaign aims not just at awareness but at removing the barriers that limit women's access to livelihood opportunities, markets, financial services, public spaces and essential services.

## What happens at the launch

The launch event will release a joint inter-ministerial advisory of 15 ministries/departments. It will set out how ministries and departments align their schemes, programmes, institutional arrangements and grassroots networks with the campaign's goals. A signed four-party memorandum of understanding (MoU) on the violence-free village initiative will also be released. The MoU has been agreed among the Rural Development Ministry, the Ministry of Women and Child Development, the Department of Justice and the National Legal Services Authority. It aims to create a framework of institutional coordination to prevent gender-based violence at the community level and to provide help and justice to affected women.

## Who will carry it forward

New gender resource centres will be inaugurated at the event. According to the notice, these centres will act as a platform giving women access to services, benefits of government schemes, support systems and grievance redress. Self-help groups, village organisations, cluster-level federations and other community institutions working under the mission's network will play an important role in implementing Nai Chetna 5.0. The event will include two panel discussions and the release of the evaluation report of Nai Chetna 4.0.

## How the campaign began

The Nai Chetna campaign was launched in 2022 under the leadership of the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana–National Rural Livelihoods Mission. Since then it has grown into a nationwide movement to prevent gender-based violence, challenge discriminatory social norms and promote gender equality. This time, the active participation of men, boys and youth will also be encouraged.
