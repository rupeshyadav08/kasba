## What the weather office said

In a press release dated 22 November 2026, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said dense fog is very likely in isolated pockets of Bihar, east Uttar Pradesh, Jharkhand and Odisha on 22 and 23 November. Dense fog is also likely over Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi and Punjab on 22 November, and over Tripura during 22-24 November.

According to the release, dense fog was reported in isolated pockets of Punjab, Himachal Pradesh and Odisha in the past 24 hours. Visibility fell to 50 metres each at Gopalpur, Jharsuguda and Bhubaneswar in Odisha.

## What about rain

Isolated to scattered rainfall is likely over east Uttar Pradesh on 22 and 23 November, and over Bihar on 22 November. The release says no significant weather is likely over west and central India during the week.

Over south India, fairly widespread to widespread rainfall is likely over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal during 24-26 November, and isolated heavy rainfall over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal during 23-27 November and over Kerala and Mahe during 24-26 November. IMD attributes this to easterly waves.

## Delhi forecast

Delhi is likely to have a mainly clear sky on 22 November, with the maximum temperature in the range of 26-28°C. On 23 and 24 November, mist or shallow fog is likely at a few places in the morning, with maximum temperatures of 24-26°C and minimum temperatures of 10-13°C. On 25 November, mist or shallow fog is likely at many places with moderate fog at isolated places.

## What you should do

If you live in these states and travel early in the morning, drive slowly and keep your distance on foggy days. The release notes that forecast accuracy decreases as the lead period increases, so keep checking the latest updates.

District-wise warnings are available at mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/districtWiseWarningGIS.php, and the fishermen warning at rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in/fishermenwarning.php.
