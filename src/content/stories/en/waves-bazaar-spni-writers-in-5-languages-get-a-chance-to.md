## What has happened

The National Film Development Corporation (NFDC) and Sony Pictures Networks India (SPNI) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) during the 57th International Film Festival of India (IFFI) in Goa. Along with it, the Waves Bazaar × SPNI Content Creator Submission initiative was launched.

## Who can take part

According to the notice, writers, creators and production houses from across the country can take part. They can put their original fiction and non-fiction concepts before SPNI in Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu and Bengali.

## What formats are allowed

Entries are invited in several formats — fiction series, premium TV+ content, reality shows, game and competition formats, talent-based shows and other non-scripted concepts. So it is not only stories; ideas for shows and games can also be sent.

## What happens next

NFDC will manage the entries through the Waves Bazaar portal (wavesbazaar.com) and prepare an initial list based on mutually agreed criteria. Selected applicants will then be invited to present their concepts before SPNI for consideration and possible development.

## What Waves Bazaar is

Waves Bazaar is NFDC's industry platform, working under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. It connects creative talent with different stakeholders in India's media and entertainment ecosystem. The aim of this partnership is to widen opportunities for original storytelling, especially for talent from regional markets, so that diverse voices and viewpoints reach a larger audience.

## What it means for ordinary people

Ordinary readers have no direct task here. The opportunity is for people who write ideas for stories, series or shows and want to take them to a big platform. Such creators can submit an entry on the wavesbazaar.com portal. The notice does not give a last date for applying.
