## What has happened

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, in cooperation with the Delhi government, is holding a 'Transport Mela' from 9:30 am on 28 October 2026. The mela is being held under the 'Parivartan' scheme.

The venue is the 'Vehicle Inspection Unit' near the District Transport Office (DTO) in Burari, North Delhi.

## What the Parivartan scheme is

Through this scheme, the government is promoting more environment-friendly vehicles in place of old trucks and buses in Delhi-NCR. In other words, owners who scrap their old vehicle are offered several incentives to buy a new one.

According to the release, these include an additional discount of at least 8 per cent over and above the OEM discount, 5 per cent interest subsidy on vehicle loans, 100 per cent road tax exemption for 10 years, a one-time benefit for electric vehicles, a monthly fuel voucher and, where applicable, exemption from registration fees.

## What you will find at the mela

Vehicle manufacturers (OEMs) and their dealers, registered vehicle scrapping facilities (RVSFs), banks and non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), oil marketing companies (OMCs) and city gas distribution (CGD) entities linked to the scheme will all be present at one place.

Their representatives will give information, guidance and help on vehicle scrapping, financing options, buying a new vehicle, fuel-related benefits and other applicable incentives. Vehicle owners will not have to go from one office to another.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit is for people in Delhi-NCR who own an old truck or bus and want to scrap it and buy a new vehicle. Such owners can visit the mela to get information on scrapping, loans and buying a new vehicle in one place.

People who do not own an old truck or bus are not directly affected by this mela.

## Registration status

According to the release, about 40,000 beneficiaries have registered on the Parivartan portal so far. The release does not mention any separate registration or application for the mela.
