## What has changed

NTA has extended the application dates for NIFTEE 2027. The last date to apply without a late fee was 16 November 2026; it is now 24 November 2026. Applications with a late fee can now be submitted from 25 to 29 November 2026.

The correction dates have also changed. Corrections to the application form can now be made from 30 November 2026 to 2 December 2026. NTA has said clearly that no further correction opportunity will be given after this period.

## Who benefits

The exam is for admission to the National Institute of Fashion Technology. NIFTEE 2027 covers admission to UG, PG, NIFT Lateral Entry Admissions (NLEA), Artisans and Ph.D. programmes for the 2027–28 session. The extended dates give a chance to candidates who could not complete the form earlier.

## How to apply

Applications are made online, and the window has been open since 16 October 2026. The form is available at https://exams.nta.nic.in/niftee/. NTA has asked candidates to complete the application within the extended schedule.

## Be careful with corrections

NTA has asked candidates to make corrections very carefully. Once the correction period ends, no further chance will be provided. Check your details before submitting.

## Where to get help

For problems with the application, contact the NTA helpdesk at 011-40759000 or 011-69227700, or email nift@nta.ac.in. NTA has also asked candidates to keep checking www.nta.ac.in and https://exams.nta.nic.in/niftee/ for updates.
