## What has happened

The Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare has said its 'Health Pavilion' is set up at the 45th India International Trade Fair (IITF) 2026 at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi. According to the Ministry, more than 12,189 people visited its stalls in the first five days of the fair, between 14 and 18 November 2026.

The fair runs from 14 to 27 November 2026 and is organised by the India Trade Promotion Organisation (ITPO). The pavilion was inaugurated on 14 November 2026 by Dr M. Srinivas, Member of NITI Aayog, in the presence of Union Health Secretary Smt Punya Salila Srivastava.

## What visitors can get

The pavilion covers 989 sq metres in Hall 4(G) on the ground floor of Bharat Mandapam. It has 39 stalls representing 24 national health programmes and partner organisations. Visitors are offered free basic health checks, including BMI assessment, blood sugar testing, blood pressure measurement, routine eye checks, oral health checks, hearing assessment and blood grouping.

The pavilion also gives information on maternal and child health, immunisation, the U-WIN digital vaccination platform and services under the Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK). Visitors can get awareness and counselling on TB, HIV/AIDS, sexually transmitted infections, vector-borne diseases, viral hepatitis and leprosy.

## Digital health schemes on display

Digital health initiatives such as eSanjeevani teleconsultation, the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and Ayushman Bharat–Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) are also being showcased. There are special services on eye and vision care, hearing, dental hygiene and organ donation awareness, along with mental health awareness and counselling that includes information on Tele-MANAS.

## Activities for awareness

The pavilion's theme is 'Young India, Healthy India'. It hosts awareness activities on vector-borne diseases and filariasis, and street plays under the 'TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyan'. Magic shows, doctors' talk shows, live CPR demonstrations and quizzes are also being held. A dedicated 'Kids Zone' encourages children to play traditional games and stay physically active without screens.

## Who benefits

The facility is for people visiting the fair at Bharat Mandapam. The pavilion remains open to the public till 27 November 2026. For those who cannot visit the fair, this is not a separate scheme or a service available from home.
