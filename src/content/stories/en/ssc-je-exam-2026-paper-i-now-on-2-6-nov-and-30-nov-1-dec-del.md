## What has changed

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) issued a notice on 26 October 2026. The notice is a corrigendum to the Important Notice dated 1 October 2026. It revises the dates of two examinations.

SSC said Paper-I (CBE) of the Junior Engineer Examination 2026 will now be held from 2 to 6 November 2026 and from 30 November to 1 December 2026. The CBE for the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination 2026 will be held from 23 to 28 November 2026.

## Who is affected

The change applies to candidates appearing for these two examinations. If you have applied for either exam, your exam date is now different from the one given earlier.

## What to do now

Candidates should plan their preparation and travel according to the new exam dates. SSC has asked candidates to visit the Commission's website regularly for updates.

## Where to get more information

For further information and updates on the exams, check the SSC website. No information beyond the dates given in the notice has been released yet.
