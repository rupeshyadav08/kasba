## What has happened

Delhi's National Zoological Park, also known as the National Zoological Park, will remain closed to the general public on 21 and 22 November 2026. The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change gave this information.

## Why the zoo is closed

According to the ministry, the decision was taken because the first Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisation Dialogue Forum is being held at Purana Qila in New Delhi. Keeping in mind the arrangements and security requirements linked to this international event, the zoo has been ordered to close temporarily after approval from the competent authority.

## Animal care continues

The ministry made clear that during these two days, all necessary work related to the care of animals, feeding them, veterinary services, their welfare and daily management will continue as usual. This means the gates will be shut for visitors, but the wellbeing of the animals will not be affected.

## What visitors should do

The zoo has requested visitors to keep this temporary closure in mind and plan their trips accordingly. So if your family was planning a zoo visit on these two dates, move it to another day.

The National Zoological Park has expressed regret for any inconvenience caused by the closure and thanked visitors for their cooperation and understanding.
