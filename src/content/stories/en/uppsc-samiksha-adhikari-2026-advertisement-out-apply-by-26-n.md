## What has been issued

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has issued Advertisement No. A-4/E-1/2026 for Samiksha Adhikari/Sahayak Samiksha Adhikari and other posts. The advertisement is dated 26 October 2026 and will be shown on the UPPSC website until 3 December 2026.

## When to apply

Online applications can be made from 26 October 2026 to 26 November 2026. The last date to pay the fee is also 26 November 2026. Fee reconciliation will be done until 3 December 2026.

## Correcting the form

If something needs to be corrected after submitting the application, corrections can be made between 26 October 2026 and 3 December 2026.

## Where to apply

Applications are made online on the UPPSC official website, https://uppsc.up.nic.in. For details on the number of posts, eligibility and the exam, see the advertisement.
