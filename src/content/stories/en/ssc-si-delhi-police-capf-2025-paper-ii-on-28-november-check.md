## What has happened

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) issued a notice on 18 November 2026. It says that Paper-II of the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination, 2025 will be held on 28 November 2026. The Commission had earlier published this date in a notice on its website.

## How to check exam city and admit card

Candidates can view their examination city details by logging in through the designated login module on the Commission's website, ssc.gov.in. The admission certificate can also be viewed there. According to the Commission, the admission certificate will tentatively be available for download 2-3 days before the respective examination date.

## Who should pay attention

This notice is for candidates appearing for Paper-II of this examination. They should log in in time to check their exam city and download the admission certificate. Detailed instructions in this regard are available in the concerned notices of examination on the SSC website.

## What to do next

Candidates should keep an eye on the SSC website and read the examination notices. The official website is the source for information on the exam city and admission certificate.
