## What has happened

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment launched the online ‘Nasha Mukt Yuva, Viksit Bharat Quiz’ on 26 October 2026 under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan. According to the ministry, more than 25,600 people had taken part by 10 November 2026.

The quiz aims to raise awareness about the harmful effects of drug use and to encourage citizens, especially young people, to contribute to building a drug-free India.

## Who can take part and till when

A senior official of the ministry said the quiz is being held from 26 October to 25 November 2026 and anyone can take part. Interested participants can join through the My Bharat portal.

## What the quiz involves

The quiz has 20 multiple-choice questions and answers must be given within 10 minutes. Every participant who successfully completes the quiz will get a participation certificate. After finishing, participants can also see the correct answers, which the ministry says will help them improve their knowledge about drug prevention.

## Background of the campaign

The Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan was launched on 15 August 2020. It aims to reduce demand for drugs through preventive education, awareness, treatment, rehabilitation and community participation. It began in 272 vulnerable districts and has now been expanded to all districts of the country.

According to the ministry, the campaign has reached more than 35.84 crore people so far, including 14.33 crore youth and 11.32 crore women. More than 33.57 lakh activities have been held in educational institutions. Over 755 drug de-addiction centres run with the ministry’s support across the country, where 39.91 lakh people have been treated and rehabilitated. More than 46.73 lakh people have taken a pledge for a drug-free life and over 2 lakh ‘Nasha Mukti Mitra’ volunteers have been registered.

## What you should do

If you want to take part, you can join through the link on the My Bharat portal before 25 November 2026. The ministry has appealed to all citizens, especially the young, to take the quiz within this period.
