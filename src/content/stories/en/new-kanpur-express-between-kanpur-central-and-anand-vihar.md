## What has started

According to the Ministry of Railways, the new Kanpur Express between Kanpur Central and Anand Vihar Terminal was flagged off on 16 November 2026. Union Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw and Kanpur MP Ramesh Awasthi sent off the train. It is a daily service, meaning it will run every day.

The notice says the train will cover 428 kilometres and will have 22 modern LHB coaches. Kanpur Mayor Pramila Pandey and Akbarpur MP Devendra Singh Bhole were also present at the event.

## Which areas gain

According to the notice, the train will run from Kanpur via Phaphund, Etawah and Tundla to the Firozabad area, then Hathras, Aligarh, Khurja, Dadri and Ghaziabad, ending at Anand Vihar Terminal. Passengers from Phaphund will find it easier to reach industrial areas of Auraiya, including NTPC Auraiya and GAIL Pata.

Hathras is known for hing processing, brass products and agriculture-based industries, while Aligarh is known for its lock industry and Khurja for ceramics and pottery. The notice says rail connectivity for these areas will be strengthened.

## Who benefits

The notice says traders, students, workers and other passengers travelling between Kanpur and Delhi will get an additional option. Anand Vihar Terminal will also make it easier to reach different parts of the NCR.

## Other projects in Kanpur

The Railway Minister said the 16 km elevated railway track between Anwarganj and Mandhana will give lasting relief from long-standing jams at railway crossings in Kanpur. The project's detailed project report is ready and the required approvals have been received; construction is expected to begin soon.

Speaking of the proposed bullet train, he said its north corridor will link Delhi, Agra, Kanpur, Varanasi, Lucknow and Ayodhya and extend to Patna, Siliguri and Guwahati. According to the notice, once it starts, Delhi to Kanpur will take about 1 hour 45 minutes by bullet train, Lucknow to Kanpur about 27 minutes and Kanpur to Prayagraj about 1 hour 15 minutes.

## What to do next

The notice does not give the train's stops, timings or fare. To book a ticket or check timings, use the official railway website or ask at the station.
