## What has happened

Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) issued Notice No. 209 on 3 November 2026. It releases the Tier-II exam marks for the post of Junior Environmental Engineer (Post Code 68/24) in Delhi Pollution Control Committee and calls for e-dossiers from shortlisted candidates.

## How many appeared

The Tier-II online examination was held on 1 July 2026. Of the 152 shortlisted candidates, 124 appeared. The marks statements for this post code have been uploaded on the Board's website dsssbonline.nic.in, and candidates can view their marks by logging into their account in the OARS module.

## Who must file the e-dossier

Based on performance in the online examination, 26 candidates (OBC category) who scored at or above the cut-off marks for their category have been provisionally shortlisted. All 26 must log in to the e-dossier module through the OARS link in their individual accounts and upload all required documents.

## Which documents are needed

Candidates must upload education certificates, mark sheets, caste certificate, EWS certificate, Ex-Serviceman/PwBD certificate where applicable, admit card and proof of government servant, among other documents. They should also ensure they fulfil all eligibility conditions as on the closing date of application, 17 April 2024.

## What is the deadline

The e-dossier link will be active from 6 November 2026 to 20 November 2026. Shortlisted candidates are also being informed separately through SMS and e-mail on their registered mobile number and e-mail ID. If a candidate fails to upload the e-dossier during this period, the candidature will be rejected and no further opportunity will be given on any ground.

## What happens next

DSSSB has clarified that uploading documents in the e-dossier module does not confer any right to the post. Final selection will be made on a merit basis against the notified vacancies, provided the candidate fulfils all eligibility conditions. The shortlisting is also subject to the outcome of pending court cases. The Board reserves the right to rectify errors and omissions detected at any stage.
