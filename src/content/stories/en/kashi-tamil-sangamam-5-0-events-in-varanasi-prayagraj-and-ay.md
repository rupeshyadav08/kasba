## What happened

According to the Education Ministry, a high-level meeting was held at Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi, on 3 November 2026. It reviewed preparations for Kashi Tamil Sangamam 5.0 (KTS 5.0) and coordination among different agencies. The meeting was chaired by Smt. Deepti Gaur Mukerjee, Secretary, Department of Higher Education.

Those who attended included BHU Vice-Chancellor Prof. Ajit Kumar Chaturvedi, Uttar Pradesh Additional Chief Secretary (Higher Education) Shri Amit Kumar Ghosh, and senior officials of the Union Education Ministry, the state government, the district administration, BHU and Indian Railways, along with other key stakeholders.

## What was discussed

The meeting covered administrative coordination between departments and at the district level. It also discussed the special rail transport arrangement and other logistics by IRCTC, infrastructure facilities, exhibitions, excursions and the cultural programmes to be held under KTS 5.0. Senior ministry officials also visited the event venues, including Namo Ghat, to assess operational preparations.

## When and where

The fifth edition of KTS is scheduled from 28 November to 12 December 2026 on the theme 'Thirukkural Payilvom - Let us practise Thirukkural'. The aim is to bring the timeless moral and character teachings of the classical Tamil text Thirukkural closer to young people and learners across the country.

About 1,400 participants from Tamil Nadu will make an eight-day trip, organised into seven groups of about 200 people each, with four days of travel and four days of visits and a planned programme. The itinerary includes Varanasi, Prayagraj and Ayodhya, with cultural, academic, subject-specific and spiritual activities. 50 teachers from Tamil Nadu will also visit Uttar Pradesh and teach Thirukkural in schools and colleges during KTS 5.0.

## What is there for students

A nationwide student engagement programme will be held through 1,000 higher education institutions across the country. It will include structured study, research and campus-based activities to spread the teachings and values of Thirukkural. Details are available on www.kts.cict.in.

## Who is organising it

The web portal and the delegate registration process for KTS 5.0 were launched by Union Education Minister Shri Pralhad Joshi on 22 October 2026. The Central Institute of Classical Tamil (CICT), Chennai, is leading digital outreach, publications and institutional engagement, while IIT Madras and BHU are the nodal coordinating institutions for Tamil Nadu and Kashi respectively.
