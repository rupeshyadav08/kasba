## What happened

According to a release from the Ministry of Civil Aviation, a helicopter crashed on 30 October 2026. It came down in a forest area near Chai village (Joshimath). The helicopter was operated by M/s TransBharat Aviation Pvt. Ltd. and was registered as VT-HCX (type AS350B3).

## Where was it flying

The release says the helicopter took off from Badrinath helipad at 13:14 and was on its route to Sahastradhara helipad in Dehradun. One pilot and five passengers were on board.

## What is happening now

The ministry said the wreckage has been found and search and rescue operations are ongoing. A team of the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) has been sent to Dehradun for the preliminary/regulatory investigation. The DGCA is the regulator for the aviation sector and conducts such investigations.

## What this means for ordinary readers

The crash took place in Uttarakhand. Ordinary readers in UP, Bihar, MP and Delhi do not need to take any action in connection with it. People planning helicopter travel to places like Badrinath should check the latest information with their operator or the local administration.

## What next

The release does not say anything about the findings of the investigation or a timeline. Until the investigation reports, no conclusion can be drawn about the cause of the crash. Further information on the matter may come from the Ministry of Civil Aviation and the DGCA.
