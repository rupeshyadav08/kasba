## What happened

Union Labour and Employment Minister Dr Mansukh Mandaviya inaugurated the EPFO lounge and the ESIC pavilion at the India International Trade Fair (IITF) 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 15 November 2026. Central Provident Fund Commissioner Ramesh Krishnamurthy, ESIC Director General Ashok Kumar Singh and other senior officials of both bodies were present.

After the inauguration, the minister reviewed the facilities at the kiosks and spoke to visitors. According to the release, the aim of these initiatives is to make social security services simpler and more accessible for workers, pensioners, employers and the general public.

## What the EPFO lounge offers

The lounge has a puppet show ('Raja Rancho') that explains the benefits of EPFO. There are also several kiosks: a 'Nidhi Aapke Nikat' grievance redressal kiosk, an e-services desk for online guidance, filing claims and KYC correction, a Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana/face authentication technology kiosk, a kiosk giving information on new EPFO schemes, a pension help desk for pension queries and digital life certificates, and an NCS/e-Shram kiosk for employment and social security services.

## What the ESIC pavilion offers

Visitors can get free health checks, including blood pressure check and blood glucose test, along with on-the-spot Aadhaar seeding and complaint resolution at the counter. They can also speak face to face with senior ESIC officials from Delhi and Haryana.

To make awareness interactive, the pavilion has digital screens, audio-visual presentations, a 'Know ESIC' quiz and activities such as 'Take a selfie and become a doctor'. Daily goodie bags and other gifts are kept for lucky participants. A QR-code based direct feedback system has also been started, and the Insurance Commissioner monitors these responses daily.

## Where and when to go

Both places are open to visitors from 14 to 27 November 2026. They are in Hall No. 4 at Bharat Mandapam: the EPFO lounge on the ground floor at stall number 8A and the ESIC pavilion on the first floor at stall number 4F-05-A.

The release invites workers, pensioners, employers, their families and the general public to visit both stalls, use the services available on the spot and learn more about the government's social security schemes.
