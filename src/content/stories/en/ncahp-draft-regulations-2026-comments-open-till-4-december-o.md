The National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions (NCAHP), which works under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, has issued a draft framework for provisional registration of allied and healthcare professionals. According to the notice dated 11 November 2026, the draft regulations are framed under the NCAHP Act, 2021, and comments have been invited till 4 December 2026.

## What the draft proposes

The notice says the draft sets standard eligibility criteria and clear procedures for provisional registration. It covers verification of certificates and documents, online applications and issue of registration certificates.

It also proposes provisions on the validity of provisional registration, extension of its period, expiry and appeals. An integrated online registration system is proposed so that the process is simpler and more uniform across all states and union territories.

## Who benefits

According to the notice, eligible professionals who are provisionally registered will get chances to take prescribed bridge courses, supervised clinical training and, where applicable, minimum qualifying examinations. This is meant to help them meet the requirements for regular registration.

Those granted provisional registration will be given a unique identification number, called a PR UID. The notice says this is for orderly identification and regulatory monitoring.

## How to send comments

The draft regulations are available on the NCAHP website, ncahp.abdm.gov.in. Professionals, their associations, academic institutions, state councils, health institutions, experts and the general public can send comments, suggestions and objections to ncahp.regulations@ncahp.mohfw.gov.in till 4 December 2026.

The notice adds that the comments received will be considered before the regulations are finalised. In other words, this is a proposal, not a rule in force yet.
