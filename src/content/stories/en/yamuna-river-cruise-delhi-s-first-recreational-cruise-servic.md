Delhi has a new recreational service on the Yamuna. On 10 November 2026, Union Minister for Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal inaugurated the city's first recreational 'Yamuna River Cruise' service at Sonia Vihar. Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta was present at the event.

## What has started

The service has been developed on the Sonia Vihar–Jagatpur stretch. It uses a 30-seat electric cruise vessel that takes visitors on a ride of about 6 km on the river. According to the notice, the use of electric vessels shows the potential of inland waterways for clean and sustainable transport.

## What else is at the site

The site has been planned as an environment-friendly tourism spot. A key attraction is a musical fountain, where a light and sound show presents the history and spiritual importance of the Yamuna. It depicts the river's connection with Surya Dev and Yamraj, and the divine deeds of Lord Krishna, especially the famous 'Kaliya Daman'.

The site also has a food court, a shopping area, a play zone for children and adventure sports including skating and rock climbing. For visitors, there are modern floating jetties, separate ticket counters, online booking, rest rooms, waiting areas and illuminated pathways.

## Yamuna's status

Under the National Waterways Act, 2016, the Yamuna has been declared 'National Waterway-110'. According to the notice, the launch of the cruise service is an important step in developing this waterway for recreation and river-based tourism.

## Who built it

The service has been developed by the Inland Waterways Authority of India (IWAI) under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. The work was done with the support of the Delhi Development Authority, Delhi Tourism and Transportation Development Corporation, Delhi Jal Board and the Delhi government's Irrigation and Flood Control Department.

## The bigger picture

According to the notice, the initiative is part of a wider revival of India's inland waterways. Cargo moved on national waterways rose from 18 million metric tonnes in 2013-14 to 218 million metric tonnes in 2025-26. The notice puts the cost of moving cargo on waterways at about Rs 1.06 per tonne per kilometre, against Rs 1.41 by rail and Rs 2.28 by road. The number of overnight river cruise vessels rose from 5 in 2013-14 to 26 in 2025-26.

The notice does not give ticket prices, timings or booking details.
