## What happened

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment issued a release on 13 November 2026. It gives an example of the help provided by the National Fellowship for Scheduled Caste students (NFSC). According to the release, a research scholar from Narsinghpur in Odisha's Cuttack district (named Arjun Naik, a changed name, in the release) was able to complete a PhD in anthropology with the fellowship's help.

## What the NFSC fellowship is

According to the release, NFSC gives financial support to eligible Scheduled Caste research scholars doing higher studies or research. The support includes a fellowship of Rs 16,000 per month, house rent allowance (HRA) and help with contingency expenses. The ministry said it is supporting the academic progress of SC students through this fellowship.

## What the scholar received

The release says he began receiving support under the National Fellowship (SC) in 2017. The money helped him meet study costs, fieldwork needs and research-related expenses. The release says limited financial resources, little academic exposure and social marginalisation had made it hard for him to reach good higher education and research opportunities. The fellowship eased this strain and let him focus on his studies.

## What happened next

According to the release, with the fellowship's support and his own sustained effort, he completed his PhD in anthropology. He now works as an assistant professor in the anthropology department of Utkal University, Bhubaneswar, where he teaches, does research and guides students. The release says this brought him professional growth and improved his family's financial situation.

## Who benefits

The information is useful to Scheduled Caste research scholars who want to pursue higher studies or a PhD and are worried about costs. Ordinary readers are not directly affected by this release. It does not state application dates, eligibility rules or where to apply. For such details, readers should check the ministry's official information.
