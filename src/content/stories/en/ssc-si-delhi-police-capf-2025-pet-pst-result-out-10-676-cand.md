## What has happened

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) declared the result of the Physical Endurance Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) of the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination, 2025 on 29 October 2026. The exam is for Sub-Inspector posts in Delhi Police and the Central Armed Police Forces.

The PET/PST was held for candidates who had qualified in Paper-I. CAPFs conducted the test from 21.07.2026 to 30.07.2026.

## How many qualified

Paper-I result was declared on 30.03.2026, in which 48,387 candidates qualified. Of these, 10,676 candidates qualified in PET/PST. As many as 26,949 candidates were absent and 10,622 were not qualified. The results of 136 candidates have been withheld, and 04 candidates were temporarily unfit.

The list of qualified candidates includes both female and male candidates. SSC has also given their category-wise numbers in the notice.

## What happens next

Candidates who qualified in PET/PST are now eligible to appear in Paper-II. Paper-II is tentatively scheduled for 28.11.2026. Admission Certificates will be issued to qualified candidates in due course by the concerned Regional Offices.

Candidates have been advised to follow the websites of SSC (HQs) and their Regional Office for information about the Admission Certificate.

## Withheld results and court cases

The results of 04 female candidates have been kept as temporarily unfit. The results of 136 candidates have been withheld for various reasons on the recommendation of BSF. Their roll numbers are given in List-III of the notice.

97 candidates have been provisionally permitted to appear in Paper-II because their matters are pending before various courts. SSC has made clear that merely appearing in Paper-II does not give them any right to final selection. Their candidature and final results will remain subject to the final outcome of the court cases.
