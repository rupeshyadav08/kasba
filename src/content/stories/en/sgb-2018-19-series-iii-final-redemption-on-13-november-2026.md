## What has happened

RBI issued a notice on 12 November 2026. It says the final redemption of Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme 2018-19 Series-III will take place on 13 November 2026. The bonds of this tranche were issued on 13 November 2018.

## What is the redemption price

According to RBI, the redemption price for this final redemption is ₹14,024 per unit of SGB. This price is based on the simple average of the closing price of 999 purity gold on three business days — 10, 11 and 12 November 2026. That closing price is published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA).

## What is an SGB

Under the Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme, the government issues Gold Bonds. The notice says the bonds are repayable on the expiry of eight years from the date of issue. The final redemption date of this tranche follows that rule.

## Who is affected

This notice is for investors who hold Gold Bonds of the 2018-19 Series-III. They will be paid at ₹14,024 per unit on 13 November 2026. People who did not buy this bond are not directly affected.

## What to do next

The RBI notice does not state any separate step for investors. For details about your bond, payment or anything else, contact the bank or post office you bought it from.
