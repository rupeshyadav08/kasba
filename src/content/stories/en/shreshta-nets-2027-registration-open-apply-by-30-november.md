## What has started

The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, in collaboration with the National Testing Agency (NTA), has started the online registration process for the Shreshta National Entrance Test (NETS) 2027. Registration began on 14 November 2026 and will remain open till 11:50 pm on 30 November 2026.

## What is the Shreshta scheme

Shreshta stands for the Scheme for Residential Education for Students in High Schools in Targeted Areas. It aims to provide quality education to meritorious students from Scheduled Castes (SC) and those Denotified Tribes (DNT) that do not fall under the SC, ST and OBC categories, by facilitating their admission to reputed private residential schools. According to the ministry, the scheme seeks to bridge educational inequalities and help students of targeted communities fulfil their academic aspirations.

## Who benefits

The opportunity is for SC and eligible DNT students in high school who wish to study in reputed private residential schools. The ministry said such initiatives give students a conducive learning environment so they can achieve academic excellence and realise their full potential.

## How to apply

Eligible candidates can submit their online applications through the NTA portal https://exams.nta.nic.in/shreshta. If any necessary correction is needed in the submitted form, a correction window will be open from 2 to 4 December 2026.

## What happens next

The Shreshta National Entrance Test (NETS) 2027 is scheduled to be held on 10 January 2027. The ministry has urged all eligible candidates to complete their registration within the deadline.

## Where to get help

For any questions or assistance related to the exam and application process, candidates can contact the NTA at shreshta@nta.ac.in or the helpline numbers 011-40759000 / 1800-180-4747. Information is also available on the NTA website www.nta.ac.in.
