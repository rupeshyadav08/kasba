## What the festival is and where it is

According to the Ministry of Rural Development, Union Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan inaugurated the Saras Shakti Food Festival 2026 at Sunder Nursery in New Delhi on 23 November 2026. The festival runs from 21 November to 8 December 2026.

The ministry said women entrepreneurs linked to self-help groups from different states are showcasing their traditional dishes, local food and regional culinary skills at the event.

## Who benefits

According to the notice, more than 90 food stalls have been set up, and over 500 women entrepreneurs from self-help groups across the country are getting a chance to display their products and cooking and to connect directly with potential buyers. The ministry says this aims to give their products a wider market, strengthen their identity and branding, and open new business opportunities.

On the other side, people in Delhi-NCR can taste traditional dishes from different states. The minister said the festival offers everything from Gir cow ghee to Uttarakhand's bal mithai and Bihar's litti-chokha.

## What dishes are on offer

The notice mentions Bihar's litti-chokha, Hyderabadi dum biryani from Telangana, Punjab's makke ki roti with sarson ka saag, Assam's mushroom pitha, dishes made from traditional forest produce of Kerala's Attappadi region and Chhattisgarh's chousela, among other regional dishes.

## When and where to go

The food festival at Sunder Nursery is open to the public every day from 11:30 am to 9:30 pm. According to the ministry, the event is an effort to bring rural women's skills and products to consumers in Delhi.

## What next

The notice mentions no application, registration or ticket for the public. For more details, readers can refer to the release issued by the Ministry of Rural Development.
