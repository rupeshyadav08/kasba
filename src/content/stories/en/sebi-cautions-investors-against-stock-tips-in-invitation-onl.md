## What SEBI has said

SEBI said some unregistered persons are offering stock tips and trading calls to investors through invitation-only WhatsApp groups. These operators pose as market experts and mostly use overseas WhatsApp numbers. According to SEBI, the calls are shared in the name of free investor education.

## How the groups look credible

SEBI said certain members of these groups act in concert with the operators and post false claims of profit along with praise for the 'expert'. This practice is known as astroturfing. It creates a false impression of genuine support and pushes unsuspecting investors to act on the calls.

## What happens when a group is suspended

According to SEBI, when such a group is suspended after action by enforcement agencies or regulatory bodies, its members get a message, usually from a new number, inviting them to join a new group. The message says the earlier group was banned due to social media platform risk control measures and that the new group will give daily stock information and trading guidance. SEBI has called such messages a red flag.

## What investors should do

SEBI has advised investors not to join invitation-only WhatsApp groups run by unknown persons or by people claiming to be stock market experts, particularly those run from overseas numbers, and not to act on tips shared there. It also said investors should not be swayed by praise, profit claims or trade confirmations posted by other members, as these may be planted.

## Money and account details

SEBI said investors should not open an account on, or transfer money to, any trading platform or app suggested in such groups. They should also not share their holdings or trading account details with such persons.

## How to check registration

SEBI has asked investors to deal only with SEBI registered intermediaries and use only authentic trading apps. The registration status of entities can be verified at sebi.gov.in/intermediaries.html, and the trading apps of SEBI registered intermediaries at investor.sebi.gov.in/Investor-support.html. SEBI said the press release was issued to caution investors and urge them to remain vigilant while dealing in the securities market.
