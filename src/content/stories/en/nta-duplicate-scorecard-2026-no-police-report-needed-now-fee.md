## What has changed

NTA issued a corrigendum on 2 November 2026 amending its office memorandum (OM) dated 7 September 2026 on the procedure for issuing a duplicate scorecard. A police or embassy report is no longer mandatory; it is to be furnished only if the applicant has lodged a report.

Some paragraphs of the OM (5.2, 5.3 and 7.3) have been deleted. The fee payment method has also been clarified. The fee is to be paid through the online payment gateway integrated with the NTA portal by NEFT to a Canara Bank account. Where online payment is not feasible, a Demand Draft in favour of 'National Testing Agency', payable at New Delhi, may be used.

## Who is affected

This change is for candidates whose NTA scorecard is lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed and who want a duplicate copy. They will no longer have to submit a police or embassy report in every case.

## What to keep in mind in the application

The application form, affidavit and indemnity-cum-undertaking in Annexures I, II and III are to be read in line with these corrections. Portions relating to loss, theft, mutilation, damage or destruction of the scorecard, lodging a police or embassy report, efforts to trace the scorecard and surrender of the original apply only where relevant. An applicant may strike out portions that do not apply and state the actual reason.

## Will a printed copy be given

No. The duplicate scorecard is an electronically generated document, so no physical copy can be provided. All other contents of the OM remain unchanged.
