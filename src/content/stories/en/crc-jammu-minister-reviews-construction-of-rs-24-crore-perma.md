## What happened

Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment Dr Virendra Kumar visited Chak Jwala village in Vijaypur, Samba district, in the Jammu division of Jammu and Kashmir. A permanent campus of the Composite Regional Centre (CRC) Jammu for persons with disabilities is being built there. The minister reviewed the progress of the ongoing construction and stressed completing the remaining work on time.

According to the release, the campus is being built at a cost of about Rs 24 crore.

## Where things stand now

The release says CRC Jammu currently works on a limited scale in Jammu city. Once the permanent campus is ready, the centre's capacity will grow and persons with disabilities in the region will be able to get rehabilitation, education and support services more widely.

During the visit, CRC Jammu Director Dr Ronika Sharma briefed the minister on the work completed so far and the work remaining. She also told him about the basic facilities needed to finish and start the campus on time.

## What the campus will offer

According to the release, activities related to rehabilitation, special education, early intervention, skill development and empowerment of persons with disabilities will be held at the campus once it opens.

The minister said physiotherapy and special training will be provided to help children with disabilities become self-reliant. Parents and caregivers will also be trained to help care for these children better.

A PM Divyasha Kendra is planned at the centre, which will help provide assistive devices to persons with disabilities. Labs for measuring, making and fitting artificial limbs and other assistive devices are also planned.

## Study and job prospects

The release says courses such as B.Ed and M.Ed related to disability are planned at the centre. This is expected to increase education opportunities and improve job prospects for trained young people in the field of rehabilitation and empowerment.

## Who runs the centre

The release says CRC Jammu comes under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. It is operated through the Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya National Institute for Persons with Physical Disabilities, New Delhi.

## What next

The release does not give a date for the campus to be completed. The minister stressed completing the construction on time and ensuring proper facilities so that persons with disabilities get effective services.
