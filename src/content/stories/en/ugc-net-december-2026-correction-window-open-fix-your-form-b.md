## What has happened

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has given candidates a fixed window to correct mistakes in the online application form for UGC-NET December 2026. According to the notice, the facility is available from 2 November 2026 to 4 November 2026, up to 11:50 PM.

UGC-NET is the exam that decides eligibility for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to Ph.D. programmes in Indian universities and colleges.

## Who can use the window

All registered candidates of UGC-NET December 2026 can use this facility. They have been advised to visit the official website https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in and check the particulars in their application forms. Corrections can be made only in the fields the notice permits.

## What can be changed

As per the notice, candidates can change their mobile number, e-mail address, permanent and present address, and the uploaded photograph and signature. They can also correct date of birth, gender, category, sub-category/PwBD (if not verified by the UDID portal) and subject.

Of the candidate's name, father's name and mother's name, only one can be changed. Examination city preferences can be changed based on permanent and present addresses, but NTA is not bound to allot a city as per the candidate's preference.

## What cannot be changed

The notice also lists fields that candidates are not allowed to change. Corrections can be submitted only once; once the corrected form is submitted, it is frozen and no further changes are permitted.

## What about the fee

If a correction increases the applicable fee, the candidate must pay the additional amount. Payment can be made only through credit/debit card, net banking or UPI. Fees already paid will not be refunded. Corrections that require an additional fee take effect only after that fee is successfully paid.

## Keep in mind

NTA has made clear that this is a one-time facility and no correction will be accepted after 11:50 PM on 4 November 2026. Fill the form carefully. For any query, contact the NTA helpdesk at 011-40759000 or write to ugcnet@nta.ac.in.
