## What has been announced

The Delhi Circle of the Postal Department will hold a Circle Level Postal Adalat on 26 November 2026. It will be held at the Office of the Chief Postmaster General, Delhi Circle, 3rd Floor, Meghdoot Bhawan, near Jhhandewalan Metro Station, New Delhi-110001, starting at 11:00 am.

## Whose complaints will be heard

The adalat will take up only complaints related to postal services under the National Capital Territory of Delhi/Delhi state. Complaints about postal services in other states will not be heard here.

There is one more condition. The Circle Level Postal Adalat will consider only those complaints that were raised at divisional-level postal adalats and whose resolution is still pending.

## How and when to send your complaint

Your complaint must reach the Assistant Director (CS), Office of the Chief Postmaster General, Delhi Circle, New Delhi-110001 by 24 November 2026 (Tuesday). Complaints received after the fixed date will not be considered.

The complaint should be clear, factual and brief. Complaints of a general nature and those related to policy matters will not be considered.

## What happens at the adalat

At the postal adalat, each complainant's problems and complaints will be considered individually and efforts will be made to resolve them.

## Who should pay attention

This notice is useful for people living in Delhi whose postal service complaint is stuck at the divisional level. They should send or deliver their complaint to the address given above before 24 November 2026. Those who have no such complaint have nothing to do here.
