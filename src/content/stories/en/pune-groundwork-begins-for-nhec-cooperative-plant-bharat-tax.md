## What happened

Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah performed the bhoomi pujan for the revival of the NHEC (National Heavy Engineering Cooperative) multi-state cooperative society at Talegaon Dabhade in Pune district, Maharashtra, on 15 November 2026. At the same event, he inaugurated the western regional office of the Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies (CRCS) digitally and launched Bharat Taxi services in Pune.

## What NHEC is and why it shut

NHEC earlier operated from this site and supplied sugar mills and centrifugal machinery around the world. According to the minister, its machines were sent to 11 countries including Zambia, Ethiopia and Vietnam. Over time the unit closed and its roughly 110 acres faced a growing risk of illegal occupation.

## How the revival was done

The Cooperation Ministry began work to revive this cooperative industrial asset. First, the National Dairy Development Board (NDDB) took over running the society as administrator, and all audits pending for the past two years were completed. Institutions such as Amul, NDDB, NCDC, IFFCO, NABARD and IPL contributed to raising the needed investment. The board of directors was reconstituted and its first meeting on 9 October 2026 resolved to rebuild the society.

## What the plant will make

The plant will make power generation units for sugar mills, compressed biogas (CBG) plants and industrial plants linked to the dairy sector. Containers will also be made for export. The minister said the earnings from this activity will go to cooperative institutions. He expressed hope that production would begin before December 2027.

## Bharat Taxi and the CRCS office

Bharat Taxi is a service based on a cooperative model, launched from Pune. According to the minister, the full earning goes directly into drivers' bank accounts without any commission. The CRCS western regional office will handle regulation of multi-state cooperative societies in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa and Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.

## What it means for ordinary people

The announcement gives no application, form or deadline for ordinary citizens. The step mainly concerns cooperative institutions, farmers, milk producers and taxi drivers.
