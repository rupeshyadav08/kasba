## What has changed

For several years, a spot near Patel Chest at Delhi University was known only as a garbage site. An old dumping house there was used to collect waste, and people walked past without paying attention.

Now the same spot has been developed as an RRR (Reduce, Reuse and Recycle) centre under Swachh Bharat Mission-Urban 2.0. A selfie point has also been built there.

## Who did the work

According to the ministry, the change was carried out under the leadership of DMSWSL with support from the Civil Lines zone of Delhi's municipal corporation (MCD). The site was not just cleaned up but also given an attractive look.

## Built from waste material

About 45 discarded tyres and more than 3,000 used bricks were creatively reused to build the place. They were turned into colourful artworks and attractive structures.

## What the RRR centre accepts

Next to it is the RRR centre, where citizens can deposit clothes, books, toys and e-waste they no longer use. Instead of being sent to landfill sites, these items are made available for reuse, refurbishing and responsible recycling. This extends their useful life and benefits the community.

## Who benefits

Students passing through the Delhi University campus now stop at the spot instead of avoiding it. Nearby residents can also bring their unused items and deposit them here. The ministry described the change as an example of a circular economy, where waste is not the end of an object but the start of something new.

## What you can do

If you have old clothes, books, toys or e-waste at home, you can deposit them at the RRR centre near Patel Chest instead of throwing them away. For more information about the site, you can contact the Civil Lines zone of Delhi's municipal corporation.
