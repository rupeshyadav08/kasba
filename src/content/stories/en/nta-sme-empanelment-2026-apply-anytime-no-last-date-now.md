## What has changed

The NTA issued a corrigendum on 7 November 2026, following its Expression of Interest (EoI) dated 10 August 2026 and a corrigendum dated 8 September 2026. Under the new corrigendum, applications for Subject Matter Expert (SME) empanelment will be accepted on a continuous basis. There is no closing date; applications can be submitted online at any time until further notice.

Two new requirements have also been added to the EoI. Applicants must now submit ten original, unpublished multiple-choice questions with answer keys, and a Declaration of Originality and Ownership of Sample Questions (Annexure-I, Section M). These new requirements do not apply to applications submitted before the date of this corrigendum.

## Who benefits

This opportunity is for teachers, researchers, scientists and professionals who want to contribute to confidential academic work for NTA exams, such as question setting, vetting, translation, moderation and other tasks. NTA conducts exams such as JEE (Main), NEET (UG), UGC-NET, CUET (UG and PG), CMAT and GPAT. Empanelment is for three years.

Applicants may apply for up to three subject domains in order of preference. Eligibility requires a Master's degree in the relevant subject with at least 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS/PwBD) and 8 years of experience.

## Who is not eligible

NTA has listed certain non-negotiable conditions. People associated with any coaching organisation, test-preparation company or tutoring platform are not eligible. Those who have authored commercial preparation material for NTA or similar exams in the last 5 years are also ineligible. Additionally, applicants must not have any active criminal case, departmental enquiry or pending disciplinary action.

## What to do next

Interested candidates can apply through the NTA online portal and upload the required documents. NTA states that submission of an application does not guarantee empanelment or allocation of assignments; applications will be evaluated periodically as per NTA's requirements. Applicants are advised to check the official NTA website regularly for updates.
