## What has happened

The Principal Controller of Communication Accounts of the Department of Telecommunications has released the schedule of life certificate camps for telecom pensioners for November 2026. The camps are being organised for pensioners' convenience at the Delhi Communication Accounts Building in Prasad Nagar, Delhi, at the extension counter at Janpath, New Delhi, and at MTNL premises at various places in Delhi-NCR.

## Where and when the camps will be held

The camps will run from 11:00 am to 4:00 pm. The schedule is:

- 03.11.2026 – C-10 Yamuna Vihar Main Exchange Building, Delhi
- 06.11.2026 – Lakshmi Nagar, MTNL Exchange Building, Delhi
- 10.11.2026 – Nehru Place, MTNL Exchange Building, New Delhi
- 13.11.2026 – MTNL Exchange Building, Sector-6, Dwarka, Delhi
- 16.11.2026 – MTNL Exchange Building, Sector-3, Rohini, Delhi
- 18.11.2026 – MTNL Exchange Building, Janak Puri, New Delhi
- 20.11.2026 – CSC BSNL Exchange Building, Sector-12, Gurugram, Haryana
- 23.11.2026 – BSNL Exchange Building, Sector GMT-15, Faridabad, Haryana
- 25.11.2026 – BSNL Telephone Exchange Building, Sector-19, Noida
- 27.11.2026 – MTNL Exchange Building, Narela, Delhi

## What help is available at the camps

At the camps, pensioners will get training on submitting a digital life certificate on jeevanpramaan.gov.in. Training will also be given on using the Jeevan Pramaan face authentication app. KYP forms will be accepted at the camps, and pensioners can submit their complaints and questions there.

## What to bring along

The department has asked pensioners to attend these camps and to bring the documents needed for verification at the time of the life certificate. For the KYP form, bring one photograph and a copy of your Aadhaar card/PAN card/PPO.

## Who does not need to attend

The department has made clear that pensioners who have already submitted their life certificate do not need to attend these camps.
