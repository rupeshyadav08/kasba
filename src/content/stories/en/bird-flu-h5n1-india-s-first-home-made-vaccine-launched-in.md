## What happened

Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan dedicated India's first indigenous chicken vaccine for bird flu (H5N1) to the nation in Bhopal on 20 November 2026. According to the notice, this is the world's first vaccine for this variant of bird flu.

The vaccine was developed at the National Institute of High Security Animal Diseases (NIHSAD) of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) in Bhopal. The notice says the plan was earlier to launch it from Delhi, but it was decided to dedicate it in Bhopal among the scientists who made it.

## Who benefits

The notice links the vaccine to the livelihood of lakhs of poultry farmers in the country. The minister said one dose is priced between Rs 1 and Rs 2 so that even the smallest poultry keeper can bear the cost.

According to the notice, India's first bird flu outbreak was in 2006 at Navapur in Maharashtra, in which 10 lakh chickens died. It says 9 lakh chickens died in 2021 and 2 lakh this year, and that India has seen more than 500 bird flu outbreaks since 2006.

## The cost of animal diseases

The minister said foot and mouth disease caused an average loss of Rs 22,000 crore, lumpy virus Rs 20,000 crore, brucellosis Rs 9,212 crore and the sheep and goat disease PPR Rs 4,683 crore. The total loss was put at Rs 56,000 crore.

The notice also says that under the National Animal Disease Control Programme, started from Mathura, 147 crore 16 lakh free doses have been given so far against foot and mouth disease.

## New lab in Bhopal

The event also announced a new high-tech lab in Bhopal at a cost of Rs 300 crore. The notice does not give a date for it.

## What to do next

The notice does not say who will get the vaccine, when, or from where. Poultry keepers should be careful of anyone making claims about it and check with their nearest veterinary office.
