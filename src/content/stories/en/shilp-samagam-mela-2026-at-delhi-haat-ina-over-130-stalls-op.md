The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment has organised Shilp Samagam Mela 2026 at Delhi Haat, INA in New Delhi. The 15-day fair will stay open for visitors till 15 November 2026. Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment Dr Virendra Kumar inaugurated the fair and visited the stalls.

## Theme and venue

The fair is held on the theme 'Prakash aur Parampara – Jyoti se Jyoti Jalayein'. It brings together India's traditional handicrafts, festive lights and cultural heritage. More than 130 stalls have been set up at the fair.

## What the fair offers

The fair is divided into seven themed zones. At Kala Aangan, craftspeople give live demonstrations of their art. Prakash Chowk shows different shades of lamps and lights. Puja Path displays items related to worship. Sajawat Bazaar has home decor products. Gharana has clothing and fashion products. Swad Chowk offers different dishes to taste, while the stage hosts cultural performances.

## Evening cultural programmes

A key attraction is the series of cultural programmes held in the evening. Artists from different states come together on one stage to present their art forms and cultural traditions. These include music, dance and other artistic performances.

## Which artisans get a chance

The stalls display handicrafts, handloom products and creative artworks made by artisans and beneficiaries from Scheduled Caste, Other Backward Class, sanitation worker and disabled communities. These artisans are supported by the National Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (NSFDC), the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation (NBCFDC), the National Safai Karamcharis Finance and Development Corporation (NSKFDC) and the National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation (NHFDC). They get free stalls as well as facilities such as travel expenses, a daily allowance and transport of their goods.

## Bharat TULIP and Purple Fest

A key attraction of the fair is Bharat TULIP, which aims to connect artisans to wider markets through e-commerce platforms. The notice says this helps them reach more customers. Through Purple Fest, the fair also encourages the creativity and contribution of persons with disabilities.

## How many fairs have been held before

The ministry has been holding Shilp Samagam fairs since 2022. Through the 15 such fairs held so far, more than 1,000 artisans from Scheduled Caste, Other Backward Class and sanitation worker communities have got the chance to display and market their products.
