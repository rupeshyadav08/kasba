## What happened

Uttar Pradesh's Food Safety and Drug Administration (UPFSDA) has uncovered a large network dealing in fake and counterfeit medicines. Its special team, along with the UP STF, raided locations in Himachal Pradesh's Solan district, where two illegal drug manufacturing units have been found so far. After Solan, an unlicensed drug unit was also found at V.D. Pharma in Juddikalan, Baddi.

## Where the probe began

The case began with a raid on 30 October 2026 at a medical store in Kurauna village, Varanasi, which was running without a drug licence. Nurokind-ic tablets sold under the Mankind name were found there. After contacting the original manufacturer and comparing samples, the tablets were confirmed fake. Other illegal medicines worth about five lakh rupees were also seized.

## What the supply chain revealed

On 2 November 2026, the team questioned people linked to Super Drug Agency. This led to a house in Parmanandpur, Varanasi, where a large stock of allopathic medicines without bills or licence was found. Some strips had serious printing and spelling errors, and samples were taken treating them as fake. Questioning revealed that the supply was made by Abhishek Singh alias Ravi of Anpara in Sonbhadra, who used an encrypted app to stay in touch.

## How the trail reached Himachal

After Abhishek Singh's arrest, questioning brought up the names of Hasin and Wasim of Chilkana, Saharanpur. According to the investigation, they got medicines made in the Solan area and supplied them. On 48 November 2026, a joint raid was held at an identified unit in the Rajpura-Khera bypass area of Nalagarh, Solan. The unit was making drugs without a licence, and material for preparing exact packaging of well-known brands was also found.

## What was seized

The factory had a blister packing machine, alu-alu packing machine, capsule filling machine, weighing machines, printed foil and stereo printing material. The Baddi unit had machines for making tablets, capsules and syrup, along with a large quantity of raw material, semi-finished and finished medicines, including material for antibiotics, antacids and cough syrup.

## What happens next

Further legal action at the Solan and Baddi units is being taken by Himachal Pradesh's drug department and local police. Traces of illegal drug manufacturing, storage and trade were also found at identified locations in Saharanpur, and the process of registering an FIR there is under way.
