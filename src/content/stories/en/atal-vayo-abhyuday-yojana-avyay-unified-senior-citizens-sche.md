## What the scheme is

The Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY) is a unified scheme for senior citizens, launched in 2021. It merges and expands the earlier National Action Plan for Senior Citizens (NAPSrC). Its aim is to give older people practical support such as food, shelter, healthcare and recreation, and to promote active ageing.

## Why it is needed

According to Census 2011, India has 10.38 crore senior citizens aged 60 and above, including 5.11 crore men and 5.27 crore women. By 2036, this number is projected to reach 23 crore. At the same time, urbanisation, migration and nuclear families are weakening the traditional family support that older people relied on.

## What happened recently

For the quarter ending 30 September 2026 in financial year 2026-27, Rs 77.98 crore was released as support grants for 755 projects under the scheme's 'Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens' component.

## How the scheme runs

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is the nodal ministry, responsible for policy, funding and monitoring. State and Union Territory governments prepare and implement their own state action plans. Selection of beneficiaries and ground-level monitoring of old-age homes is done by district committees headed by the District Collector/Magistrate.

## What support is provided

The Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSRC) gives grants directly to NGOs, Panchayati Raj institutions and local bodies to run and maintain old-age homes, continuing care centres, mobile medical units, physiotherapy clinics and regional resource and training centres. Currently, 764 senior citizen homes, 13 continuing care centres, 17 mobile medical units and 3 physiotherapy clinics are being supported.

The National Vayoshri Yojana, launched in 2017, is now part of this scheme. Under it, eligible senior citizens facing age-related physical disability or weakness are given assistive devices. So far, more than 10.45 lakh senior citizens have received 55,87,653 assistive devices.

## Where to get help

The Elderline helpline for senior citizens is 14567, launched in October 2021. It offers free information, guidance and emotional support, and works with district administrations and NGOs to provide on-ground help where needed. In May 2026, the 'Jeevan' mobile app and the 'Shatayu' care training dashboard were also launched, helping users find scheme information, emergency support and trained caregivers.
