## What has happened

The Competition Commission of India (CCI) has approved a proposed deal. Under it, a company called MIH Payments Holdings BV will acquire some shareholding in Navi Ltd. The release was issued on 31 October 2026.

The release describes this as a "proposed combination", meaning a share-related deal between two companies. The CCI's approval means the commission has given its clearance to the deal.

## Who is buying and where

According to the release, the acquirer is MIH Payments Holdings BV. It is an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Prosus NV. The release says this company does not carry on any business itself apart from investing in its portfolio companies, and those companies have business activities in India.

The other side is Navi Ltd, described in the release as the target. It is the holding company of the Navi group. According to the release, it offers accessible financial services through a single consumer-facing mobile app and, through its subsidiaries, provides transparent and user-friendly financial products available on all mobile devices.

## Who benefits

The approval relates directly to a share deal between companies. The release does not state anything that gives ordinary consumers, employees or farmers a direct benefit or loss.

For customers who use financial services through the Navi app, the release also states no new condition, rate or change.

## What ordinary readers should do

Ordinary readers do not need to do anything in this matter. It concerns shareholding between companies, and the release gives no application, form or deadline.

## Where to get more information

The details of this deal are given in the release issued by the Competition Commission of India. No further information beyond what the release states is available at this time.
