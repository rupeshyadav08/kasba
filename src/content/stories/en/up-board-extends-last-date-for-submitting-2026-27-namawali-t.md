## What has happened

The Uttar Pradesh Board of Secondary Education (UPMSP) said in a notice dated 23 October 2026 that the date for submitting the namawali of registered students of Class 9 to Class 12 for the academic session 2026-27 has been extended to 26 October 2026. The notice was issued by the Secretary of the Board.

## What the earlier dates were

According to the notice, the namawali had to be generated or downloaded and submitted at the District School Inspector's office along with one copy of the treasury challan by 5 October 2026. This was extended to 22 October 2026. Even after that, 940 schools did not generate the namawali. The Board has now given the concerned schools a final chance to complete the work by 26 October 2026.

## What schools have to do

The concerned schools have to generate or download the namawali from the Board's portal. After that, it has to be submitted at the District School Inspector's office with one copy of the treasury challan. The notice also says that accountability will be fixed on the principals of schools that do not complete the work by the fixed date.

## Why the list is getting stuck

The Board has also issued a letter on the same subject, mentioning problems reported by principals of some districts. These include a shortfall in the prescribed fee being deposited or the fee challan not being uploaded, the same candidate being registered from more than one school, and required records or photographs not being uploaded on the portal. The Board has also given directions to get these problems resolved.

## What this means for ordinary people

This notice is mainly for schools, so ordinary citizens or students do not have to do anything separate under it. Parents of students registered in these classes can ask the school whether the namawali has been submitted. More information is available on the Board's website, upmsp.edu.in.
