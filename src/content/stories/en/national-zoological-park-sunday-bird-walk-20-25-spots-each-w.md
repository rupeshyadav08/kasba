## What happened

The National Zoological Park (NZP), under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, marked its 67th foundation day on 1 November 2026. The same day, the 19th edition of 'Sunday Bird Walk @ NZP' was held from 7:30 am to 10:30 am. The walk was organised with the help of dedicated volunteers and under the expert guidance of ornithologist Dr Paritosh Ahmed.

## What this edition recorded

According to the notice, 18 participants joined the walk, including bird lovers, nature enthusiasts, students, wildlife photographers and citizens interested in biodiversity. Participants recorded 46 bird species and saw more than 500 individual birds in the designated area. These observations added to area-level biodiversity documentation and gave participants a hands-on chance to take part in citizen science.

The expert shared insights on bird ecology, behaviour and conservation. He highlighted the role birds play in keeping ecological balance, including natural pest control, and their value as indicators of ecosystem health.

## What the director said

Addressing participants, the Director (NZP) said a walk in nature strengthens the skill of recognising the area around you. It deepens understanding of ecological relationships and the food web, supports biodiversity documentation and builds a lasting appreciation for wildlife conservation.

## Can you join too

Yes. According to the notice, the Sunday bird walk is limited to 20-25 participants each week so that learning and participation stay meaningful. Advance registration is compulsory, and spots are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Where to register

The registration form opens every week from Monday to Friday. The link is available through the National Zoological Park's official website and the NZP Sathi mobile application. Anyone interested in birds and nature can apply through that link.
