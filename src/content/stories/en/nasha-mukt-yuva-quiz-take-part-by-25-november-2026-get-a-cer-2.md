## What is this quiz

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment launched the 'Nasha Mukt Yuva, Viksit Bharat Quiz' on 26 October 2026. The online quiz is part of the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan. The ministry's aim is to raise awareness about the harms of substance use and to draw citizens, especially young people, into the campaign.

By 13 November 2026, a total of 28,876 citizens had taken part. According to the ministry, the quiz runs from 26 October to 25 November 2026 and is open to all citizens.

## What you have to do

The quiz has 20 multiple-choice questions, to be answered in 10 minutes. A participation certificate is prepared for every participant who successfully submits the quiz. After finishing, participants can also see the correct answers, which helps them understand drug prevention better.

## How to take part

Interested people can reach the quiz through the My Bharat portal. The ministry shared this participation link: https://mybharat.gov.in/quizzes/yuva/detail/Url9dlIwCinQVSf8lwPB9w/

## What is the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan

The Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan was launched on 15 August 2020. It works to reduce the demand for drugs through preventive education, awareness, treatment, rehabilitation and community participation. It began in 272 sensitive districts and has now spread to all districts of the country.

According to the ministry, the campaign has reached more than 35.89 crore people, including 14.36 crore youth and 11.34 crore women. More than 34.08 lakh activities have been held in educational institutions. Through more than 755 de-addiction centres supported by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment across the country, 39.91 lakh people have received treatment and rehabilitation. More than 48.21 lakh drug-free pledges have been taken and over 2 lakh Nasha Mukti Mitras have registered.

## What to do next

The ministry urged all citizens, especially young people, to take part in the quiz before 25 November 2026. If you want to join, complete the quiz on the My Bharat portal before the date passes.
