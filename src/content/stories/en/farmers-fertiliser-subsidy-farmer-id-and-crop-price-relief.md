## What happened

Union Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan spoke directly to farmers and agriculture scientists at the Krishi Vigyan Kendra in Samrala (Ludhiana), Punjab, on 17 November 2026. The event was held under the 'Khet Bachao Abhiyan', which is running from 22 October to 30 November 2026. He spoke about fertiliser, Farmer IDs, stubble and crop prices.

## What he said on fertiliser and Farmer ID

The minister said the government is giving farmers DAP bags worth about Rs 5,000 at Rs 1,350 and urea bags worth Rs 3,000 at Rs 266. To stop this subsidised fertiliser from being diverted to industry or misuse, Farmer IDs are being made quickly. 6 lakh Farmer IDs have been made in Punjab so far. This will help a farmer get fertiliser on time as per the real need of his land and crop.

## What will be done about fake pesticides

The minister said the Centre will soon bring a new and stricter Pesticide Act and a seed law to protect farmers from fake pesticides and bad seeds being sold in the market.

## What happens if crop prices fall

The notice mentions two reliefs. First, if prices of potato, onion or tomato fall in Punjab while big cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai offer good prices, the Centre will bear the full transport cost of taking the produce there through state government agencies. Second, under the Marketing Intervention Scheme, if a crop sells at a very low price, the Centre and the state will intervene in the market with a 50-50 share and the price difference will go straight into the farmer's bank account.

## What he said on stubble and farming methods

The minister said stubble is food for the field and mixing it into the soil raises organic carbon. He promised to make stubble-management machines like the seeder cheaper. He asked farmers to try natural farming on half their land rather than all of it. He also urged them to add fruit, flowers, vegetables, medicinal farming, animal husbandry, goat rearing and fish farming to grain farming, instead of growing only wheat and paddy.

## What this means for farmers

This news is mainly for people who farm. The notice does not say where to apply for a Farmer ID or to claim any scheme benefit. Farmers can get information from their district agriculture office or the nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra.
