## What has been released

The Board of Secondary Education, Uttar Pradesh (UP Board) has made public the proposed list of exam centres for the High School and Intermediate examinations of 2027. According to the notice, the list covers centres selected through an online process and includes the allotment of students to schools. It has been uploaded on the Board's website upmsp.edu.in.

## How the centres were selected

The notice says the centres were selected on the basis of basic school information certified or updated by the tehsil-level committee. This was done in line with government orders, including the order dated 4 July 2026 and the amended orders issued after it. The list has now been sent for examination and approval to the district-level centre determination committee, which is headed by the District Magistrate.

## How and when to object

If a school, student, parent, principal or manager has an objection to a selected centre, they can file an online application till 28 October 2026. The application must be made on the format given in the notice, with valid reasons and evidence, on upmsp.edu.in. The notice states clearly that no application will be accepted after this date.

## What happens next

The District Inspector of Schools will examine the objections received and settle them in time. After that, the list or report approved or recommended by the district-level centre determination committee will be uploaded online on the Board's website by 6 November 2026.

## Who should pay attention

This information is mainly for schools and their students and parents who will appear in the UP Board examinations in 2027. Parents can check their school's centre on the website and, if they have any doubt, file an objection in time.
