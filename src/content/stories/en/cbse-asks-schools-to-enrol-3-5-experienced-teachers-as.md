## What is MITRA

The National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), under the Ministry of Education, has started the Mentoring Initiative for Teaching, Research and Assessment (MITRA). It is envisaged under Para 15.11 of the National Education Policy 2020. The aim is to build a national pool of experienced professionals who mentor school teachers across the country for their professional growth and capacity building.

Under the initiative, mentors (experienced professionals) and mentees (teachers seeking help) engage with each other. It covers several mentoring domains and sub-domains, listed in the Annexure to the circular, spanning educational, pedagogical, professional and personal development.

## What CBSE has asked schools to do

In its circular dated 16 November 2026, CBSE asked principals of all affiliated schools to spread the information and facilitate the enrolment of at least 3-5 eligible and experienced teachers per school as mentors on the NMM Portal. The board said this would strengthen the national pool of mentors.

## Who can become a mentor

According to the circular, a mentor needs a minimum of 8 years of professional experience. Applicants should also have demonstrated expertise in one or more identified mentoring domains, effective communication, digital skills and a willingness to contribute to the mission's objective.

## How to register

The mentor enrolment link is nmm.ncte.gov.in. The circular also gives a link to a step-by-step onboarding video. The NCTE will hold an online orientation every Tuesday from 3:30 PM to 4:30 PM to explain the portal, the registration and onboarding process and how to conduct sessions, and to answer queries.

## Who needs to act

This circular is for school principals and teachers. Students and parents have nothing to fill or apply for. Teachers who wish to become mentors can talk to their school principal.
