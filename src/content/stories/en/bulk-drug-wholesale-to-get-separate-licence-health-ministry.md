## What has changed

The Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare has proposed amendments to the Drugs Rules, 1945. The proposal comes through draft gazette notification G.S.R. 987(E) dated 2 November 2026.

The aim is to introduce a separate application form (Form 19-BC) and a separate licence form (Form 20-BC) for the wholesale sale of bulk drugs, meaning active or inactive bulk substances.

## What the current system is

At present, one common licence covers wholesale of both formulations and bulk drugs. The proposal seeks a dedicated licensing framework for bulk drugs, so that their wholesale sale gets more focused regulatory oversight and the pharmaceutical supply chain becomes more accountable.

According to the Ministry, the proposal was agreed in the 67th meeting of the Drug Consultative Committee (DCC) held on 17 November 2025 and the 93rd meeting of the Drug Technical Advisory Board (DTAB) held on 16 February 2026.

## Who is covered

Under the proposed framework, the licence will list the categories of bulk drugs a wholesaler handles, including medicines covered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985.

The Ministry says the sale of bulk drugs will be under the personal supervision of a competent person, strengthening accountability in their distribution and handling. Once in force, the proposal is meant to give better control over bulk drug sales and stronger enforcement on antimicrobial resistance and the movement of NDPS drugs.

## What wholesalers must do

Under the proposal, wholesalers of bulk drugs will need to obtain a separate licence for sale. Existing licensees will be given enough time to comply with the new provisions.

These requirements will apply when their current retention period ends, or 12 months after the amendment's final publication, whichever is later.

## How to send objections

The Ministry has invited objections and suggestions from stakeholders and the public. These can be sent to the Under Secretary (Drugs), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, U-6, Work Hall, C Wing, First Floor, Kartavya Bhawan-1, New Delhi-110001, or by email to drugsdiv-mohfw[at]gov[dot]in.

The time limit is 30 days from the date copies of the draft rules in the gazette are made available to the public. The draft rules can be read on the e-Gazette.
