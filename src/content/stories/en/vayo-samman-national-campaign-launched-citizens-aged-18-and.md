The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is launching the 'Vayo Samman National Campaign' in the national capital. It is a national intergenerational engagement campaign, meaning it works on bringing senior citizens and younger generations closer together. Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment Dr Virendra Kumar will formally launch it. The campaign is being run under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY), the ministry's scheme for the welfare, care, protection and empowerment of senior citizens.

## Why the campaign was started

According to the ministry, India's elderly population is growing steadily. The number of people aged 60 and above was about 10 crore in 2011 and is estimated to reach 23 crore by 2036, with their share in the total population rising from 8.4 per cent to 14.9 per cent. At the same time, urbanisation, migration and changing family structures have reduced chances for generations to meet regularly in many households. The campaign aims to address these changes and keep senior citizens active, respected and connected to society.

## How the campaign will work

The campaign is based on a '5C' model — Connect, Converse, Co-create, Contribute and Continue. This means creating opportunities for seniors and young people to meet, encouraging conversation and sharing of experiences, working together on activities, and keeping the process going regularly rather than as one-off events. It will be implemented across 12 thematic areas, including activities such as '30 Minutes Together', 'Teach Me – I Will Teach You', 'My Story, My India', 'Digital Grandparents / Digital Saathi', 'Health and Wellbeing Across Generations' and 'Silver Skills – Seniors as Mentors'.

## What is a Vayo Saathi volunteer

Along with the launch, a volunteer initiative called 'Vayo Saathi' will begin. Citizens aged 18 years and above can join it, including students, young professionals, community volunteers, civil society organisations and institutional partners. Volunteers will act as friends, facilitators and contact points for senior citizens, helping them reach information, welfare services, community activities and digital support. Young people will also be encouraged to help seniors use digital services and spot possible cyber fraud.

## How to register

Interested citizens can register as Vayo Saathi volunteers through the Senior Citizens Welfare (SCW) inclusion portal or the Jeevan app. Registered volunteers will receive a Vayo Saathi certificate in recognition of their participation. The ministry has urged citizens, educational institutions, universities, youth organisations, NGOs, resident welfare associations, community institutions and CSR partners to take an active part in the campaign.

## What else is planned

At the launch, a booklet on the campaign will be released, setting out its objectives, implementation framework, thematic activities and the roles of states, districts and community institutions. The department will also sign two memoranda of understanding (MoUs) with Shri Ramchandra Mission and the Adhyatma Vigyan Satsang Kendra, Jodhpur, aimed at promoting the overall wellbeing of senior citizens through programmes related to meditation, yoga and health and wellness.
