## What has changed

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has extended the last date for submitting the online application form for the UGC-NET December 2026 Examination. Candidates can now apply until 11:50 PM on 31 October 2026. The last date for a successful transaction of the examination fee is also the same.

The notice states that the extended date comes without any late fee. Candidates who have not yet completed their application can therefore still apply in the normal way.

## What the exam is for

UGC-NET is conducted to determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to Ph.D. programmes in Indian universities and colleges.

JRF is a research fellowship given to students doing research. Assistant Professor is a teaching post in a university or college.

## Who benefits

Candidates who want to appear for UGC-NET December 2026 but have not yet submitted their online application or paid the fee. NTA said the decision was taken in the interest of candidates.

## What to do next

NTA has advised candidates to complete their application well before the revised deadline so that they do not face last-minute technical difficulties.

According to the notice, all other terms and conditions mentioned in the Information Bulletin and earlier public notices remain unchanged. Only the date has changed, not the other application rules.

## Where to get information

Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official NTA websites https://nta.ac.in and https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in for the latest updates. The notice also lists the e-mail ugcnet@nta.ac.in for contact.
