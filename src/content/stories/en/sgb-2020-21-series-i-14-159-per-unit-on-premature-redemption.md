## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issued a press release on October 27, 2026. It says the next date for premature redemption of Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) 2020-21 Series I is October 28, 2026. On that date, holders will get ₹14,159 per unit.

## What premature redemption means

An SGB is a gold-linked bond issued by the government. According to the RBI, a bond can be redeemed early after five years from its issue date, on a date when interest is payable. This series was issued on April 28, 2020, and the next redemption date is October 28, 2026.

## How the price is fixed

The RBI said the redemption price is based on the simple average of the closing price of 999 purity gold published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA) for the previous three business days. This time the average is taken from October 23, 26 and 27, 2026. On that basis, the rate is ₹14,159 per unit.

## Who is affected

The rate is for investors who hold SGB 2020-21 Series I bonds and want to redeem them early on October 28, 2026. People who do not hold bonds of this series are not directly affected.

## What to do next

If you hold bonds of this series and want to redeem, check the process with your bank or the place you bought the bonds from. You can confirm the price and date from this RBI press release.
