## What has happened

The Ministry of Civil Aviation has started a trial of 'Digi Yatra' for international passengers at seven airports in India. The trial will run from November to December 2026. The ministry described it as another step towards adopting digital technology in the country's aviation sector.

## Which airports

The trial is being held at Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi, Mumbai, Chennai and Amritsar airports. Eligible passengers can use the facility at terminal entry points, security hold areas (SHA) and boarding gates — but only where the necessary infrastructure is available.

## Who benefits

The facility is for eligible Indian citizens who hold an e-passport, and only when they are travelling on select international flights of Air India and IndiGo. The notice does not mention any change for other passengers during this trial.

## What you do on the app

To use the facility, a passenger must give e-passport-based verifiable information (VC) through the Digi Yatra mobile app. This involves scanning the e-passport's machine readable zone (MRZ), reading the chip in the passport using a suitable smartphone's Near Field Communication (NFC) feature, taking a selfie and verifying the details. This information is stored in the passenger's Digi Yatra wallet on their own device and can be used at enabled airport touchpoints during the trial.

## What happens to the existing process

The ministry made clear that the facility is not meant to replace existing travel processes but to assist them. Passengers must keep their travel documents with them and complete the airline's required document checks, immigration processes and all other travel formalities.

## What next

A plan for wider expansion in 2027 will be prepared based on feedback received during the trial. According to the ministry, Digi Yatra has more than 26.5 million users and has facilitated over 110 million journeys at 39 domestic airports while serving domestic airlines. There is a plan to bring 25 more domestic airports under its scope by 2027.
