## What has happened

Indian Railways has approved a new weekly train service between Janakpur Dham in Nepal and Ayodhya Cantt in Uttar Pradesh. The announcement was made on 14 November 2026. According to the Ministry of Railways, the step is aimed at strengthening rail connectivity, religious tourism and India-Nepal ties.

Janakpur Dham is believed to be the birthplace of Mata Sita and Ayodhya the birthplace of Lord Shri Ram. Both places are closely linked to the Ramayana tradition. The new train will create a direct rail link between these two religious and cultural centres.

## Where the train will stop

The service will cover a distance of about 604 km and pass through several towns and cities in Bihar and Uttar Pradesh. Besides the terminal stations, there will be commercial stoppages at Jaynagar, Madhubani, Shisho, Sitamarhi, Raxaul, Narkatiaganj, Gorakhpur, Basti, Mankapur and Ayodhya Dham.

The train will run once a week. It will leave Janakpur Dham on Saturday and Ayodhya Cantt on Monday. The release does not give a start date, fare or booking details.

## Who will benefit

According to the railway, the service will make cross-border travel easier for pilgrims, tourists and other passengers. Religious travel between Janakpur and Ayodhya will become more convenient, and Ramayana-related tourism is expected to get a boost. People travelling for study, business or family reasons will also get another option.

Better rail access is expected to create opportunities for tourism operators, hospitality businesses, local traders and traditional artisans. For communities in the border areas of Bihar and Nepal, the service will offer an additional means of passenger transport.

## What happens next

The release only announces approval of the service. It does not say when the train will start, how tickets will be booked or what the fare will be. Travellers planning a trip on this route should keep an eye on official railway information such as station notices, IRCTC or official railway pages.
