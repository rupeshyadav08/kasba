## What has happened

RBI issued a notice on 26 October 2026. It assigns lead banks for Delhi's new districts. The notice is addressed to chairmen and managing directors of the banks concerned.

## What changed in the districts

The Delhi government reorganised districts through a gazette notification dated 25 December 2025. This formed three new districts — Old Delhi, Central North and Outer North. Shahdara district has ceased to exist.

## What a lead bank is

A lead bank coordinates banking work and related schemes in a district. RBI has divided this responsibility for the new districts as follows:

- Old Delhi — Canara Bank, district working code 03E
- Central North — State Bank of India, district working code 03F
- Outer North — Punjab National Bank, district working code 03G

## Who benefits

The arrangement is for banks and district-level banking work in Delhi. It sets how banking work will be coordinated in the new districts.

## Effect on ordinary people

Ordinary depositors or customers do not have to fill any form for this. The notice gives no date by which customers must do anything. If your account is with a bank in these districts, it is not directly affected.

## Where to get more information

The full notice can be seen on the RBI website. Information can also be taken from your bank branch.
