## What has happened

The Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) signed a memorandum of understanding with the Tribal Cooperative Marketing Development Federation of India (TRIFED) on 5 November 2026. TRIFED works under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.

The aim is to strengthen market access for eligible tribal artisans registered under the PM Vishwakarma scheme. The ministry's PM Vishwakarma framework will be linked with TRIFED's existing Tribes India marketplace and retail network.

## What is PM Vishwakarma

PM Vishwakarma was launched on 17 September 2023. It aims to improve the quality and reach of products and services made by traditional artisans and craftspeople.

The scheme supports artisans whose skills pass from one generation to the next through the traditional guru-shishya model of training within families and informal groups.

## Which artisans are covered

The agreement lists trades such as carpenter (sutar), weapon makers (astrakar), sculptors, goldsmiths, potters, cobblers, basket, mat and broom makers, coir weavers, doll and toy makers and garland makers.

The cooperation covers only tribal artisans who are registered and eligible under PM Vishwakarma.

## What changes

TRIFED will facilitate the onboarding and capacity building of eligible tribal artisans referred by the MSME Ministry. Subject to applicable onboarding conditions and policies, they will get market access through the Tribes India retail network and its online marketplace.

Products made by participating artisans will be displayed under the proposed 'Tribes India–PM Vishwakarma Collection' on the Tribes India retail network and tribesindia.com. This is subject to seller consent and compliance with applicable TRIFED policies.

TRIFED will help with aggregation, quality assurance and packaging support for market-ready products. It will also work with the MSME Ministry to give initial product imaging and cataloguing support to identified sellers.

## What the MSME Ministry will do

The ministry will identify potential sellers and encourage them to list products in various categories on tribesindia.com. It will organise awareness workshops and help coordinate between artisans and TRIFED.

TRIFED will promote products made by identified tribal Vishwakarmas through its e-marketplace. It will also provide dedicated display and sale space at its outlets, subject to the MoU's provisions and applicable conditions.

## What this means for ordinary readers

The agreement does not start anything new for ordinary consumers or citizens. The benefit is meant for tribal artisans registered under PM Vishwakarma.

For such artisans, it is useful to keep their registration and eligibility in order. The notice does not mention any application date or separate form. More information can come through the MSME Ministry's awareness workshops and the Tribes India network.
