## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended the directions in force on The Irinjalakuda Town Co-operative Bank Ltd., Kerala. In its notice dated October 29, 2026, RBI said the directions will now continue for three more months after October 30, 2026, that is, up to close of business on January 30, 2027.

## What happened earlier

RBI first issued directions to the bank on July 29, 2025, for six months. These were later extended up to January 30, 2026, and then up to October 30, 2026, through a directive dated July 21, 2026. The period has now been extended once again.

## What RBI said

RBI said it was satisfied that extending the directions further was necessary in the public interest. It also stated clearly that the extension should not be taken to mean that it is satisfied with the bank's financial position. The other terms and conditions of the directions remain unchanged.

## Who is affected

This decision concerns only this co-operative bank. It does not directly affect other banks or ordinary people in general. Customers of this bank can check with their branch or RBI's notice to understand what rules apply to their deposits and withdrawals.
