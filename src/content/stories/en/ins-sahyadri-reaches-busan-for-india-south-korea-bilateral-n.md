## What has happened

Indian Navy ship INS Sahyadri reached Busan naval port in South Korea on 26 October 2026. According to the Defence Ministry, the visit is to take part in the India-South Korea Navy (IN-ROKN) bilateral exercise.

## What INS Sahyadri is

As per the release, INS Sahyadri is an indigenously designed and built guided-missile stealth frigate of the Indian Navy. A frigate is a type of warship.

## What will happen at the port

The South Korean Navy (ROKN) welcomed INS Sahyadri. During the harbour phase, personnel of both navies will take part in professional interactions, visits to each other's warships, joint training activities and sports events. This will help exchange operational experiences and best practices and build mutual understanding.

The Commanding Officer of INS Sahyadri will also meet senior officers of the South Korean Navy.

## What will happen at sea

After the harbour phase, coordinated maritime activities will be held at sea. These will involve INS Sahyadri and the South Korean Navy ship ROKS Munmu the Great. The release says these activities will strengthen operational coordination and professional ties between the two navies.

## What the aim is

According to the release, the exercise reflects the shared commitment of both countries to strengthen maritime cooperation and contribute to peace, stability and security in the Indo-Pacific region. It is also part of the Indian Navy's continuing efforts to build long-term partnerships with friendly navies through professional dialogue and operational cooperation.

## What this means for ordinary people

This news has no direct bearing on the everyday life of ordinary citizens. No one has to apply, and no date or rule has changed. It is information about a military exercise between the two navies.
