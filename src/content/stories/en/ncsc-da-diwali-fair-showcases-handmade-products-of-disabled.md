## What happened

The National Career Service Centre for Differently Abled (NCSC-DA), which works under the Directorate General of Employment (DGE) of the Labour and Employment Ministry, organised a Diwali fair. The notice is dated 29 October 2026. The aim was to bring before people the handmade products, craft skills and vocational skills of disabled trainees and alumni.

## Whose products were on display

Products made by disabled trainees and alumni of NCSC-DA centres in Delhi, Thiruvananthapuram, Dehradun, Bhubaneswar, Kolkata and Mumbai were brought together at the fair. These included jute and cloth bags, candles, electronic items, paintings, soaps, crochet products, dress-making and textile goods, knitting, embroidery, woodwork and decorative festive products.

## What the stalls offered

According to the notice, the stalls gave participants a chance to show their creativity, talk directly to visitors and get their feedback. This helped them explore opportunities in sales, self-employment and market links. The products reflected both the traditional crafts and the modern vocational training taught at NCSC-DA centres.

## What the Ministry said

Labour and Employment Secretary Dr Chandra Bhushan Kumar said the fair reflects the Ministry's commitment to empowering disabled people through skill development, the dignity of labour and economic independence. He said that with the right training and opportunities, disabled people can excel as skilled artisans and entrepreneurs and contribute to the national economy.

## What happens next

The Ministry plans to gradually expand the Diwali fair into a larger national platform. According to the notice, future events are expected to display handmade products and crafts from additional NCSC-DA centres in states and union territories. This could give disabled trainees and artisans wider reach, market opportunities and recognition.

## What NCSC-DA does

NCSC-DA is a special wing of the Labour and Employment Ministry for the vocational rehabilitation of disabled people across India. The notice says 24 NCSC-DA centres currently offer career counselling, vocational guidance, informal vocational training, placement assistance and outreach services. Training is given in trades such as dress-making, sewing, knitting and embroidery, woodwork, computer applications, electronics, general mechanics and other employment-oriented skills.
