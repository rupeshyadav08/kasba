## What has happened

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has issued supplementary result notice no. 1670 dated 3 November 2026. It provisionally nominates 2 candidates against the 2 PGT (Sociology) Male posts that had been left pending. These posts are in the Directorate of Education (DoE) under Post Code 814/23.

## What happened earlier

DSSSB had advertised 5 vacancies for this post through Advertisement No. 07/23 dated 22 February 2023. The Tier-I (Technical/Teaching) exam for the post was held on 27 July 2025 and 3 August 2025. A total of 668 candidates appeared, and their normalized marks were uploaded on the DSSSB website on 27 March 2026 vide notice no. 1605. Of these, 53 candidates were shortlisted to upload documents in the e-dossier module and exercise at least one preference in OARS.

Later, Result Notice No. 629 dated 27 May 2026 provisionally nominated some candidates. At that time, one UR post and one horizontal PwBD Cat (c) post for PGT (Sociology) Male were kept pending, awaiting clarification from DoE.

## What has changed now

After clarification conveyed by DoE vide Letter No. 990 dated 3 September 2026, DSSSB has nominated candidates against these pending posts. According to the notice, 1 candidate in the UR category and 1 in the PwBD Cat (c) category have been provisionally nominated. The PwBD Cat (c) candidate is nominated against a future vacancy in the EWS category, in terms of Para 9.1 of DoPT O.M. dated 5.10.2018.

## Who is affected

This notice concerns candidates who took part in this recruitment process. The 2 candidates named in the notice will not get an appointment letter straight away. According to the notice, DoE will first verify their documents, eligibility, caste/EWS certificates and identity. Candidature can be cancelled if any shortcoming is found.

## What happens next

The notice makes clear that mere inclusion of a name in the result notice does not confer any right over the post. The appointment letter will be issued only after the competent authority of DoE is satisfied. The result is also subject to the outcome of court cases, if any, pending about this post.

## Where to check

The full notice is available on the DSSSB website, dsssb.delhi.gov.in.
