## What is this mission

The Pradhan Mantri Janjatiya Vikas Mission (PMJVM) works to raise the income of tribal families. Many of India's 10.4 crore Scheduled Tribe people earn 20 to 40 percent of their yearly income from minor forest produce (MFP). This includes honey, tendu leaves, mahua flowers, tamarind, lac, bamboo and wild herbs. Earlier, tribal people sold this produce to middlemen at very low prices. The mission started in 2021-22.

## What has changed

The government now buys minor forest produce at a pre-fixed minimum support price (MSP). MSP has been notified for 87 minor forest produce items under PMJVM. Tribal people are also organised into Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (VDVK). One centre links 15 self-help groups with about 300 tribal members, and at least 60 percent of members must be from Scheduled Tribe communities.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit goes to tribal families who collect and sell forest produce. Till March 2026, 4,172 Van Dhan Vikas Kendras were approved and 12.48 lakh tribal members were linked to them. Till 31 July 2026, 2,911 centres were running and their sales were about Rs 168 crore. Since 2013-14, 2,67,954 metric tonnes of minor forest produce has been bought, worth Rs 693.98 crore.

## What support is given

Tribal people get skill and technical training at the centres. Self-help groups get space at a member's home or a panchayat building, cutting and sorting tools, dryers and packaging support. Thirty members are trained in sustainable harvesting and value addition. Working capital is arranged through banks and the National Scheduled Tribes Finance and Development Corporation (NSTFDC).

## How they reach the market

Van Dhan Vikas Kendras are set up near local haat markets so that selling is easier. There are more than 5,000 haats in tribal areas, which are being modernised in phases. TRIFED shares daily rates and e-commerce information with the centres. Products also reach markets through fairs, exhibitions and e-commerce platforms.

## What happens next

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is observing Janjatiya Gaurav Pakhwada from 1 to 15 November 2026. Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas will be celebrated on 15 November, the birth anniversary of Bhagwan Birsa Munda. More than 300 projects will be launched on the occasion, including 228 for inauguration and 74 for foundation stone laying. The Jan-Hit portal and a National Forest Rights Act (FRA) digital platform will also be launched.
