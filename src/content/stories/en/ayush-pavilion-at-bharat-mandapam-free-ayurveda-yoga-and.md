## What is happening at the fair

The AYUSH Ministry has set up a pavilion at the 45th India International Trade Fair. The fair is being held at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi from 13 to 27 November 2026, and the AYUSH pavilion is in Hall 2. Traditional medicine systems from across the country are being shown together in one place.

According to the release, the pavilion aims to bring visitors closer to healthy lifestyles through health consultations, interactive activities, traditional foods, medicinal plants and educational displays.

## What consultations are on offer

The Central Ayurveda Research Institute is offering Ayurveda OPD services and a Prakriti Pariksha facility. The Morarji Desai National Institute of Yoga is holding regular yoga sessions and demonstrations on a special stage in the pavilion, and giving personal yoga advice based on a visitor's age, health problems and lifestyle.

At the Unani stall, 151 patients were given free consultation and medicines through the Unani OPD on 18 and 19 November. There is also a digital Mizaj assessment kiosk where visitors can check their temperament and get diet suggestions.

## What else you can see

The All India Institute of Ayurveda is displaying commonly used medicinal herbs and spices, including Ashwagandha, Shatavari, Arjuna, Amla, Haridra and Guduchi. Visitors are also being told about the right dose and the importance of a specialist's advice before use.

The National Institute of Ayurveda, Jaipur is showing products such as ragi cookies, Triphala jam, aloe vera gel and herbal lip balm. There is an Ayurveda-themed snakes and ladders game and a virtual reality experience that explains the body's systems. The National Medicinal Plants Board is distributing Shatavari plants, and QR-coded plants let visitors look up details about them.

## Study and career information

The Siddha stall explains the principles of Siddha medicine, preventive care and treatment methods. It also gives information on BSMS, MD (Siddha) and PhD courses, so students can learn about study and career options in the field. At the Sowa-Rigpa stall, experts offer guidance on the ancient Himalayan medical tradition.

## What visitors should do

Those planning to visit the fair can go to the AYUSH pavilion in Hall 2 until 27 November 2026 and use the free consultations and yoga sessions. For readers far from Delhi, the event is not something they can directly use. Before starting any herb or medicine on your own, consult a doctor or a qualified specialist.
