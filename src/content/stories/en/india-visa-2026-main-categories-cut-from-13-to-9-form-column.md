## What has changed

The Home Ministry on 27 August 2026 simplified India's visa system. Main visa categories were cut from 13 to 9 and sub-categories from 49 to 23. According to the notice, this followed an earlier simplification in October 2025, when main categories were cut from 22 to 13 and sub-categories from 63 to 49.

The number of columns to be filled in visa application forms for all 23 categories has also been reduced by 65%-70%. The Ministry says this will cut the time and effort needed to complete an application.

## Who benefits

The change is for foreign nationals coming to India — tourists, patients, students, researchers, investors and business professionals. The notice says the aim is to boost arrivals of foreign tourists, medical value travel and foreign students. It adds that the changes reduce overlap between visa categories and make it easier to pick the right visa.

## What changed in each category

The earlier tourist visa is now called Visitor Visa, split into V-1 and V-2. V-1 covers short activities such as business meetings, medical consultation, wellness retreats, short medical treatment and short courses in Indian languages, music and arts and crafts; it is issued for 30 days with multiple entry. V-2 is for longer trips, valid for 1, 5 or 10 years, and also covers mountaineering.

Medical visas now have M-1 (treatment other than AYUSH systems) and M-3 (treatment under AYUSH systems), which can be issued by default for one year with multiple entry. Attendants get M-2 and M-4 visas. Student visas have S-1 for school, graduate and post-graduate study, while S-2 covers PhD and research scholars, visiting faculty, internships and exchange students. Students enrolled through the Study in India portal can get an e-S1 visa for up to five years or the course duration, whichever is shorter, with multiple entry.

Business visas are now B-1 (long-term, licence-based work) and B-2 (short-term, contract-based work). Foreign experts coming to Indian companies for installation, quality checks, training and technical support can get a Production Investment Business visa under B-2, earlier called e-B4.

## When it starts

The notice says the IT system upgrade to implement the changes is in its final stage and visas under the new system will start being issued by the end of the year. e-Visa is currently available to citizens of 172 countries.
