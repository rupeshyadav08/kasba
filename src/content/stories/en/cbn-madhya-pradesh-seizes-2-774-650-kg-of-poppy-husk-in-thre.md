## What happened

Over the past few days, the Madhya Pradesh unit of the Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN) carried out three operations. Together they led to the seizure of 2,774.650 kg of poppy husk (doda chura) and five vehicles, and the arrest of four people. The details are from a Finance Ministry release dated 31 October 2026.

## First operation: container on the expressway

On 27 October 2026, acting on specific intelligence, a team stopped a container truck registered in Rajasthan near Sitamau on the Mumbai-Delhi Expressway. There was reason to believe it was carrying a large quantity of poppy husk towards Punjab. A search found 2,308 kg packed in 115 white bags, hidden under 598 white bags of gram husk being used as cover cargo. The goods and the container were seized and one person was arrested.

## Second operation: Ishakpur in Mandsaur

On the intervening night of 28 and 29 October 2026, a joint team acted at Ishakpur village in Mandsaur tehsil of Mandsaur district. They stopped a Mahindra pickup truck with a suspected fake registration number and a Mahindra Bolero. One person was caught, while another suspect fled leaving a Bolero behind after a chase by a second CBN team. A search of the two vehicles found 416.560 kg of poppy husk in 22 plastic bags — 17 in the pickup and five in the Bolero. Both vehicles and the goods were seized and one person was arrested.

## Third operation: Fatehgarh-Piplia Mandi route

On 28 October 2026, a team stopped a Hyundai Grand i10 car registered in Madhya Pradesh and a TVS motorcycle on the Fatehgarh-Nayakheda-Piplia Mandi route. Intelligence indicated the motorcycle was guiding the car, which was suspected to be carrying about 50-60 kg of poppy husk from Fatehgarh Fanta towards Piplia Mandi. A search of the car found 50.090 kg packed in two bags. The goods and both vehicles were seized and two people were arrested.

## What next

Further investigation is ongoing in all three cases. According to the release, CBN is working to enforce the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985 and to contribute to the national mission of a drug-free India.
