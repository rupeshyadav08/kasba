## What happened

The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying announced the winners of the National Gopal Ratna Award 2026 on 20 November 2026. The award recognises people working to promote indigenous breeds of cows and buffaloes. The department said the winners in each category were announced after the set process was completed.

## What this award is

The department launched the Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM) in December 2014 to conserve indigenous animal breeds and develop them scientifically. The National Gopal Ratna Award (NGRA) was started to recognise and encourage those working in this field. It has three categories: Best Dairy Farmer, Best Dairy Cooperative Society/Milk Producer Company/Dairy Farmer Producer Organisation, and Best Artificial Insemination Technician (AIT). A separate special award is given for the North East region and Himalayan states.

## How much prize money

Winners in the first two categories get a certificate of appreciation, a memento and a cash prize. The first prize is Rs 5,00,000, the second Rs 3,00,000 and the third Rs 2,00,000. The special award for the North East/Himalayan states is Rs 2,00,000. Winners in the AIT category get a certificate of appreciation and a memento.

## How many applications came

Applications were invited on the Home Ministry's awards.gov.in portal from 10 August 2026 to 30 September 2026. A total of 1,583 applications were received.

## Who won

In the Best Dairy Farmer category (non-North East), first place went to Shri Muralidharan Nair of Kottayam, Kerala. Second was Shri Odedra Rajshibhai of Porbandar, Gujarat, and third was Shri Madan Mohan Yadav of Gurugram, Haryana. The special award for the North East/Himalayan states went to Shri Sagar Singh Rawat of Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand.

In the Best Dairy Cooperative Society/MPC/FPO category, first place went to TND 93 Thachur MPCS of Kallakurichi, Tamil Nadu; second to Dinka Milk Producers Cooperative Society Ltd of Mandya, Karnataka; and third to Eruthenpathy Ksheerolpadaka Sahakarana Sangam of Palakkad, Kerala. The special award went to The Chak Devia Dairy Cooperative Ltd of Kathua, Jammu and Kashmir.

In the Best Artificial Insemination Technician category, first place went to Shri Shubhankar Nandi of Hooghly, West Bengal; second to Shri Rajesh Kumar Mahto of Giridih, Jharkhand; and third to Shri Sheikh Shafi of Nellore, Andhra Pradesh. The special award went to Shri Sudhesh Kumar of Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand.

## When the awards will be given

The awards will be given on 26 November 2026 at the National Milk Day 2026 function at Bapu Sabhagar, Muradpur, Patna, Bihar. Union Minister of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying Shri Rajiv Ranjan Singh alias Lalan Singh will present them. Minister of State Prof. S.P. Singh Baghel will also be present.
