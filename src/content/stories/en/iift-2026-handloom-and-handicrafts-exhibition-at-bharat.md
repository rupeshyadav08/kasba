## What happened

Union Textiles Minister Giriraj Singh inaugurated a "Special Handloom and Handicrafts Exhibition-cum-Sale" at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi on 16 November 2026. The exhibition is being held during the India International Trade Fair (IIFT) 2026. Senior officials of the Textiles Ministry and the National Handloom Development Corporation (NHDC) were present at the opening.

## What this year's theme is

This year's pavilion theme is "Vikas aur Virasat" — development and heritage. The pavilion presents India's craft traditions as a journey through four regions. The North section shows traditional motifs such as Ganesh and Ram Darbar along with Banarasi weaving. The South section shows the wood, silk and metal crafts of Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, and the art of Nataraja. The East section shows the weaving traditions of Odisha and Bengal. The West section shows mirror work, Lippan art and carved wood crafts.

## How many artisans and stalls

The exhibition has 200 stalls in all — 100 of handloom artisans and 100 of handicraft artisans. Participants from more than 21 states and union territories are displaying their crafts. In all, 17 award-winning artisans are presenting their work at the pavilion, including 7 national award winners, 3 state award winners, 2 Sant Kabir award winners and more than 5 artisans with multiple awards.

## Who benefits

The pavilion is managed by NHDC so that handloom weavers and handicraft artisans get direct access to the market. The minister said the handloom and handicrafts sector is linked to more than one crore artisans and supports the livelihoods of lakhs of people directly and indirectly. He said the government is working to strengthen the whole chain, from raw material support to technology, design and market links.

## What is there for the public

The exhibition is open to the public from 13 to 27 November 2026. It can be visited daily from 10:00 am to 7:30 pm at Hall No. 5, ground floor, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. It is an exhibition-cum-sale, so items can also be bought there.
