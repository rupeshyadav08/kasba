## What has happened

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) released on 13 November 2026 its details of action taken against unwanted commercial communication (UCC), or spam, during the second quarter of financial year 2026-27, from 1 July to 30 September 2026. The figures are compiled from information given by telecom service providers.

## How many complaints came in

A total of 1.529 million UCC complaints were registered in the quarter, of which 0.723 million were actionable. Against 714.01 billion calls on telecom networks, that works out to about 2.14 complaints per million calls, according to the report. The largest share came through the TRAI DND app: 1.421 million complaints, or 92.9 per cent of the total.

## What action was taken on senders

On a first violation, 1,10,325 telecom resources were barred for 15 days. Connections of repeat violators, 13,777 in number, were cut for one year. A total of 374 senders were blacklisted for one year and 3,725 SMS headers were blocked. Overall, action was taken against more than 124 thousand telecom resources, the report says.

## What you can do

If you do not want promotional calls or SMS, you can register or change your DND preference through the TRAI DND app, your telecom operator's app or website, or by calling or sending an SMS to 1909. As of 30 September 2026, 224.49 million preferences were registered in the DND registry, 16.64 per cent of the total subscriber base of 1.349 billion. The report says more than 1.12 billion customers have registered no preference so far.

## Knowing numbers by their series

Promotional calls come from the 140 series, which are checked against customer preferences before delivery. The 1600 series is for service and transactional calls from banking, financial services, insurance and government communication. The 1601 series was launched on 10 August 2026 for service calls from utility, logistics and delivery sectors.

## What else was done

Service providers have deployed AI-based systems, which flagged 18.28 billion incoming calls and 1.62 billion incoming SMS as suspected spam during the quarter. eKYC was done for 34.03 thousand suspected UCC senders, and 9.97 thousand connections were cut after KYC verification failed. The full report is on TRAI's website; feedback can be sent to advqos@trai.gov.in.
