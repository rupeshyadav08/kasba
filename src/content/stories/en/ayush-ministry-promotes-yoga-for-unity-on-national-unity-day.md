## What has happened

The Ayush Ministry marked National Unity Day 2026 with the message 'Yoga for Unity'. The day was observed on the 151st birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. The Ministry linked this message with the Yoga 365 initiative, which encourages people to make yoga part of their daily life.

## What Yoga 365 is

Yoga 365 is an initiative that asks people to practise yoga throughout the year. According to the release, the idea is to show that yoga is not limited to a single day or a special occasion. It can be adopted as a lasting daily practice that supports a person's health and also strengthens ties within the community.

## What the Ministry is doing

The Ministry is encouraging its yoga and naturopathy institutes across the country to hold activities that carry the 'Yoga for Unity' message to communities. These include regular yoga practice, group yoga sessions and programmes that spread awareness of yoga's benefits. The Ministry says this will help bring in wider public participation.

## What the Minister and Secretary said

Union Minister of State for Ayush (Independent Charge) and Minister of State for Health and Family Welfare Shri Prataprao Jadhav said Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel united the country through his vision and determination. He said yoga brings people together across differences of age, region and background.

Ayush Ministry Secretary Vaidya Rajesh Kotecha said yoga is a simple and accessible way to improve physical and mental well-being. He said the Yoga 365 initiative encourages people to practise yoga all year, so that preventive health care becomes part of everyday life.

## What this means for ordinary people

This is not a scheme with an application, a payment or a last date. The release mentions no registration, form or benefit amount. It is an awareness campaign asking people to adopt yoga daily. If you would like to join a group yoga session, you can ask your nearest yoga or naturopathy institute whether any such activity is being held.
