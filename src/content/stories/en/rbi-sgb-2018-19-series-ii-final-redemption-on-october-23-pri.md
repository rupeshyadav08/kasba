The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issued a press release on October 22, 2026, about the final redemption of Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) of 2018-19 Series-II. It said the final redemption date for this tranche is October 23, 2026. Under the government notification on the SGB Scheme, the bond is repayable on the expiry of eight years from the date of issue.

According to RBI, this tranche was issued on October 23, 2018, so the eight-year period ends on October 23, 2026, which is also the final redemption date.

On the price, RBI said the redemption price of SGB is based on the simple average of the closing price of gold of 999 purity, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA). The average is taken for the previous three business days from the date of redemption.

Following this rule, the redemption price for the final redemption due on October 23, 2026, is ₹14,425 per unit of SGB. This is based on the simple average of the closing price of gold for three business days: October 19, October 21 and October 22, 2026.
