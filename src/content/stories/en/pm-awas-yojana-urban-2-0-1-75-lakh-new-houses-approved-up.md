## What happened

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs has approved 1.75 lakh houses under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0). The decision was taken at the 11th meeting of the Central Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (CSMC) on 19 November 2026, chaired by Shri Satyendra Singh, Secretary, Department of Urban Development.

## State-wise numbers

The approval covers the Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC) and Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP) components. Uttar Pradesh gets the largest share with 1,01,493 houses. Others are Maharashtra 19,778, Tamil Nadu 14,350, Madhya Pradesh 10,913, West Bengal 10,159, Gujarat 5,793, Assam 4,841, Rajasthan 4,572, Odisha 2,681, Puducherry 937 and Goa 6.

Separately, in-principle approval was given for 1,27,349 houses in Andhra Pradesh and 22,484 in Maharashtra. This approval is subject to the states submitting required compliance documents and following the scheme guidelines.

## The running total

With this, the total number of houses approved under PMAY-U 2.0 has crossed 24.15 lakh. Under PMAY-U and PMAY-U 2.0 together, 1.29 crore houses have been approved so far and 1.01 crore have been completed.

## What states were told

The Secretary asked states and union territories to speed up spending and make full use of approved funds. They were told to release money on time to beneficiaries and implementing agencies. He also stressed stronger monitoring, geo-tagging of houses and full verification of all pending applications within a set time.

## About the scheme

PMAY-U 2.0 was launched in September 2024. It aims to help eligible EWS, LIG and MIG families build or buy a pucca house. It also covers rental housing needs of urban migrants, working women and the urban poor. The meeting also advised states to frame affordable housing policies and carry out reforms to create a supportive environment.
