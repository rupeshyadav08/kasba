## What happened

Union Minister for Youth Affairs and Sports Dr Mansukh Mandaviya and Telangana Chief Minister A Revanth Reddy unveiled the logo and mascot of the 8th Khelo India Youth Games on 15 November 2026. The event will be held in Telangana from 11 to 22 December 2026.

## Where and when the Games will be held

The Games will be spread across six Telangana cities — Hyderabad, Khammam, Karimnagar, Hanumakonda, Mahbubnagar and Nizamabad. According to the notice, about 8,000 athletes and participants are expected to take part.

## Which sports are included

This edition will have 29 disciplines in all, made up of 27 competitive sports and two demonstration sports. The competitive sports include archery, athletics, badminton, basketball, boxing, cycling, fencing, football, gatka, gymnastics, hockey, judo, kabaddi, kalaripayattu, kho-kho, mallakhamb, rowing, shooting, swimming, softball, table tennis, tennis, thang-ta, volleyball, weightlifting, wrestling and yogasana.

Softball has been added as a competitive sport in the Khelo India Youth Games for the first time. Sailing and Karra Samu will make their debut as demonstration sports.

## What the logo and mascot show

According to the notice, the logo brings together Telangana's cultural heritage and sporting aspirations through eight key elements. These include the Khelo India symbol, Gandhi Tower, the Kakatiya art Thoranam, the Buddha statue at Hussain Sagar, the Tangedu flower, seven sports silhouettes and blue, green and yellow ribbons. The tagline is "Khel ka Junoon, Telangana ki Jaan aur Pehchaan".

The mascot is named "Neelamani" and is described as a symbol of energy, agility and determination.

## What this means for ordinary readers

This is mainly information about a sports event. The notice gives no application, registration or last date for the general public. Athletes taking part should get details about their participation from their state sports department or coach.
