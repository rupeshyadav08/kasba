## What has been announced

The Steel Ministry said on 23 October 2026 that it will hold an Open House to discuss problems related to steel imports. The meeting will be held in New Delhi on 28 October 2026.

## Where and when

The Open House will run from 11:00 am to 5:00 pm at the 'Steel Room', 3rd Floor, GPOA-3, Netaji Nagar, New Delhi. According to the Ministry, companies and organisations can raise their problems on relevant subjects at this meeting.

## Who it is for

The meeting is meant for companies and organisations facing problems with SIMS, SARAL SIMS or QCO exemption for steel imports. Such companies have been advised to send their request by 12:00 noon on 26 October 2026 so that a time slot can be fixed.

## How to get a slot

To take part, send an email to tech-steel[at]nic[dot]in. A fixed time slot will be communicated to you by email. The Ministry has made clear that walk-ins without prior information are not allowed, to avoid crowding.

## What to write in the email

Include the name of the company or organisation, the industry and product type (such as auto, aerospace, telecom or defence), the issue concerned (SIMS/SARAL SIMS/QCO exemption), the reference of the exemption application if there is one, and a brief description of the problem in up to 50 words.

Also give the participant's name and designation, along with mobile number and email. According to the Ministry, representation by a third party is not allowed, and to let as many people as possible attend, only one representative per organisation is permitted.

## Effect on ordinary people

This meeting does not directly concern the general public. It is for companies and organisations that import steel, so ordinary citizens do not need to do anything for it.
