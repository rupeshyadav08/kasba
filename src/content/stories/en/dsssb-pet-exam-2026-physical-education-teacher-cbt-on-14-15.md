## What has happened

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has issued the schedule for the online examination for Physical Education Teacher, post code 41122. The exam will be a Computer Based Test (CBT) for posts under the Directorate of Education, Delhi.

According to the notice, the exam will be held on 14 December 2026 (Monday) and 15 December 2026 (Tuesday).

## Shifts and timings

On 14 December 2026 there are three shifts. Shift I runs from 09:00 AM to 11:00 AM, Shift II from 01:00 PM to 03:00 PM and Shift III from 05:00 PM to 07:00 PM.

On 15 December 2026 there are two shifts. Shift I runs from 09:00 AM to 11:00 AM and Shift II from 01:00 PM to 03:00 PM.

## Who is affected

This information is for candidates who have applied for the post of Physical Education Teacher, post code 41122. The schedule tells them their exam date and shift.

## What candidates should do

The notice does not state the date for downloading the e-admit card. Candidates should keep checking the DSSSB website and the OARS portal regularly.

The notice also says that if a candidate does not get any information on the registered mobile number for any reason, it does not give a right to an extension for downloading the e-admit card or to a re-examination. Candidates are therefore advised to check the Board's website and the OARS portal themselves.

## Where to get more information

For exam-related information, check the official DSSSB website. As stated in the notice, the Board uploads information on its website and the OARS portal from time to time.
