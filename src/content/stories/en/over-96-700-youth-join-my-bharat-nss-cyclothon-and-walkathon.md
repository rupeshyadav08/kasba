## What happened

MY Bharat-National Service Scheme (NSS) organised cyclothons, walkathons and runs across the country on 30-31 October 2026. More than 96,700 people from over 200 institutions took part. The activities were held at public places and prominent civic spots, bringing together students, NSS volunteers and community members.

## Which campaign it was part of

The events were part of the seventh week of the 100-week 'Drug-Free Youth Resolution for a Developed India' campaign. In its second month, the theme was 'Healthy Youth, Drug-Free Youth' (Shakti and Seva). The aim was to spread awareness about the harmful effects of substance use and encourage fitness, a healthy lifestyle and community service.

## How many took part where

NSS units held cycle rallies of 10-15 km and walkathons and runs of 3-5 km. Walkathons and runs together drew over 23,300 participants, while cyclothons saw over 8,000 youth take part. School groups, women's groups and senior citizens also joined the walkathons.

## Beyond the run

Volunteers also held street plays, slogan-writing activities and songs to get people talking about substance use, peer pressure and recovery. Street plays drew over 5,600 participants and members of the public, while slogan-writing and music activities involved over 11,800 people.

## Cleanliness and environment

The campaign also included 'plogging' - picking up litter while jogging or walking - along rally routes. Over 11,400 youth took part, collecting waste and handing segregated waste to local municipal bodies.

## How it ended

The activities closed with a collective 'drug-free pledge', in which about 26,000 participants and community members committed to building a drug-free society. Youth ambassadors also shared reels and photos of the events on Instagram using #NashaMuktYuva.

## What this means for ordinary people

This is an awareness campaign, not a new government scheme. Ordinary people do not need to apply for anything and no last date has been set. Students and volunteers linked to schools, colleges or NSS units can get information about future activities from their institution.
