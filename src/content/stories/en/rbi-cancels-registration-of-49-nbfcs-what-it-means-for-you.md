## What happened

On 16 November 2026, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) cancelled the Certificate of Registration of 49 NBFC companies. RBI gave this information in its official press release.

RBI took this action under Section 45-IA (6) of the RBI Act, 1934. The cancellation orders for the companies in the list were issued between 1 October 2026 and 29 October 2026.

## Which companies are covered

The list includes companies registered in Kolkata, Chennai, Mumbai, Salem, Howrah, Ahmedabad and New Delhi. It names companies such as J S Enclaves Pvt Ltd, Kavery Commodities Pvt Ltd, Falcon Complex Private Limited and Gandhinagar Leasing and Finance Ltd. The full list is available in the RBI press release.

## Impact on ordinary people

RBI has only announced the cancellation of registration. This does not directly affect the daily life of ordinary people. If you have taken a loan from or deposited money with any of these companies, you should keep your documents and transaction records safe.

## What to do next

RBI has not given any specific instructions on what people connected to these companies should do. For more information, you can check the press release on the RBI website.
