## What has happened

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment issued a release on 30 October 2026. It said that under the National Action for Mechanised Sanitation Ecosystem (Namaste) scheme, sanitation workers are being given safety equipment, health cards and training.

The release gives the example of a 37-year-old sanitation worker, whose name is changed to Ramesh Kumar. He works as an assistant in removing human waste from septic tanks.

## What changed under the scheme

According to the release, during 2025–26 the worker received a PPE kit, an Ayushman Bharat health card, and skill and safety training. A PPE kit is a set of personal protective equipment that helps protect the body during work.

The training improved his understanding of work hazards and safety measures. The Ayushman Bharat card strengthened his access to health services. The release also says that working in a safer way helped raise his income to some extent.

## What the problems were earlier

The release says such workers faced dangerous working conditions, a lack of safety equipment and limited access to welfare facilities. Social stigma, low pay and financial hardship added to the difficulties.

## Who benefits

The scheme is mainly for sanitation workers who do hazardous work such as cleaning septic tanks. The release does not say which states or districts it covers, and it gives no application date or process.

## What to do next

The release lists no step for ordinary people. A sanitation worker who wants information about the scheme should ask the concerned department in their district. The ministry said it is working to give sanitation workers the safety, health support and respect they deserve.
