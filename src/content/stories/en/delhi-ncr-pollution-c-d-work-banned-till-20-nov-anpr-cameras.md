## What happened

Union Minister for Environment, Forest and Climate Change Bhupendra Yadav on 14 November 2026 reviewed the steps taken by Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan to reduce air pollution in Delhi-NCR. Delhi's Environment Minister Sardar Manjinder Singh Sirsa, Haryana's Rao Narbir Singh and Uttar Pradesh's Arun Kumar Saxena joined the meeting virtually.

According to the ministry, more than 30 review meetings have been held with NCR state governments and municipal bodies over the past year.

## What has changed

With pollution rising in winter, the minister asked all agencies to move beyond planning and act strictly on the ground. As per CAQM's order of 30.10.2026, dust-generating construction and demolition activities are banned across NCR from 5 to 20 November 2026.

On road dust, the minister stressed repairing potholes and fixing damaged footpaths and central verges. The notice said only 3,221 km of the 5,480 km of targeted roads has been redeveloped so far. Mechanical road sweeping machines are to be run in double shifts and deep-cleaning drives stepped up.

## Who benefits

The measures are meant to keep the air cleaner for everyone living in Delhi and nearby cities. Construction companies, industries and transport departments will have to meet the rules within the set timelines.

On industries, the notice said 2,298 of 2,345 targeted industries were connected to OCEAMS, while the remaining 47 units were shut or sealed. CPCB was directed to immediately seal 191 non-compliant industries.

## What happens next

The minister said all concerned states should install ANPR cameras. If not, CAQM will start criminal proceedings after 30.11.2026 against erring transport department officials. States were asked to nominate secretary-level officers for 7 cities in Haryana, 4 in UP and 4 in Rajasthan, who will do field visits and report to CAQM every week.

Immediate action was ordered at 233 identified congestion points in NCR, and MCD was asked to complete a multi-lane free flow system at Delhi's 154 border entry points by 31.12.2026.

## What citizens need to do

The meeting announced no application, form or deadline for ordinary citizens. The directions are mainly for government agencies, municipal bodies, industries and transport departments. The minister also called for awareness campaigns involving citizens, RWAs, transport associations and the construction industry.
