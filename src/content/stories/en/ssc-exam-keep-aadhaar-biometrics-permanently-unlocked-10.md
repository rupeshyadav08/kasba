## What the advisory says

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) issued an advisory on 17 November 2026 for candidates appearing in its examinations. It asked them to make sure the details and biometrics in their Aadhaar are updated, so that identity verification at exam centres stays smooth and secure.

## What needs to be updated

According to the advisory, candidates should keep demographic particulars such as name, date of birth and address updated in Aadhaar. Biometric records, including fingerprints, iris scans and facial data, should also be updated. SSC referred to advisories from the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) on Aadhaar authentication and biometric validation.

## The rule on unlocking biometrics

UIDAI offers both temporary and permanent unlocking of Aadhaar biometrics. SSC said a temporary unlock is valid for only 10 minutes, which is not enough for the multi-step checks at an exam centre, covering entry, biometric authentication, in-exam verification and exit.

Candidates must therefore keep their biometrics permanently unlocked for the entire duration of the examination process. They may re-lock their biometric credentials after the examination concludes successfully, if they wish.

## Who should pay attention

This applies to anyone who sits for SSC examinations. SSC strongly advises candidates to complete all necessary changes and verify their Aadhaar authentication status well in advance, so that there is no last-minute inconvenience. People with no connection to an SSC examination are not directly affected.
