## What has happened

The Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare has proposed an amendment to the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019. A draft gazette notification, G.S.R. 983 (E), was issued for this on 6 November. Under the proposal, 'antimicrobials' will be included in the definition of a 'new drug' given in Rule 2(1)(w)(v).

## Why this is being done

According to the ministry, the move follows concern that manufacturing licences for antimicrobial products are issued without adequate regulatory checks. These checks include studies such as bioavailability/bioequivalence and stability. The notification says that without such assessment, the quality, safety and effectiveness of these products can suffer. Poor-quality or ineffective antimicrobials can add to the growth and spread of antimicrobial resistance (AMR), which creates a risk to patient safety.

## What AMR is

Antimicrobial resistance means germs stop responding to medicines that once worked against them. The notification calls AMR a major global public health concern and says coordinated action is needed to address it. It also refers to the G-20 New Delhi Leaders' Declaration, which stressed tackling AMR through a 'One Health' approach.

## Who is affected

The proposal directly affects drug manufacturers. Under the proposed arrangement, manufacturers would need permission from the Central Licensing Authority (CLA) before applying to the concerned State Licensing Authority (SLA) for a manufacturing licence. The ministry expects this to strengthen regulatory oversight at the central level and help ensure the quality, safety and effectiveness of these products.

## What ordinary people should do

The proposal gives ordinary patients no application, form or deadline. Before finalising the amendment, the ministry has invited objections and suggestions from stakeholders and the general public. Any suggestion can be sent within 30 days of the date the draft rules are made available to the public in the gazette, to the Under Secretary (Drugs), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, U-6, Work Hall-C Wing, First Floor, Kartavya Bhawan-1, New Delhi-110001. These can also be emailed to drugsdiv-mohfw[at]gov[dot]in. The draft gazette notification is available on the e-Gazette website.
