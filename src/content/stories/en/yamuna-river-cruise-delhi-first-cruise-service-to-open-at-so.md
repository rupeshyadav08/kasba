Delhi is set to get a recreational cruise service on the Yamuna river. According to a release from the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Union Minister Sarbananda Sonowal will inaugurate it at Sonia Vihar on 10 November 2026. Delhi Lieutenant Governor Taranjit Singh Sandhu and Chief Minister Rekha Gupta will also be present.

## What is starting

The service has been developed on the Sonia Vihar–Jagatpur stretch of the Yamuna. The ministry says the aim is to promote river-based tourism and give tourists visiting the capital a new means of recreation. Before the launch, a trial run of the cruise vessel was successfully held last month in the presence of senior IWAI and Delhi government officials.

## The Yamuna connection

Under the National Waterways Act, 2016, the Yamuna has been declared National Waterway number 110. The release describes the cruise as an important step in using this waterway's potential for recreational navigation and river tourism.

## Who is building it

The initiative is led by the Inland Waterways Authority of India (IWAI), which comes under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. It is being developed with the support of the Delhi Development Authority, the Delhi Tourism and Transportation Development Corporation, the Delhi Jal Board and the Irrigation and Flood Control Department of the Delhi government.

## Who benefits

According to the release, Delhi residents and tourists will get a unique experience and public connection with the Yamuna will grow. The cruise vessels are electric-powered, which the release links to clean and sustainable transport.

## What is not yet known

The release gives no information on tickets, fares, timings or booking. So it is not yet clear when and how ordinary passengers can take a ride. For details, readers can contact IWAI or the Delhi tourism department.
