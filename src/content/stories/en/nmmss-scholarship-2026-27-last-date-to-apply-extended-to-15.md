## What has changed

The Union Ministry of Education has extended the last date for submitting applications under the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) for the academic session 2026-27 to 15 November 2026. According to the ministry, this gives eligible students extra time to complete their application on the National Scholarship Portal and also helps officials verify applications on time.

## What are the verification dates

The ministry has issued a revised schedule. The last date to submit applications is 15 November 2026. The institute nodal officer (L1) has to complete verification by 30 November 2026 and the district nodal officer (L2) by 15 December 2026.

Students who have already applied must make sure their application is verified by the concerned institute's nodal officer by 30 November and by the district nodal officer by 15 December 2026.

## What is the scheme

The scheme aims to support meritorious students from economically weaker sections and encourage them to continue their studies after Class 8 up to Class 12. Under the scheme, one lakh new scholarships are given every year to eligible students entering Class 9. For this, students have to pass a selection test conducted by the concerned state or union territory government. The scholarship is renewed from Class 10 to Class 12 if the student meets the prescribed academic criteria.

## Who benefits

Eligible students studying in state government schools, government-aided schools and schools run by local bodies get a scholarship of Rs 12,000 every year. The amount is sent directly to their bank accounts through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

To appear in the selection test, students must have at least 55% marks or an equivalent grade in Class 7. Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe students get a relaxation of five percentage points in marks. The applicant's parents' annual income must not be more than Rs 3.5 lakh.

## How many applications so far

The application process for this scheme on the National Scholarship Portal began on 1 June 2026. Before applying for the scholarship, students must complete a one-time registration (OTR) on the portal. According to the ministry, 90,483 fresh and 1,99,531 renewal applications had been submitted under the scheme till 30 October 2026.

## How to apply

Students who have not applied yet should complete the OTR registration on the National Scholarship Portal and submit their application by 15 November 2026. After applying, they should stay in touch with their school's nodal officer so that verification is completed within the stated dates.
