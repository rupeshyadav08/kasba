## What has happened

Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN), which works under the Department of Telecommunications, signed a 'usage agreement' at Sanchar Bhawan in New Delhi on 30 October 2026. The agreement is between Digital Bharat Nidhi, the Department of Telecommunications, the Tamil Nadu government and Tamil Nadu FibreNet Corporation (TANFINET).

The aim is to use the BharatNet network in Tamil Nadu so that rural households can be connected under the Amended BharatNet Programme (ABP) through a state-led model.

## What the Amended BharatNet Programme is

The Union Cabinet approved the Amended BharatNet Programme on 04 August 2023. Its purpose is to upgrade, strengthen and expand the existing BharatNet network.

The programme aims to give all gram panchayats and villages strong, future-ready broadband connectivity on a demand basis. The Government of India has approved financial subsidy support for the use of the BharatNet network under this programme.

## What changes in Tamil Nadu

According to the notice, the initiative is expected to bring fibre connections to more than 5 lakh rural homes in Tamil Nadu with financial assistance from the Government of India. This can improve access to digital services in the state's rural and remote areas.

These services include e-governance, online education, telemedicine, digital payments and citizen services. In addition, 4,767 villages in Tamil Nadu will get connectivity on a demand basis to strengthen last-mile connectivity.

## What Digital Bharat Nidhi is

Digital Bharat Nidhi (earlier USOF) was set up under the Indian Telegraph (Amendment) Act, 2003, effective from 01.04.2002. Under the Telecommunications Act, 2023, the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) is now Digital Bharat Nidhi.

It is an attached office of the Department of Telecommunications under the Ministry of Communications. Its main aim is to support universal service by improving the reach and availability of telecom services in rural, remote and urban areas where such services are not adequately available.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit is expected to go to rural households in Tamil Nadu, which may get fibre connections and better connectivity. According to the notice, the agreement is expected to speed up implementation of the programme in the state.

Ordinary readers in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh or Delhi are not directly affected by this agreement. The notice also does not say where to contact for a connection or what conditions must be met. For such details, readers can reach out to the Department of Telecommunications or Tamil Nadu FibreNet Corporation.
