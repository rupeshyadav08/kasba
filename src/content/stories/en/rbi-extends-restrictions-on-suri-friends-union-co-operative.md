## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended its directions on The Suri Friends' Union Co-operative Bank Ltd., Suri, West Bengal. The directions will now continue for three more months, from close of business on October 22, 2026 to close of business on January 22, 2027. The RBI said this in a press release dated October 22, 2026.

## When did the directions begin

The RBI issued the directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The first directive came on July 21, 2022, for six months, up to close of business on January 22, 2023. It was extended from time to time. A directive dated July 14, 2026 had extended the period up to close of business on October 22, 2026.

## What has changed now

The RBI said it was satisfied that, in the public interest, the directions needed to continue beyond October 22, 2026. It has therefore extended them for a further three months, up to close of business on January 22, 2027, subject to review. All other terms and conditions of the directive remain unchanged.

## What the RBI clarified

The RBI also stated that the extension should not by itself be taken to mean that it is satisfied with the bank's financial position. In other words, the extension is not a comment on the bank's financial health.

## Who is affected

The decision mainly concerns the bank's customers and account holders, for whom the existing conditions continue as before. There is no direct effect on the general public.

## What to do next

If you have an account with this bank, ask your branch what services are allowed under the directions. You can also read the RBI's press release on its website for reliable information.
