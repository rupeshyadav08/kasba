## What has changed

The Commerce Department changed the guidelines of four interventions under the 'Niryat Disha' sub-scheme of the Export Promotion Mission on 17 November 2026. The four are Market Access Support (MAS), Trade Regulations, Accreditation and Compliance Enablement (TRACE), and Logistics Interventions for Freight and Transport (LIFT).

Under MAS, the maximum eligible export turnover for airfare reimbursement has been raised from Rs 75 lakh to Rs 50 crore. Micro and small exporters can now get up to 95% reimbursement and other eligible exporters up to 80%. The compulsory membership period with export promotion councils has been cut from six months to three months.

## Certification and freight

TRACE helps micro enterprises meet internationally recognised quality, safety and technical requirements for foreign markets. The list of certificates eligible for reimbursement has been raised from 462 to 600.

LIFT addresses the logistics gap faced by MSMEs in low export-intensity areas. Coverage of low export-intensity districts has been expanded from 13 to 34 states and union territories. Land Customs Stations and Integrated Check Posts have been added to the eligible port categories, and waterways are now included alongside road, rail and air.

## Other changes

Air freight support for export shipments will now also cover Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, besides the North-East. The minimum distance criterion between origin and destination ports has been cut from 200 km to 100 km. All exportable goods are now within the scope of support, except items on the negative list of export products and categories.

## What the Mission is

The Union Cabinet approved the Export Promotion Mission on 12 November 2025, with a total outlay of Rs 25,060 crore for 2025-26 to 2030-31. It focuses on MSMEs, first-time exporters and low-representation regions. It runs through two sub-schemes: Niryat Protsahan (trade finance and financial support) and Niryat Disha (market access and non-financial barriers). Eleven interventions have been rolled out under the two so far.

## Who benefits

The benefit goes to exporters and MSMEs who attend buyer-seller meets, trade fairs and exhibitions, or get standards certificates for foreign markets. Salaried workers, farmers and consumers are not directly affected.
