Two industrial disputes involving Bihar Gramin Bank have now been settled. According to a release from the Ministry of Labour and Employment, the disputes were resolved amicably on 22 and 23 October 2026 through conciliation efforts by the Assistant Labour Commissioner (Central), Patna. This office functions under the Chief Labour Commissioner (Central).

## What happened

Two separate settlements were reached between the bank management and its employee organisations. The first was signed with the Bihar Gramin Bank Officers Association, Patna on 22 October 2026. The second was signed with the Bihar Gramin Bank Workers Organisation (BGBWO) on 23 October 2026.

## Who gains and how much

According to the release, more than 10,900 employees of the bank are expected to get a total financial benefit of about Rs 77.69 crore. This includes about Rs 9.02 crore in recurring annual benefit.

The first settlement is expected to benefit about 8,400 employees and involves a financial benefit of about Rs 67.17 crore. The second settlement is expected to benefit about 2,500 employees, with a financial benefit of about Rs 10.52 crore.

## Which demands were addressed

The first settlement addresses key employee demands, including payment of performance-linked incentive (PLI), work allowance, pay revision, higher travel allowance, promotions and payment for compassionate appointments.

The second settlement addresses demands related to effective implementation of the 12th bipartite settlement, higher house rent allowance, higher transport allowance for employees with disabilities, overtime payment, transparency in promotion processes and setting up a consultative council.

## Why it matters

The release says both settlements were made possible through constructive dialogue and the active participation and cooperation of employee organisations and bank management. This helped ensure an amicable resolution of the disputes and strengthen harmony and coordination in industrial relations.

## Effect on ordinary people

The matter concerns the bank's employees and officers. Ordinary depositors or customers have no action to take. Bank staff can get details of the settlements from their organisation or the bank management.
