## What has been decided

The RBI issued a press release on 29 October 2026. It said that the interest on the Government of India Floating Rate Bond 2034 (FRB 2034) for the next six months will be 7.26 per cent per annum. This rate is for the period from 30 October 2026 to 29 April 2027.

## What FRB 2034 is

FRB 2034 is a Government of India bond whose interest rate is not fixed but changes from time to time. Such bonds are called Floating Rate Bonds. The interest rate is set afresh for every six-month period.

## How the rate is set

The RBI said the rate on this bond is made of two parts. First, the average Weighted Average Yield of the last three auctions of 182-day T-Bills, taken from the rate fixing day, which is 30 October 2026. Second, a fixed spread of 0.98 per cent is added to it. The rate that comes out of these two is applied to the bond.

## Who benefits

This rate applies to people, companies and institutions that hold the FRB 2034 bond. They will get interest at 7.26 per cent per annum for the next six months. Those who do not hold this bond are not directly affected.

## What ordinary people need to do

Ordinary people do not need to do anything. The RBI fixes the interest rate and bond holders get interest automatically. For more details, the press release can be seen on the RBI website.
