The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting has invited applications from startup companies working in film and related technologies. These companies can be part of the WaveX Startup Pavilion at WAVES Bazaar, held during the International Film Festival of India (IFFI), Goa 2026. The notice is dated 7 November 2026.

## What the opportunity is

At WAVES Bazaar, emerging startups in film and related technology will get a chance to display their products, build recognition and network. According to the ministry, the aim is to give such companies a dedicated platform where they can present their products, technologies and solutions before the national and international film and media industry.

It will also offer chances to connect, exchange ideas and collaborate with filmmakers, production houses, investors and people from the global creative industry.

## Who can apply

DPIIT-registered startup companies working in the film and related technology sector are eligible to apply. Eligible companies will be selected based on a screening and evaluation process by a selection committee set up by WaveX.

## How to apply

See the website wavex.wavesbazaar.com for the registration form and more information. For any information, email Wavex-mib[at]gov[dot]in. The notice does not state a last date for applications.

## What WaveX is

WaveX (WAVES Startup Accelerator) is a startup promotion and incubation initiative of the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. It works to encourage innovation and entrepreneurship in the media and entertainment sector. The initiative focuses on supporting startups in areas such as animation, visual effects, gaming, comics, extended reality (AVGC-XR) and media technologies.

## About IFFI

Established in 1952, the International Film Festival of India (IFFI) is counted among Asia's leading film festivals. Held every year in Goa, it brings together people from the film world across the globe. The festival aims to showcase diverse cinematic works, promote creative exchange and encourage international cooperation in cinema.
