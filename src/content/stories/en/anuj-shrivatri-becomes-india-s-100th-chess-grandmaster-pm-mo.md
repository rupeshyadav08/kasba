## What happened

According to a Prime Minister's Office release dated 10 November 2026, Anuj Shrivatri has earned chess's prestigious title. With this, he has become India's 100th chess Grandmaster.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi appreciated the achievement and congratulated Anuj Shrivatri. He called it exceptional and a wonderful success.

## What has changed

As per the release, the number of chess Grandmasters in India has now reached one hundred. The Prime Minister said India reaching 100 Grandmasters is a remarkable milestone.

He said it reflects the immense talent, dedication and hard work of the country's players. It also shows the growing popularity of chess among the youth.

## What else the Prime Minister said

The Prime Minister said it is particularly heartening to see young chess talents emerging from different parts of the country and making their mark on the global stage. He hoped the achievement will inspire many more youngsters to take up chess and pursue excellence.

In his post on X, the Prime Minister wrote, "India has hit a century in chess!" He congratulated Anuj Shrivatri there as well and called it a wonderful achievement.

## What this means for ordinary readers

This news has no application, form, money or deadline for the general public. It is an inspirational story for children and young people who play or want to learn chess. Families whose children are learning the game can take it as an example.

Grandmaster is a prestigious title in chess. The release does not describe the process or conditions for earning the title.
