## What happened

According to the Defence Ministry, water entered INS Kesari on the afternoon of 31 October 2026. At the time of the incident, the ship was berthed at the Naval Dockyard in Visakhapatnam.

## No report of casualties

The ministry said there is no report of any loss of life or of personnel being injured. In other words, as per the information given, no one died and no one was hurt in the incident.

## What is being done now

Work to remove water from the affected parts is actively continuing. The ministry said the situation is being monitored continuously.

## About the investigation

The ministry said a formal investigation will be started to find out the exact cause of the incident. The release does not say what the investigation has found or when it will be completed.

## What this means for ordinary people

This is an incident involving a naval ship that was standing at the dockyard. The release gives no instruction, application or deadline for ordinary citizens, so readers do not need to take any action.
