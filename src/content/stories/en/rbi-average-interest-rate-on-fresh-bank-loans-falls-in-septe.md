## What was released

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) released its monthly data on lending and deposit rates of scheduled commercial banks on 30 October 2026. The data is based on information received during October 2026 and excludes regional rural banks and small finance banks.

## What changed in lending rates

According to the RBI, the weighted average lending rate (WALR) on outstanding rupee loans of these banks was 8.96 per cent in September 2026, the same as in August 2026. So this rate did not change.

The WALR on fresh rupee loans declined to 8.51 per cent in September 2026 from 8.61 per cent in August 2026. The 1-year median Marginal Cost of Funds based Lending Rate (MCLR) declined to 8.55 per cent in October 2026 from 8.61 per cent in September 2026. MCLR is the minimum rate banks use as a base to set loan interest rates.

## Deposit rates

The weighted average domestic term deposit rate (WADTDR) on outstanding rupee term deposits declined marginally to 6.55 per cent in September 2026 from 6.56 per cent in August 2026.

The WADTDR on fresh rupee term deposits declined to 5.61 per cent in September 2026 from 5.67 per cent in August 2026.

## Who is affected

The data shows which way average bank interest rates are moving. A lower average rate on fresh loans means new borrowers paid slightly less on average. Lower average deposit rates mean people opening new term deposits earned slightly less on average.

These are averages across banks. The rate on any single bank, scheme or customer can be different.

## What ordinary people should do

This report has no direct effect on ordinary people and there is nothing they need to do. To know the actual rate on your loan or fixed deposit, contact your bank. The full data is available on the RBI website.
