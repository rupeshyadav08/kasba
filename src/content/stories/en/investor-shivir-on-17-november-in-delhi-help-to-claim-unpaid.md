## When and where is the camp

IEPFA, which works under the Corporate Affairs Ministry, is holding a one-day Investor Shivir on 17 November 2026 at Jacaranda Hall, India Habitat Centre, New Delhi. SEBI and market infrastructure institutions (MIIs) are supporting the event.

## Who is it for

The camp is for investors and shareholders. It is especially for those whose unpaid dividend is lying with companies, whose shares have been transferred to IEPF, or who have a pending complaint related to their claim.

## What help is available on the spot

Investors can get direct help in recovering unpaid dividend amounts and shares lying with companies for six to seven years. They can update their Know Your Customer (eKYC) details and get nomination-related services at the venue. Special service desks will be set up at the venue to give immediate help and resolve problems related to claims pending with IEPFA.

## What is new this time

The camp model promotes direct contact between investors, companies and Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs) without middlemen. For the first time, companies holding a large number of unpaid dividend accounts are taking part directly, so eligible and genuine claimants can get guidance from the companies themselves.

## How to register and get more information

Interested claimants can register for the camp through the link given on IEPFA's website www.iepf.gov.in. More information is also available on the same website. The camp is part of IEPFA's nationwide investor outreach and awareness programme, which aims to strengthen financial literacy and make it easier to recover unclaimed investments.
