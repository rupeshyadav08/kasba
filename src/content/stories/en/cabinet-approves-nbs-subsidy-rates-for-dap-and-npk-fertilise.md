## What happened

The Union Cabinet, chaired by Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, on 11 November 2026 approved nutrient-based subsidy (NBS) rates for phosphatic and potassic (P&K) fertilisers for Rabi 2026-27. The period covered is 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.

## Who benefits

The government says this will ensure farmers get fertilisers at concessional, affordable and reasonable prices. The subsidy will be given on P&K fertilisers, including DAP and NPK grades, at the approved rates so that these fertilisers remain smoothly available.

## What has changed

According to the release, the subsidy on P&K fertilisers has been rationalised in view of recent trends in international prices of fertilisers and raw materials. The estimated budgetary requirement for Rabi 2026-27 is about Rs 62,370.02 crore, which is about Rs 24,417.73 crore more than the Rabi 2025-26 requirement. The budget for Rabi 2025-26 was Rs 37,952.29 crore.

## How the subsidy reaches farmers

The government is making 28 grades of P&K fertilisers, including DAP, available to farmers at concessional prices through fertiliser manufacturers and importers. Fertiliser companies will be given subsidy as per the approved and notified rates. Subsidy on P&K fertilisers has been governed by the NBS scheme since 1 April 2010.

## What farmers need to do

The release does not mention any separate application, registration or deadline for farmers. Buy fertilisers from your dealer as usual. For information on rates and availability, you can ask your local fertiliser dealer or the agriculture department office.
