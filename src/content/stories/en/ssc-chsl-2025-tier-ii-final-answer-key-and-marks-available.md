## What has happened

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has uploaded the final answer key, candidates' response sheets and marks for the Tier-II of the Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2025 on its website. The facility opened at 11:00 pm on 17 November 2026.

SSC had declared the final result of this examination on 07.10.2026. Candidates can now view their scores and their response sheets.

## Who benefits

The facility is for candidates who appeared in the CHSL 2025 Tier-II paper. According to the notice, marks of both qualified and non-qualified candidates have been made available on the website.

## Till when and how to check

Candidates can view their final answer key, response sheet and marks from 17.11.2026 (11:00 pm) to 16.12.2026 (11:00 pm). For this, they have to log in on ssc.gov.in with their registered ID and password.

## What to keep in mind

The notice clearly says these documents will not be made available after the specified time limit. Candidates should therefore take a printout of their answer key, response sheet and scorecard.

It also says the saved question paper is strictly for personal use and self-analysis.

## Where to get help

Full details and the login link are available on the SSC website, ssc.gov.in.
