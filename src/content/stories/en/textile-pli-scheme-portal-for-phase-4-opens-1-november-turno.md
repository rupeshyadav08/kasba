## What has happened

The Ministry of Textiles has said it will reopen its application portal to accept fresh applications under the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme. This is for the fourth phase of the scheme, and the portal will stay open from 1 November 2026 to 31 December 2026.

According to the ministry, the aim is to promote the manufacture of man-made fibre (MMF) garments, MMF fabric and technical textiles, raise domestic manufacturing capacity, attract investment and create jobs.

## What has changed

The ministry listed three changes to the scheme. First, the minimum turnover requirement under the scheme's 'Part 2' category has been cut from Rs 200 crore to Rs 100 crore. The ministry says this will allow more participation by micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) and medium-sized manufacturing units.

Second, the scheme's duration has been extended, but only for applicants approved under the fourth phase. For them it will run up to financial year 2033-34, giving them a longer investment and operating window to set up and expand manufacturing capacity.

Third, companies already in the scheme can also move to Phase 4. This applies to all existing participant companies of Phase 1, 2 and 3, in any part of the scheme, that have not claimed or received any incentive so far. Such companies must withdraw their existing application to move to Phase 4.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit goes to companies making textiles and technical textiles, especially smaller and medium units whose turnover now fits the new Part 2 threshold. Ordinary citizens, farmers or job seekers do not have to apply for anything here.

## How to apply

The ministry said the online application portal https://pli.texmin.gov.in will be open from 1 November 2026 to 31 December 2026. Eligible companies have been advised to submit their applications within the deadline as per the applicable scheme guidelines.

## Where to check more

For the scheme's rules, conditions and application details, readers can refer to the portal and the scheme guidelines issued by the Ministry of Textiles.
