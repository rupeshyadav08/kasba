The registration framework for consent managers under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 came into force on 13 November 2026. As part of this phase, Electronics and IT Ministry Secretary Shri S. Krishnan launched the online portal dpbi.gov.in so that eligible companies can apply to register as consent managers.

## What has changed

Companies that want to work as consent managers now have an online route to apply. The Ministry described this as part of implementing the DPDP framework in phases, aimed at building a system that gives people more control over consent linked to their personal data.

## What a consent manager is

A consent manager is an entity registered with the Data Protection Board of India. It helps people give, manage, review and withdraw consent through a simple, transparent and interoperable platform. According to the Ministry, the framework seeks to strengthen trust and accountability in the digital ecosystem, with consent managers answerable to data principals.

## Which companies can apply

Companies incorporated in India with adequate technical, operational and financial capacity, including at least Rs 2 crore in total assets, can seek registration as consent managers. The Ministry said these requirements are meant to ensure that registered entities have the resources and ability to provide safe, reliable and accountable services.

## How to apply

Eligible companies can apply for registration to the Board through the newly launched dpbi.gov.in portal. The Board will assess applications as per the applicable requirements and may register eligible companies. The list of registered consent managers will be made publicly available on the Board's website.

## What comes next

Other key provisions relating to the rights of data principals, the obligations of data fiduciaries, and the Board's inquiry and penalty processes are to take effect on 13 May 2027.

## Where to get help

More information on the DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 is available on the Ministry's website meity.gov.in. Questions can be sent by email to secretary[at]dpbi[dot]gov[dot]in.
