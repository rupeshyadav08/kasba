## What the fair is and who runs it

Shilp Samagam Mela 2026 is being organised by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. It is being held at Delhi Haat, INA, in Delhi and will continue till 15 November 2026. The 15-day fair is themed 'Prakash aur Parampara – Jyoti se Jyoti Jalayein'.

On 6 November 2026, Union Minister of State Ramdas Athawale visited the fair and inspected the stalls.

## What visitors will find

According to the minister, the fair has more than 130 stalls displaying handicrafts, handloom products, traditional textiles, festive decoration items, lamps, home decor and other indigenous products. The fair is divided into seven sections — Kala Aangan, Prakash Chowk, Puja Path, Sajawat Bazar, Gharana, Swad Chowk and Manch.

In the evening, cultural programmes are held at the 'Manch', where artists from different states perform folk dances, traditional music and regional arts.

## Who benefits

The platform is meant for Scheduled Caste, Other Backward Class, sanitation worker and divyangjan artisans and beneficiaries to display and sell their products. They are supported by the National Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (NSFDCF), the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation (NBCFDC), the National Safai Karamcharis Finance and Development Corporation (NSKCFDC) and the National Divyangjan Finance and Development Corporation (NDFDC).

The minister said artisans are given free stalls, travel expenses, a daily allowance and transport facilities for their goods. The ministry has been holding such fairs since 2022, and through them more than a thousand artisans from SC, OBC and sanitation worker communities have got a chance to display and sell their products.

## How much was sold

A senior ministry official said total sales of the four corporations between 1 and 5 November 2026 were about Rs 37.90 lakh. The official described this as a positive market response to the products and growing opportunities for participating artisans.

## Other support for artisans

The official said the fair gives artisans a chance to widen their market reach through initiatives such as 'Bharat Tulip', which connects artisans to e-commerce platforms. 'Purple Fest' celebrates the creativity and abilities of divyangjan.
