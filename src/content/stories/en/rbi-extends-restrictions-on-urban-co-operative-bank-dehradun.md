## What has happened

On 5 November 2026, the RBI extended its directions on Urban Co-operative Bank Limited, Dehradun. The directions will now remain in force from close of business on 10 November 2026 up to close of business on 10 February 2027. The extension is for three months and is subject to review.

## What came before

The RBI first issued these directions on 9 February 2026, for six months from close of business on 10 February 2026. They were extended once before, on 5 August 2026. The RBI has now said it is satisfied that, in the public interest, the directions need to continue beyond 10 November 2026.

## Under which law

The directions are issued under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. This law gives the RBI powers to place conditions on banks.

## What has changed

Only the period of the directions has been extended. The RBI said all other terms and conditions of the directions remain unchanged.

## What the RBI cautioned

The RBI said the extension should not by itself be taken to mean that it is satisfied with the bank's financial position. In other words, the extension is not a comment on the bank's health.

## Who is affected

This concerns the customers and others connected with this bank. Other ordinary readers have nothing to do because of it. The notice sets out no new condition, date or application step for the general public.
