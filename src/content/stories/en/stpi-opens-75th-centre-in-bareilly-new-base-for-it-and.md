## What has happened

Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), an organisation under the Electronics and Information Technology Ministry, has opened a centre in Bareilly. It was inaugurated on 27 November 2026. With this, Bareilly has become STPI's 75th centre in the country.

According to the release, the centre is built on two acres of land at an investment of about Rs 18.25 crore.

## What STPI does

STPI is a government science and technology organisation that promotes the IT/ITES industry, innovation, research, startups and emerging technologies. Its areas include the Internet of Things, blockchain, AI, machine learning, robotics, data science, gaming, fintech, agritech, medtech, cyber security and drones.

Through its centres and incubation facilities, STPI gives startups and technology enterprises space, links with industry, and help with product development and creating intellectual property.

## What changes with the Bareilly centre

The release says the centre will give IT/ITES firms, startups and new entrepreneurs in the Rohilkhand region an institutional platform. Union Minister of State Shri Jitin Prasad said young people in Bareilly as well as Pilibhit, Shahjahanpur and Budaun will get technical facilities in their own region and will not have to move to cities like Mumbai, Noida or Hyderabad for such work.

The release also says 67 of STPI's 75 centres are in tier-2 and tier-3 cities, which shows the organisation is taking technology infrastructure beyond the big metros.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit is for people who work in technology or want to start a venture — software companies, small IT firms, local MSMEs and new entrepreneurs. The release adds that if local industry and entrepreneurs use the centre to its full capacity, more buildings and technical facilities can be added.

## What it means for ordinary readers

The notice announces no application, registration, payment or last date for ordinary citizens. The centre is mainly for technology enterprises and startups. Those who work in IT or want to start a company can ask STPI for details about the facility.
