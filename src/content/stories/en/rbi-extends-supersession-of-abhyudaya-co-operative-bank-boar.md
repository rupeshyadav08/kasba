## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) said on 9 November 2026 that the period of supersession of the Board of Directors of Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank Ltd., Mumbai, has been extended for one more year from 24 November 2026. The decision was taken under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

## Why the board was superseded earlier

According to the notice, the RBI had superseded the bank's board for one year on 24 November 2023. The reason given was material concerns arising from poor governance standards within the bank. The period was then extended by one year from 24 November 2024 and again by one year from 24 November 2025.

## What changes now

After a review, the RBI said it was satisfied that, in the public interest, the arrangement needed to continue beyond 23 November 2026. The supersession has therefore been extended for a further one year from 24 November 2026. This means the bank will continue to run under the RBI's supervision.

## Who will run the bank

As per the notice, Shri Satya Prakash Pathak will continue as the Administrator of the bank. He will be assisted by the present Committee of Advisors comprising Shri Venkatesh Hegde, Shri Devendra Kumar and Shri Suhas Gokhale.

## What this means for ordinary people

This decision concerns people who have an account or money in this bank. They do not have to fill any form or meet any date. The RBI's control over the bank will continue as before. The notice does not mention any change in deposits, interest or services.
