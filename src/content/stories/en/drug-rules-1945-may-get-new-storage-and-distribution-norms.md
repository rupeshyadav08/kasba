## What has happened

The Union Health and Family Welfare Ministry issued a proposal on 19 November 2026 to include Good Storage and Distribution Practices (GSDP) in the Drugs Rules, 1945. The aim stated is to keep the quality, safety, effectiveness and reliability of medicinal products intact at every step, from the manufacturer or importer to the patient.

## What GSDP means

GSDP stands for Good Storage and Distribution Practices. The proposed framework is based on GSDP guidelines prepared by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO). These cover activities such as purchase, storage, distribution, transport, documentation and record keeping.

## Why the ministry says it is needed

According to the ministry, quality has to be protected even after a medicine is made. Poor storage or handling can lower quality, cause contamination, or lead to substitution, forgery, diversion and unauthorised distribution. The proposed framework is meant to address these risks in the supply chain.

## Who is expected to benefit

The ministry expects the change to strengthen the regulatory framework for medicine distribution. It says central and state drug control authorities will be better placed for monitoring, inspection and enforcement. Documentation, record keeping and product tracking are also expected to improve. The ministry says this will help patients and health care providers get medicines of assured quality.

## Who it covers

The proposal says it will raise accountability of manufacturers, importers, wholesalers, distributors, stockists and other entities involved in distribution. In other words, it concerns people in the medicine trade.

## How to send suggestions

Stakeholders and the public have been invited to send suggestions and objections to Under Secretary (Drugs Regulation), Health and Family Welfare Ministry, Government of India, U-6, Work Hall, C Wing, First Floor, KartaVya Bhavan-1, New Delhi-110001. These can also be emailed to drugsdiv-mohfw[at]gov[dot]in. The full proposal can be read on the official gazette link shared by the ministry.
