## What has been launched

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment launched two digital platforms on 25 November 2026. The first is the 'Samavesh' portal, which gives one place to reach the ministry's schemes, services and organisational portals. The second is a new 'e-Anudaan' portal that makes the grant application process simpler. Union Minister Dr. Virendra Kumar and Minister of State Shri B.L. Verma launched both at the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre in Delhi.

## What has changed

According to ministry secretary Shri Sudhansh Pant, 33 separate portals have now been brought into the single 'Samavesh' portal. It includes six major scheme portals such as Senior Citizens Welfare, National Overseas Scholarship, Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan, the Sambal helpline, PM-AJAY and SMILE. Twelve organisational portals are also part of it, including bodies such as the Dr. Ambedkar Foundation, the National Institute of Social Defence and the National Commission for Scheduled Castes.

Under e-Anudaan 2.0, the number of forms has been cut from 283 to 182, reducing paperwork for NGOs and institutions. The portal offers a common project ID, pre-filled information, a dashboard showing application status and online notifications.

## What ordinary people get

The main feature of 'Samavesh' is an AI chatbot called 'Samajik Sahayak'. Through Bhashini, it gives information on schemes, eligibility and services in 22 Indian languages. The ministry says this will remove language barriers and let people get government information in the language they prefer. The portal is also linked to DigiLocker and PFMS, making it easier to view documents and payment-related information.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit goes to people who look for information on the ministry's schemes, especially those from deprived and marginalised sections. NGOs and institutions will have less paperwork in grant applications. Separately, a memorandum was signed with the National Skill Development Corporation for skill development and employment support for beneficiaries under PM-AJAY.

## Where to look

The 'Samavesh' portal is at https://www.dosje.gov.in/samavesh-citizen-portals/ and the new e-Anudaan portal at https://eanudaan.dosje.gov.in/.
