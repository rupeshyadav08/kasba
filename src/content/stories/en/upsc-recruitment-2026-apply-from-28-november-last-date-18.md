## What has happened

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has invited applications from eligible candidates for direct recruitment to various posts in the central government. The Commission issued the notice on 27 November 2026.

UPSC is the body that conducts recruitment exams for the central government. This notice does not say which posts are on offer; that information will be in the advertisement.

## When and where to apply

Interested candidates can apply online through the portal https://upsconline.nic.in from 28 November 2026 to 18 December 2026. The last date to apply is 18 December 2026.

Read the advertisement carefully before applying. The Commission has also urged candidates to follow the detailed instructions given in it.

## Where to find the advertisement

The detailed advertisement, number 14/2026, will be uploaded on the Commission's website https://upsc.gov.in. It will include instructions for candidates.

Details such as the number of posts, eligibility, age limit and application fee are not given in this notice. All of that should be taken from the advertisement itself.

## Who should take note

This recruitment is for those who are eligible for direct recruitment to central government posts; the advertisement will set out who is eligible.

Candidates who wish to apply should note the dates and complete their application in time after reading the advertisement. For more information, check the Commission's website.
