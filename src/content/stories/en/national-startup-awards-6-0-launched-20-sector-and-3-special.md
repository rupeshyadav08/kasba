## What has been launched

The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) has launched the sixth edition of the National Startup Awards (NSA 6.0) under the Startup India initiative. The aim is to recognise innovation and entrepreneurship and to strengthen the country's startup ecosystem.

The awards began in 2019 and five editions have been held so far. The year 2026 also marks 10 years of the Startup India initiative, which was launched in January 2016.

## Who can win

NSA 6.0 has 20 sector categories and 3 special categories. The sector list includes agriculture and food systems, deep tech, climate tech, fintech, healthcare, sports tech, space tech and tourism, among others.

The three special categories are women-led startup, North East region startup, and student innovator/emerging entrepreneur.

## What is new

This edition adds 12 new sector categories, such as bio-manufacturing, consumer industries, defence and security, rural economy and traditional industries, education and skill development, electronics and semiconductors, sports tech, future mobility, ocean and blue economy, space tech and drones, telecom, and travel and tourism.

For the first time, a Student Innovator/Emerging Entrepreneur award has been introduced. It is meant to encourage a culture of innovation among school and college students in all 36 states and union territories.

## How much is the prize

Winners in the 20 sector categories and the two startup-focused special categories will be eligible for a cash prize of Rs 25 lakh per category, subject to the applicable award guidelines. The Student Innovator/Emerging Entrepreneur award has a total prize pool of Rs 25 lakh across four sub-categories.

Beyond the money, the awards aim to help startups with mentoring, investor engagement, market access, industry partnerships and greater visibility.

## How to apply

Applications opened on 27 October 2026 and will remain open until 20 November 2026. Eligible startups and student innovators can apply through the official Startup India portal, www.startupindia.gov.in.

DPIIT said it will work with central ministries, state governments, incubators, academic institutions, banks, investment funds and industry bodies to support outreach, participation and evaluation. The awards also aim to encourage participation from startups in tier-2 and tier-3 cities.
