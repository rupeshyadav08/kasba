## What happened

The Diversefarm-India project has shared the results of its work on India's community seed banks (CSBs). According to the release, the country has at least 1,000 community seed banks, and 652 of them have been mapped in the project's digital atlas.

The findings were presented at a national workshop on the progress of CSBs held at NASC in New Delhi on 17 and 18 November 2026. Along with this, a GIS-based atlas, a policy brief on CSB types, a roadmap for Odisha and a new capacity-building manual were released.

## What a community seed bank is

A community seed bank is a local arrangement for saving seeds at village or community level. The release quotes Dr Rengalakshmi R, executive director of MSSRF, as saying that these are not just storehouses but living institutions for conserving seeds locally that strengthen farmers.

Diversefarm-India is a project led by the Fridtjof Nansen Institute (FNI) and the M.S. Swaminathan Research Foundation. Its aim is to strengthen seed systems through community seed banks and improve food, nutrition and livelihoods for small farmers.

## Six types and a roadmap for Odisha

The study identified six types of CSB based on management structure. According to the release, recognising these differences is important so that policy and support reach them in a targeted way.

The policy brief for Odisha sets out a 10-point roadmap. It recommends a secretariat under the state's Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Empowerment to shape and maintain an enabling environment for seed banks in the state.

## What the new manual covers

The capacity-building manual is based on a detailed case study in Odisha. It brings together technical guidance, organisational approach and policy context.

The manual was released at the National Agricultural Science Complex in New Delhi by Dr Trilochana Mohapatra, chairperson of the Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Protection Authority (PPVFR Authority). According to the release, he said that with growing government support, community seed banks will flourish further.

## Law and the way ahead

The project also examined India's legal and policy landscape. The Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Protection Act and the National Biodiversity Act were identified for their ability to strengthen CSBs. The discussion pointed to building coordination between the two laws.

Dr Devendra Kumar Yadav, director of ICAR-National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources, said community gene banks and national gene banks can be strengthened together. The release also notes that committed funding and capacity building were described as the need of the hour.

## What this means for you

The release announces no application, form, money or deadline for farmers. Those who run a seed bank or want to start one can look at the manual, atlas and policy brief at communityseedbanks.in.
