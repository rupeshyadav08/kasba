## What has happened

SSC has issued a notice for candidates of the Coast Guard Enrolled Persons Test (CGEPT) Batch 01/2027 and 02/2027 (CBE). It states that the exam will be held from 17.11.2026 to 18.11.2026. The exam city details of candidates have been made available on the examination portal.

## How to check your exam city

According to the notice, candidates should log in to the portal and check their exam city. For this, visit the SSC website ssc.gov.in and click on the "JOIN INDIAN COAST GUARD" link. You will then reach the Indian Coast Guard examination portal cgept.parikshasaarthi.in. Log in there with your registered credentials to view the details.

## When the admit card will be available

The notice says the Admission Certificate will tentatively be available for download 2-3 days before the respective exam date. Candidates have been asked to read the instructions given on the E-Admit Card and follow them strictly.

## Who needs to pay attention

This notice is only for candidates who applied for CGEPT Batch 01/2027 and 02/2027. If you are a candidate for either batch, check your exam city now and download your admit card on time when it is released. For more information, visit the SSC website.
