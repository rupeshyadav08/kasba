## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended the period of its directions on Lokapavani Mahila Sahakari Bank Niyamitha, Mandya, a cooperative bank in Karnataka. In its directive dated October 28, 2026, the RBI said the restrictions will continue beyond the close of business on October 30, 2026, up to the close of business on January 30, 2027.

## What came before

The RBI first issued directions to the bank on July 29, 2025, for a period of six months up to the close of business on January 30, 2026. The RBI said it was satisfied that, in the public interest, it was necessary to extend the operation of the directive further. The extension is subject to review.

## Who is affected

The directions affect only the customers and account holders of this cooperative bank. They have no effect on the general public or on customers of other banks. The RBI also said that all other terms and conditions of the earlier directive remain unchanged, so no new conditions have been added.

## What the RBI clarified

The RBI stated that the extension should not by itself be taken to mean that it is satisfied with the financial position of the bank.

## What to do next

If you hold an account with this bank and want to know about your money or the conditions in place, contact the bank or refer to the RBI's earlier directive. This notice does not set any new action or application date for the public.
