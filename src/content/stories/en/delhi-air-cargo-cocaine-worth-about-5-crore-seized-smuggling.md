## What happened

Officers of Air Cargo Import, Delhi Customs, foiled an attempt to smuggle cocaine through the New Delhi air cargo complex. According to the department, the action was taken on the basis of data analysis and intelligence.

The seized narcotic is estimated to be worth about ₹5 crore.

## What was found in the consignment

The consignment had come from Entebbe in Uganda and was declared as "beans cloths". After arriving, it lay unclaimed for a long time and no bill of entry was filed to clear it.

Officers then examined the consignment closely. They found raw pulses and beans along with about 1.975 kg (total weight) of cloth suspected to contain a narcotic. A separate packet of 350 g (total weight) containing a white powder was also found.

## Why cocaine is suspected

Initial examination of the white powder indicated it may contain cocaine. On that suspicion, the 1.975 kg of cloth and the 350 g packet of white powder were seized under the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985.

According to the department, the street value of the seized cocaine is about ₹5 crore, while its international market value is put at ₹10–15 crore per kg.

## What next

According to the department, the investigation is ongoing. This news has no direct effect on the daily life of ordinary people and they do not need to do anything.
