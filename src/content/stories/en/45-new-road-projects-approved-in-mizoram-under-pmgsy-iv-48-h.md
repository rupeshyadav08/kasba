## What has been approved

The Ministry of Rural Development has approved a proposal for 45 road works in Mizoram under the first batch of PMGSY-IV (financial year 2026-27). The roads have a total length of 509.54 km and an estimated cost of Rs 710.40 crore.

According to the ministry's release dated 31 October 2026, these roads will give 48 habitations in the state all-weather road connectivity for the first time.

## What PMGSY is

PMGSY, or Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana, is a central scheme to connect rural areas with roads. Its aim is to bring proper roads to villages that do not yet have good year-round connectivity. These new projects are approved under the scheme's fourth phase, PMGSY-IV.

## Who benefits

The release says that with road connectivity, people in these habitations will be able to reach schools, hospitals and rural markets. It says this will help the social and economic development of the people living there.

The government describes this as a step towards its goal of a developed India.

## Works approved in the state so far

The ministry said that apart from these new approvals, 367 road works (4,935 km) and 07 bridges have already been approved in Mizoram under PMGSY. These are stated to involve a total of Rs 3,412 crore.

## What ordinary people need to do

This approval concerns road projects in Mizoram only. Readers in UP, Bihar, MP or Delhi do not need to apply for anything or meet any date because of it.

The release does not say when construction will begin or be completed. For such details, readers can contact the Ministry of Rural Development or the concerned state department.
