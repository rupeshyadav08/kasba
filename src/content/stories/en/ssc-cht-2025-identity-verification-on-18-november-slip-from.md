## What has happened

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has issued a notice for candidates shortlisted in the First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA) of Combined Hindi Translators Examination, 2025. The Commission declared the FRTA result on 5 November 2026. Now the Sliding Process and Identity Verification (IV) for these candidates will be held on 18 November 2026.

## Where and how it will be held

The process will take place in the city of the concerned Regional Office. Eligible candidates can log in to the designated module on the Commission's official website, ssc.gov.in, from 13 November 2026, 5:00 PM onwards, and download their sliding process/identity verification slip.

## What candidates must note

According to the notice, physical presence at the designated venue on the scheduled date is strictly mandatory. A candidate who fails to attend in person will be treated as absent and will not be considered for the final result. Any request to change or re-allot the venue or date of IV will not be entertained. The Commission's decision will be final and binding.

## What to do next

Candidates whose names are in the FRTA list should download their slip from the SSC website after 5:00 PM on 13 November 2026 and reach the designated venue on 18 November 2026 on time. For more details, check the official SSC website, ssc.gov.in.
