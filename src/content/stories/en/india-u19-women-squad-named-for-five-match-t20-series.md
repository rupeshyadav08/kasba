## What has happened

BCCI's Women's Selection Committee has announced the India U19 squad for a five-match T20 series against England U19. BCCI shared the details in a media advisory dated 21 November 2026.

According to BCCI, the series will serve as preparation for the ICC Under-19 Women's T20 World Cup 2027, which is to be held in January.

## Who is in the squad

Bhavika Ahire is the captain and also the wicketkeeper, with Anaadi Tagde as vice-captain. The rest of the squad is Ira Jadhav, Nidhi Mahto, Purva Siwach, Mehek Narwase, Maitri Maniar, Ishwari Awsare, Deeya Yadav, Shreya S Chawan (wicketkeeper), Samaira Raghav, Kumari Palak, Uma Kashvi, Achsah Parmar and Palak Singh.

## When and where the matches are

The first T20 is on 26 November 2026 at 6:00 PM in Vadodara, and the second on 28 November at 1:00 PM, also in Vadodara.

The third match is on 1 December at 6:00 PM in Surat, the fourth on 3 December at 6:00 PM in Surat, and the fifth on 6 December at 1:00 PM in Surat.
