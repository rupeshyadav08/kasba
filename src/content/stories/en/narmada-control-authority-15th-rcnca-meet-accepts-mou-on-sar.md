## What happened

The 15th meeting of the Review Committee of the Narmada Control Authority (RCNCA) was held on 27 October 2026 through video conferencing. It was chaired by Union Jal Shakti Minister and RCNCA chairman C. R. Patil. Gujarat Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel, Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis, Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Mohan Yadav and Rajasthan Chief Minister Bhajan Lal Sharma took part.

## What was approved

The committee accepted the opinion of the Attorney General of India and the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) signed by the four states in New Delhi on 7 July 2026. The MoU was signed in the presence of Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah and Union Jal Shakti Minister C. R. Patil. It has been accepted as a one-time settlement of inter-state outstanding amounts, claims and financial liabilities. The meeting also approved the process for paying the outstanding amount.

## Which dispute it addresses

According to the release, accepting the MoU is a historic step and significant progress towards ending long-pending disputes among Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Rajasthan and Maharashtra over sharing the construction cost of the Sardar Sarovar project. Under it, the pending outstanding amount will be settled in one go.

## Payment for NWDT award projects

The release says cooperation was thanked for finalising, within financial year 2026-27, the process for paying the outstanding amount of NWDT award projects.

## What next

The minister said efforts are being made to resolve inter-state river water disputes. He thanked officials of the concerned states, the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation (DOWR, RD & GR) and the Narmada Control Authority (NCA) for working on the MoU and holding the 15th RCNCA meeting to confirm it.

## What it means for ordinary people

This is a financial settlement between state governments. The release gives no application, eligibility rule or deadline for citizens, so readers do not need to act. For more information, they can contact the Ministry of Jal Shakti or their state water resources department.
