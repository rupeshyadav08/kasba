## What is this campaign

Khet Bachao Abhiyan - Rabi 2026 is a nationwide campaign running from 22 October to 30 November 2026. It aims to promote soil health, balanced use of fertilisers and sustainable farming. The campaign covers 34 states and union territories, more than 700 districts and 50,000 villages, with a target of reaching about 50 lakh farmers.

## Why soil health matters

According to the notice, more than 46 per cent of India's workforce is engaged in agriculture and allied activities. Healthy soil helps raise crop yields and reduces dependence on purchased farm inputs. The campaign addresses concerns such as unbalanced use of fertilisers, declining organic matter in soil, rising cost of cultivation and degradation of natural resources.

## What happens during the campaign

Under the campaign, 4,150 teams have been formed involving KVKs and ATMAs, supported by more than 12,000 scientists and officials. The target is to hold 50,000 field demonstrations and 11,750 training sessions. Social media outreach is expected to reach 1.5 crore farmers, while overall digital outreach is planned to extend to 2.5 crore farmers. Directions have been given to prioritise 100 districts with high fertiliser consumption.

## What farmers will be told

The campaign stresses balanced nutrient management, based on the 4R principle of fertilisers — right source, right quantity, right time and right place. It also covers natural farming, integrated nutrient management, crop residue management, crop diversification, climate-resilient farming, information on government farm schemes and safe use of pesticides. There is special focus on discouraging crop residue burning in Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh.

## An example from East Champaran, Bihar

The notice says a farmer outreach programme in Pantoka panchayat of Raxaul block in East Champaran district was attended by 62 farmers, including 41 women. Scientists said dhaincha can contribute about 50-60 kg of nitrogen per hectare, equal to 110-130 kg of urea. Summer moong can contribute 25-30 kg of nitrogen per hectare, equal to 55-65 kg of urea. Farmers were encouraged to use cow dung manure, compost, bio-fertilisers and crop residues together.

## An earlier campaign was held too

According to the notice, Khet Bachao Abhiyan began on 1 June 2026 as a one-month campaign. During that period, 1,657 multidisciplinary teams were deployed and more than 1.34 lakh awareness programmes were held for 92.44 lakh farmers. There were 6,685 training sessions on green manure and bio-fertilisers, benefiting 4.94 lakh farmers. Overall, the campaign directly reached about 1.07 crore farmers and other stakeholders.

## What farmers can do

For details of campaign activities, farmers can contact their nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), agriculture department office or Kisan Sarathi. The campaign runs till 30 November 2026.
