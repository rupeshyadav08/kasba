## What has happened

The UP Police Recruitment and Promotion Board has announced the dates and rules for the driving proficiency test for selection to the post of Police Driver. According to the board, 936 eligible candidates from Constable Civil Police, Constable Armed Police and Constable PAC will take this test for selection as Police Driver.

## When and where

The test will be held from December 7, 2026 to December 30, 2026. There will be no test on Sundays and gazetted holidays. The venue is the ground of the State Disaster Response Force (SDRF), Noornagar Bhadarsa, Tehsil Sarojini Nagar, Lucknow.

## What the test involves

The driving proficiency test will have three parts. First, the garaging test: the candidate has to park the vehicle in a garage. Those who park without touching or damaging the symbolic walls or boundaries of the garage will be considered fit for driving. Those who touch the walls or boundaries or damage the garage markers will be treated as unskilled and declared unfit in the driving test. Such candidates will be out of the selection process. The test will also be videographed.

Second, the road driving test: candidates who pass the garaging test will drive on the road following traffic rules, at the prescribed speed and according to traffic signals. Those who drive correctly and safely will be considered fit; those who do not will be considered unfit.

Third, the parallel parking test: candidates who pass the garaging and road driving tests will take this test. They have to park the vehicle in a 45-foot gap between two light vehicles, without looking back while parking.

## What candidates should do

Appearing in the test is compulsory. The test date and time will be given in the admit card, which candidates will receive through their department head or office head. On the test day, candidates must reach the test ground by 8 am in the prescribed uniform so that there is no delay.

## Why this notice was issued

The board said this notice is being given for the convenience of candidates, for practice ahead of the test. The board does not recognise this notice for any other use.
