## What has happened

The Union Cabinet has approved continuing the Stand-Up India scheme for five years, till 31 March 2031. The decision was taken at a Cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. A loan guarantee fund of Rs 4,111 crore was also approved.

The aim is to encourage entrepreneurship among women, Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. The government has set a target of bringing at least 2.5 lakh more entrepreneurs under the scheme over the next five years.

## Since when has the scheme run

The scheme began in April 2016. Since then, more than 2.75 lakh women, Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs have been helped to get loans. It has now been extended by five more years.

## Who can get a loan

Under the scheme, women, Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs who want to set up new (greenfield) enterprises can take loans. Loans are available for amounts above Rs 10 lakh and up to Rs 1 crore. Borrowers can choose either a composite loan covering working capital and term loan, or a term loan alone. The loan can be up to 85 per cent of the project cost.

## Which activities are covered

Manufacturing, services, trading and agriculture-related activities fall within the scheme. This means those starting a small factory, shop, service business or a farm-linked venture can fall in these categories.

## Guarantee and fee

Loan guarantee cover applies to 85 per cent of the loan amount. The standard guarantee fee is 0.50 per cent. The government says this will make credit easier to access for these entrepreneurs and encourage new enterprises.

## What to do next

The notice does not say where or how to apply. Check with your bank branch or an official government source for details.
