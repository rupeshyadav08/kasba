## What has happened

The Department of Pensions and Pensioners' Welfare (DOPPW) held a large camp at the NDCC Convention Centre in New Delhi on 2 November 2026, with support from the State Bank of India (SBI). The camp is part of the nationwide Digital Life Certificate (DLC) Campaign-5. Pensioners and senior citizens took part in the camp.

According to the department, the campaign aims to make submitting a life certificate easier through technology. Under the campaign, the target is to issue more than two crore digital life certificates.

## What has changed

Speaking at the camp, DOPPW Secretary Nivedita Shukla Verma said face authentication has changed the way life certificates are submitted. Pensioners can now create their digital life certificate themselves on a smartphone, without going to banks or other offices.

## How SBI is helping

Under the current campaign, SBI is holding special DLC camps in 115 cities. The bank is also helping people through its branches and dedicated support teams. Special arrangements have been made at branches with a large number of pensioners.

The department said that during last year's campaign, the bank helped create about 25 lakh digital life certificates.

## Who benefits

The facility is for people who receive a pension, especially senior citizens. DOPPW Joint Secretary Dhruvjyoti Sengupta urged all pensioners to use the facilities available under the campaign. He asked them to encourage other pensioners to adopt digital services too, so that the pension system becomes more accessible, transparent and citizen-centred.

## What to do next

If you are a pensioner and want to get a digital life certificate made, you can contact your SBI branch or ask there about the camps being held under the campaign. The department has not mentioned any last date for this.
