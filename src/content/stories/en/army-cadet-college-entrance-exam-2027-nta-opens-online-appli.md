## What has happened

The National Testing Agency (NTA) issued a public notice on 6 November 2026 announcing the opening of online registration for the Common Entrance Examination for Army Cadet College (ACC) January 2027. The Ministry of Defence has entrusted the NTA with conducting this exam. It covers ACC (0+2), SCO Ser 60/Med (NT), PC (SL) and ACC (Graduate) 36 for January 2027.

## Who should apply

This notice is for candidates who want to appear for this exam. Applications are accepted only online through the NTA website https://exams.nta.nic.in/acccee/. Forms sent in any other mode will not be accepted. A candidate must submit only one application.

## Dates and fee

Online registration and application submission start on 6 November 2026 and close on 30 November 2026 at 9:00 PM. The application fee is to be paid through credit/debit card, net banking or UPI, with the last time for payment being 30 November 2026 at 5:30 PM. Candidates who miss this can apply from 1 to 3 December 2026 with an additional late fee of ₹500; the last time for payment with late fee is 3 December 2026 at 5:30 PM. As per the table in the notice, the application fee is ₹950 for General and ₹950 for SC/ST.

## What the exam will be like

The exam will be a Computer Based Test (CBT) with objective-type MCQ questions and a duration of 80 minutes. It is scheduled for 4 January 2027. The exam centre, date and shift will be indicated on the admit card. After the exam, recorded responses and provisional answer keys will be displayed on the website for challenges. The result date will be announced later on the website.

## Points to remember while applying

Candidates must follow the instructions in the Information Bulletin available on the NTA website; those not complying may be disqualified. Use the same mobile number that is registered with the Army Education Directorate while filling the form, as all information will be sent by NTA to the registered email or mobile number only. Where possible, use Aadhaar verification while completing the form. Fill in particulars carefully, as no changes will be allowed after final submission.

## Where to get help

For any difficulty, contact the helpline numbers 011-40759000 or 011-69227700, or email acccee@nta.ac.in. The notice also states that the dates given are tentative and applicants will be informed if there is any change.
