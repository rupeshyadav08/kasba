## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) on October 28, 2026 extended its directions on Sonpeth Nagari Sahakari Bank Maryadit, Sonpeth, District Parbhani, by three months. The restrictions will now run from after the close of business on October 30, 2026 to the close of business on January 30, 2027. The RBI used its powers under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to do this.

## What happened earlier

The RBI first placed the bank under directions on July 29, 2025 for six months, up to January 30, 2026. These were later extended up to October 30, 2026 by a directive dated July 22, 2026. The RBI has now said it is necessary in the public interest to extend the period further.

## Who is affected

The order directly concerns customers of this co-operative bank. The restrictions will now continue till January 30, 2027. The RBI also made clear that all other terms and conditions of the earlier directive remain unchanged, so no new condition has been added for customers by this extension.

## What the RBI cautioned

The RBI said the extension should not by itself be taken to mean that it is satisfied with the bank's financial position. The RBI stated this in plain words in its press release.

## What ordinary people should do

Ordinary people do not need to do anything new because of this order. Those who have an account in this bank can get the applicable conditions and information from the bank or from the RBI's released information.
