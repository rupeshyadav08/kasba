## What happened

Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal on 30 October 2026 chaired a high-level CEO roundtable called 'Bharat Commerce Chintan Shivir' at Vanijya Bhawan in New Delhi. The meeting was organised by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) and the Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONOC).

More than 40 leaders of major fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) companies came together at the roundtable. The main topic was strengthening India's general trade ecosystem through ONOC's DigiDukaan platform and discussing a shared roadmap for its digitisation.

## What is DigiDukaan

DigiDukaan makes kirana shopkeepers digital buyers. Through a single digital interface, retailers can search for and order products from different brands, distributors and wholesalers, with clear information on prices, trade schemes and product availability.

According to the release, the initiative is designed to strengthen existing distribution relationships rather than replace them. It seeks to reduce the complexity and cost of buying.

## How many retailers have joined

As of September 2026, DigiDukaan connected over 21,000 retailers with more than 450 brands in Hyderabad, Jaipur and Mumbai. In the previous quarter the number was over 12,000.

Hyderabad has over 16,000 retailers onboarded, where the monthly repeat ordering rate is stated at 73-78%. In an early ONOC retailer study, 40% of surveyed retailers said they kept less inventory for products bought through DigiDukaan.

## What was discussed

The meeting discussed five key areas. These include developing a shared digital identity for kirana retailers, building shared logistics and micro-warehousing infrastructure, and expanding access to formal credit through financial solutions linked to digital buying and transactions.

Participants also focused on simplifying technology integration through plug-and-play distributor management system (DMS) connectors, standardised catalogues and shared digital interfaces. They also discussed restructuring commercial incentives for FMCG companies, sales teams, distributors and channel partners.

## Who benefits

According to the release, the initiative aims to improve the competitiveness of kirana shops and supply-chain efficiency. The National Traders Welfare Board (NTWB), chaired by Sunil J. Singhi, is working with ONOC to speed up DigiDukaan adoption.

The release states no direct action or benefit for ordinary customers. The matter mainly concerns shopkeepers, distributors and companies.
