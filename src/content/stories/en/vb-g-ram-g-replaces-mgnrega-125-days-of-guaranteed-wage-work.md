## What has changed

The government has replaced Mahatma Gandhi NREGA with the new Viksit Bharat - Rozgar aur Aajivika Guarantee Mission (Rural), or VB-G RAM G. The VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 came into force on 1 July 2026 and has been rolled out across all rural areas of the country. According to the notice, the shift from NREGA to the new framework happened without disruption, because the digital systems needed for it were already running from the start.

## Who benefits

The law guarantees 125 days of wage employment in every financial year. This guarantee covers all rural households whose adult members come forward for unskilled manual work. The notice says there were 10.88 crore active workers in 2026-27, with women's participation at 61.65 percent. The average wage rate is given as Rs 300-450 per day.

## How village plans are made

A central part of the law is the Viksit Gram Panchayat Plan (VGPP). These plans are prepared with the participation of the Gram Sabha and Gram Panchayat, and the Gram Sabha gives the required recommendation and approval. The process starts with a list of existing assets and gaps in the panchayat, and then uses digital tools such as GIS, Yuktdhara and PM Gati Shakti to see where gaps remain. This decides which works get priority.

## What work is prioritised

The framework focuses on four areas: water security, basic rural infrastructure, livelihood-linked infrastructure and protection from adverse weather events. It lists 318 types of permissible works across these four areas. The plans are linked at block, district, state and national levels.

## How work and money are tracked

Attendance is recorded through e-KYC and face or biometric authentication, and work is monitored through geo-tagging and electronic measurement books. Wages are paid through DBT. Up to 24 September 2026, more than 31.69 crore person-days of work were provided and 2.83 lakh assets were created. For financial year 2026-27, states and union territories were given an interim allocation of Rs 93,841 crore, of which Rs 25,880 crore was released as the first instalment.

## Where to get help

More information is available on the Ministry of Rural Development website, vbgramg.dord.gov.in.
