## What has been approved

The Union Cabinet has cleared the extension of the Aqua Line under the Noida Metro Rail project. The extension will run from Noida Sector 51 to Greater Noida Sector 4. The approval was given at a Cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi.

## How long the line is and how many stations

The corridor will be 8.449 km long. It will have 5 stations, all elevated. According to the release, once this corridor opens, Noida and Greater Noida will have a total active metro network of 71.878 km.

## Who benefits

The release says the extension will improve public transport in Noida and Greater Noida, where better transport links are lacking at present. The new line will also connect with the Blue Line, where an interchange facility is available at Noida Sector 61.

According to the release, this phase aims to link key areas directly to the metro network. These include commercial centres such as Gaur City Mall, H and S Mall and office space, Blue Sapphire Mall and commercial space in Greater Noida Sector 4, IKEA in Noida Sector 51 and Anthurium Mall in Noida Sector 73. It also lists offices of Tech Mahindra and TCL Tech in Noida Sector 60, the Oil Industry Development Board in Noida Sector 73, and Golden Eye office and commercial space in Greater Noida Sector 4.

On education and health, the release names Delhi Public School in Noida Sector 122, Kailash Hospital, Rama Hospital in Noida Sector 70 and Sector 62, and Cloud 9 Hospital in Noida Sector 51. Among housing societies it names Cleo County Residency in Noida Sector 122 and Gaur City 1 and 2 in Greater Noida Sector 4.

## Effect on roads and the environment

The release says the metro extension is expected to reduce traffic congestion in Noida and Greater Noida. Fewer vehicles on the roads should make movement smoother, cut travel time and improve overall road safety, it says. It also states that the metro, compared with petrol- and diesel-run transport, will bring a significant reduction in carbon emissions.

## What you need to do now

The approval gives no application, registration or deadline for ordinary passengers. It is news of a clearance for the project. The release does not state when the new line, stations or service will start.
