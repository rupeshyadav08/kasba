The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment said on 5 November 2026 that more than 16,517 citizens have taken part in the online 'Nasha Mukt Yuva, Viksit Bharat' quiz. According to the Ministry, the growing participation shows people, especially the young, joining the effort to build a drug-free India.

## What the quiz is

The quiz was launched on 26 October 2026 under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan (NMBA). Its aim is to deepen awareness of the harmful effects of drug use and encourage citizens, especially the young, to contribute actively to a drug-free India. The quiz runs from 26 October to 25 November 2026 and is open to all citizens.

## Questions and time

The quiz has 20 multiple-choice questions to be answered within 10 minutes. A participation certificate is prepared for every participant who successfully submits it. After completing the quiz, participants can also see the correct answers, which helps them better understand drug-use prevention.

## How to take part

Participants can reach the quiz through the MY Bharat portal. The Ministry has urged all citizens, especially young people, to take part before 25 November 2026.

## What the campaign is

The Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan was launched on 15 August 2020. It aims to strengthen efforts to reduce demand for drugs through preventive education, awareness, treatment, rehabilitation and community participation. It began in 272 vulnerable districts and has now expanded to all districts of the country.

## The campaign's reach

According to the Ministry, the campaign has reached more than 35 crore people, including young people and women. Lakhs of activities have been held in educational institutions, and more than 755 DoSJE-supported de-addiction centres are working across the country, where lakhs of people have been treated and rehabilitated. Lakhs of drug-free pledges have been taken under the campaign, and more than 2 lakh Nasha Mukti Mitras are registered.
