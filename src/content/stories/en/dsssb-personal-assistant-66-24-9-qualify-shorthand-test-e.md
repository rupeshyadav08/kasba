## What has happened

Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) declared the result of the shorthand skill test for the post of Personal Assistant (Post Code 66/24) on 26 November 2026. The post is in Delhi State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation Ltd. (DSIIDC). According to the notice, only 09 of the 43 candidates who appeared in the test have qualified.

## How many posts and candidates

DSSSB had advertised 04 vacancies for this post through Advertisement No. A5124 dated 05.03.2024 — UR-03 and OBC-01. The online written examination was held on 26.10.2025 and 628 candidates appeared in it. Their marks have been uploaded on the OARS module, where candidates can log in to their account to view them.

Based on written exam performance, 101 candidates were shortlisted for the shorthand test. Of these, 43 appeared and 58 were absent.

## What the qualifying requirement was

As per the notice, candidates had to take 10 minutes of dictation at the speed of 100 WPM and do 40 minutes of transcription in English. Candidates were treated as qualified on this basis.

## What to do now

Candidates who qualified the shorthand test and secured at least the minimum marks fixed for calling e-dossiers must fill their e-dossier and upload documents in the OARS module. The e-dossier link will be open for 15 days, from 01.12.2026 to 15.12.2026.

The documents to be uploaded include educational certificates, category certificate, disability certificate, proof of government service or Ex-Servicemen status, and the first page of the admit card signed by the invigilator at the examination centre, as applicable.

## Points to keep in mind

The notice states clearly that the e-dossier link will be activated only for candidates in the consideration zone of selection. If a candidate fails to upload the e-dossier in time, the candidature will be rejected and no further opportunity will be given. Not receiving an SMS or email will not give any right to an extension.

If documents cannot be uploaded because of a technical glitch in the OARS module, candidates may report it with supporting evidence such as screenshots to the DSSSB helpdesk at helpdesk-dsssb@delhi.gov.in. Such complaints can be sent for 48 hours after the e-dossier period ends, and if the glitch is substantiated, an additional three days may be granted for uploading.

The notice also says that merely being asked to upload the e-dossier gives no right to selection. Final selection will be made purely on merit, provided the candidate fulfils all eligibility criteria as on the cut-off date of 17.04.2024. The result is subject to any court case pending. Candidates are advised to visit the Board's website and the OARS portal regularly.
