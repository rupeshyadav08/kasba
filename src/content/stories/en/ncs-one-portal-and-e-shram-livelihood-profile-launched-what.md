## What happened

The Ministry of Labour and Employment held a two-day chintan shivir for labour and employment ministers of states and union territories in Gandhinagar on 21-22 November 2026. On the second day, Union Minister Dr Mansukh Mandaviya launched the NCS One Portal and the e-Shram livelihood profile module.

## What is new in the NCS One Portal

The portal connects job seekers' apps, employers' apps, state employment portals, private job platforms and educational campuses. It adds new features such as a voice bot, a chat bot and the Occupational Shortage Index (OSI). OSI is a data-based tool that shows where demand for work is high and supply is short.

## What the e-Shram livelihood profile is

It records a worker's livelihood information. It also records details of five social security protections: food security, health security, life insurance, accident and disability protection, and pension security.

## Where the career lounges are

Dr Mandaviya launched eight career lounges virtually in Jaipur, Ludhiana, Bhubaneswar, Kanpur, Bengaluru, Varanasi, Hyderabad and Guwahati. These are being set up as an expansion of Model Career Centres under the NCS framework. With industry partners, 200 centres will be set up in employment offices, universities and educational institutions, mainly in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

Career lounges will offer career guidance, skill and aptitude assessment, employable training, interaction with industry, internships, apprenticeships and placement support. They will also give information on international job opportunities, global labour market trends and international skill requirements, including language preparation.

## What else happened

The minister also released two publications: an addendum to the National Classification of Occupations (NCO-2015) and a compilation of existing and emerging green occupations. The shivir also marked one year since the labour codes came into force on 21 November 2025.
