## What the notice says

In a press release issued on 23 November 2026, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said the weather will change in several parts of the country over the next few days. According to the release, dense fog is very likely over Bihar, Punjab, Chhattisgarh and Odisha on 23 November. Dense fog is also likely over East Uttar Pradesh on 23 and 24 November.

## Where else fog is likely

The release says dense fog conditions are very likely over Assam and Meghalaya during 24-25 November. Himachal Pradesh and Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram and Tripura may see similar conditions during 23-25 November. In the past 24 hours, dense fog was reported in isolated pockets of Meghalaya, Odisha, Himachal Pradesh and Chhattisgarh.

## Delhi forecast

For Delhi, the release says the sky will be mainly clear on 23 November, with maximum temperatures likely in the range of 24-26°C. From 24 to 26 November, mornings may see mist and shallow fog. Minimum temperatures during these days are likely to be between 9°C and 12°C.

## How cold it will get

According to IMD, minimum temperatures will fall gradually over Central India till 26 November, over Northwest India till 28 November and over East India till 27 November. No significant change is expected after that. The rest of the country is likely to see no significant change in minimum temperatures over the next seven days.

## What you should do

On foggy mornings, take care while travelling and drive slowly. The release also mentions rainfall and thunderstorm activity over several southern states between 23 and 29 November, while West and Central India are expected to see no significant weather this week. District-wise warnings are available on the IMD website.
