## What has been announced

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has issued Advertisement No. A-5/E-1/2026. It invites online applications for the Assistant Architect Planner Examination-2026, Sports Officer Examination-2026 and Programmer Grade-2 Examination-2026. The advertisement is dated 04/11/2026 and will be shown on the Commission's website until 11/12/2026.

## Who is it for

This advertisement is for candidates who want to appear in any of these three examinations. If you wish to apply for these posts, this is your chance. People with no connection to these posts are not directly affected.

## When and how to apply

Online applications can be made from 04/11/2026 to 04/12/2026. The last date to pay the fee is also 04/12/2026. Applications can be made online on the UPPSC official website, https://uppsc.up.nic.in.

## Corrections and fee reconciliation

Corrections to a submitted form can be made from 04/11/2026 to 11/12/2026. The last date of fee reconciliation is stated as 11/12/2026.

## Where to get more information

For full details such as the number of posts, eligibility, age limit and fee, candidates should read the advertisement on the UPPSC official website.
