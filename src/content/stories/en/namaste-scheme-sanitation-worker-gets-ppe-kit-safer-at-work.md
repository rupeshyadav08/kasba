## What happened

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment issued a release on 29 October 2026. It tells the story of a 27-year-old sanitation worker from Rajasthan, named in the release as Ramesh Kumar, which it says is a changed name. According to the release, he received a PPE kit and a safety kit under the NAMASTE scheme in 2025.

## What is the NAMASTE scheme

NAMASTE stands for National Action for Mechanised Sanitation Ecosystem. According to the release, the scheme works to protect the health, safety and dignity of sanitation workers. It focuses on increasing the availability of safety equipment, promoting better ways of working and looking after the welfare of sanitation workers.

## Ramesh's story

The release says Ramesh comes from an economically weak family and depends on daily wage work. He works as a sewer worker, a job that carries several occupational hazards. Without enough safety equipment, the risks in his daily work were higher.

The release says that despite these challenges, Ramesh kept working to improve his family's situation. Getting proper safety equipment was an important step for him.

## What changed with the safety kit

According to the release, after getting the kit Ramesh now works with more confidence. The risk of harm during work has come down. The help also strengthened his livelihood security and improved his family's standard of living.

## Who benefits

The scheme is mainly for sanitation workers and sewer workers who work in unsafe conditions. The release does not say that any other group is directly covered.

## What it means for ordinary readers

The release gives no application step, deadline or rule for the general public. It is a story showing how the scheme changed one sanitation worker's life. If you are a sanitation worker or have one in the family, you can ask your local municipal body or the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment for details about the scheme.
