## What happened

The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) issued a clarification on 19 November 2026. It said that some vehicle drivers using national highways are receiving SMS messages about vehicle insurance renewal, and these are part of a trial of a proposed public awareness initiative.

## What happens at toll plazas

NHAI has made it clear that no challan will be issued and no fine will be imposed at toll plazas based on these SMS messages. The SMS system is not linked to any enforcement or penal action at toll plazas.

## What the SMS is for

The messages are meant only to inform vehicle owners about the upcoming expiry date of their motor insurance policy. This is so they can renew their insurance on time and keep their insurance cover continuous. NHAI says that by giving advance reminders, it wants to promote responsible vehicle ownership.

## Who should pay attention

This information is for people who use national highways and are receiving SMS messages about insurance renewal. If you have received such a message, you do not need to worry about a challan or fine at a toll plaza. You may check your insurance policy's expiry date and renew it on time if you wish.
