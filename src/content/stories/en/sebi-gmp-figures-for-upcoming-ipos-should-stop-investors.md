## What SEBI said

On 23 November 2026, SEBI issued an advisory. It said that ahead of upcoming IPOs, GMP or Grey Market Premium figures are widely published on websites, digital platforms, newspapers and by some SEBI-registered intermediaries and market participants, where they are shown as a sign of the likely listing price or returns. SEBI cautioned investors that such figures are unofficial, unverified and unregulated.

## What GMP is

GMP is the supposed premium at which shares are said to be traded before an IPO. According to SEBI, these transactions take place outside the stock exchange framework. They are not under regulatory oversight, carry no settlement guarantee or investor protection, and follow no formal price-discovery process.

## What changes

SEBI said that to protect investors and keep the market transparent, the publication, display, circulation or dissemination of GMP through any mode or platform may be discontinued. All SEBI-registered intermediaries and market participants are advised not to publish, display or broadcast GMP figures on their websites, platforms or any communication channel, including email, SMS and social media.

## What investors should do

SEBI advised investors not to treat GMP as an indicator of listing price or expected returns. They should refrain from any GMP-linked transactions, as these fall entirely outside the regulatory framework. They should transact in IPOs only through SEBI-registered intermediaries and authorised platforms.

## No help in a dispute

SEBI made clear that in any dispute arising from GMP transactions, investor protection benefits under SEBI or stock exchange jurisdiction will not be available. Grievance redressal mechanisms run by stock exchanges and the Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) portal (smartodr.in) run by exchanges and depositories will also not be available.
