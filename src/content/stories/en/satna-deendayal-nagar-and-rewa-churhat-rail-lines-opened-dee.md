## What happened

The Railway Ministry on 24 October 2026 inaugurated two new rail lines in Madhya Pradesh's Vindhya region — Satna–Deendayal Nagar and Rewa–Churhat. On the occasion, a new Deendayal Nagar–Churhat–Deendayal Nagar passenger train was flagged off. The events were held at Churhat and Nagaud railway stations, with Union Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw joining by video conference and Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Dr Mohan Yadav present.

## What has changed

A new line of about 53 km from Satna to Deendayal Nagar has been built at a cost of about ₹873 crore. The project was completed with the finishing of the last 8 km between Fulwari and Deendayal Nagar. The Rewa–Churhat line, about 65 km long, was built at a cost of about ₹1,070 crore; about 22.5 km of it, from Rampur Naikin to Churhat, was completed last. Both sections are part of the Lalitpur–Singrauli rail project.

## Who benefits

People of Sidhi district get a rail link to the national network through Churhat. The notice says students will find it easier to reach educational institutions, patients to reach better medical facilities, and young people and working people to reach jobs and business opportunities. Farmers and rural traders in the Deendayal Nagar, Nagaud and Panna areas will get better options to reach larger markets.

## Effect on industry and freight

Satna and nearby areas have limestone, cement and other mineral-based industries. Better rail links are expected to make it easier to move cement, clinker and other goods, reduce dependence on road freight and open possibilities for industrial expansion. Given limestone and other minerals in the Sidhi area and prospects for the cement industry, industrial activity and freight transport are expected to get a boost.

## Tourism and jobs

The Vindhya and Bundelkhand regions are known for natural beauty and historical heritage. Better rail links are expected to make it easier to reach tourist spots in and around Panna, Khajuraho and Rewa. These include the white tiger safari, Mukundpur, Chachai and Bahuti waterfalls, Rewa fort and Govindgarh. More tourists may benefit hotels, local transport, guides and small businesses.

## What next

According to the Railway Minister, railway projects worth about ₹1 lakh crore are under way in Madhya Pradesh, including new lines, doubling, third and fourth lines and gauge conversion. Their completion will open the way for new rail services in the state. Station redevelopment work is going on at several stations, including Khajuraho.
