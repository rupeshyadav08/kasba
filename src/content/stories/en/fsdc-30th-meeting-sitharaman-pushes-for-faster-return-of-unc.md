## What happened at the meeting

Union Finance and Corporate Affairs Minister Nirmala Sitharaman chaired the 30th meeting of the Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC) in New Delhi on 23 October 2026. Members of the council were present. The council discussed issues related to the country's overall financial stability and India's preparedness to deal with them.

## Which risks were discussed

According to the release, the council noted emerging trends in the domestic and global financial situation. These included geopolitical uncertainty, the growing use of Artificial Intelligence and a sharp rise in global bond yields. The council stressed the need for vigilance and for adopting safeguards in advance to reduce possible risks to financial stability. Members decided to strengthen coordination among regulators for the wider development of the financial sector.

## Focus on cyber security

The council reviewed the cyber security capacity of India's financial sector. It focused on the Financial Sector Cyber Security Strategy (FSCSS), prepared by an inter-ministerial group of financial regulators and cyber security agencies with coordination from DEA, DFS and MeitY. The meeting also looked at India's preparedness to protect critical information infrastructure and at improving the cyber security readiness of Regional Rural Banks (RRBs).

## Push on unclaimed money

The Finance Minister asked regulators and stakeholders to speed up the process of returning unclaimed assets to their rightful owners. These assets include bank deposits, dividends, shares, post office accounts, insurance and pension funds. The council appreciated the progress made under the "Aapka Paisa, Aapka Adhikar" campaign at the district level and directed ministries and regulators to make extra efforts so that money reaches the rightful owners on time.

## Other issues discussed

The council discussed ways to implement earlier decisions and Budget announcements. These included strengthening the domestic investment ecosystem and improving the flow of resources for investment, including GIFT-IFSC; simplifying and digitalising KYC and digital onboarding for NRIs and FPIs; reforms to promote Digital Public Infrastructure with special focus on MSMEs, including Account Aggregator and UPI frameworks; and reducing intermediation costs, improving accountability of regulations and rationalising credit guarantee schemes in India.

## What next

Citing growing geopolitical tensions and global uncertainty, Sitharaman urged the council to hold FSDC meetings at regular intervals as per the schedule. The meeting was attended by RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra, secretaries of the Finance Ministry, heads of SEBI, IRDAI, IFSCA, PFRDA and IBBI, the CERT-In Director General and the FSDC Secretary.
