## What has happened

The Uttar Pradesh government has said that 438 students from the top 40 coaching centres of the Chief Minister Abhyuday Yojana cleared various competitive exams between 2024 and 2026. The Information and Public Relations Department released this information on 19 November 2026.

## What the scheme is

The Chief Minister Abhyuday Yojana runs under the Social Welfare Department. Its aim is that financial hardship should not stop a talented young person from pursuing their goals. The state has 457 centres under the scheme, where free preparation for competitive exams is provided.

## What students get at the centres

Under the scheme, students get guidance from subject experts, regular classes, study material and practice tests. According to the government, this reduces dependence on expensive private coaching and gives young people from poorer families a chance to reach good institutions and services.

## Which exams they cleared

According to the available figures, of these 438 students, 47 cleared NEET, 27 cleared JEE and 59 cleared other competitive exams. Five students were selected in the civil services category.

## Which districts did well

Lucknow centre had the highest number of selections at 27. Mirzapur had 47, Mau, Unnao and Kannauj 44 each, Varanasi 43, Barabanki 44 and Mahoba 20. Azamgarh and Gorakhpur had four students each.

## What to do next

The notice does not mention any new recruitment, application date or admission process. If you want to know about free preparation at an Abhyuday centre, contact the Social Welfare Department.
