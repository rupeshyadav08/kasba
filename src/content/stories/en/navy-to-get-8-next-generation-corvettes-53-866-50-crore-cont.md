## What has happened

The Defence Ministry has signed contracts with Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers Limited (GRSE) and Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL) to build eight Next Generation Corvettes (NGC) for the Indian Navy. The contracts are worth ₹53,866.50 crore in total. They were signed on 2 November 2026 at Kartavya Bhavan-2 in New Delhi in the presence of Defence Secretary Shri Rajesh Kumar Singh.

## Who builds how many

Under the contracts, GRSE will build five corvettes and GSL will build the remaining three. A corvette is a naval warship, generally smaller than larger vessels and used for quick deployment.

## What is special about this project

The Defence Ministry calls it a major achievement for India's indigenous warship design and building capacity. According to the ministry, this is the first project in which the ships will be designed indigenously by the Indian Navy and built by defence public sector undertakings (DPSUs) selected through a competitive process.

## What the ships will carry

The corvettes will have advanced missiles, weapons and sensor suites. According to the ministry, they will be able to carry out missions in air, surface and underwater areas. Their induction is expected to strengthen the Navy's operational capability, including protecting sea trade routes, countering possible attacks from the surface and from submarines, strengthening coastal defence, carrying out coastal warfare and doing maritime presence and surveillance missions.

## Impact on jobs and industry

The ministry says the project will give a push to India's shipbuilding ecosystem by strengthening cooperation among DPSUs, private shipyards and MSMEs. Over the next ten years, about 72 lakh person-days of employment are expected in shipyards and 460 lakh person-days in supplier industries and MSMEs.

## What it means for ordinary people

There is no application, form or deadline for the public in this contract. The news matters most to people linked to shipyards, supplier industries or MSMEs, where the ministry expects more work. For other readers, it is information about the Navy and domestic manufacturing.
