## What happened

The Union Cabinet, chaired by Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, has approved the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM VGSY) as a centrally sponsored scheme. According to the Ministry of Panchayati Raj, the scheme will be implemented from 2026-27 to 2030-31, ending with the term of the Sixteenth Finance Commission.

## Why the scheme was brought in

The government says national reforms and initiatives over the past twelve years have created opportunities to strengthen village economies and public services. To use these opportunities, villages need aware participation and local planning. The Sixteenth Finance Commission has recommended about ₹4.35 lakh crore in grants for rural local bodies during 2026-27 to 2030-31, so panchayats need the ability to bring together national priorities, government schemes, available resources and local needs.

## Cost and who benefits

The total outlay is ₹7,395.55 crore, with the Centre's share at ₹4,869.48 crore and the states' share at ₹2,526.07 crore. The scheme will directly benefit about 32 lakh elected representatives of over 2.55 lakh panchayats and traditional local bodies, along with panchayat officials and other stakeholders. Each awareness programme aims to reach at least 1 crore citizens.

## What changes in villages

Gram panchayats will be placed at the centre of local planning and coordination of development work. Help will be given to prepare 2.55 lakh Gram Panchayat Development Plans every year on the eGramSwaraj portal, combining village priorities, targeted work of departments and available resources. Support will also be given to prepare at least 2.55 lakh online audit plans each year through AuditOnline and to complete audits on time. This is meant to improve accounting, public disclosure and own-source revenue, and make panchayats more financially self-reliant.

## Where and how it will run

The scheme will apply in all states and union territories. It will also cover rural local institutions (traditional local bodies) in non-Article IX areas where panchayats do not exist. Work will be done through state governments and union territory administrations, which will submit annual action plans in line with the scheme's objectives. A designated central empowered committee will evaluate and approve these plans. No permanent posts will be created, though contractual human resources may be appointed at state, district and block level as needed.

## What it means for ordinary people

Ordinary citizens do not receive any direct money under this scheme and do not have to apply for anything. Its effect will be on how panchayats work — in preparing village plans, sharing information about government schemes and encouraging discussion in gram sabhas. For more information, readers can refer to the Ministry of Panchayati Raj release and their state's panchayati raj department.
