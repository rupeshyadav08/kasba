## What happened

The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRl) carried out two back-to-back operations in Mizoram and Assam. A total of 50 kg of methamphetamine was seized, including 30 kg of tablets (Yaba) and 20 kg of crystal methamphetamine. Four people were arrested under the NDPS Act, 1985, and three vehicles were seized.

## The first operation

On 29 October 2026, DRl officers, with operational support from the Assam Rifles, stopped a Mahindra Bolero camper on the Aizawl-Champhai road on the outskirts of Aizawl. A search found 30 brick-shaped packets of pink-orange tablets weighing 30 kg, hidden in a specially built cavity inside an office desk along with a storage cabinet. According to the notice, using furniture as cover cargo to hide drug transport is a new modus operandi.

## The second operation

On 30 October 2026, in another operation, officers stopped an Ashok Leyland truck and a Bolero camper at Dholai, about 40 km from Silchar. The truck search yielded 20 packets of crystal methamphetamine weighing 20 kg. The packets were hidden above the driver's cabin and packed in tea packets with foreign markings. A field test indicated the presence of amphetamine in the seized substance.

## Earlier seizures

The action follows two recent major DRl seizures of 232 kg and 56 kg of methamphetamine in the Northeast. According to the notice, these repeated seizures show the scale of cross-border synthetic drug trafficking.

## What it means for the public

The notice does not ask citizens to apply, pay or meet any deadline. It is information about an agency action. DRl said it is committed to disrupting organised drug trafficking networks and preventing the spread of synthetic drugs.
