## What has happened

In a notice dated 27 November 2026, the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) said that questions were repeated in the question paper of Shift-1 of the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination 2026 (Paper-1), held on 26 November 2026. Because of this, the Commission has decided to cancel the examination held in that shift.

## What happens next

The Commission will conduct a re-examination only for the candidates who appeared in that Shift-1. The re-exam will be held on 2 December 2026 (Wednesday), which means those candidates will have to take the paper again.

## New venue and admit card

According to the notice, details of the revised venue and the facility to download the Admission Certificate will be made available on the Commission's website from 28 November 2026. Candidates have been advised to visit the website regularly for further updates.

## Who needs to pay attention

This concerns candidates who appeared in Shift-1 of Paper-1 on 26 November 2026. They should download their new admit card from the website and be ready for the re-exam on 2 December 2026. The notice refers only to this shift.
