## What has happened

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has issued a notice about the Skill Test for two posts in Urdu Academy: Junior Assistant (Post Code-59/24) and Stenographer (Post Code-33/25). The test is for candidates shortlisted for these posts.

## Where and when

According to the notice, the test will be held at Netaji Subhash University of Technology (East Campus), Geeta Colony, Delhi-003. The Junior Assistant test is in Shift-I, with reporting time from 09:00 AM to 09:30 AM. The Stenographer test is in Shift-II, with reporting time from 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM.

## When the e-Admit Card is available

Candidates can download their e-Admit Card from 09.04.2026. The notice states clearly that the name of the examination centre, the date of the examination and the timing will be mentioned in the e-Admit Card, so check it before the test.

## What to do if there is a problem

If a candidate faces difficulty in downloading the e-Admit Card, they may contact the Board office in person during the dates and timings given in the notice. They can also email dsssb-secy@nic.in. The notice says no request through any other source of correspondence will be entertained.

## What candidates should keep in mind

Candidates are advised to update their email address and mobile number for future communication. The notice also asks them to visit the DSSSB website and the OARS portal regularly for further information.

## Where to read about the test

The scheme of the Skill Test is given in the General Instructions to Candidates on the DSSSB website. The notice also points to Vacancy Notice Advertisement No.05/2024 dated 05.03.2024 for Post Code-59/24 and Advertisement No.02/2025 dated 03.07.2025 for Post Code-33/25, and advises candidates to go through them.
