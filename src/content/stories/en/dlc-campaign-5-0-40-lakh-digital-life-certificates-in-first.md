## What happened

Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh launched the nationwide Digital Life Certificate (DLC) Campaign 5.0 on 6 November 2026. According to the release, about 40 lakh digital life certificates were generated in the campaign's first five days. In the same period last year, about 25 lakh certificates were made — a rise of about 60 per cent.

In the same period, the adoption of face authentication went up from 61 per cent to about 80 per cent. The minister described this as a sign of growing trust in pension services.

## Why a life certificate is needed

To keep a pension running, a pensioner has to confirm every year that they are alive — this is called a life certificate. The release says the government first relied on the traditional life certificate, then on biometric authentication through fingerprints. Now, with face recognition technology, a pensioner can generate a DLC on a mobile phone without going to a bank or office.

The minister said that of the 1.92 crore DLCs generated last year, about 1.17 crore were made through face authentication.

## Who benefits

The campaign is for central government pensioners. According to the release, the number of central government pensioners is now about 70 lakh, against about 50 lakh serving employees. Earlier the position was the opposite, when serving employees numbered about 55-60 lakh and pensioners about 50 lakh.

Pension-disbursing banks, the Railways and Defence ministries, the Department of Telecommunications, the Department of Posts, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, UIDAI, state governments and pensioners' associations are working together on the campaign.

## Where to get help

According to the release, camps are being held at about 3,000 locations to reach pensioners. Home-visit services are also arranged for elderly pensioners who cannot reach a camp.

Nivedita Shukla Verma, Secretary of the Department of Pension and Pensioners' Welfare, said print and social media, WhatsApp networks, short explainer videos and pensioners' associations are being used to spread awareness. The release also mentions pension adalats and complaint redressal through CPENGRAMS.
