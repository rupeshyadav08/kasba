The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting has issued a final reminder for startups that want to be part of the WaveX Startup Pavilion at the Waves Bazaar, held alongside the 57th International Film Festival of India (IFFI) in Goa. According to the ministry, the registration window closes on 13 November 2026.

## What the opportunity is

WaveX is the ministry's dedicated startup accelerator initiative, aimed at promoting innovation, entrepreneurship and technological progress in the country's media and entertainment and AVGC-XR ecosystem. Under it, selected startups get to display their products, technologies and innovative solutions at Waves Bazaar.

Waves Bazaar is described as a major networking hub expected to draw key stakeholders from the global media and entertainment industry. According to the notice, startups will get a chance to interact with domestic and international filmmakers, production houses, studios and investors, opening the way for collaboration and business partnerships.

## Who can apply

Registration is open only to DPIIT-registered startups working in film, media and allied technology, including animation, visual effects, gaming, comics and extended reality (AVGC-XR).

A constituted WaveX selection committee will evaluate and shortlist the applications. Registering does not by itself confirm a place; the committee's selection decides.

## How to register

Interested startups can complete their registration and book an exhibition pod on the official portal wavex.wavesbazaar.com by 13 November 2026.

## What happens next

The 57th IFFI will be held in Goa from 20 to 28 November 2026, while the Waves Bazaar startup showcase will run from 20 to 24 November 2026. IFFI is an annual Indian international film festival held in Goa and is counted among Asia's leading film events.

## What it means for ordinary readers

This notice is meant for startups; there is no application or deadline in it for the general public. Startups that fall in this category should register on the portal before 13 November 2026.
