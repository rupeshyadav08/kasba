The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment said on 23 November 2026 that 60,693 citizens have so far taken part in the online 'Nasha Mukt Yuva, Viksit Bharat Quiz' being held under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan.

## What the quiz is

The quiz is being run through the My Bharat portal and was started on 26 October 2026. According to the ministry, its aim is to deepen public understanding, especially among young people, of the harmful effects of drug use.

## What a participant has to do

The quiz has 20 multiple-choice questions that have to be answered within 10 minutes. Every participant who completes and submits the quiz is issued a participation certificate. After finishing, participants can also see the correct answers, which the ministry says helps improve their understanding of drug prevention.

## Who is being asked to join

The ministry has appealed to all citizens, especially the young, to take part before 25 November 2026. Interested people can join through the participation link given on the My Bharat portal.

## What the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan is

The campaign was launched on 15 August 2020. It aims to reduce the demand for drugs through preventive education, public awareness, treatment, rehabilitation and community participation. It began in 272 vulnerable districts and was later expanded to all districts of the country.

## Other figures from the campaign

According to the ministry, the campaign has reached more than 35.96 crore people, including 14.39 crore youth and 11.37 crore women. More than 34.36 lakh activities have been held in educational institutions. Through a network of more than 755 de-addiction centres supported by the ministry across the country, 39.91 lakh people have been treated and rehabilitated. More than 50.19 lakh people have taken a de-addiction pledge, and more than 2 lakh 'Nasha Mukti Mitra' volunteers have registered.
