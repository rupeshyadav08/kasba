## What happened at the meeting

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE) held a national review meeting with central public sector enterprises (CPSEs) at Kaushal Bhawan in New Delhi on 28 October 2026. The aim was to strengthen apprenticeship engagement in public sector companies and establish apprenticeship as a key route from learning to livelihood.

## How apprenticeships should be seen

The review stressed that apprenticeship should not be treated as an option of last resort but as a trusted and attractive path. It combines classroom learning with practical experience at the workplace, helping young people learn industry-relevant skills and become job-ready.

## What the Secretary said

MSDE Secretary Smt. Debashree Mukherjee said apprenticeship should become a main and attractive route from learning to livelihood. She said the focus should be not just on the number of apprentices but on what apprenticeship enables young people to become. Success should be measured by completion of apprenticeship, certification, employability, and joining and staying in the workplace.

## The numbers so far

According to the notice, the apprenticeship ecosystem has grown significantly under the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAP S-2). From FY 2022-23 to 22 September 2026, 44.75 lakh apprentices were engaged with the support of 47,452 active establishments. Optional trades accounted for 76.27 percent of total apprenticeship engagement.

## What CPSEs were asked to do

All CPSEs were asked to bridge the gap between apprenticeship potential and the number of apprentices actually engaged. For this, each enterprise must prepare practical hiring plans in line with the mandatory apprenticeship requirement of 2.5 percent to 15 percent. Companies were encouraged to verify their workforce and portal data, prepare a time-bound action plan for hiring, monitor its implementation, and appoint senior-level nodal officers to remove hurdles.

## What this means for ordinary people

This notice is mainly for government companies and gives no direct instruction or date for ordinary people. The meeting did say CPSEs should increase participation of women and under-represented groups, so that young people can get workplace experience, industry-relevant skills and livelihood opportunities.
