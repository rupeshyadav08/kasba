## What has been released

According to the notice, the National Testing Agency (NTA) has released the subject-wise schedule for the UGC-NET December 2026 examination. The exam will be held in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode. It will run from 4 to 8 December 2026 and on 24 December 2026.

## Which subject is on which day

The day and shift for each subject are given in Annexure-I of the notice. Candidates can check their subject code and name there to find their date. The exam has two shifts — Shift-1 from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM and Shift-2 from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

## When the exam centre will be known

The intimation of the city of the exam centre will be displayed on the NTA website 2 days before the examination. Aspirants are advised to regularly visit https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in/ for updates, instructions and notices.

## Where to get help

For any clarification, candidates may call 011-40759000 or email ugcnet@nta.ac.in.
