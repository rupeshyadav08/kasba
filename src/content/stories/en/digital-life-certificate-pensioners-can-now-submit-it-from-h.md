## What happened

The Department of Pensions and Pensioners' Welfare (DoPPW) held a mega camp on 3 November 2026 at the Sansad Marg branch in New Delhi with Punjab National Bank, under the 5th nationwide Digital Life Certificate campaign. The camp aimed to help pensioners and family pensioners submit digital life certificates through face authentication technology, and to spread awareness about digital services.

## Why a life certificate matters

To keep receiving a pension, a pensioner has to confirm from time to time that they are alive — this is called a life certificate. According to the department, a digital life certificate lets a pensioner use services without depending on others.

## What has changed

Joint Secretary Shri Dhrubajyoti Sengupta said digital life certificate services have grown considerably since they began in 2014, and the process became easier after face authentication technology started in 2021. In the last campaign, digital life certificates made through face authentication rose from 50 lakh to about 1.25 crore.

## Who benefits

The facility is for every pensioner and family pensioner. Secretary Smt. Nivedita Shukla Verma said the campaign's success depends on reaching every pensioner, including those in remote areas. She said that in the last campaign, more than 14 lakh pensioners aged 80 and above submitted their digital life certificates.

## What you should do

According to the department, pensioners can make their digital life certificate from home using a smartphone. They do not need to depend on another person or visit a bank branch. For those unable to go to camps or complete the process themselves, banks and post offices have arrangements to provide services at home.

## What else happened

The camp also held an interaction session with senior pensioners, and a medical camp was set up alongside the digital life certificate services. The department encouraged pensioners to share their experiences and inspire fellow pensioners to adopt face authentication.
