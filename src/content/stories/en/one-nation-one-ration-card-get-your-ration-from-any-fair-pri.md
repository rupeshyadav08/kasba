## What the scheme is

The One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme was launched in 2019 by the Department of Food and Public Distribution. It is a major reform in the Public Distribution System (PDS). The aim is that people holding ration cards under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) can get food grain anywhere in the country.

In simple terms, the NFSA decides who is legally entitled to subsidised food grain, and the Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS) delivers it to eligible people. ONORC adds portability to this system.

## What has changed

Earlier, people could get ration only where their ration card was registered. Now, through ONORC, beneficiaries can collect their food grain entitlement anywhere in the country. They do not need to make a new ration card for this. The facility is especially useful for migrant workers and families who live in another city or state for work.

According to the department, ONORC is implemented in all 36 states and Union Territories, and 100 per cent of PMGKAY beneficiaries are covered under it.

## How many people benefit

As per the department's figures, about 3.27 crore portability transactions take place every month through ONORC. Since its launch, more than 233 crore inter-state and intra-state portability transactions have been recorded, through which over 614 LMT of food grain has been distributed.

## Where the digital system stands

By October 2026, 100 per cent digitisation of ration cards and beneficiary databases was completed in all states and Union Territories. This covers about 78.65 crore beneficiaries, and the data is available on state transparency portals. Of the 20.51 crore ration cards recorded in the central system, 98.8 per cent are linked with Aadhaar. As of 30 September 2026, eKYC had been done for 71.52 crore beneficiaries.

## What the Mera Ration app offers

The department has launched the Mera Ration mobile app, available in 13 languages. It lets you see your monthly food grain entitlement and past transactions, confirm Aadhaar-seeding status and check eligibility for national portability. Using GPS, the app can also help you find nearby fair price shops, and you can voluntarily register for ONORC.

## Where to complain

Transparency portals, an online grievance redressal system and a toll-free helpline are running in all states and Union Territories. According to the department, 99.8 per cent of the 5.51 lakh fair price shops operate with ePOS devices.
