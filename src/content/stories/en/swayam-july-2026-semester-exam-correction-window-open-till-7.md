## What has happened

The National Testing Agency (NTA) issued a public notice on 6 November 2026. It says candidates who have filled the online application form for the SWAYAM July 2026 Semester Exam can correct details in their forms. This facility is being given only once.

## When and how the exam will be held

According to the notice, the exam will be held on 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 December 2026 at examination centres in different cities across India, in CBT mode. CBT means a computer-based test.

## The correction window

Candidates can make corrections from 6 November to 7 November 2026, up to 7:50 PM. NTA has made clear that no correction will be entertained after this under any circumstances.

## What can and cannot be changed

The mobile number and e-mail address cannot be changed. Several other details can be changed: the candidate's name, father's name, mother's name, photograph, signature, date of birth, gender, category, sub-category/PwD/PwBD status, and university and college details. All four exam city preferences can also be changed, and the option to edit the course will be available during the correction period.

## What about the fee

If a change in category, PwD/PwBD status or course affects the fee amount, the candidate will have to pay an additional fee. It can be paid through credit/debit card, net banking or UPI. The notice also states that any excess payment made will not be refunded.

## Keep in mind

The notice says that once the corrections are done and submitted, the form will be frozen and no further opportunity will be given. So make the corrections carefully.

## Where to check and whom to ask

Candidates can visit https://exams.nta.nic.in/swayam/ to verify their particulars and make corrections. For the latest updates, they should keep checking www.nta.ac.in and the link above. For any query, they can write to swayam@nta.ac.in. The notice also gives NTA's helpline number.
