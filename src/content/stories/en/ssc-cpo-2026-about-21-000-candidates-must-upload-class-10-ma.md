## What happened

In a notice dated 13 November 2026, SSC said that discrepancies have been noticed in the matriculation (Class 10) qualification details of about 21,000 candidates for Paper-I of the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination, 2026. These candidates are provisionally being permitted to appear in the examination.

## What such candidates need to do

Candidates with a discrepancy must log in to the candidate portal on ssc.gov.in and visit the City Live / Admit Card Status page for the SICPO 2026 examination to upload a copy of their Class 10 marksheet. This must be done before 20 November 2026 to avoid any inconvenience during result processing. SSC Regional Offices are also contacting such candidates by email.

## Exam dates and admit card

Paper-I is scheduled from 23 November 2026 to 28 November 2026. Candidates can view their examination city by logging in on ssc.gov.in. The Admission Certificate will tentatively be available for download about 2-3 days before the respective examination date. Detailed instructions are available in the concerned examination notices.

## What to keep in mind

If your Class 10 details are correct, you do not need to upload the marksheet. Candidates who have received an email from SSC or whose details have a discrepancy should upload the marksheet before the deadline. For more information, check the SSC website ssc.gov.in.
