## What has been released

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the category-wise list of recommended candidates for selection list year 2020 of the Combined Stenographer (Grade ‘B’/Grade-I) Limited Departmental Competitive Examination, 2019, 2020 and 2021. The list is based on the results of the written part for the year 2020, held in December 2025, and on the evaluation of service records in November 2026. The list is in order of merit.

## Which services are covered

Category IV is Private Secretary Grade of the Central Secretariat Stenographers Service. Category V is Private Secretary Grade of the Stenographers cadre of Indian Foreign Service Branch ‘B’. Category VI is Private Secretary of the Armed Forces Headquarters Stenographers Service. Category VII is Grade ‘B’/Private Secretary Grade of the Railway Board Secretariat Stenographers Service.

## Vacancies and recommended candidates

In Category IV, 01 candidate has been recommended against 334 vacancies. In Category V, 02 candidates are recommended against 43 vacancies; in Category VI, 22 against 26 vacancies; and in Category VII, 05 against 47 vacancies. In Category IX, no candidate passed the written examination.

## Some results kept in sealed cover

According to the Commission, the results of 18 candidates — 17 from Category IV and 01 from Category VI — and of other Category IV candidates who are applicants in various Original Applications (OAs) have been kept in a sealed cover.

## When will final selection happen

The Commission said final selection and declaration of results for the various categories will be made as per the guidelines issued by the Department of Personnel and Training through Office Memorandum No. 36012/16/2019-Estt (Res.) dated 12.04.2022. This will remain subject to the final outcome of certain judicial cases, including Civil Appeal No. 629/2022 in the Supreme Court (the Jarnail Singh group of cases) and OA Nos. 3801/2025, 3828/2025 and 3863/2025.

## Where to get help

Candidates can contact the Commission's call centre at 011-40303444 or 24041001 from 9 am to 8 pm for any query. The result is also available on www.upsc.gov.in. Marks and cut-off for all selection list years will be put on the website within fifteen days of the date the final results are declared.
