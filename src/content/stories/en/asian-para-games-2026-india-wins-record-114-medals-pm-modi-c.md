## What happened

The Asian Para Games 2026 were held recently. According to a release from the Prime Minister's Office, India's para-athletes won a record 114 medals at the Games. The release says that with this medal count, it is India's best-ever performance at the Games.

## What the Prime Minister said

Prime Minister Narendra Modi appreciated the para-athletes' achievement in the release dated 25 October 2026. He said this is an occasion of immense pride and joy for the entire nation. He congratulated every member of the contingent for their exceptional determination.

The Prime Minister also said that each athlete contributed to this historic achievement and inspired countless Indians with their accomplishments.

## Who is affected

The news is directly about the country's para-athletes and people connected with disability sports. Such achievements encourage young people and athletes interested in sports. For ordinary readers, there is no application, form or deadline in this notice.

## What next

The release does not mention any further process, ceremony or award. It only gives information about the success at the Games and the Prime Minister's reaction.
