## What has happened

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare said on 10 November 2026 that the new All India Institutes of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) set up under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) recorded more than 6 crore outpatient department (OPD) consultations from 2021-22 to 31 October 2026. Adding up all the years, the ministry puts the total at about 6.01 crore.

## What has changed

According to the release, 22 new AIIMS have been approved in different phases under PMSSY, and 18 of them are fully functional. These institutes offer OPD and inpatient (IPD) services along with emergency and trauma care, diagnostics, medical education and research. The stated aim is to reduce regional gaps in access to affordable and reliable specialist care.

## How patient numbers grew

The ministry gave year-wise OPD figures. Consultations were 50.03 lakh in 2021-22 and rose to 77.95 lakh in 2022-23. They were 100.52 lakh in 2023-24 and 129.98 lakh in 2024-25, reaching 144.96 lakh in 2025-26. In 2026-27, up to October 2026, 97.41 lakh OPD consultations were recorded.

## Who benefits

The direct gain is for patients who earlier had to travel to big cities for specialist tests or treatment. The release says the new AIIMS have also expanded undergraduate, postgraduate and nursing education opportunities, and that specialist clinical facilities are being developed there.

## What to do next

The release announces no new scheme, application or deadline, so readers have no form to fill. If a new AIIMS is near you and you need to be seen, get its OPD timings and available departments from the hospital itself. More information is available in the Health Ministry's release and from these hospitals.
