## What the meeting decided

The Panchayati Raj Ministry held a review meeting of the SVAMITVA scheme in New Delhi on 20 November 2026. It was addressed by Union Panchayati Raj Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh and Minister of State Prof. S. P. Singh Baghel. Ministers from nine states, ministry officials, state and union territory officials and representatives of the Survey of India took part.

The minister asked all states to prepare time-bound action plans to achieve 100% of the scheme's target by 31 March 2027. He also urged that the remaining drone surveys and the preparation and distribution of property cards be completed in mission mode.

## What the scheme is

SVAMITVA was launched on 24 April 2020. Its aim is to create clear ownership records for land and property in rural areas. Drone technology and GIS mapping are used to fix property boundaries, and rural households are given legal ownership records, known as property cards.

According to the ministry, this is helping reduce land disputes. The minister also said clear ownership records are promoting the economic empowerment of women.

## Progress so far

According to the release, drone survey is complete in 3.28 lakh of the 3.33 lakh targeted villages, or 98%. Maps for 3.17 lakh villages have been prepared and handed to states, and ground-level verification has been done in 2.69 lakh villages.

Maps were displayed for objections and claims in 2.46 lakh villages. So far, 3.47 crore property cards have been prepared for 2.20 lakh villages, which is 66% of the target.

## From card to loan

The minister said banks are now accepting SVAMITVA property cards as security for loans. This allows people to borrow against their property, start enterprises and depend less on costly informal sources of credit.

The meeting was told that 16,614 loans worth Rs 1,769.56 crore have been sanctioned in Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Mizoram, Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh.

## What comes next

The minister asked states to make the most of the high-resolution spatial data generated under the scheme, to assess solar energy potential, plan drainage networks and support local planning in gram panchayats. This would strengthen Gram Panchayat Development Plans.

Ordinary readers do not have to fill any form or apply because of this meeting. To know the status of the survey or property card in your village, contact your gram panchayat or the state revenue department.
