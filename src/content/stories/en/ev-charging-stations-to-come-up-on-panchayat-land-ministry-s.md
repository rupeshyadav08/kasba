## What happened

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj held a National Workshop on Jan Yojana Abhiyan (PPC) 2026-27 in New Delhi on 30 October 2026. More than 400 participants from across the country took part. The PPC 2026-27 booklet was released in Hindi and English at the workshop.

On the same occasion, the Ministry signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU). The agreement is for setting up electric vehicle (EV) charging and battery swapping stations on panchayat land across the country.

## Who is the EV station deal with

The MoU was signed with Convergence Energy Services Limited (CESL). CESL is a wholly owned subsidiary of Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL), which comes under the Ministry of Power.

According to the agreement, the Ministry will coordinate with state panchayati raj departments to identify suitable sites and monitor them. CESL will handle site assessment, demand aggregation, supply, installation, testing, commissioning, operation and maintenance.

## What panchayats get

According to the Ministry, this will create opportunities for own source revenue (OSR) for gram, block and district panchayats. Panchayat land can become a productive community asset. It can generate recurring income for citizen services, infrastructure and community welfare.

The step is also meant to take clean mobility infrastructure beyond urban centres. This includes suitable locations along state and national highways and in panchayat premises, for two-wheelers, three-wheelers, four-wheelers and medium and heavy vehicles.

## What is already running

The workshop discussed the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM VGSY), which the Union Cabinet has approved. Its aims include spreading awareness about government schemes, integrating central and state schemes on a common digital platform, and ensuring timely preparation of Gram Panchayat Development Plans (GPDP).

The Ministry had also recently issued the Model Panchayat Tax, Rates and Fees Rules (Model OSR Rules). These give panchayats a framework for raising revenue in a transparent way.

## What happens next

After the workshop, orientation programmes will be held at state, district and block levels. These will guide panchayats in preparing good, participatory and convergent development plans for the financial year 2027-28.

## What this means for ordinary people

This decision is not directly linked to the daily life of an ordinary citizen. Its direct effect is on panchayats, which can host EV charging stations on their land and increase their income. No application, form or deadline has been announced for ordinary people at this stage.
