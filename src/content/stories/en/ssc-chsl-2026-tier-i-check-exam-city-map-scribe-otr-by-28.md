## When and where is the exam

In its notice dated 23 November 2026, SSC said the Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2026 (Computer Based Examination) (Tier-I) is scheduled from 03.12.2026 to 31.12.2026. Candidates can view their examination city by logging in through the designated login module on the Commission's website, ssc.gov.in.

## When the Admission Certificate comes

The Admission Certificate will tentatively be available for download two or three days before the respective examination date. It can be accessed through the same login module. Detailed instructions are given in the notice published on 07.09.2026.

## What changed for own scribe users

Candidates who want the own scribe facility may need to do a fresh scribe registration on the Commission's website, because scribe registrations made before 05.11.2025 have been disposed of. Scribe registration is now integrated with Aadhaar Authentication, so everyone who wants to enrol or appear as a scribe must complete a fresh registration.

## Last date to map Scribe OTR

Candidates using an own scribe must submit the scribe's registration number so that the scribe's Entry Pass can be generated. The Commission has decided that the maximum permissible age of a person appearing as own scribe will be as per updated policy No. PP001/14/2024PP (e-10280) dated 04.11.2025. The rest of the scribe guidelines remain the same as in the examination notice.

Candidates who have opted for an own scribe can map their scribe's OTR till 28.11.2026 (11:00 PM). A Scribe Entry Pass will be generated only for candidates who successfully map their scribe's OTR.

## What to do next

Candidates appearing in the exam should log in on ssc.gov.in in time to check their exam city and watch for the Admission Certificate. Own scribe users should complete the scribe's fresh registration and OTR mapping before 28.11.2026. More details and instructions are available on the Commission's website, ssc.gov.in.
