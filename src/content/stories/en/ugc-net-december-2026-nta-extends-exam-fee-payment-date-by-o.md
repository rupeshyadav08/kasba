## What has changed

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has extended the last date for successful transaction of the examination fee for the UGC-NET December 2026 Examination by one additional day. Candidates whose application forms have already been submitted can now pay the fee till 1 November 2026, up to 11:50 PM.

Earlier, a public notice dated 27.10.2026 had extended the last date for submission of the online application form and for successful transaction of the examination fee up to 31 October 2026 (11:50 PM).

## Who gets this extension

The extension applies only to candidates who completed and submitted their online application forms but could not complete the fee payment within the stipulated timeline. NTA said the decision was taken in the interest of such candidates.

## What has not been extended

There is no extension for registration, submission of the online application form, or upload of images. These activities closed on 31 October 2026 (11:50 PM). Candidates who have not yet submitted a form cannot use this extra day.

## What the exam is for

NTA conducts the UGC-NET December 2026 Examination to determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to Ph.D. programmes in Indian universities and colleges.

## What candidates should do

NTA has advised candidates to complete the fee payment well before the revised deadline. After a successful payment, candidates should download and retain the Confirmation Page generated after payment.

All other terms and conditions mentioned in the Information Bulletin and earlier public notices remain unchanged. Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official NTA websites nta.ac.in and ugcnet.nta.nic.in for the latest updates.
