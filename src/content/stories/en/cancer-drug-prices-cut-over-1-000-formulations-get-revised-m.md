## What has happened

The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA), under the Department of Pharmaceuticals in the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, has capped trade margins on the maximum retail price (MRP) of 137 cancer drugs. The decision follows recommendations of an expert committee set up by the Directorate General of Health Services under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare on 17 October 2026. The revised prices take effect from 9 November 2026.

## How many medicines are cheaper

So far, 76 drug companies have shared revised MRPs for 1,225 formulations under 121 drugs. MRP has come down for 1,031 formulations. Prices of 600 brands fell by more than 50%, and the maximum reduction recorded was 96.45%.

## What the price cuts look like

The medicines listed include drugs used for lung, breast, prostate, blood and other cancers. For example, Bevacizumab 100 mg injection Brixsta has moved from Rs 25,995 to Rs 3,318. Abiraterone Acetate 250 mg tablet Abiron has moved from Rs 41,250 to Rs 3,218.23. Pertuzumab 420 mg injection Luphir 2 has moved from Rs 83,520 to Rs 37,358.35.

## Who benefits

The cuts directly affect the bills of cancer patients and their families. The government estimates consumers will save about Rs 3,502.31 crore a year. Of the 137 identified drugs, 10 to 15 are not yet launched in India, but the trade margin rule will apply to them once they reach the market.

## What shops and hospitals must do

Every retailer, dealer, hospital and institution must prominently display the revised MRP list of cancer drugs on its website and on its premises. State drug controllers will monitor compliance by manufacturers, dealers, hospitals and medical institutions.

## How to check prices and complain

Consumers can check applicable prices on the Pharma Sahi Dam mobile app and on the NPPA website, www.nppa.gov.in. If a rule is not followed or you are charged more than the revised price, you can complain to NPPA by email at monitoring-nppa[at]gov[dot]in or on helpline 1800-11-1255. Complaints can also be sent by post to Deputy Director, Monitoring, NPPA, 3rd Floor, YMCA Cultural Centre Building, 1 Jai Singh Marg, New Delhi-110001.
