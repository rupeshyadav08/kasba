## What has happened

The Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare has proposed an amendment to the Drugs Rules, 1945. The proposal comes through draft gazette notification G.S.R. 985(E) dated 6 November, published in the Gazette of India.

The aim is to give flexibility in the revised Schedule M requirements for three categories of products: medicinal gases, empty gelatin capsules and disinfectant liquids.

## Why this step was taken

According to the Ministry, stakeholders had sent representations raising concerns. These concerns related to G.S.R. 922(E) notified on 28 December 2023, through which certain provisions of the revised Schedule M were applied to these product categories.

The matter was discussed in detail at the 93rd meeting of the Drugs Technical Advisory Board (DTAB) held on 16 February 2026. The Board found that some requirements under the revised Schedule M may not be relevant to the manufacturing processes of these three categories. It therefore recommended that the exemptions available before the Schedule M amendment be continued for them.

## What changes

The proposal aims to make regulatory requirements suit the nature of each product category and its manufacturing process, while keeping regulatory oversight in place. It also provides for licensing authorities to take product-specific decisions based on relevant scientific and practical aspects.

The Ministry has stated that the proposal will not lower the existing standards of quality, safety or regulatory oversight. It says the aim is a practical, product-specific framework that ensures continued compliance with applicable quality requirements.

## Who is affected

The direct effect is on units manufacturing medicinal gases, empty gelatin capsules and disinfectant liquids, and on the authorities that license them. Ordinary patients or consumers do not need to do anything under this proposal.

## Where to send objections and suggestions

The Ministry has invited objections and suggestions from stakeholders and the general public. These can be sent within the period fixed from the date the draft notification was published in the official gazette.

Comments can be sent to the Under Secretary (Drugs), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, KartaVya Bhavan-1, New Delhi-110011. They can also be emailed to drugsdiv-mohfw[at]gov[dot]in. The draft gazette notification can be viewed on the e-Gazette website (egazette.gov.in).
