## What happened

The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) busted an illegal laboratory in Ghazipur, New Delhi, on 15-16 November 2026. From outside, the premises looked like a computer shop, but inside a full operation to make alprazolam was running. The raid was carried out on the basis of intelligence input.

During the search, officials found equipment needed for chemical reactions, a water chiller, a vacuum exhaust machine and other tools used in manufacturing. In other words, this was a fully working secret laboratory, not a small-time operation.

## What was seized

Officials seized 148 kg of alprazolam intermediate in solid and liquid form. In addition, about 1,046 kg of raw material and chemicals and 188 litres of additional chemicals or liquids were recovered.

Under the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985, the laboratory equipment and related material were also seized. The person running the laboratory was arrested under the same Act.

## What alprazolam is and why it matters

Alprazolam is a psychotropic substance of the benzodiazepine class. In the market it is usually sold under brand names such as Zenax and Alprax. It is a prescription medicine, mainly used to treat anxiety and panic disorders.

However, its misuse, the risk of dependence and its supply through illegal channels are a serious public health concern. Alprazolam is a notified psychotropic substance under the NDPS Act, 1985. Its unauthorised manufacture, possession, sale and distribution attract penal provisions under the Act.

## An earlier crackdown

This operation came within weeks of a DRI action on 29 October 2026, when a large secret synthetic drug-making laboratory was busted in Jalaun district of Uttar Pradesh. The Uttar Pradesh ATS provided operational help in that operation.

In the Jalaun action, about 1,100 kg of drugs and precursor chemicals and manufacturing equipment were seized, and two persons were arrested. The busting of another lab in Delhi so soon after shows the DRI's growing focus on identifying and destroying illegal drug-making sites, removing large quantities of intermediates and precursor chemicals from the illegal supply chain, and stopping the networks behind them.

## What should ordinary people do

There is no application, form or deadline for ordinary citizens in this news. One thing to keep in mind: take medicines like alprazolam only on a doctor's prescription and never buy them from unknown or suspicious sellers. If you see such medicines being sold illegally, you can inform the local police or the concerned department.
