## What has happened

The Board of High School and Intermediate Education, Uttar Pradesh (UP Board) has said it received complaints that schools uploaded wrong educational details of students in the advance registration for Class 9 and Class 11 for academic session 2026-27 (exam year 2028). To fix these errors, the board has made a portal active on its website upmsp.edu.in.

## How long the window stays open

The portal will work from 20 November 2026 till midnight 12:00 of 30 November 2026. The notice states clearly that no chance for correction will be given after this period.

## What can be corrected

According to the notice, corrections can be made in subject group, student's name, mother's or father's name, gender, medium (Hindi or English), caste, date of birth and photo. For Class 11 registration, a wrong high school roll number can also be fixed. Students' details can also be deleted or restored as per rules.

## Who makes the correction

Students do not do this themselves. The notice says the concerned principal will make these corrections by logging in to the website within the given time. So students whose details are wrong should contact their school and inform the principal.

## Where to get help

The full information is on the UP Board website upmsp.edu.in. The notice also gives the board's office and helpline numbers for queries.
