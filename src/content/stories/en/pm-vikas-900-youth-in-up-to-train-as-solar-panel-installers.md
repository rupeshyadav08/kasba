## What happened

The Ministry of Minority Affairs held a programme called “Samavesh se Vikas – Implementation Review and Stakeholder Dialogue” at the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre in New Delhi on 4 November 2026. More than 80 participants attended. The event reviewed the progress of the Pradhan Mantri Virasat Ka Samvardhan (PM VIKAS) scheme and discussed ways to improve training quality and placements.

## What PM VIKAS is

PM VIKAS is a scheme of the Ministry of Minority Affairs. It aims to give skill training to youth from minority communities so they can become job-ready. The ministry wants every trainee to complete training, get a certificate and find work.

## Which institutions signed agreements

At the event, agreements (MoUs) were signed with three institutions — Pithampur Auto Cluster (PAC), the Centre of Excellence in Maritime and Shipbuilding (CEMS) and the Institute of Entrepreneurship Development (IED). Separately, on 3 November 2026, the ministry signed an agreement with the Central University of Kashmir for the traditional skills part of the scheme.

## How many youth will be trained, and where

Under the PAC agreement, 2,400 candidates in Madhya Pradesh will be trained for roles such as electric vehicle service technician, CNC milling programmer, warehouse associate and supply chain executive (sustainability).

Under the CEMS agreement, 6,300 candidates across Karnataka, Puducherry, Maharashtra, Telangana, West Bengal, Odisha and Gujarat will be trained. Roles include business analyst, warehouse supervisor, AI technocrat and guest service executive (front office).

Under the IED partnership, 900 candidates in Uttar Pradesh will be trained as solar PV installers (Suryamitra). The aim is to prepare youth for jobs in the renewable energy sector.

In Ganderbal district of Jammu and Kashmir, 120 candidates will be trained as willow craft artisans through a 390-hour training programme. This is meant to preserve traditional craft skills.

## Focus on monitoring and better results

The event noted that the Quality Council of India (QCI) and the Indian Institute of Management, Bengaluru carry out independent monitoring. OneStep Foundation presented technology tools, including BlueDot to improve placement outcomes and voice AI to collect candidate feedback. The role of the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) in strengthening industry links was also discussed.

Neha Giri, Director in the ministry, said all project implementing agencies (PIAs) should prioritise placement outcomes, employer engagement and timely reporting of verified placement data.

## What to do next

The notice does not say when or how candidates can apply for these training programmes. Youth interested in them should watch for updates from the Ministry of Minority Affairs and their local skill centres.
