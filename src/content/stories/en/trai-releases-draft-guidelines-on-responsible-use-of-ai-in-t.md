## What has been released

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) released a draft of 'Guidelines on Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Telecom Networks and Provision of Telecom Services' on 30 October 2026. TRAI has invited comments from stakeholders on the draft.

## Why this step was taken

According to the release, the Government of India issued the 'India AI Governance Guidelines' in February 2026. Their aim is to harness AI's transformative potential for inclusive growth and global competitiveness while addressing potential risks to individuals and society. These guidelines rest on seven principles — trust is the foundation, people first, innovation over restrictions, fairness and equality, accountability, understandable design, and safety, resilience and sustainability. Part 4 of these guidelines gives industry and regulators practical guidance.

TRAI said AI is being rapidly integrated into telecom networks and services. To apply the India AI Governance Guidelines in the telecom sector in a consistent and responsible way, and to raise clarity, predictability and accountability, TRAI decided to prepare this draft after consulting stakeholders.

## Why the telecom sector matters

The release says the telecom sector is a key part of India's digital infrastructure. It helps with connectivity for the public, availability of public and essential e-services, running of trade and commerce, and the safe and smooth working of the country's critical information infrastructure. TRAI described it as vital not only for social, economic and business activity but also for national security and emergency response.

## Who is affected

The draft mainly concerns telecom companies, the technology industry and regulators. The release states no direct instruction or change for ordinary consumers.

## How to send comments

The draft is available on TRAI's website www.trai.gov.in. Stakeholders can send written comments until 27.11.2026. Comments can be sent electronically to TRAI's Advisor (Networks, Spectrum and Licensing) Shri Akhilesh Kumar Trivedi at advmn@trai.gov.in.

For any clarification or information, Shri Akhilesh Kumar Trivedi can be contacted on telephone number +91-11-20907758.
