## What happened

Uttar Pradesh agriculture, agricultural education and research minister Surya Pratap Shahi held a detailed review of departmental schemes, seed availability and fertiliser distribution at the Krishi Bhavan auditorium on 18 November 2026. Officials were told to ensure that high-quality certified seed and adequate fertiliser reach farmers on time for rabi sowing. They were also asked to speed up budget spending on departmental schemes and to continue strict action against hoarding and over-rating.

## Where seed and mini-kits stand

The rabi 2026-27 sowing target for the state is 440.47 lakh hectares, up from 439.47 lakh hectares last year, with special expansion in the area under mustard, toria, gram, pea and lentil. Against a target of distributing 42 lakh quintals of certified seed through government agencies, 9,75,942 quintals are available and 6,96,895 quintals have been distributed to farmers. To promote pulses and oilseeds, 7,37,894 rabi mini-kits are available against a target of 7,66,057, and 5,88,492 mini-kits have been distributed.

## How much fertiliser is available

The review was told that as of 18 November the state has 50.58 lakh metric tonnes of fertiliser. This is 8.34 lakh metric tonnes more than the 42.40 lakh metric tonnes available in the same period last year, and well above the November target of 32.70 lakh metric tonnes. Even after distribution, all 75 districts are said to have adequate stocks of 20.03 lakh metric tonnes of urea, 4.02 lakh metric tonnes of DAP and 5.49 lakh metric tonnes of NPK across cooperative and private channels.

## Action against hoarding

In the drive against hoarding and over-rating, 7,364 raids have been conducted and 945 samples taken so far. 694 show-cause notices were issued, 436 licences suspended and 35 cancelled. 49 shops were sealed, 30 first information reports filed and 266.60 metric tonnes of illegal stock seized.

## Spending on schemes

In financial year 2026-27, up to 18 November, Rs 6,285.98 crore was sanctioned against a budget provision of Rs 10,07.45 crore, and Rs 3,868.82 crore spent. Spending under departmental schemes was Rs 3,470.97 crore this year against Rs 2,766.94 crore last year. Payment of the Rs 4,800 crore grant to UP Power Corporation for free electricity to farmers' private tubewells has been made in full.

## What farmers should do

The department has appealed to farmers to complete 100% farmer registry according to their agricultural land. It also said fertiliser supply is adequate, so farmers should not store it in advance unnecessarily.
