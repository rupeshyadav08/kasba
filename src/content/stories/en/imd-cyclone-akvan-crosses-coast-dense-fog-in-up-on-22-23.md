## What happened

According to an IMD press release dated 20 November 2026, Cyclonic Storm Akvan crossed the north Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha coasts close to Gopalpur. The system was a Severe Cyclonic Storm a day earlier and weakened into a Cyclonic Storm at 2:30 am on 20 November.

At landfall, wind speed was 70-80 kmph, gusting to 90 kmph. At 8:30 am, the storm lay centred over south coastal Odisha and adjoining north coastal Andhra Pradesh, close to Gopalpur. IMD says it is likely to move north-northeastwards across Odisha and weaken further over the next few hours.

## Where it rained

In the past 24 hours, extremely heavy rain was recorded over Odisha, with Patrapur recording 21 cm. Srikakulam in coastal Andhra Pradesh recorded 19 cm, Kalyani in Gangetic West Bengal 9 cm and Sonamura in Tripura 8 cm.

IMD has forecast isolated very heavy rainfall over Odisha on 20 November.

## Who should take care

Bihar may see isolated to scattered rain during 20-22 November, with isolated heavy rain likely on 21 November. Dense fog is very likely over Uttar Pradesh on 22-23 November. Dense fog is also likely over Delhi, Haryana-Chandigarh and Punjab during 20-22 November.

In central India, isolated to scattered rain is likely over East Madhya Pradesh during 20-22 November.

## Advice for fishermen

Fishermen are advised not to venture into the westcentral Bay of Bengal till the night of 20 November and into the northwest Bay of Bengal till the morning of 21 November. The release says winds along the coasts will decrease gradually and sea conditions will improve.

## Where to get more information

For district-wise weather warnings, IMD has pointed to the districtWiseWarningGIS page on mausam.imd.gov.in. The fishermen warning is available on the fishermenwarning page at rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in.
