## What has happened

DSSSB has released the marks of the online examination for the post of Technical Supervisor (Radiology) and called 29 candidates to upload their e-dossiers. The post is under Post Code 24/25 in the Health and Family Welfare department.

According to the notice, the marks statements have been uploaded on the Board's website. Candidates can view their marks by logging in to their account in the OARS module on www.dsssbonline.nic.in.

## How many posts and candidates

Advertisement No. 02/25 had advertised a total of 09 vacancies for this post — UR-06, OBC-02, SC-01 and PwBD-01. The online examination was held on 02.07.2026 and a total of 141 candidates appeared.

A total of 29 candidates who obtained marks above or equal to the cut-off marks in their respective category have been provisionally shortlisted for the e-dossier. No candidate was available or qualified in PwBD Sub Cat (b).

## What to upload in the e-dossier

All 29 shortlisted candidates must log in to the e-dossier module and upload documents such as education certificates, admit cards, category certificates, marksheets, Category-Sub Category Certificate and, as applicable, Govt. servant/Ex Serviceman/PwBD/EWS certificates.

Candidates should also ensure that they fulfil all the eligibility criteria and essential qualifications for the post as on the closing date of application, 16.09.2025.

## Deadline and things to note

The e-dossier link will be active from 09.11.2026 to 23.11.2026. If a candidate fails to upload the documents during this period, the candidature will be rejected and, as per the notice, no further opportunity will be given on any ground.

In case of a technical glitch, candidates must email help-dsssb@delhi.gov.in with supporting evidence such as screenshots within 48 hours after the e-dossier submission period ends. If the glitch is substantiated, an additional period of three days will be granted to upload the documents.

The notice also states that being called for the e-dossier does not give any right to selection. Final selection will be made purely on the basis of merit, provided the candidate fulfils all the requisite eligibility conditions. The shortlisting is subject to the outcome of pending court cases.
