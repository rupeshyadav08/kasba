## What happened

The Department of Telecom's (DoT) Telecom Cyber Security Incident Response Team (T-CSIRTI) held an awareness webinar in October 2026 on cyber fraud, cyber attacks and cyber hygiene. It was part of National Cyber Security Awareness Month (NCSAM) 2026. The release was issued on 29 October 2026.

The webinar was inaugurated by Director General Telecom Anil Kumar Gupta and Additional Director General, DGT headquarters, Suresh Puri, in the presence of DDDG (T-CSIRTI) Yashwant Kumar.

## Who took part

According to the notice, more than 190 participants joined from DoT headquarters, licensed service areas (LSAs), NCA-T and NCCS. Industry stakeholders such as telecom service providers (TSPs) and internet service providers (ISPs) also attended the session.

## What was discussed

Experts from T-CSIRTI covered cyber fraud and cyber hygiene, social engineering and the cyber kill chain, types of malware and common website attacks. They also gave practical insight into how attackers work and how such threats can be detected and countered.

## What it means for ordinary users

The session highlighted awareness of phishing and fraud prevention, safe handling of links and attachments, timely software updates and quick reporting of cyber incidents. Officials stressed that as cyber threats grow in scale and sophistication, every person's vigilance matters.

## What to do

The notice gives no application, registration or deadline for the general public. For more information, readers can follow the Department of Telecom's social media handles on X, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, whose links are given in the notice.
