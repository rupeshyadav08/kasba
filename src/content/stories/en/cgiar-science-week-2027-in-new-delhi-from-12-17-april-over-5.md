## What has been announced

ICAR and CGIAR, under the Department of Agricultural Research and Education (DARE) of the Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Ministry, have announced that the CGIAR Science and Partnership Week 2027 will be held in New Delhi from 12 to 17 April 2027. According to the notice, more than 5,000 delegates are expected to take part.

## Who will take part

Researchers from around the world, farmers, government representatives, startups and industry bodies will come together at the event. Discussions will focus on innovations that are already reaching farmers, and on identifying where more effort is needed so that these reach more farmers.

## Why India is hosting

According to the notice, ICAR leads one of the largest national agricultural research systems in the world, and its scientists have worked with CGIAR for more than five decades. At present, ten CGIAR research centres work in India on crops, water, livestock, fisheries, nutrition and policy issues.

## What it aims to change

Dr M. L. Jat, Secretary of DARE and Director General of ICAR, said CGIAR and ICAR will coordinate their work more closely with India's national priorities. He said India will promote South-South cooperation and share its farm innovations with less developed countries. He referred to India's progress in climate-resilient agriculture, digital agriculture, farm mechanisation, millets (Shree Anna), natural farming and sustainable practices.

## What will be discussed

The notice says the event will also discuss climate resilience, water management, soil health, public-private partnership, startups, participation of youth and women, and the role of the media in communicating farm science and innovations. It will also look at how agricultural research and innovation can contribute to the goal of a developed India by 2047.

## Where it was held before

CGIAR's first Science Week was held in Nairobi, Kenya, in April 2025, where CGIAR launched its current research programme. Dr Ismahane Elouafi, Executive Managing Director of CGIAR, said the 2027 edition will celebrate the long partnership between India and CGIAR and also plan the next 50 years of agricultural cooperation.

## What happens next

The detailed programme and the list of speakers will be announced in the coming months.
