## What has happened

C-DAC, an autonomous scientific society under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, and Bank of India signed a memorandum of understanding on 22 October 2026. The aim is to work together on quantum financial innovation and the adoption of emerging technologies in banking.

## What the agreement sets out

The memorandum creates a framework for the two organisations to explore joint research, development, experimental implementation, technology demonstration and capacity-building in technologies that matter for the banking and financial services sector. C-DAC's strengths in high performance computing, artificial intelligence, quantum technologies, blockchain, cyber security and indigenous technology development will be paired with Bank of India's banking expertise and its large technology environment.

## Focus on quantum technology

A key goal is to develop quantum mechanisms for financial innovation, so that banking systems are prepared for the opportunities and emerging security challenges linked to quantum technologies. The two organisations will jointly explore quantum-inspired and quantum-safe technologies that can strengthen security, trust, resilience and decision-making in the banking system.

## What happens next

According to the agreement, the partners will first identify a priority-based portfolio of banking use cases. They will then show proofs of concept in a secure sandbox environment and assess technical feasibility and potential banking value. After that they will work on standardising emerging approaches against existing systems and build a roadmap for further development, pilot projects or adoption.

## Beyond quantum

The memorandum also provides a framework for cooperation in blockchain, artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies related to financial services. Possible areas include intelligent document processing, customer and employee support, fraud analysis, credit and financial analysis, secure AI platforms, responsible AI, smart contracts, digital identity and secure data sharing.

## Who benefits

The partnership will also focus on building a future-ready talent pool through training and capacity-building programmes in areas such as quantum computing, quantum security, artificial intelligence and blockchain. At the signing, MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan said that with rising cyber security concerns and emerging technological threats, building a quantum-resistant financial ecosystem is necessary.

## What ordinary people need to do

Nothing for now. This is a research and testing agreement between a bank and C-DAC, so no application, fee or deadline has been set for account holders. Any change in banking services will be communicated separately.
