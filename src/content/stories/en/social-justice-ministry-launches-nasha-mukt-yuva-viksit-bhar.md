## What has been launched

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment has launched the 'Nasha Mukt Yuva, Viksit Bharat Quiz'. The online quiz is being held under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan (NMBA). According to the ministry, the aim is to raise awareness about the harmful effects of drug use and to involve people, especially the young, in the collective effort to build a drug-free India.

## When it runs and who can join

The quiz will run from 26 October to 25 November 2026. A senior official of the ministry said any citizen of the country can take part. Participants can join through the link given on the My Bharat portal.

## What the quiz contains

The quiz will have 20 multiple-choice questions, to be answered within 10 minutes. Every participant who submits the quiz will get a participation certificate. After completing the quiz, participants can also see the correct answers, which the ministry says will help them better understand how to stay away from drugs.

## What the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan is

The campaign was launched on 15 August 2020. Its aim is to strengthen efforts to reduce the demand for drugs, using methods such as preventive education, awareness, treatment, rehabilitation and community participation. It was initially implemented in 272 vulnerable districts, but has now been extended to all districts of the country.

## How far the campaign has reached

According to the official, the campaign has so far reached more than 35 crore people, including 13 crore youth and 10 crore women. The ministry says the quiz will encourage people to make responsible choices and bring them together as 'ambassadors' who spread awareness.

## What you can do

If you want to take part, open the quiz on the My Bharat portal before 25 November 2026 and answer the 20 questions within 10 minutes. For more details, you can check the information given on the portal.
