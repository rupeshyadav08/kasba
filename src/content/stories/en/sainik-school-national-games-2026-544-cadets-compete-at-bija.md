## What is happening

The Sainik Schools Society of the Defence Ministry is holding the fourth edition of the All India Sainik Schools National Games (AISSNG) 2026. The five-day event is running from 3 to 7 November 2026 at Sainik School Bijapur, Vijayapura, in Karnataka. The notice is dated 6 November 2026.

## How many cadets and which schools

A total of 544 participants are taking part, including 296 boys and 248 girls. They represent 30 old and 13 new Sainik Schools. The teams include the winners from all eight regions that took part in regional competitions.

## Which sports are included

The championship includes basketball for junior boys and girls and volleyball for junior boys. Track and field events are being held for both boys and girls in the sub-junior and junior categories. These include the 100m, 200m and 400m sprints, the 1500m race and the 4x100m relay. Long jump is also being held in both categories. Debate and quiz competitions are part of the event as well.

## Who will attend the closing ceremony

The five-day games will close on 7 November 2026. Defence Minister of State Sanjay Seth will be chief guest at the ceremony. Navy Chief Admiral Krishna Swaminathan and M B Patil, an alumnus of Sainik School Bijapur, will also attend. Patil is Karnataka's Minister for Large and Medium Industries and Infrastructure Development. Other dignitaries, senior officials and distinguished alumni are also expected.

## About the host school

Sainik School Bijapur has completed 63 years of service in providing quality education. Its motto is "Ajeet Hai Abheet Hai". According to the notice, hosting the national games reflects the schools' effort to carry forward their traditions of discipline, harmony, sportsmanship and excellence.

## What this means for ordinary readers

The event is for Sainik School cadets, so there is nothing for the general public to apply for and no deadline to meet. Parents whose children study at a Sainik School can check with the school whether their team is taking part.
