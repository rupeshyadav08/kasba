## What happened

The National Zoological Park in New Delhi held the 22nd edition of 'Sunday Bird Walk @ NZP' on 22 November 2026 from 7:30 am to 10:30 am. Nature lovers, students, wildlife photographers and citizens interested in birdwatching took part.

## What was recorded

Guided by subject experts and volunteers, participants documented 45 bird species and more than 400 birds in the designated area of the park. Ten participants joined this walk.

## Why birds matter

Discussions during the walk highlighted that birds naturally help control insect populations and maintain ecological balance. Birds are also seen as indicators of environmental quality and the health of an ecosystem.

## What the park said

NZP Director Dr Sanjeet Kumar said citizen science initiatives like the Sunday Bird Walk connect people with nature and build skills in identifying birds. He said systematic documentation of biodiversity with public participation helps raise awareness about conservation. The programme ended with an interactive discussion and feedback session.

## How to join next time

The National Zoological Park holds the Sunday Bird Walk regularly. Advance registration is compulsory. Registration can be done every week from Monday to Friday, and participants are selected on a first-come, first-served basis. Each walk is limited to 20 to 25 participants. The registration link is available on the park's official website and the NZP Sathi mobile app.
