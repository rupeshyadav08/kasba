## What happened

Union Minister for Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal condemned repeated attacks on commercial ships, especially those involving Indian seafarers. He reviewed the situation to strengthen maritime security and rescue measures and issued directions to officials.

According to the ministry, nine ships flagged in India or linked to Indian interests are currently being tracked west of the Strait of Hormuz in the Persian Gulf region. Indian-flagged ships or ships carrying cargo for India have made a total of 86 transits through the strait.

## What changes

The minister directed the Directorate General of Maritime Administration (DGMA) and all concerned officials to further strengthen maritime security measures. These include better monitoring of activity in high-risk areas, timely sharing of security advisories, regular communication with shipping companies and ship operators, and greater awareness among Indian seafarers about possible threats and precautions.

He also asked officials to work closely with the Ministry of External Affairs, Indian diplomatic missions, maritime stakeholders and international agencies. The aim is to ensure immediate help and effective action in case of any incident involving Indian-flagged ships or Indian seafarers.

## How seafarers are tracked

The ministry's 'e-Navik' seafarer tracking module keeps watch on ships and seafarers. According to the release, it is monitoring more than 800 ships in the Persian Gulf west of the strait, while more than 200 ships are being watched in the Gulf of Oman, east of the Strait of Hormuz.

## Compensation reviewed

The minister also reviewed the status of compensation for victims and asked the Director General of Shipping to personally report progress in the matter. He also sought information on what steps are being taken to ensure timely help to victims' families.

## What it means for ordinary people

This matter does not directly touch the daily life of ordinary readers. It concerns Indian seafarers who pass through high-risk sea routes and the shipping companies that carry cargo through them. The minister said the safety, security and well-being of every Indian seafarer is the government's top priority.
