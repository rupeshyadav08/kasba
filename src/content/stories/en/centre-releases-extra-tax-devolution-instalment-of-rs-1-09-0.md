## What happened

The Finance Ministry released an additional tax devolution instalment of Rs 1,09,019 crore to state governments on 31 October 2026. This amount is in addition to the regular monthly tax devolution. It is the second additional instalment in the current financial year.

## Why this instalment was released

According to the release, the money was released keeping the coming festive season in mind. The aim is to help state governments speed up their capital expenditure and fund development and welfare-related spending.

## How much each state got

Uttar Pradesh received the largest share at Rs 19,208 crore. Bihar got Rs 10,845 crore and Madhya Pradesh got Rs 8,010 crore.

Among other states, West Bengal got Rs 7,866 crore, Maharashtra Rs 7,022 crore, Rajasthan Rs 6,460 crore, Odisha Rs 4,819 crore, Andhra Pradesh Rs 4,597 crore, Karnataka Rs 4,504 crore, Tamil Nadu Rs 4,466 crore and Gujarat Rs 4,094 crore.

## Does this affect ordinary people

The money has been given by the central government to state governments. It does not go directly into any citizen's account and no one needs to apply for it. States can use the amount for their development and welfare work.

## What next

The release does not say which works the state governments will spend this money on. For details, the concerned state government departments can be contacted.
