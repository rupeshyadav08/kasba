## What happened

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw on 1 November 2026 flagged off the Vande Bharat Sleeper Express from SMVT Bengaluru to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT), Mumbai. Union Ministers Prahlad Joshi and H. D. Kumaraswamy, Minister of State for Railways V. Somanna and Minister of State Shobha Karandlaje were present at the ceremony.

According to the Railway Ministry, the train links Bengaluru, the country's IT and startup capital, with Mumbai, its financial capital. It is an overnight sleeper service, so passengers travel through the night and reach their destination the next day.

## Timings and running days

From Bengaluru the train departs at 19:45 and reaches Mumbai at 11:55 the next day. On the return trip it leaves CSMT at 20:10 and reaches Bengaluru at 12:00 the next day. The service will run six days a week. The train from Bengaluru will not run on Monday and the train from Mumbai will not run on Tuesday.

## Stations on the route

The train will pass through stations including Anantapur, Guntakal, Raichur, Yadgir, Wadi, Kalaburagi, Solapur, Pune and Kalyan. The ministry says this will give several parts of north Karnataka better overnight rail links, areas that did not earlier have premium, world-class services.

## Part of a wider roll-out

The release says Vande Bharat Sleeper services began on the Howrah-Guwahati corridor in January 2026, and the Varanasi-New Delhi Vande Bharat Sleeper was flagged off a day before this train. The Bengaluru-Mumbai service extends that network.

## What was announced next

The Railway Minister said the next Vande Bharat Sleeper service for Karnataka will start from Bengaluru, linking the state's northern parts with the Hubballi region and going on towards Pune and Mumbai. He also said Vande Bharat services between Bengaluru and Mangaluru are expected to start in the next few weeks, after electrification work on the route is completed and trials are successful.

## Who benefits

The ministry says the service will help professionals, entrepreneurs, business travellers, students, traders and tourists. Kumaraswamy said it will also benefit the many migrant workers and their families from states such as West Bengal, Assam and Bihar who live in Bengaluru.

## What it means for readers in the north

The train runs between Bengaluru and Mumbai, so it does not add a direct service from UP, Bihar, MP or Delhi. For people who travel between these two cities, it is an extra overnight option. Check official railway information for fares, booking and stoppages.
