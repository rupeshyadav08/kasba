## What has happened

The RBI on November 6, 2026 extended the directions in force on Sri Guru Raghavendra Sahakara Bank Niyamitha, Bengaluru. The directions will continue beyond November 10, 2026, and now run till May 10, 2027. The action is taken under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

## What was in place earlier

The RBI had issued these directions to the bank on January 2, 2020, for a period of six months. The central bank said it was satisfied that, in the public interest, the period needed to be extended beyond November 10, 2026.

## Who is affected

The decision directly affects people who hold accounts in this cooperative bank, as the restrictions will stay in place for them for longer. Ordinary people outside the bank are not affected.

## What the RBI clarified

The RBI said the extension should not by itself be read to mean that it is satisfied with the bank's financial position. It also said the other terms and conditions of the directions remain unchanged.

## What to do next

If you have an account in this bank, contact the bank or check the RBI's notice for information about your account and money.
