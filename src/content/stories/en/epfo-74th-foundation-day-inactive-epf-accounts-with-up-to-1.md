## What is happening

The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) is marking its 74th Foundation Day in New Delhi. According to the release, the event is on 1 November. Union Labour and Employment, Youth Affairs and Sports Minister Dr Mansukh Mandaviya will inaugurate it.

Senior officials of the Labour and Employment Ministry, members of the Central Board of Trustees, EPFO officers and staff, employers, workers and other stakeholders will attend.

## What changes

On the occasion, Dr Mandaviya will launch key EPFO initiatives. These include automatic settlement of eligible inactive EPF accounts with a remaining balance of up to ₹1,000. In other words, such small leftover accounts will be settled on their own.

The second initiative is the launch of EPFO services through WhatsApp. The release says there are other steps too, but does not give their details.

## Who benefits

The most direct benefit goes to employees whose EPF accounts have up to ₹1,000 left and are lying inactive. Once WhatsApp services start, employees can get EPFO-related work done from their phones.

## What else is on the agenda

The minister will visit exhibition stalls at the venue and release several publications. According to the release, these reflect EPFO's commitment to knowledge creation, institutional learning, legal awareness and compassionate governance.

Future Fund Awards 2026 will also be given at the event. The awards recognise the excellent contribution of regional offices, zonal offices, individual officers and employees.

## What it means

The release says the aim of the 74th Foundation Day is to reaffirm that EPFO's journey is not just a story of institutional growth. It is a continuing commitment to making sure the fruits of a worker's years of labour turn into security, dignity and peace of mind for the worker and their family.

For ordinary readers, this is an institutional event and they do not need to do anything for it. Employees with EPF accounts can use the new services once they begin.
