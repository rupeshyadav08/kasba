## What has happened

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) has released the results of an assessment of mobile network quality in Gonda, Bahraich, Shravasti and Balrampur towns under the Uttar Pradesh East (UPE) LSA during September 2026. The exercise was carried out as an 'Independent Drive Test' (IDT) through an agency appointed by TRAI. According to TRAI, the aim is to give ordinary telecom consumers information and to encourage telecom service providers (TSPs) to improve their services.

## When and where the tests were done

The drive tests were conducted between 7 September 2026 and 10 September 2026. They included a 367.7 km city drive in Gonda, Bahraich, Shravasti and Balrampur, a 2.7 km walk test in Gonda and Balrampur towns, and a 256 km highway drive between Prayagraj and Gonda and between Gonda and Bahraich. The tests were carried out under the supervision of TRAI's Bhopal regional office.

## What was measured

The tests measured coverage gaps, that is, samples where signal strength was below the minimum set for the technology (2G/3G/4G/5G). For voice services, they looked at call setup success rate, dropped call rate, call setup time, call silence (mute) rate and speech quality. For data services, they measured download and upload throughput, latency, jitter and packet drop rate.

## Signal and call results

In Gonda town, poor signal strength was found in 818 of Airtel's 19,284 samples, 7,289 of BSNL's 16,823, 919 of RJIL's 18,995 and 1,775 of VIL's 19,266. In Bahraich town, it was 485 of Airtel's 23,477, 11,807 of BSNL's 19,472, 1,796 of RJIL's 23,226 and 1,369 of VIL's 23,442. In Balrampur town, it was 114 of Airtel's 8,605, 3,271 of BSNL's 7,994, 411 of RJIL's 8,466 and 159 of VIL's 8,666.

On dropped calls, Gonda town recorded 1/171 for Airtel, 23/162 for BSNL, 0/171 for RJIL and 0/171 for VIL. Bahraich town recorded 1/205 for Airtel, 6/159 for BSNL, 0/206 for RJIL and 1/205 for VIL. Balrampur town recorded 2/78 for Airtel, 2/72 for BSNL, 0/78 for RJIL and 0/78 for VIL.

## How data speeds performed

In Gonda town, average download speed was 94.98 Mbps for Airtel (5G), 1.15 Mbps for BSNL (4G), 167.44 Mbps for RJIL (5G) and 34.31 Mbps for VIL (5G). In Bahraich town, it was 105.94 Mbps for Airtel (5G), 7.30 Mbps for BSNL (4G), 146.74 Mbps for RJIL (5G) and 41.12 Mbps for VIL (5G). In Balrampur town, it was 130.93 Mbps for Airtel (5G), 4.09 Mbps for BSNL (4G), 167.93 Mbps for RJIL (5G) and 31.66 Mbps for VIL (4G).

## Which places were covered

In Gonda, places included Banghusra, Jhanjhari, Police Lines, Balpur Jot, Keshavapur Paharwa, Virpur, Janakinagar, Semra Damman, Tulsi Pur and Sekhapur. In Bahraich, they included Begampur, Godan Basahi, Mohammad Nagar, Bakshi Pura, Dikauli, Doob Kalan, Pachdevri, Gilaula, Khutehna, Chakujot, Jagatpur and Kamla Bhari. In Balrampur, they included Harbaspur, Bishunipur, Bishunapur, Dhusah, Benijot, Pahlawara Koiliha, Shravasti, Ikauna, Khargaura Basti, Gopalpur and Ararpakar. Hotspots included Gonda district court, Bahraich district hospital, Balrampur district hospital, Gonda government bus stand, Government Polytechnic College Gonda, and Bahraich and Balrampur railway stations.

## What it means for ordinary people

The report is for information only. Consumers do not need to fill any form or pay anything. TRAI says the findings reflect the performance of service providers in the tested area or route on the day and time of the test.
