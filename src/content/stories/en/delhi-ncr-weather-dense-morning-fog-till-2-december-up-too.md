## What the notice says

In its press release of 28 November 2026, IMD said no significant weather is likely over most parts of the country during the next week. However, dense fog may form in the morning hours in some states.

Dense fog is very likely in the morning over Delhi-NCR, Haryana-Chandigarh and Punjab till 2 December. In Uttar Pradesh and in Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram & Tripura it may last till 30 November, and in Himachal Pradesh till 3 December.

## Delhi forecast

Delhi will have a mainly clear sky on 28 November, turning partly cloudy towards the afternoon or evening, with the maximum temperature likely between 26°C and 28°C.

On 29 and 30 November, mist or shallow fog is likely at many places and moderate fog at isolated places in the morning. The maximum temperature is likely to be 25-27°C and 24-26°C respectively, with the minimum around 9-11°C. On 1 December the sky will be partly cloudy, with the maximum likely at 23-25°C and the minimum at 8-10°C.

## Where rain is likely

According to the press release, isolated heavy rainfall is likely over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry & Karaikal on 28 November. Over the Andaman & Nicobar Islands, isolated heavy rainfall is likely on 30 November and 1 December.

In the past 24 hours, heavy rainfall was reported at Adamangalam in Tiruvannamalai district of Tamil Nadu and at Urumi in Kerala. Visibility below 200 metres was reported at Amritsar in Punjab, Rourkela in Odisha, Imphal in Manipur and Barapani in Meghalaya.

## Cold and fishermen warnings

IMD said no significant cold wave or cold day conditions are likely during the next 7 days. Minimum temperatures over Maharashtra may fall by 2-4°C over the next four days.

A fishermen warning has been issued for the Bay of Bengal and the Andaman Sea between 30 November and 2 December. For more details, IMD has asked readers to check the national forecast and district-wise warnings on mausam.imd.gov.in.
