## What happened

Union Minister of State for Ayush (Independent Charge) and Minister of State for Health and Family Welfare Prataprao Jadhav gave ‘Ayush Quality Mark’ certificates to hospitals and manufacturers in New Delhi on 19 November 2026. According to the government, the aim is to ensure quality, standardisation and global trust in Ayush products and health services.

The minister said the mark stands for public trust in the safety, purity and authenticity of Ayush products and services. He stressed keeping strict standards in raw material purchase, manufacturing, testing and labelling.

## Which scheme is it linked to

The minister said the Ayush Ministry has given Ayush Excil in-principle approval to implement the ‘Ayush Quality Mark’ under the Ayush Drug Quality and Production Promotion Scheme (AOGUSY). He said this will provide an institutional framework for quality assurance and help build trust among domestic consumers and international buyers.

## Who received the certificates

Those who received certificates include Satkar Sanjeevani, Sri Sri Wellbeing, SNA Ayurvedic Nursing Home and National Ayurveda College, Hisar. Two units of Apollo Ayurveda Hospital also received the mark. For medicinal products, Adven Biotech Private Limited and Maharshi Ayurveda Limited were given the mark.

## What was said to manufacturers

The minister said global demand for traditional and herbal health products is rising. He said following WHO-GMP requirements, complying with pharmacopoeial standards, quality testing and transparent labelling will help Indian Ayush manufacturers compete better in international markets. He asked Ayush Excil to support manufacturers, especially small and medium enterprises, through technical guidance and capacity building.

## What it means for ordinary people

The notice gives no application, eligibility rule or deadline for the public. The certificates were given to hospitals and companies. As a consumer, it is useful to know that institutions carrying such a mark are being linked to stated quality standards when you choose an Ayush medicine or treatment.
