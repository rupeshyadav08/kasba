## What happened

Under Uttar Pradesh's Food Processing Industry Policy-2023, a total of 52 new investment proposals were received through the online portal. On 17 November 2026, an appraisal committee meeting was held at the Food Processing Directorate, chaired by Additional Chief Secretary B.L. Meena. The committee examined every proposal against the policy's provisions, eligibility standards and available records.

After the review, 48 proposals were recommended to be placed before the State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) for consideration, subject to compliance with prescribed conditions. The remaining 4 proposals were not recommended because shortcomings were found; they will be reconsidered as per rules once those shortcomings and formalities are completed.

## What kind of units are proposed

The recommended proposals cover new units and expansion of existing ones for bakery and biscuits, flour processing, spices, dairy and milk products, namkeen and other food products. They also include proposals for solar power plants, to increase the use of renewable energy in food processing units.

The proposals include micro and small enterprises, units run by women entrepreneurs, and units linked to Take Home Ration (THR). According to the notice, this is expected to encourage women's entrepreneurship and create local employment opportunities in rural areas.

## Progress so far

According to the notice, about Rs 600 crore of incentive money has been disbursed in the food processing sector so far and 400 projects have been completed. Two instalments have been given to 200 projects and the first instalment to 300 projects.

In 25 districts of the state, 48 investment proposals worth about Rs 4,000 crore have been selected. Proposals for setting up 35 solar power plants are also in progress to promote green energy.

## What units have been told

The committee directed the units to provide the necessary certificates, income tax and GST returns for the past two years, and other required records as per the prescribed conditions. It also stressed ensuring raw material supply through local farmers and milk producers, and using production capacity as per the set standards.

## What it means for ordinary people

This notice is not about applying to a new scheme or remembering a date. Ordinary readers do not need to do anything. The benefit may reach those who run food processing businesses or want to set up a unit, and farmers who supply raw material to such units.
