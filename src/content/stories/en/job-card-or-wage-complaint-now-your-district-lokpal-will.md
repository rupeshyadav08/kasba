The Rural Development Ministry has issued guidelines for Lokpals and appellate authorities to strengthen transparency and accountability in the implementation of the Viksit Bharat – Employment and Livelihood Mission (Rural) under the VB-Jee Ram Ji Act, 2025. The guidelines were issued on 18 November 2026.

## What has changed

According to the guidelines, Lokpals will be appointed at the district level. They will settle complaints related to job cards, employment, wage payment, delay compensation, facilities available at the worksite, muster rolls and denial of statutory rights.

The guidelines also cover matters of discrimination, harassment and violation of workers' rights, including the rights of women and weaker sections. To strengthen the institutional capacity of Lokpals, the pool of eligible candidates has been widened to include those with experience in rural development, public administration, law, education, social work and management. Provisions related to tenure and remuneration have also been amended.

## How complaints will be filed

A key feature of the guidelines is a dedicated digital platform for Lokpals. It will make it easier to register and track complaints online, keep records of proceedings, upload awards and monitor the action taken. Photographs with geo-tags and time-stamps taken during field visits will further help document and bring transparency to the complaint redressal process.

## Role in social audits

The guidelines also strengthen the role of social audits. Lokpals will attend at least three social audit public hearings or Gram Sabhas every month, so that citizens' concerns can be heard directly and followed up properly. The findings of social audits and community feedback, along with individual complaints, are described as helping identify gaps and take corrective action.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit will go to rural workers, who will now have a defined channel at the district level for complaints. According to the ministry, the aim is to keep the scheme's implementation transparent, responsive and accountable.
