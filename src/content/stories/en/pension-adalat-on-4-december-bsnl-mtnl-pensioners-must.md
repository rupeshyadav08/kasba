## What is a Pension Adalat

A Pension Adalat is a meeting where pensioners can place their complaints directly before the concerned office. The Principal Controller of Communication Accounts office in Delhi is holding one on 4 December 2026, where an attempt will be made to settle pending pension complaints on the spot.

## Who it covers

The facility is for pensioners of the Department of Telecommunications, Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited (BSNL) and Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Limited (MTNL) under Delhi Circle. They must live in Delhi-NCR and their pension records must be held by this office. Such records cover matters like issue of the Pension Payment Order (PPO), maintenance of service books and disbursement of pension.

## What complaints can be taken up

Pensioners can take part for complaints about pension fixation and payment that are currently pending. In other words, matters that have not yet been resolved can be put directly before the office.

## How to register

The last date to register a complaint is 27 November 2026. There are three ways to register. First, by email to cca.dl-dot[at]nic[dot]in. Second, on the toll-free helpline 1800118119. Third, by post to Sanchar Lekha Bhawan, Prasad Nagar, New Delhi-110005.

## What to include in the complaint

The office has asked pensioners to give certain details with the complaint. These are the name of the pensioner or family pensioner, the PPO number, the name of the unit last served in, the date of retirement or death, a detailed description of the complaint and a contact number.

## Points to note

Only registered complaints will be heard at the Pension Adalat. Complaints relating to matters pending in court will not be considered. The office says it is committed to providing pension-related services in a time-bound and effective manner.
