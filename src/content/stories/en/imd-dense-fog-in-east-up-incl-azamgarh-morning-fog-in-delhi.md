## What the release says

In a press release dated 26 November 2026, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said dense fog is very likely in isolated pockets of East Uttar Pradesh, Haryana-Chandigarh and Delhi, Punjab and West Uttar Pradesh during 26-28 November. In Himachal Pradesh the fog spell may run from 26 to 30 November.

In the past 24 hours, visibility fell to 50 m at Azamgarh in East Uttar Pradesh. At Koraput in Odisha it was 20 m.

## Delhi-NCR forecast

Delhi is expected to have a mainly clear sky on 26 November, with the maximum temperature likely in the range of 25-27°C. On 27 November, mist or shallow fog is likely at isolated places in the morning. On 28 November, mist or shallow fog is likely at a few places with moderate fog at isolated places, and on 29 November mist or shallow fog at many places with moderate fog at a few places.

Between 27 and 29 November, Delhi's maximum temperature is likely to be 24-27°C and the minimum 08-11°C.

## Where the rain is

The release says isolated heavy rainfall is likely over Kerala and Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal on 26-27 November. Isolated heavy rainfall is also likely over Andaman & Nicobar Islands during 30 November-1 December. No significant weather is likely over West and Central India during the week.

## What you can do

If you travel early in the morning in a fog-hit area, drive slowly and keep a safe distance from the vehicle ahead. IMD points readers to its district-wise multi-hazard warning page for warnings over the next five days; the link is in the release.
