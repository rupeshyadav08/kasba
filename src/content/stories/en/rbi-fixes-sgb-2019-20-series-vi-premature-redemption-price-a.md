## What has happened

The RBI issued a press release on 29 October 2026 announcing the premature redemption price for SGB 2019-20 Series VI. The price is fixed at ₹14,213 per unit of SGB.

## Which series is this about

This series was issued on 30 October 2019. According to the government notification dated 30 September 2019, a Sovereign Gold Bond can be redeemed prematurely after the fifth year from the issue date, on a date when interest is payable. For this series, the next such date is 30 October 2026.

## How the price was worked out

According to the RBI, the redemption price of an SGB is based on the simple average of the closing price of 999 purity gold for the three business days before the redemption date, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA). This time those three days are 27, 28 and 29 October 2026.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit goes to investors who bought this series and choose premature redemption on 30 October 2026. They will get ₹14,213 for each unit. People who did not invest in this series are not affected by this announcement.

## What to do next

If you are an investor in this series and want to redeem early, check the process with your bank or the institution you bought the bond from. More details are available in the RBI press release on its website.
