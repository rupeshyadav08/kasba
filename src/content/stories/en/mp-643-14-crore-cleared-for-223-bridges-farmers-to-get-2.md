## What happened

According to a release from the Rural Development Ministry, Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare and Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan held a meeting with Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Mohan Yadav. The meeting reviewed proposals on farm development and rural infrastructure in the state. State ministers Prahlad Singh Patel and Edal Singh Kansana and senior officials were also present.

## What was decided on bridges and roads

The release says the Centre has approved ₹643.14 crore for building 223 bridges in Madhya Pradesh, described as a step to strengthen rural connectivity. Road construction proposals worth about ₹600 crore were also presented and discussed at the meeting. The release does not say in which districts or villages these bridges and roads will come up.

## What was announced for farmers

Chouhan said a special scientific agriculture model will be prepared for Madhya Pradesh with the help of agriculture scientists, to raise farm productivity and promote long-term farming practices. To encourage modern farming and add value to farm produce, the state will be allotted two agriculture clusters and two centres of excellence.

Special nurseries will also be developed in the state under the Clean Plant Programme, so that farmers can get disease-free, high-quality planting material.

## What was said on irrigation

The meeting discussed in detail providing wide support under the Per Drop More Crop (PDMC) scheme, which is about micro-irrigation, or growing more with less water. Other irrigation proposals put forward by the state were also discussed to find effective solutions.

## What should ordinary people do

The release gives no application, form or deadline for the public. It reports decisions taken at a meeting. Details of where and when the bridges, roads, agriculture clusters, centres of excellence and nurseries will come up will be known only when the concerned departments share them.
