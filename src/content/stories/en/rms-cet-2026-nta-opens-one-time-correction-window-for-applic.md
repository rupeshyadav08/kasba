## What has happened

The National Testing Agency (NTA) issued a public notice on 27 October 2026. It says candidates will get a chance to correct certain particulars in the online application form for the Rashtriya Military School Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026. The decision was taken after the NTA received representations from candidates asking for this opportunity.

## Who is covered

The facility is for all candidates registered for RMS CET 2026. They are advised to visit https://exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet/ and verify their particulars, and make corrections if needed.

## Dates and time

Corrections can be made from 27 October 2026 to 28 October 2026, up to 4:50 PM. The NTA has made clear that no correction will be entertained after this under any circumstances. This is a one-time facility, so candidates should correct carefully. Once corrections are submitted, the form will be frozen.

## What can and cannot be changed

According to the Annexure attached to the notice, candidates cannot change their mobile number, e-mail address, permanent and present address, emergency contact details or photograph.

Among the candidate name, father's name and mother's name, only one field can be changed. Candidates can change class 5 and class 8 equivalent details, examination city selection, date of birth, gender, category, signature, thumb impression, domicile certificate, birth certificate and Aadhaar details. Aadhaar details can be changed only for candidates who registered with an identity other than Aadhaar.

Examination cities can be changed based on permanent and present addresses. The notice also states that the NTA is not bound to follow the choices given by the candidate.

## What about the fee

If a correction has an impact on the fee, the additional fee will be charged as applicable. It can be paid through credit card, debit card, net banking or UPI. The notice also says the fee will not be reimbursed in any case.

## Where to get help

For queries, candidates can call the helpline numbers 0-40759000 or 0-69227700, or write to rmscet@nta.gov.in. Candidates are advised to regularly check the official NTA website for the latest updates.
