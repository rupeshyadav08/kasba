The Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare released the National Health Accounts (NHA) 2023-24 estimates on 15 November 2026. According to the ministry, government health spending has tripled over the past decade while the share families pay from their own pockets has fallen sharply.

## What has changed

The ministry says government health expenditure rose from Rs 1,29,778 crore in 2013-14 to Rs 4,14,196 crore in 2023-24. Over the same period, the share of out-of-pocket expenditure (OOPE) in total health spending fell from 64.2% to 42.7%, a drop of 21.5 percentage points. The report says the decline continued from 43.4% in 2022-23 to 42.7% in 2023-24.

## Per person and primary care spending

According to the report, per capita government health expenditure rose from Rs 1,042 in 2013-14 to Rs 2,959 in 2023-24. Government spending on primary health care reached about Rs 1.53 lakh crore. The ministry links this investment to the expansion of Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, which offer services such as preventive care, screening for non-communicable diseases, essential medicines and diagnostics closer to communities.

## Insurance and social security

Social security spending on health rose from 6% of total health expenditure in 2013-14 to 11% in 2023-24. The report lists Ayushman Bharat-Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana, state-specific health insurance schemes, social health insurance and medical expenditure for government employees among these arrangements. Private health insurance spending also rose from 3.4% to 10.2%.

## Who benefits

The report says the shift matters most for families that pay directly for treatment, medicines and tests. It mentions free drugs and diagnostics initiatives, the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme, Jan Aushadhi and AMRIT pharmacies, and cashless hospitalisation benefits under Ayushman Bharat as steps supporting affordable care.

## What to do next

The report gives no application, registration or deadline for ordinary people. Those who want information on schemes can ask at their nearest government hospital or Ayushman Arogya Mandir. The full estimates can be seen at nhsrcindia.org/national-health-accounts-records, as the ministry states.
