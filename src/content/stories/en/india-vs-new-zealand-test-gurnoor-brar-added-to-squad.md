## What has changed

BCCI's Men's Selection Committee has added Gurnoor Brar to the Indian squad for the two-match Test series against New Zealand. It has also released Kuldeep Yadav from the Test squad so he can play the ongoing domestic cricket in India. The information is from BCCI's media advisory dated 14 November 2026.

The series starts on 19 November 2026 in Wellington.

## What else changed in the squad

According to BCCI, wicketkeeper-batter Rishabh Pant has joined the members of the Test squad in Wellington. Gurnoor Brar will now link up with the squad as well.

Since Gurnoor Brar is with the Test squad, he will not be available for the fifth ODI. BCCI made clear that there will be no replacement for the final ODI.

## The updated Test squad

Captained by Shubman Gill, the squad is: Yashasvi Jaiswal, KL Rahul (vice-captain), Devdutt Padikkal, Rishabh Pant (wicketkeeper), Dhruv Jurel (wicketkeeper), Ravindra Jadeja, Anshul Kamboj, Jasprit Bumrah, Prasidh Krishna, Akash Deep, Sai Sudharsan, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Washington Sundar, Mohammed Siraj and Gurnoor Brar.

## The fifth ODI squad

For the fifth ODI, India's squad is: Shreyas Iyer (captain), Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, Tilak Varma, Ishan Kishan (wicketkeeper, vice-captain), Axar Patel, Naman Dhir, Auqib Nabi, Ruturaj Gaikwad, Arshdeep Singh, Kumar Kushagra (wicketkeeper), Mohammed Siraj, Yash Thakur and Kuldeep Yadav.

## What this means for ordinary readers

This is only a team announcement. There is nothing for the public to apply for and no deadline to meet. Fans who follow Indian cricket can now see who is in the Test squad and who is returning home for domestic cricket.
