## What has been launched

The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is running a digital initiative called Shatayu, short for Comprehensive Care Support and Training for Senior Citizens. It has been developed as a centralised national elderly caregiver dashboard and directory.

According to the ministry, the initiative was launched on 22 May 2026 during a national workshop on the theme of building a well-organised care economy. Its aim is to reduce the distance between professionally trained caregivers and senior citizens, their families and institutions that need special care services.

## What the directory holds

A ministry official said the portal's national directory currently has details of 30,151 caregivers. This has created a digital database of care professionals working across states and union territories.

Using the directory, a person or family can search for caregivers by their geographical area, including state/UT and district. This makes it easier to identify the care services available locally.

## Who benefits

According to the official, the portal includes digital features such as OTP-based verification of callback requests, viewing caregiver profiles, tracking registration status, and feedback and ratings. These make communication easier between caregivers and those looking for services.

With the 'request' feature, users can choose their state/UT and district, describe the type of care needed and enter their contact details through an OTP-based verification process. The request is then made available to caregivers in the selected district. The facility covers both full-time and hourly care needs.

## What care services are covered

The official said the services include general old-age care, post-surgery care, care for people with dementia and Alzheimer's, physiotherapy and mobility support, palliative care and other needs related to the care of the elderly. These also cover help with daily activities, rehabilitation and services for senior citizens who need special care.

## How caregivers can register

The official said eligible professionals can register on the platform by submitting their personal information, address details, course qualifications and training certificates. Applicants can also check their registration status through the dashboard. After verification and approval, the details of eligible caregivers are listed on the platform, making it easier for prospective employers and families to contact them directly.

## Training and support

According to the official, four broad categories of professional training have been laid down: elderly care provider, elderly caregiver (institutional and home care), wellness therapist (senior citizens) and elderly care associate. These programmes train people in skills such as overall care of the elderly, monitoring physical health, handling emergencies, bed mobility support, help with activities of daily living, therapeutic rehabilitation, participation in cognitive activities, emotional support and social inclusion.

The training framework has the support of the National Institute of Social Defence under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and the National Skill Development Corporation under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.

## Where to find the portal

The Shatayu portal can be accessed at https://shatayu.dosje.gov.in/. Searching the caregiver directory and filing a callback request are completely free.
