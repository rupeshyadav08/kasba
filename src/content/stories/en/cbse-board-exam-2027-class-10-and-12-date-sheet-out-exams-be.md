## What has been released

CBSE has released the date sheet for the Class 10 and Class 12 Board Examinations 2027. According to the Board, the exams will begin on 16 February 2027 (Tuesday). The date sheet can be viewed and downloaded from the official CBSE website, www.cbse.gov.in.

## How the date sheet was prepared

CBSE said the date sheet was prepared using the data schools submitted in the List of Candidates (LOC). The LOC process is complete, so the Board has the final information on the subject combinations students have chosen. According to the notice, care was taken that examinations of any two subjects opted by a student do not fall on the same date. More than 40,000 subject combinations were factored in for this.

## What was kept in mind

The Board said adequate gaps have been provided between examinations of subjects commonly offered together. The tentative schedules of major entrance examinations for Class 12 students were also taken into account, so that board exams finish well before such entrance exams. The number of candidates appearing at each examination centre on a particular day was also considered to help conduct exams smoothly.

## How many students will appear

According to the notice, over 26.5 lakh students in Class 10 and over 9.8 lakh students in Class 12 have registered for the 2027 Board Examinations. Students will appear in 83 subjects in Class 10 and 120 subjects in Class 12. All examinations will commence at 10:30 a.m. (IST).

## Why it was released early

CBSE said the date sheet is being released 100 days in advance of the exams. This gives students time to plan and organise their preparation and can reduce exam-related anxiety. Families and teachers can plan their summer vacation engagements around the examination and evaluation schedules, and schools designated as examination centres get time to make arrangements.

## What to do now

Students appearing in the 2027 board exams should check the date sheet on the website, note the dates for their subjects and plan their study schedule accordingly. There is no form or application to submit at this stage.
