## What happened

The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment (DoSJE), under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, and the Directorate General of Defence Estates (DGDE) under the Defence Ministry signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) in Delhi on 16 November 2026. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment Dr. Virendra Kumar were present.

The aim is to strengthen the care and overall welfare of senior citizens living in cantonment areas.

## What changes

Under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY), new day-care centres will be set up in cantonment areas. Where possible, the plan is for new day-care centres and senior citizen homes with a residential capacity of 25 or 50 beds. Existing facilities run by cantonment boards will also be supported and strengthened.

Youth in cantonment areas will be trained as geriatric caregivers. The partnership will also focus on awareness, skill development and bringing generations closer together.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit goes to elderly people living in cantonment areas, who may get care and support close to their homes. Young people in these areas can gain caregiver training, which may open up work opportunities.

The release notes that the ministry supports initiatives under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana, including senior citizen homes, day-care centres, caregiver training and the national helpline for senior citizens, Elderline (14567).

## What each ministry will do

The Directorate General of Defence Estates will help provide infrastructure, premises and essential civic amenities. The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment will provide financial support, technical guidance, training and operational support. A joint working group will be formed to monitor implementation and coordinate the work. The MoU is valid for five years and can be renewed.

## What is Vayo Saathi

At the event, Dr. Virendra Kumar appealed to citizens to register as Vayo Saathi volunteers. He said the initiative, launched on 4 November 2026 along with the Vayo Samman national campaign, encourages people to spend time with senior citizens, help with their daily needs and assist them in getting welfare schemes. Citizens aged 18 and above can join as volunteers.

## What next

The release does not say which cantonment areas will get the new centres or when they will start. It also does not say where or how volunteers can register for Vayo Saathi.
