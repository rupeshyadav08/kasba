## What has happened

Union Health and Family Welfare Minister Jagat Prakash Nadda dedicated an advanced radiotherapy facility at AIIMS Rajkot on 2 November 2026. The facility has been set up under the Pradhan Mantri Health Security Yojana at a cost of about Rs 39 crore.

Radiotherapy is a way of treating cancer in which radiation is used on the affected part of the body.

## Who will benefit

According to the notice, the facility is expected to benefit about 1.54 crore people in the Saurashtra region. This includes the districts of Rajkot, Jamnagar, Junagadh, Porbandar, Amreli, Bhavnagar, Morbi, Surendranagar, Gir Somnath, Devbhumi Dwarka and Botad.

The minister said people in this region earlier had to travel to distant cities such as Ahmedabad and Mumbai for specialised treatment. The facility will now be available in Rajkot itself.

## What will treatment cost

At AIIMS Rajkot, eligible beneficiaries of the Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana will get free cancer treatment. Patients not covered by the scheme will be treated at concessional rates so that the financial burden on them is lower.

## What machines and techniques are in place

The facility has a high energy linear accelerator (linac), a wide bore CT simulator and a high dose rate (HDR) brachytherapy unit. The linac will allow radiation treatment using modern techniques such as IMRT, VMAT, IGRT, SRS, SRT and SBRT, which deliver radiation to the affected area with millimetre-level precision.

The wide bore CT simulator will help prepare a three-dimensional plan for treatment. HDR brachytherapy will allow targeted internal radiotherapy for gynaecological cancers, head and neck cancers, sarcoma and other tumours.

## Since when is the department running

AIIMS Rajkot's radiation oncology department has been working since 29 May 2026 and provides radiotherapy and chemotherapy services. It works as part of an integrated cancer care setup supported by chemotherapy and facilities for head and neck, orthopaedic, gynaecological and general surgery.

Initially, about 500 to 700 patients are expected to be treated there every year. These will include patients with head and neck, gynaecological, breast, bone and soft tissue cancers, among others.

## What else was said

The minister referred to screening drives being run through Ayushman Arogya Mandirs. According to the notice, since 2018 about 37.7 crore people have been screened for oral cancer, and about 2.5 lakh patients were detected. About 17.7 crore women were screened for breast cancer, with about 95,000 cases found, and about 9.8 crore women were screened for cervical cancer, with about one lakh patients detected.

Health Secretary Punya Salila Srivastava referred to the National Health Policy 2017, more than 1.8 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandirs and steps to make cancer medicines cheaper.
