## What has happened

The Union Cabinet has approved continuing the Stand-Up India scheme for another five years, till 31 March 2031. The decision was taken at a Cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship among women, Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

## What the scheme is

Stand-Up India was launched in April 2016. Under it, women, Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs are helped to get bank loans to set up new businesses. According to the release, more than 2.75 lakh such entrepreneurs have received loan support so far. The government now aims to bring 2.5 lakh more entrepreneurs under the scheme over the next five years.

## How much loan is available

The scheme offers loans of over Rs 10 lakh and up to Rs 1 crore. Borrowers can take a composite loan covering both working capital and term loan, or choose only a term loan. The loan can be up to 85 percent of the project cost.

## Who benefits

The scheme is for women, Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs who want to set up a new (greenfield) enterprise. Eligible sectors include manufacturing, services, trading and agriculture-related activities. The release says this will make credit easier to access for these groups and help new enterprises come up.

## About the guarantee

The scheme provides a credit guarantee cover of 85 percent of the loan amount, meaning that share is guaranteed if the loan is not repaid. A standard guarantee fee of 0.50 percent applies. The Cabinet also approved a credit guarantee fund of Rs 4,111 crore.

## What to do next

If you want to use the scheme, you can contact your bank. The release does not state an application date, process or list of documents, so get the full details from the bank.
