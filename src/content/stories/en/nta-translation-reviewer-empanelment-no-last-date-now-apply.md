## What has happened

The National Testing Agency (NTA) issued a corrigendum on 7 November 2026. It says applications for empanelment as Translation Reviewers will now be accepted on a continuous basis. The earlier notice had a fixed application period; now there will be no closing date.

The corrigendum follows the Expression of Interest (EoI) issued on 10 August 2026, in which NTA invited applications for Translation Reviewers. An earlier corrigendum had come on 8 September 2026.

## What the role involves

NTA says translation will now happen in two parts. Most of the translation will be done by an AI-based translation platform deployed on NTA's premises. After that, the empanelled experts, the Translation Reviewers, will check whether the translation is accurate. They will mark each translated item as 'accepted', 'accepted with corrections' or 'rejected with reasons'.

According to NTA, a reviewer will not see the assembled paper. Items will be shown one at a time, in a randomised order. The work will be done only at NTA's designated secure facility, on workstations provided by NTA. No personal devices of any kind will be allowed in the work area.

## Who should apply

NTA has listed subject domains including school education subjects, sciences, agriculture, engineering, medical and health sciences, humanities, commerce, law, languages and literature, and fine arts. Applicants must apply for the domain in which they are qualified. The language list includes Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Urdu and several regional languages.

## How to apply

Applications are to be submitted only through NTA's online portal. NTA has stated that physical applications will not be accepted. Applicants should read the eligibility criteria and instructions given on the portal before applying.

## Points to keep in mind

NTA says submitting an application does not guarantee empanelment or allocation of assignments. Applications will be evaluated periodically, as and when required by NTA. Empanelment is valid for three years from the date of the empanelment letter, with an annual review.

For pre-application clarifications, NTA says written queries may be sent to the e-mail address notified on the NTA portal while applications remain open. Applicants are advised to visit the official NTA website regularly for updates.
