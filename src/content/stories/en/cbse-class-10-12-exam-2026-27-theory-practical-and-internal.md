## What has been issued

CBSE has issued the subject-wise marks bifurcation for Class X and Class XII Examination 2026-27. The circular lists, against each subject, the marks for theory, practical, project and internal assessment. It also states whether an external practical examiner will be appointed for a subject.

## What it says for Class 10

In most Class 10 language subjects, theory carries 80 marks and internal assessment 20 marks. The practical and project columns are blank for these, and no external practical examiner is appointed. Mathematics (Standard) has 80 theory, 50 practical and 20 internal assessment marks. Science, Social Science and Hindi Course-B have 80 theory and 20 internal assessment marks.

Some music-related subjects have a different split, such as 30 theory, 50 practical and 20 internal assessment marks.

## What it says for Class 12

In Class 12, English Language & Literature has 80 theory and 50 practical marks. Mathematics (Basic) has 80 theory and 20 internal assessment marks. Some subjects carry 70 theory marks, with 30 marks for practical.

## What it means for students

The circular helps students see how many marks the written paper carries in each subject and how many are added through practical or internal assessment. They can plan their preparation accordingly.

## What to do next

Students can check the marks for their subjects in this CBSE circular. For any question about a subject's marks or practical work, they should contact their school.
