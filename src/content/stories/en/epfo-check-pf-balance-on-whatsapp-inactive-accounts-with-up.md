## What happened

The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) marked its 74th Foundation Day on 1 November 2026 at Dr. Ambedkar International Centre in New Delhi. Union Labour and Employment Minister and Chairman of the Central Board of Trustees (EPF) Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya chaired the event.

Two major facilities were launched on the occasion — EPFO services through WhatsApp and automatic settlement of eligible inactive PF accounts.

## What the WhatsApp service offers

Through EPFO's verified WhatsApp service, members can view their PF balance, the last five transactions and passbook details. They will also get personal guidance and support for PMVBRY-related needs.

The government says this will put everyday services on a familiar mobile platform and reduce the need to visit an EPFO office.

## Money in inactive accounts

Members whose Aadhaar-verified PF account is inactive and holds up to Rs 1,000 will not need to file a separate claim. EPFO will begin the settlement process itself after the prescribed identity and bank account verification.

About one lakh accounts are expected to benefit, with a total amount of around Rs 5 crore.

## Who benefits

The facility is for people who are or have been contributing EPF members. EPFO currently serves about 8 crore members and 85 lakh pensioners, and safeguards a retirement corpus of about Rs 32 lakh crore.

The event noted that claim settlement has improved through CITES 2.01, with the average settlement time at 2.57 days in October 2026.

## What members should do

Members with an inactive account should keep their Aadhaar and bank account verification complete so they can get the benefit of automatic settlement. For the WhatsApp service, use only EPFO's verified WhatsApp channel.

## What else happened

Seven publications were released at the event, including the EPFO State Profile 2026, a compilation of judicial decisions 2026 and the first volume of The Indian Journal of Social Security. Provident Fund Awards 2026 were also given for good work by regional offices, zonal offices and individual officers and staff.
