## What happened

The Department of Pensions and Pensioners' Welfare (DoPPW) took part in the nationwide Digital Life Certificate (DLC) Mega Camp-5 held at the Divisional Railway Manager's office of Prayagraj Division on 4 November 2026. The camp was organised by the Prayagraj Division of North Central Railway with the support of the State Bank of India (SBI).

## Who benefits

The facility is meant for people who receive a pension. DoPPW Joint Secretary Shri Dhruvjyoti Sengupta spoke to pensioners at the camp and encouraged them to actively join the nationwide DLC Campaign-5 by submitting their digital life certificates using face authentication technology. He said pensioners can easily create their life certificate through a smartphone and do not need to visit banks or other offices again and again.

## What changes

A life certificate is the way a pensioner confirms that he or she is alive. It can now be created on a smartphone using face authentication, which reduces paperwork and repeated visits. Shri Sengupta spoke about the importance of digital empowerment in making life easier for senior citizens.

## What to do next

Pensioners have been urged to understand this digital facility and to encourage others, especially senior citizens, to benefit from it. Officials have been asked to speed up efforts to adopt digital services so that no eligible pensioner is left out.

## Who attended

DRM of Prayagraj Division Shri Rajnish Agarwal, UIDAI Director Shri Akash Deep, PCPO of North Central Railway headquarters Shri Mudit Chandra and other dignitaries were present at the camp. The release does not mention any last date.
