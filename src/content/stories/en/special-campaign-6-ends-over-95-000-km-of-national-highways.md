## What happened

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways has completed Special Campaign 6 (SCDPM 6) for settling pending matters. The campaign ran from 2 to 31 October 2026. According to the ministry, this year highway repair work was added to the cleanliness drive so that people get cleaner roads and a better travel experience.

## Work on the roads

As reported by regional offices, over 95,000 kilometres of national highways and service roads were made pothole-free. Road signs and markings were improved on more than 65,000 km. Plantation work was also carried out on over 7,000 km of highway medians.

## Toll plazas and roadside spots

More than 500 toilets at toll plazas were renovated and about 5,000 working toilets were cleaned. The ministry and its agencies held cleanliness activities at over 4,960 locations, including offices, construction camps and sites, toll plazas, roadside amenities, dhabas, bus stops and flyovers. Office premises also saw space management and beautification work.

## Clearing pending work

During the campaign the ministry reviewed 5,960 public grievances, 1,013 grievance appeals, 3 IMC references and 14,625 old physical and e-files. It also settled 304 pending MP references, 77 state government references, 4 parliamentary assurances and 17 PMO references. Over 1,134 kg of scrap, including e-waste, was disposed of, fetching Rs 1,20,100 in revenue.

## Why the campaign was held

The ministry carried out cleanliness, maintenance and improvement work linked to its core functions under Swachhata Hi Seva (17 September 2026 to 2 October 2026) and Special Campaign 6 (2 October 2026 to 31 October 2026). The aim was to ensure cleanliness and proper upkeep of national highways. The ministry's secretary reviewed progress every week, and the nodal officer coordinated with all agencies.

## What it means for you

This is a report on government work, with no application or deadline for the public. Travellers may see cleaner roads, better signboards and renovated toilets at toll plazas. Complaints about roads or cleanliness can be filed through the public grievance route.
