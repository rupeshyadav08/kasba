## What has happened

The Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises has signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Samsung India Electronics Private Limited. The MoU was signed in the presence of Dr Rajnish, Additional Secretary and Development Commissioner of the ministry. The aim is to give young people skills that industry needs and to open up jobs and self-employment for them.

## Where and for how long

The agreement will initially be valid for 3 years. It will be implemented at 15 MSME technology centres across the country. These include centres in Ludhiana, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Aurangabad, Delhi's Ramnagar extension centre, Chennai, Varanasi, Jamshedpur, Bengaluru, Greater Noida, Baddi and Kanpur.

## What will be taught

Training at the technology centres will cover electronics, mobile phones and home appliances, including installation, servicing and repair. The courses will follow the National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) and be industry-oriented. To keep training quality high, Samsung will provide special training, training of trainers (ToT), technical support and continuous handholding.

## What trainees get

Young people will receive hands-on practical training, industry exposure, career counselling, internship and placement support. Successful trainees will get a 3-month internship with a stipend of ₹12,000 per month paid through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Eligible women and persons with disabilities among the trainees will also be supported through scholarships, and merit-based awards are planned for top performers.

## How many youth will be covered

In the first year, the programme targets 1,600 trainees, with gradual growth in the coming years. Besides building a pool of skilled, industry-ready workers, the tie-up is meant to strengthen industry-institution links and help industries and MSMEs, especially in the electronics and home appliance service sectors, meet their need for skilled workers.

## What to do next

The notice does not say when or how applications for the training will be held. Interested youth living near the listed technology centres can contact their nearest centre for information.
