## What happened

Mera Yuva Bharat (My Bharat), under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, held a series of activities in the sixth week of the 'Viksit Bharat Ke Liye Nasha Mukt Yuva' campaign. According to a release dated 26 October 2026, provisional reports say 2,11,139 young people and citizens were connected through 694 programmes across 36 states and union territories.

## What the campaign is

This is a 100-week campaign that aims to connect young people and communities against drug use through awareness programmes, participatory activities and youth-led initiatives. The release says the goal is to encourage young people to adopt a healthy, drug-free lifestyle and to inspire others to stay away from intoxicants.

## What happened in week six

The main activities of the week were the 'burning of the Ravan of addiction/slaying of the Mahishasura of addiction', traditional sports and 'Pados Yuva Sansad' discussions on a drug-free youth for Viksit Bharat. According to the release, these culturally linked initiatives carried the message of overcoming addiction and refusing drugs. Traditional sports promoted physical fitness, a healthy lifestyle and community participation. The Pados Yuva Sansad sessions gave young people and community members a platform to discuss the harmful effects of substance use, prevention strategies and the role of youth in building a drug-free society.

## Who partnered in the programmes

The release says the programmes were held with partners including Fit India, Brahma Kumaris, Heartfulness, FICCI, CIPU, ASSOCHAM and Project Point. This collaborative approach helped bring together youth networks, community institutions and partner organisations to widen the campaign's reach and encourage collective action against substance abuse.

## Pledges and message

Under the campaign, 2,11,139 pledges were recorded towards the goal of a drug-free India. The release says the activities aimed to turn awareness into personal commitment and community-level action.

## What this means for ordinary people

The release does not announce a new government scheme, an application process or a deadline. So ordinary people have no direct paperwork or money-related step to take because of it. It only shows that anti-drug awareness programmes are being held across the country.
