## What has happened

On 28 October 2026, the National Testing Agency (NTA) issued a public notice for candidates registering for Joint Entrance Examination (Main) – 2027, Session 1. NTA said JEE (Main) 2027 will be conducted in two sessions — Session 1 in January 2027 and Session 2 in April 2027. The online application form for Session 1 is available on the NTA website https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/.

## What NTA has advised

The notice says these clarifications are meant to make registration smooth and streamlined. Candidates are advised to log in using their own DigiLocker, JanParichay or e-Pramaan (MeriPehchaan) credentials. According to NTA, this helps avoid discrepancies in identity verification and biometric data at later stages of the application process.

Candidates are also advised to do Re-KYC through DigiLocker before filling out the JEE (Main) 2027 online application form. NTA says this ensures that their latest details are fetched. The notice includes a link for the Re-KYC process.

## Care with name and date of birth

The notice states that a candidate's name and date of birth in the application form must be the same as mentioned in the Class 10 certificate or mark sheet. In other words, there should be no difference between the form and the mark sheet.

## What to do next

NTA has asked candidates to regularly visit the official NTA website for further updates, instructions or notices regarding JEE 2027. The contact details given in the notice are the website https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/ and the e-mail jeemain@nta.ac.in.

## Who is affected

This advisory is for students who will fill the JEE (Main) 2027 Session 1 form. It does not directly affect the general public — it is information meant for exam candidates only.
