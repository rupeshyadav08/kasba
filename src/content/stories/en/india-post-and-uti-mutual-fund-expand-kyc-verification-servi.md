## What happened

India Post (Department of Posts) and UTI Mutual Fund have strengthened their long-standing partnership. A memorandum of understanding was signed on 29 October 2026 at UTI AMC's corporate office in Mumbai. Shri Adnan Ahmed signed for the Department of Posts and Shri Vetri Subramaniam for UTI Asset Management Company Limited.

## What is the aim

According to the notice, the partnership aims to strengthen financial inclusion, improve investor convenience and make mutual fund investing easier to access. Both organisations will work together to widen access to financial services across the country.

## What changes in KYC verification

Under the 'Know Your Customer' pan-India KYC verification project, India Post will use its large postal network. This will allow verification at investors' doorsteps, including in remote and underserved areas. The notice says the aim is to make the verification process more convenient while following regulatory requirements.

## What happened earlier

The notice says KYC improvement efforts were made earlier as well. Those efforts helped investors complete pending KYC, improve compliance and resolve gaps in documents. The expanded cooperation is expected to give investors better service and bring more people into mutual fund investing.

## Who benefits

The direct benefit is for people who invest in mutual funds, or want to, and whose KYC is incomplete. Doorstep verification can be a convenience especially for investors in small towns and villages. The notice says the step also strengthens India Post's role in providing accessible, customer-focused financial services through its nationwide network.

## What to do next

The notice does not say when the service will start, where to contact for it, or whether any fee applies. Investors with pending KYC can ask at their nearest post office or contact UTI Mutual Fund for details.
