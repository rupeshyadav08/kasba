## What has happened

In a press release issued on November 16, 2026, IMD said a deep depression lies over the southeast and adjoining southwest Bay of Bengal. It is very likely to intensify into a cyclonic storm by the evening of November 16 and into a severe cyclonic storm by the evening of November 17.

## What happens next

According to IMD, the system will move northwestwards and may reach near the Andhra Pradesh coast as a very severe cyclonic storm around the night of November 19, 2026. Under its influence, heavy to very heavy rainfall is likely at a few places over coastal Andhra Pradesh and Yanam on November 19-20.

## Who needs to be careful

Coastal areas of Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Telangana and Odisha may experience strong winds and rain. IMD has issued warnings for wind speed and sea conditions in these areas.

## Impact on North India

This cyclone does not directly affect Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh or Delhi. However, IMD has warned of dense fog over East and West Uttar Pradesh from November 16 to 18. Delhi, Haryana and Punjab may see dense fog from November 17 to 20.
