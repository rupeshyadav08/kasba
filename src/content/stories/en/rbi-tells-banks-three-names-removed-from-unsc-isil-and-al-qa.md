## What has happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) on 27 October 2026 sent a notice to all banks and financial companies. It said the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) has removed three individuals from its ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List.

The information came to the RBI from the Ministry of External Affairs and is based on UNSC press release SC/16488 dated 26 October 2026.

## Which names were removed

According to the RBI letter, the three removed names are Shafi Sultan Mohammed Sultan Al-Ajmi, Bassam Ahmad Al-Hasri and Iyad Nazmi Salih Khalil.

The names were removed from the list linked to resolutions 1267 (1999), 1989 (2011) and 2253 (2015). The UNSC Committee took the decision after completing its consideration of a delisting request from the designating State.

## What changes now

According to the RBI, the assets freeze, travel ban and arms embargo no longer apply to the removed names. These measures were set out under UNSC resolution 2734 (2024).

## What banks have to do

The RBI asked banks to follow the procedure laid down under Section 51A of the UAPA and Chapter IX of the RBI's KYC Directions, 2025. Banks must also ensure they do not hold any account in the name of a person or entity on the UNSC list.

If a bank receives a request to remove a name from the list, it must send it electronically to the Joint Secretary (CTCR) at the Ministry of Home Affairs.

## Impact on ordinary people

This notice is mainly for banks and financial companies. Ordinary depositors, account holders and customers do not need to do anything.

The updated sanctions list can be seen on the UNSC website. A request to be removed from the list can also be made to the UN Ombudsperson, whose details are available on the UNSC website.
