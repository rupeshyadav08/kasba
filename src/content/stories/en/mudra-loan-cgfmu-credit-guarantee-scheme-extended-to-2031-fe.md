## What has happened

The Union Cabinet has approved continuing the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU) for five years, till 31 March 2031. A fund of Rs 5,000 crore has been approved for it.

The fund works through the National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Ltd (NCGTC). Its job is to assure lending institutions that if a borrower fails to repay, part of the loss will be covered.

## Which loans are covered

The guarantee covers three kinds of loans. First, loans up to Rs 20 lakh given to eligible micro enterprises under PM Mudra Yojana. Second, the overdraft facility up to Rs 10,000 that banks give under PM Jan Dhan Yojana accounts. Third, loans to self-help groups whose portfolio is between Rs 10 lakh and Rs 20 lakh.

## What has changed

The most direct change is in the guarantee fee. It will be cut from 1 per cent a year to 0.75 per cent a year. There is no change for self-help groups; they will continue to pay 0.25 per cent in the first year and 0.50 per cent in later years.

The scheme will apply to all loans given under PM Mudra Yojana during the term of the 16th Finance Commission, that is, from financial year 2026-27 to financial year 2030-31.

## How much has been done so far

Started in January 2016, CGFMU has played an important role in spreading loans under Mudra Yojana. From the scheme's launch till September 2026, lending institutions sanctioned more than 60.36 crore loans worth Rs 43.34 lakh crore.

## What happens next

According to the government, this extension will help provide Rs 12.6 lakh crore of Mudra loans over the next five years. Loans under Mudra Yojana, given without any collateral, have brought people into formal banking who were earlier left out.

## What you need to do

An ordinary borrower does not have to apply separately for this. The guarantee goes to banks and lending institutions. If you want a Mudra loan or a Jan Dhan overdraft, contact your bank branch or lending institution and get the details there.
