## What was released

Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan released the third advance estimates of area and production of horticulture crops for 2025-26 on 17 November 2026. The Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare prepared the estimates from information received from states, union territories and other government agencies.

## How much output is expected

Total horticulture output in the country is projected at 3869.15 lakh tonnes. That is 161.77 lakh tonnes, or 4.36 per cent, more than 3707.38 lakh tonnes in 2024-25. Notably, output is expected to rise even as the area falls. Total horticulture area in 2025-26 is estimated at about 297.21 lakh hectares, against 301.36 lakh hectares in 2024-25.

## Which crops are up

Fruit output is expected to rise by 54.18 lakh tonnes, or 4.60 per cent, from 1176.49 lakh tonnes to 1230.66 lakh tonnes, helped by good yields of banana, mango, papaya, apple, pomegranate and jackfruit.

Vegetable area is seen rising from 117.91 lakh hectares to 119.31 lakh hectares, and output from 2177.97 lakh tonnes to 2272.68 lakh tonnes, up 4.35 per cent. Potato output is estimated at 655.70 lakh tonnes, up 11.95 per cent from 585.71 lakh tonnes, and tomato at 214.11 lakh tonnes, up from 205.99 lakh tonnes. Onion output is seen at 288.96 lakh tonnes.

Flower output is expected to rise about 14.45 per cent from 42.65 lakh tonnes to 48.82 lakh tonnes. Plantation crops such as areca nut, coconut, cashew, betel leaves and cocoa are seen at 182.64 lakh tonnes, up 7.56 per cent. Aromatic and medicinal plants may rise from 9.01 lakh tonnes to 9.28 lakh tonnes. According to the Directorate of Arecanut and Spices Development, spices area is expected at 47.56 lakh hectares with output of 123.54 lakh tonnes.

## What it means for farmers

The minister said farmers are moving beyond traditional crops to fruits, vegetables, flowers, spices and plantation crops, which opens new sources of income. Note that this is only a production estimate. The release announces no new scheme, subsidy or payment, so farmers do not have to apply for anything.
