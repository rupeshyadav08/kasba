## What happened

The Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) held a stakeholder dialogue with exporters and government agencies in Varanasi on 20 November 2026. The meeting discussed key issues in the export of agricultural and processed food products.

The programme was chaired by APEDA Chairman Abhishek Dev. Discussions covered export facilitation, packaging, logistics, market access, regulatory requirements and institutional support.

## What was sent from Farrukhabad

At the programme, the first export shipment of frozen green peas and mixed vegetables from Farrukhabad in Uttar Pradesh was flagged off for Qatar. The shipment was flagged off virtually.

According to the release, the consignment carried 63 metric tonnes of frozen green peas and mixed vegetables. It was exported by M/s Shri Lakshmi Frozen Foods. The shipment left Farrukhabad and was sent onward through Nhava Sheva port for Doha, Qatar.

## What this means

The release calls this an important step in diversifying Uttar Pradesh's agri export products, especially value-added and frozen food items. It says Farrukhabad and nearby areas have wide potential for vegetable production and processing.

The release states that this will create new opportunities for farmers, Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), processors and exporters. In other words, the news is mainly for people connected with farming and food processing.

## What the government said

The APEDA Chairman said the authority will give member exporters the support they need. He shared information on the recently launched Bharat Krishi Niryaat Yojana (BKN Y), which aims to encourage farm exports and strengthen export infrastructure.

He also highlighted APEDA's 'Bharati' initiative, which aims to strengthen FPOs and link them to export value chains so they can reach international markets. Senior officials from APEDA, the Uttar Pradesh agriculture department, ECGC, DPPQS, AICLASS and other institutions took part, along with exporters, FPOs and representatives of the agriculture and food processing sectors.

## For ordinary readers

There is no application, deadline or rule here for ordinary consumers. Farmers, FPOs or exporters who want to move ahead can approach APEDA, which said in the release that it will provide support.
