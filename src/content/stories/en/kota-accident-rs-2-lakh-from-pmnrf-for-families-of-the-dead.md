## What happened

According to a Prime Minister's Office release dated 22 November 2026, an accident took place in Kota, Rajasthan, in which people lost their lives. Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed deep grief over the loss of lives. He offered condolences to the bereaved families and wished the injured a quick recovery.

## How much relief has been announced

The release says the Prime Minister announced ex-gratia relief from the Prime Minister's National Relief Fund (PMNRF). Rs 2 lakh will be given to the family of each person who died in the accident. The injured will be given Rs 50,000 each.

## What PMNRF is

PMNRF is the Prime Minister's National Relief Fund, a fund used to help people affected by disasters and accidents. The release says the relief for the Kota accident victims will come from this fund.

## Who is covered

The relief is for the families who lost a member in this accident and for those who were injured. The release does not say how the money will be paid, where to apply or by what date.

## What it means for ordinary readers

People who are not directly connected to this accident do not need to do anything about this announcement. The information is for the families and injured persons affected by the Kota incident.
