## What happened

The GRAP sub-committee of the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) on 17 November 2026 lifted the Stage 3 restrictions in Delhi-NCR with immediate effect. The committee met to review air quality in the region and also went through weather data and air quality forecasts from IMD/IITM.

The review found that Delhi's air quality index had improved and was recorded at 204 on 17 November 2026. That is why the restrictions issued on 6 November 2026 under Stage 3 were lifted.

## What still applies

The commission's order of 30 October 2026 will remain in force. It banned dust-generating demolition and open-area construction activities across NCR, except for exempted projects. The order is available on the CAQM website.

Under the same order, state government and private offices in Delhi and nearby districts were told to work with at most 50 per cent of their total staff from 5 November to 20 November. This was enforced under paragraphs 6(i) and 6(ii) of the order.

## What happens next

IMD/IITM forecasts the air quality index to stay in the 'poor' and 'very poor' categories in the coming days. Agencies have therefore been told to monitor the situation closely and to step up Stage 1 and Stage 2 measures in particular, so that Stage 3 restrictions do not have to be reimposed in NCR. The commission is watching the situation and will review air quality regularly in the coming days.

## What residents can do

The committee has asked citizens to follow the guidelines in the citizen charter for GRAP Stages 1 and 2. These include checking the AQI on the Sameer app and the GRAP stage in force on the CAQM website, getting vehicle engines checked regularly, keeping correct tyre pressure, making sure the pollution control certificate is valid, switching off the engine at red lights, and preferring public or shared transport.

The charter also asks people not to litter open spaces, to dispose of waste only at places fixed by municipal bodies, to clear construction and demolition waste promptly, to plant more trees, and to avoid diesel generator sets except for essential activities. It advises celebrating festivals in an eco-friendly way and avoiding firecrackers. Air-polluting activities can be reported through the 311 app, the Green Delhi app and the Sameer app. Those whose work allows it may work from home.

## Where to get more information

The current GRAP order is available on the CAQM's official website.
